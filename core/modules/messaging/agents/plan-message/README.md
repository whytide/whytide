# messaging.plan-message

The planner (alpha-p9-020), the first of the writer's two calls (F09 §5.1): from the dossier, the angle, the facts and the call to action of one message, a LinkedIn text or an email (gamma-p3-075): a lead's first, or a sequence step's (beta-p3-010). [docs/flows.md](../../docs/flows.md) says how a draft is made, [docs/security.md](../../docs/security.md) what the model sees.

- **Task:** `task.ts`: `kind: generation`, no tools, prompt version 3, a budget of 8,000 input tokens, 500 output tokens and 0 credits (not priced in alpha; the wrapper's cost event records the call). The model is `AI_MODEL_MESSAGING_PLAN_MESSAGE`, else the LLM port's first. The module never imports the file: `writeDraft` takes the task from the agent registry.
- **Prompt:** `prompt.v3.md` (gamma-p3-075; `prompt.v1.md` and `prompt.v2.md` stay for the drafts written on them): v2's rules (beta-p3-010), and only what an email and a signal's topic add. The blocks, in order: `<channel>`, `<dossier>` (the sender and the offer), `<lead>` (with their company), `<why_now>` and `<step>` (all three untrusted, the third line of the header says so), `<failures>` and `<replaced>` (`domain/drafts/blocks.ts` builds them; `schemas/writer.ts`). `<step>`, empty for a lead's draft, is a sequence step's kind and the same person's earlier approved texts: `first_message` plans no earlier conversation, call or reply; `follow_up` plans something the earlier texts did not say and no claim that the lead answered; `email_first` and `email_follow_up` the same for an email. `<channel>` is a LinkedIn text's characters, or an email's (gamma-p3-070): its body's smallest and largest number of words and, in `subject`, a first email's subject's most words and characters, null for a follow-up, which goes in the first email's thread. A signal angle is built on the post's topic: what the post was about, or what the lead said in a comment, never that they liked, reacted to or commented on it. Once drafts have run on it, a changed prompt is `prompt.v4.md` with `promptVersion: 4`, and runs the evals.
- **Output:** `angle` (`signal_comment`, `signal_reaction`, `pain_point`, `proof_point`), `factIds` (one to eight), `cta` (`question`, `soft_ask`, `resource`). The module's gate `facts_used` checks the ids against the blocks the planner read, after the call.
- **Evals:** `core/packages/ai/evals/messaging.plan-message/`, eighteen synthetic leads: alpha's ten, four with `<step>` (beta-p7-040: two `first_message`, two `follow_up`) and four emails, the email writer's leads with an email's `<channel>` (gamma-p3-075: two `email_first`, two `email_follow_up`); `outcome` and `angle` pass when the plan cites only the case's facts, `<step>` holding none, with the expected angle (`pnpm -F ai eval messaging`).
