# contacts: flows

- **Imports and signals** (alpha-p5-040, alpha-p8-030), in their own tenant run: `upsertPersonFromSource` normalizes the URL, refuses an erased hash, finds the person by the URL and writes the merge; `upsertCompany` and `linkPersonToCompany` when the source names the company; then `createLeadForPerson` with the import job's or the signal's id, which makes the lead or adds the source to it. A chunk goes through `upsertPersonsFromSource` and `createLeadsForPersons` instead (alpha-p10-080): one read of the erased hashes, one of the stored persons, one `INSERT … ON CONFLICT` of the persons, a read of their rows, then one of the leads and a read of theirs.
- **Qualification** (alpha-p6-040), in its own tenant run: `leadsPendingQualification({ limit, icpVersion, hardFilterReasons, currentSnapshotIds })`, then per lead `applyQualification` with the decision (`pending` first for a lead `hard_filtered` for a retired reason, title-check-ai).
- **Discovery** (alpha-p5-030): `isHashed({ linkedinUrls })`, one query for a file's URLs, before its rows become candidates; a rejected one is `recordHash({ linkedinUrl, kind: "rejected_candidate", dropReasons })`.
- **The routes**: the api host resolves the workspace and checks `contacts.read`, then the handler reads in the request's run.
- **The lead list** (alpha-p6-050, alpha-p10-080): `/w/{workspace}/leads?status&tier&sort&review&outreach` reads `GET …/leads/counts` and `GET …/leads` of the address's view (`status=qualified&sort=fit` without one), `limit=20`, on the server, as the member; a filter or sort change goes into the address (`history.pushState`), then each change, page, Back or Forward reads `GET …/leads` again from the browser, and the answer to the last request is the one shown. A row's link carries the view to the lead page.
- **The lead page** (alpha-p6-060, alpha-p10-080): `/w/{workspace}/leads/{personId}?status&tier&sort&review&outreach` reads, on the server as the member, `GET …/leads/{personId}`, whose name titles the document; a 404 is the page's not-found state, with nothing more read; then, at once and under the loading state, qualification's `GET …/qualification/leads/{personId}/snapshot`, `GET …/leads/{personId}/neighbours` with the query's view and linkedin's `GET …/linkedin/accounts` (beta-p2-030), a 404 of the snapshot showing the page without it. The panels in the page's slots render on the server with it, each reading its own module's routes, each slot showing the loading state until its panels answer; a slot whose panel throws shows its message and Try again, rendered in the browser, which reads the page again and renders the slot anew, and the rest of the page stays as it was.
- **Review** (alpha-p9-040): the lead page's review panel reads `GET …/leads/{personId}/reviews` on the server, as the member; Confirm posts `POST …/leads/{personId}/review` from the browser, which, in one transaction, appends the decision, makes it the lead's `review_status` and publishes `contacts.lead_reviewed`; the panel puts the answer at the head of the history. `GET …/reviews/summary?week` computes the week's numbers in code from the stored decisions.
- **Exports** (alpha-p9-050): the lead list's Export reads `GET …/exports/leads.csv` with the filters set, from the browser, and hands the answer to the browser as a file. The route writes the byte-order mark and the header, then, a hundred leads at a time by the list's order, reads the page and the latest decisions of its reviewed leads in a run of its own and writes their lines; a client that leaves stops it. Qualification's golden set reads `latestReviews({ week })` in its own run.
- **Approve** (beta-p2-030): `POST …/leads/{personId}/approve`, with an `Idempotency-Key`, in the request's run and its one transaction: `approveLead` locks the lead's row, refuses a lead in outreach, or a `suppressed` one with a change to `in_campaign` on record (`lead_in_outreach`), then asks `suppressionOf(personId, { newEnrollment: true })` and refuses a suppressed person with its reason (`person_suppressed`); then `recordLeadStatus(approved)`, the user as the actor, nothing for a lead already approved, and `contacts.lead_approved { personId, approvedBy }`, published again on a lead already approved. The same key again answers the first answer and runs nothing. Campaigns enrolls the lead on the event (beta-p2-040); without a connected account it ends the enrollment with `no_sender` and leaves the lead `approved`, for Approve again. The lead page disables Approve unless the user has a connected LinkedIn account of their own; the list's row menu does not ask.
- **A status change**: `recordLeadStatus(personId, to, { reason?, templateId?, actor })`, in the caller's run, locks the lead's row (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE`), so two changes queue, writes nothing when the status is already `to`, else sets `outreach_status` and `outreach_reason` with a raw `UPDATE` and inserts the change. Campaigns calls it for `in_campaign`, `replied` and `finished` (beta-p2-040, beta-p4-020); `GET …/leads/{personId}/status-changes` reads the hundred newest.
- **Erasure** (alpha-p10-030): the deletion script (core/modules/privacy) finds the person by id (`getPerson`) or by URL (`findPersonByLinkedinUrl`), runs the other modules' erasers, then `erasePerson`, in the same run, which writes the erased hashes, its address hashes' among them (gamma-rf-040), empties the URL of the person's suppression entries (beta-p2-010) and the address of the entries on its address hashes, keeping them, and deletes its address hashes, the review decisions, the status changes (beta-p2-030), the lead and the person, the one path that deletes a decision or a change, and returns the counts and the URL's hash for the script's log.
- **Purge**: `purgeExpiredHashes()`, per workspace, from a worker schedule later.
- **Adding to the never-contact list** (beta-p2-010): `POST …/suppression`, in the request's run: `addSuppression` finds what the entry is on (the person's profile URL; the company's domain, else its page; or the value, normalized: a person's value that is no profile is an email address, an `email` entry, gamma-rf-040), hashes it, inserts the entry with `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` on the active key, then reads the active entry with that key. The entry is on `channel`, `all` unless the add names `linkedin` or `email`, and a company's `relationship` makes a `relationship` entry on its domain (gamma-p2-040). A new one publishes `contacts.suppression_added { suppressionId, layer, channel }` in the same transaction; an existing one is answered as it is, and nothing is published. `addOptOut(personId, …)` does the same on the `opt_out` layer, always on every channel, whatever found it: a member by hand, a reply's "stop" (beta-p5-010), or, as their tasks call it, the notice's opt-out, an unsubscribe or a complaint.
- **The leads an entry blocks** (beta-p2-030): then, for an entry on every channel only, in the entry's transaction, `addSuppression` and `addOptOut` move each lead the entry holds, not yet `suppressed`, to `suppressed` with `recordLeadStatus`, in person-id order: for a person entry, the lead of the person with the profile's URL, or for an `email` entry the person who holds its hash in `person_email_hashes` (gamma-rf-040); for a company entry, the leads of the persons of each company of the workspace whose hashes hold the entry's (its domain or a subdomain, or its page). The reason is the check's word (`opted_out`, `never_contact_person`, `never_contact_company`), the actor the entry's creator, else the system. It runs on every add, an existing entry's too; a lead made after the entry keeps its status until then, and the check refuses it all the same.
- **An entry on one channel** (gamma-p2-040): a `linkedin` or `email` entry stops that channel's reads and the reads with no channel, never the other channel's, and moves no lead, since the other channel stays open.
- **Removing**: `DELETE …/suppression/{id}`, an Owner's or an Admin's (`suppression.remove`), sets `removed_at` and `removed_by` on the active `blocklist` or `relationship` entry, which stays; an opt-out is refused. A `suppressed` lead stays so: Approve moves it (beta-p2-030).
- **Importing an exclusion list** (gamma-p2-045): `POST …/exclusions/imports?relationship`, outside the request's run: `receiveFile` (`api/multipart.ts`) hands the file's bytes, as they arrive, to `readExclusionFile`, which checks them and parses the rows, each a domain (`domainOfCell`), `domain_invalid` or a `duplicate` in the file; a refusal stops the reading. Then, in one run of the request's workspace (`inRun`, 60 s), `importExclusionList` writes the list, then per 1,000 domains one `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` of the entries on the active key, whose answer tells the domains an active entry held, and moves the leads of the persons at a company of those domains or their subdomains to `suppressed` (`suppressLeadsAtDomains`, the uploader as the actor); then the list's counts and, when it added an entry, one `contacts.suppression_added` with `listId`. The screen reads the lists and the never-contact list again. review-gaps: the run takes the workspace's advisory lock first (`EXCLUSIONS_LOCK`), and each chunk first writes every domain it names, duplicates included, to `exclusion_list_domains` in one statement.
- **Removing an exclusion list**: `DELETE …/exclusions/lists/{listId}`, an Owner's or an Admin's, in a run of its own (`whytide:own-runs`, 75 s; review-gaps): `removeExclusionList` takes the workspace's advisory lock, waiting for an import of the workspace to end, sets `removed_at` and `removed_by` on the active list, moves each of its active entries whose hash another active list names in `exclusion_list_domains` under the earliest created of them, with its relationship, in one statement, then sets `removed_at` and `removed_by` on its other active entries; an entry removed by hand before stays as it was. A `suppressed` lead stays so until Approve, and the check reads the entries afresh at the next action.
- **A hand add meets a list's entry** (review-gaps): `addSuppression` on the key of an active `csv` entry takes it over in one conditional update (`source` `manual`, no list, `created_by` the adder) and answers it with `created: false`; a removal that lifted the entry first leaves the update matching nothing, and the add writes a new entry.
- **Blocking from a lead** (beta-p2-020): the lead page's Never contact menu, or a row's menu on the lead list, posts `POST …/suppression` from the browser, `{ kind: "person", personId }` or `{ kind: "company", companyId }`, with an `Idempotency-Key` per click that its retry reuses. The lead page then reads `GET …/leads/{personId}` again (`router.refresh()`), whose `suppression` is `suppressionOf(personId, { newEnrollment: false })` in the request's run, and its header shows the reason's badge; the list shows the rows the entry holds as `suppressed` until its next read (beta-p2-030). The never-contact screen reads `GET …/suppression` and `GET /v1/me` from the browser, adds by value through the same `POST` and removes with `DELETE …/suppression/{id}`, then reads the first page again.
- **The check**: every outbound path calls `suppressionOf(personId, { newEnrollment, channel? })` in its own run just before it acts (beta-p2-050): one read of the person with their last touch, one of their address hashes (gamma-rf-040), one of their company, one of the active entries holding the profile's hash, one of the address hashes or one of the company's, then the reasons in order, the entries on every channel first; an entry on an address hash is the person's own (`opted_out` or `never_contact_person`). A person with no address hash is matched as before. `suppressionsOf(personIds, …)` makes the same four reads for up to 500 persons (gamma-p2-040). Each touch then moves `last_touch_at` with `recordTouch` (beta-p4-020). `suppressedAt(personId, at, { channel? })` reads the entries as they stood at `at`, and the address hashes recorded by then, for the report on past sends (beta-p7-020), per channel when it names one.
- **Recording an address** (gamma-rf-040): enrichment's `keepAddress` calls `recordPersonEmail({ personId, address })` in the lookup's run before it stores a contact point: one read that no erased hash holds the address's hash, an `INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` of the person's row in `person_email_hashes`, a read of the holder when another row held it, then one of the active entries on every channel or on `email` holding the hash. `other_person` writes nothing; `suppressed` tells enrichment to keep nothing. An entry on every channel moves the person's lead to `suppressed`, as an add does, and publishes nothing: the gate's own check exits the enrollment at the person's next step. `pnpm -F enrichment emails:record` records every contact point kept before the deploy the same way.
