# contacts: data

Seven tenant tables in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/contacts.prisma`, created by the migrations `20261022150500_contacts_initial`, `20261022180000_contacts_leads`, `20261022210000_contacts_review_decisions`, `20261029091739_contacts_suppression` and `20261029101240_contacts_outreach_status`. Every id is `<prefix>_<ULID>` (`ids.ts`), every row has its `workspace_id`, every time is a `timestamptz`.

**PII classes** (`domain/fields.ts`; a `/// pii: <class>` line above each such column; data model §2.6): `business_contact` for the `persons` columns about the person (`linkedin_url`, `provider_refs`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `headline`, `title`, `company_name`, `location_text`, `location_country`); `personal` for `person_hashes.hash` and `suppression_entries.hash`, pseudonymous; `business_contact` for `leads.why`, whose bullets may quote the profile, for `review_decisions.reason_text` and `good_reason_text`, the tester's words, and for `suppression_entries.linkedin_url`; `none` for the rest, every `workspace_companies` and `lead_status_changes` column, every other `leads` column, `persons.last_touch_at` and `suppression_entries.domain` (as `workspace_companies.domain`) included: a lead copies no field of its person. `tests/schema.test.ts` holds the tables to this list.

**Provenance**: `field_provenance` is `{ <field>: { source, provider, at } }`, one entry per field with a value, keyed by the camelCase name; `provider_refs` is `{ <provider>: <member id> }`.

**Retention**: a person, its lead, its review decisions and its status changes until `erasePerson` (or, later, the workspace's deletion); a `rejected_candidate` hash 90 days, then `purgeExpiredHashes`; an `erased` hash for good; a suppression entry for good, a removed one with its removal.

**Suppression entries** (beta-p2-010): `hash` is `hashOf` of the normalized value, `url:` of a profile (`https://linkedin.com/in/<slug>`) or a company page (`https://linkedin.com/company/<slug>`), `domain:` of a domain (lowercased, without a scheme, `www.`, a port or a path); `linkedin_url` or `domain` keeps the value itself, for the list to show. The match reads `hash` only. `erasePerson` sets `linkedin_url` to null on every entry holding the person's profile hash, in both layers, and keeps the row and its hash, so that the person imported again is still suppressed; a company's entry stays as it is.

**Outreach status** (beta-p2-030): `recordLeadStatus` locks the lead's row (`FOR UPDATE`), writes `outreach_status` and `outreach_reason` with a raw `UPDATE`, so that `updated_at`, the list's time of qualification, stays, and inserts one `lead_status_changes` row. `erasePerson` deletes the person's changes before the lead, the one path that deletes one; its counts leave them out.

**Sorts** (`domain/leads.ts`): `fit` by `fit_score`, none last; `newest` by `created_at`; `unreviewed_first` by `review_status`, none first, then the enum's order (`good_lead`, `not_a_fit`), then as `fit`; each ties by the person id.
