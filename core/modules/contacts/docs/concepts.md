# contacts: concepts

- **Person**: someone a workspace may reach, once per workspace by the normalized LinkedIn URL. Business profile fields only: name, headline, title, employer, location; never an email, a phone, a photo, pronouns, gender, age or a special category.
- **Company**: the workspace's record of an employer, once per workspace by its LinkedIn page or its domain. A person links to one by `company_id`; `company_name` is the employer as the source names it.
- **Source and provider**: where a value came from (`linkedin`, `import`, `website`) and through which adapter (`unipile`, `csv`, or none).
- **Provenance**: for every field with a value, its source, provider and `at`, the time the source read it (the profile's `fetchedAt`, or now). A field takes the value a source brings unless the stored one is newer; a field the source leaves out or empty keeps its value and provenance. `location_country` takes the location's.
- **Normalized URL**: `https://linkedin.com/in/<slug>`, lowercased, without `www.`, the query, the fragment and the trailing slash; any other host, path or scheme is no profile (`linkedin_url_invalid`).
- **Hash**: the HMAC under `HASH_SALT` of a normalized URL or a provider id, prefixed with its kind (`url:`, `pid:`), all that is left of a person who is not a row: a `rejected_candidate` for 90 days, with its drop-reason codes counted, or an `erased` person for good. An erased hash stops every later upsert of the URL or the provider id (`person_erased`).
- **Country**: `location_country`, the ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code of the country the location names, if it names one.
- **Lead**: a person's one record in the workspace, keyed on the person, so that a second source never makes a second lead: where it came from (`sources`, `{ kind, ref, at }` each, with the `label` and `url` the module that adds it gives, a signal source's label and post; `source_label`, the first one's kind, `imported` or `signal`; `first_source_at`) and what qualification decided (the status, the fit score from 0 to 100, its confidence, the tier and up to three why bullets, the snapshot and ICP version they came from, and the reason of a hard filter or of the status). It starts `pending`; qualification moves it to `hard_filtered`, `qualified`, `not_qualified`, `needs_review` or `ai_failed`, each decision replacing the last. The person's fields are never copied into it: the list and the read join them.
- **Review decision** (alpha-p9-040): a tester's label on a lead, appended and never changed: Good lead with a sentence on why (200 characters at most), or Not a fit with one reason code and, for `other`, a text (500 at most). It keeps the lead's snapshot, fit and tier at that time, who decided and when; the lead's `review_status` and `reviewed_at` hold the latest. The fifteen codes, ten common ones, then F04 §9's five: `company_competitor`, `company_wrong_industry_or_type`, `company_wrong_size`, `company_wrong_location`, `contact_wrong_title_or_function`, `contact_wrong_seniority`, `contact_known_or_duplicate`, `data_incorrect_or_outdated`, `signal_not_relevant`, `other`, `company_existing_customer`, `company_in_open_deal`, `contact_not_decision_maker`, `contact_left_company`, `bad_timing`.
- **The weekly rate**: for an ISO week in UTC, the leads created during it that are qualified now, the top twenty by fit (ties by person id); the rate is how many of them have Good lead as their latest decision over how many there are, an unreviewed one counting as not good, and null for none. The summary also counts the decisions made during the week, the latest per lead, by kind and by reason code.
- **The list's view** (alpha-p6-050, alpha-p10-080): a status, a tier or all, Unreviewed only or not, and a sort: best fit first, newest first, or unreviewed first (the leads without a decision, then Good lead, then Not a fit, each by fit). The list's address holds it, and so do the lead page's links, so that the neighbours, the way back, a reload and Back find it.
