# contacts: concepts

- **Person**: someone a workspace may reach, once per workspace by the normalized LinkedIn URL, or, for a person without a LinkedIn profile, by the hash of an email address they hold (gamma-rf-050). Business profile fields only: name, headline, title, employer, location; never an email, a phone, a photo, pronouns, gender, age or a special category.
- **Who a person is** (gamma-rf-050): a person's keys are their URL and the hashes of their addresses (`person_email_hashes`, gamma-rf-040); every person has one at least, kept in code, since one command creates a person, in one run with their key. No URL and no address hash is ever on two persons of a workspace: an upsert whose URL and address name two persons, or whose address's person has another URL, is `person_conflict` and writes nothing, so a merge stays its own task. A person known by an address alone has a null URL: no LinkedIn link, no LinkedIn step (campaigns' `no_linkedin_profile`), never-contact entries on their address hashes, and an erasure by `pnpm erase --email` (privacy).
- **Company**: the workspace's record of an employer, once per workspace by its LinkedIn page or its domain. A person links to one by `company_id`; `company_name` is the employer as the source names it.
- **Source and provider**: where a value came from, its source class (`linkedin`, `import`, `website`, then enrichment's, gamma-en-040: `open_data`, open data such as Wikidata; `data_vendor`, any other company-data adapter's, a vendor's; `web_search`, a search's results; `ai_inferred`, what the AI concluded from the rest), and through which adapter or source, by its name (`unipile`, `csv`, `wikidata`, …), or none. The lead page names the class in words, never the provider.
- **Provenance**: for every field with a value, its source, provider and `at`, the time the source read it (the profile's `fetchedAt`, or now). A field takes the value a source brings unless the stored one is newer; a field the source leaves out or empty keeps its value and provenance. `location_country` takes the location's. A member's value (`member`, lead-fields-edit) outranks every source's, a later one too, and a field a member cleared stays empty.
- **Normalized URL**: `https://linkedin.com/in/<slug>`, lowercased, without `www.`, the query, the fragment and the trailing slash; any other host, path or scheme is no profile (`linkedin_url_invalid`).
- **Hash**: the HMAC under `HASH_SALT` of a normalized URL or a provider id, prefixed with its kind (`url:`, `pid:`, and `email:` for an address, gamma-rf-040), all that is left of a person who is not a row: a `rejected_candidate` for 90 days, with its drop-reason codes counted, or an `erased` person for good. An erased hash stops every later upsert of the URL, the provider id or the address (`person_erased`).
- **Country**: `location_country`, the ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code of the country the location names, if it names one, or that a source found apart from the location (`recordPersonCountry`, one of icp's `COUNTRIES`; gamma-en-040). A location names a country by a country's name, or, through the **place list** (gamma-en-075, README "The place list"), by a division's or a city's name: a city's name is read only when no place of that name in another country is more than a tenth as large, so an ambiguous name ("Cambridge") names none. A source's location the list cannot read keeps the country recorded for the same text.
- **Enrichment's hold** (gamma-en-040): a new lead's wait before its first judgement, `CONTACTS_ENRICHMENT_HOLD_MINUTES` (60 by default, 0 for none) from its creation, so that enrichment can fill its company and its country first. Qualification leaves a held `pending` lead out until the hold ends, enrichment releases it, or it is judged; a judged lead has none, so only a first judgement waits.
- **Company facts** (gamma-en-040): a company's `employee_count`, its headcount, and `description`, a short summary, from enrichment, with their provenance as any field's. A count given without a range writes `size_range` as the count's text (`1234`), which qualification's size filter places in its band (1001-5000).
- **A company's three layers** (gamma-rf-010): the workspace's row, with the name and the keys (the LinkedIn page, the domain), which suppression matches; the **shared company**, one per page or domain in the instance, with the facts every organization reads, those of a shared class (`open_data` and `website` by default, `CONTACTS_SHARED_FACT_SOURCES`); and the **sharing group's row**, one per organization and shared company, with the facts its own accounts, imports, vendors and members brought. A read takes each fact from the layer whose stamp wins, a member's first. A one-person company's facts describe a person, so they stay with the group, whatever their class.
- **Lead**: a person's one record in the workspace, keyed on the person, so that a second source never makes a second lead: where it came from (`sources`, `{ kind, ref, at }` each, with the `label` and `url` the module that adds it gives, a signal source's label and post; `source_label`, the first one's kind, `imported`, `signal` or `manual`, a member's by hand on the Leads screen (lead-manual-create), whose `ref` is the member's user id; `first_source_at`) and what qualification decided (the status, the fit score from 0 to 100, its confidence, the tier and up to three why bullets, the snapshot and ICP version they came from, and the reason of a hard filter or of the status). It starts `pending`; qualification moves it to `hard_filtered`, `qualified`, `not_qualified`, `needs_review` or `ai_failed`, each decision replacing the last. The person's fields are never copied into it: the list and the read join them.
- **Review decision** (alpha-p9-040): a tester's label on a lead, appended and never changed: Good lead with a sentence on why (200 characters at most), or Not a fit with one reason code and, for `other`, a text (500 at most). It keeps the lead's snapshot, fit and tier at that time, who decided and when; the lead's `review_status` and `reviewed_at` hold the latest. The fifteen codes, ten common ones, then F04 §9's five: `company_competitor`, `company_wrong_industry_or_type`, `company_wrong_size`, `company_wrong_location`, `contact_wrong_title_or_function`, `contact_wrong_seniority`, `contact_known_or_duplicate`, `data_incorrect_or_outdated`, `signal_not_relevant`, `other`, `company_existing_customer`, `company_in_open_deal`, `contact_not_decision_maker`, `contact_left_company`, `bad_timing`.
- **The weekly rate**: for an ISO week in UTC, the leads created during it that are qualified now, the top twenty by fit (ties by person id); the rate is how many of them have Good lead as their latest decision over how many there are, an unreviewed one counting as not good, and null for none. The summary also counts the decisions made during the week, the latest per lead, by kind and by reason code.
- **The list's view** (alpha-p6-050, alpha-p10-080, beta-p2-030): a status, a tier or all, Unreviewed only or not, an outreach status or all, and a sort: best fit first, newest first, or unreviewed first (the leads without a decision, then Good lead, then Not a fit, each by fit). The list's address holds it, and so do the lead page's links, so that the neighbours, the way back, a reload and Back find it.
- **Suppression entry** (beta-p2-010): a row of the workspace's never-contact list, on a person, by their profile URL, or on a company, by its domain, else its LinkedIn page (`https://linkedin.com/company/<slug>`). An entry on a domain holds for its subdomains too, never for a lookalike or a parent. It matches by `hash`, `hashOf` of the normalized value (`url:` for a profile or a page, `domain:` for a domain), never by the value itself. A removed entry keeps its row with `removed_at` and `removed_by`, so the list can be read as it stood at any instant.
- **Layer**: `blocklist`, the never-contact list a member adds to and an Owner or Admin removes from; `opt_out`, a person who asked not to be contacted, by hand or found by the system (`reason` `reply_opt_out`, `email_unsubscribe`, `notice_opt_out` or `complaint`), never removed; or `relationship` (gamma-p2-040), a company excluded by its domain as a customer or a competitor. The layer is part of the active entry's key, so a blocked person can also opt out, and removing the block leaves the opt-out.
- **Channel** (gamma-p2-040): what an entry stops, `all` (every channel, the default), `linkedin` or `email` alone, for a tester's own choice such as "no email, LinkedIn is fine". An opt-out always stops every channel.
- **Relationship** (gamma-p2-040): `customer` or `competitor`, the kind of a `relationship` entry, a company the workspace never reaches, matched by its domain and its subdomains as a company entry is.
- **Exclusion list** (gamma-p2-045): the companies of one CSV file a member imported, as customers or as competitors, each a `relationship` entry with the list's id, removed as a whole by an Owner or an Admin.
- **Suppressed**: whether outreach to a person is refused, and why: the first of `person_not_found` (an unknown id), then on the entries on every channel `opted_out`, `never_contact_person`, `never_contact_company` (a relationship entry's too), then, for a new enrollment only, `recently_contacted`, then an entry on one channel. A read on a channel counts the entries on it and on `all` and names the channel that decided; a read with none counts every entry.
- **Recent touch**: `persons.last_touch_at`, the person's latest outbound touch, which only ever moves later. A person touched less than `CONTACTS_RECENT_TOUCH_DAYS` days ago, 90 unset (README.md "Settings"), is refused a new enrollment; the next step of an enrollment is not.
- **Outreach status** (beta-p2-030): where a lead stands in outreach, apart from its qualification and its review: `pending_review` (to approve, where every lead starts), `approved`, `in_campaign`, `replied` or `finished` (the last three in outreach), or `suppressed`, which a never-contact entry or an opt-out sets from any state; `outreach_reason` is the latest change's code, such as `opted_out`. Every change is kept as a **status change**: from and to, the reason, the campaign's template, and who made it, a user or the system. A change to the status the lead already has is none.
- **Approve** (beta-p2-030): a member's go to reach one lead, apart from review and qualification: from `pending_review`; again from `approved`, when no enrollment followed; from `suppressed` once the never-contact check allows a new enrollment and the lead was never `in_campaign`. It is refused for a lead in outreach (`lead_in_outreach`) and for a person the check refuses as a new enrollment (`person_suppressed`, `recently_contacted` included). Campaigns enrolls an approved lead, once at most, since Approve is refused after `in_campaign`.
- **A company's name and keys** (gamma-en-050): the name, the LinkedIn page and the domain find a company, so one equal to the stored value keeps its provenance when another source brings it; the source and provider that stamped it take the stamp again. Enrichment writes a company's facts with its stored name and keys, and so never re-stamps them.
