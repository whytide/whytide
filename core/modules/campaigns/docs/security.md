# campaigns: security

- **Personal data:** none stored. The tables hold ids, codes, hashes, times and the template's ids ([data.md](data.md)); no name, URL or message text, in a row, an event, a workflow input or a log line. A reason code is a word of a closed list, checked before it is written, and an error names the column, never the value.
- **Who:** `campaigns.read` for the enrollments route, which answers ids, codes, instants and the sender's zone, read in the request's tenant run: another workspace's rows never come, and a person the workspace does not hold is an empty list, never a 404. `campaigns.write` (Owner, Admin, Member) for the pause and resume routes of a campaign and of an enrollment and for the sequence, the name and the launch, each in the request's tenant run, so another workspace's id is a 404; then the row's check (gamma-p6-015): platform's `canWriteOwned` allows the campaign's owner and a holder of `campaigns.manage` (Owner, Admin), in `lockCampaign` and, for an enrollment, `assertEnrollmentWriter`, so a Member gets 403 `forbidden` on another member's campaign, its enrollments and the seeded campaign, before anything is written (`core/apps/api/tests/members-own-agents.test.ts`, `campaigns-pause.test.ts`). A pause only stops; a resume only clears the pause and the steps' codes, and every step still passes its approval, the plan and the gate. Enrolling and the suppression subscriber run only in the worker, for the event's workspace, as `service:worker`, through the tenant client.
- **The send path:** a step goes out only from its account's sender, through linkedin's send activities, after the gate passed twice, at the claim and right before the call, and both reservations, the credits and linkedin's slot, which every refusal and block releases (`core/apps/worker/tests/safety/sender-workflow.test.ts`). Every step starts `planned`; only the decision subscriber moves one to `scheduled`, on its item approved by the sender account's owner, and the gate's last check finds that approval again, with the text unchanged since, right before the send, for every action but a withdrawal (failing closed for an action added later) (`not_approved`, `content_changed`, `tests/safety/approval-check.test.ts`). A suppressed person, opted out, never to be contacted or touched in the last 90 days (contacts' `CONTACTS_RECENT_TOUCH_DAYS` unset), gets no step at all, read at the delivery with contacts' `suppressionOf`; the gate checks again just before each send (beta-p2-050).
- **Twice:** a person has one `active` enrollment at most (a partial unique index), a redelivered event changes nothing (`source_event_id`), and the database refuses a second row with a key entering `executing` while one is `executing` or `succeeded`, so the same step content is never sent twice to a person (`tests/safety/enrollment.test.ts`).
- **Sender:** an enrollment goes out only from its campaign owner's own connected account, never the approver's, and its email steps only from that member's mailbox (gamma-p6-040); an owner without an account, or no longer an active member of the workspace, exits it `no_sender`. A removed member's live enrollments end at once (the `tenancy.member_removed` subscriber), and the gate reads the sender's owner's membership again right before each send, before any reservation (`owner_removed`, `sender_mismatch`; `core/apps/worker/tests/safety/senders-per-member.test.ts`). Each account's plan run holds an advisory lock on it, so two attempts never place its steps together.
- **Erasure:** `erasePerson` deletes the person's enrollments and steps (privacy's deletion script).
- **Stops:** the gate (`checkGate`) checks the enrollment, the suppression, a reply since the start and the day's touch right before each send, in the sender's run and with no cache, so an entry or a reply committed a moment before stops the step; a blocked step never reaches linkedin's reservation, so it takes no slot and no credit. A reply holds every pending step of the person in the transaction that stores it (`holdForReply`), and a suppression added exits each affected active enrollment (the `contacts.suppression_added` subscriber); each stop blocks the person's pending approval items in its transaction, so a late approval answers 409. Nothing but the sender settles an `executing` step (`tests/safety/gate.test.ts`).
- **Erasure order:** privacy erases campaigns' rows after the platform's outbox events, so an erasure during a `contacts.lead_approved` delivery also takes the enrollment it wrote.
- **The plan:** a step reaches the sender only with a time inside its account's window, Monday to Friday in the account's zone, within `remainingToday`'s numbers and the actions left of the 200, a gap from every other time of the account, and one per person a day, never on a day contacts counts a touch. The plan writes nothing but a `scheduled` step's time and quota day, never an `executing` step (`tests/safety/plan-bounds.test.ts`). It only keeps steps from being refused: the caps and the hours hold, whoever asks, through linkedin's `reserveSend` (beta-p1-030). The leads' priorities (gamma-p5-070, qualification's `leadPriorities`) order the candidates inside messages and inside invites, and nothing else: whatever they are, every rule above holds, and a kept step keeps its time and its room; the safety case gives its persons random priorities, some none, and checks that no kept step is moved, or released for a candidate.
- **The first message:** never due under 10 minutes after the acceptance's detection, nor outside the sender account's working hours or days, nor on a Saturday or Sunday in its zone; inside the window, 1 to 3 hours after it (`firstMessageDueAt`, `tests/safety/first-message-timing.test.ts`). The acceptance subscriber writes the `connect` step's `accepted_at` and `message_1`'s first `due_at`, nothing else: no status, no provider call. The step still waits for its owner's approval, the plan's time in the window and the gate, and a second acceptance moves nothing. A connection request to a connection already (connect-already-connected) is never sent twice nor counted as sent: LinkedIn's refusal, or the read before it, skips it (`already_connected`) with its credits freed, and `message_1` falls due by the same rule from that settle's time, then passes every gate as after an acceptance (`core/apps/worker/tests/safety/already-connected.test.ts`).
- **The sender's history:** `campaigns_sender`'s inputs, results and history hold ids, codes, booleans, counts, instants, the request file's key and the approved text's hash: never a text, a note or a member ref (`core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-sender.test.ts` reads the history back). Its one log line of its own, `campaigns: send_uncharged`, names the step and the usage only.
- **The request file:** the text and the member ref reach linkedin's activity only in `raw/campaigns/<stepExecutionId>/send.json`, in the workspace's files: written after the run that read them, never inside one; under `raw/`, whose files live 72 hours at most (the files port's lifecycle); deleted once the step is settled or blocked, and kept only by a step left `executing`, for beta-p4-040's check. The gate right before the call hashes the file's text against the approved hash, so a file changed after the approval sends nothing (`content_changed`).
- **Twice, in the sender:** one run per account (`oneAtATime`); the claim conditional on the status and the time read; linkedin's activity checks before any call on a retry or a step claimed again (`stepAttempt` above 1); a send whose outcome is not known stays `executing` with its key, never `failed`; a send that went out is always recorded, `succeeded` even when its credits' lease has ended (logged uncharged).
- **Withdrawals:** a connection request is withdrawn only by its account's sender, as any send: the plan's time in the window, the gate twice, linkedin's `reserveSend` with its 15 a day, the 200, the weekdays and the switches, then `linkedin_withdraw_invite`. No approval item and no credits, as it reaches nobody's inbox; never one accepted (`accepted_at`, read by the job and again by the gate); once per enrollment (`<connect key>:withdraw`); and the touch it records refuses the person a new enrollment for 90 days, `CONTACTS_RECENT_TOUCH_DAYS` unset (`core/apps/worker/tests/safety/withdrawal.test.ts`).
- **The profile read (beta-p4-030):** the person's stored URL reaches linkedin's read only in `raw/campaigns/<stepExecutionId>/profile.request.json`, and the profile, member id included, comes back only in `raw/campaigns/<stepExecutionId>/profile.json`: both written outside the runs, under `raw/` (72 hours at most), and both deleted by `campaigns_profile_settle` whatever the answer; a run failing before the settle leaves them to `raw/`'s lifecycle. The workflow carries their keys, the read's answer (its key or a code) and the settle's `{ stored, readAt, waitMs }` only, and the member id is written to contacts' `providerRefs` alone (`core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-member-reference.test.ts` reads the history, the log lines and the events back). Every read is reserved first, no invite follows one within 3 minutes, and the gate runs again after the wait (`core/apps/worker/tests/safety/profile-read-before-invite.test.ts`).
- **The recovery:** a send activity has one attempt, never retried by Temporal; a step whose answer was lost stays `executing` until the account's next sender run checks it with linkedin (`linkedin_check_sent`) before any claim, at most once a quota day while the provider fails, so a crash, a 5xx or an outage never sends twice nor loops; the gate before the call is asked again when its pass is stale, and a step left `executing` counts in the account's gap (`core/apps/worker/tests/safety/recovery.test.ts`, beta-p4-040).
- **Versions and email steps (gamma-p3-030):** an edit never reaches an enrollment in flight: `app` never updates or deletes a version's rows (`REVOKE`), and an enrollment's steps, keys and due times are its own version's. A step unchanged across versions keeps its hash and so its key, and the partial key still refuses the same content twice to a person in one campaign. An email step has no sender account (the CHECK `step_executions_channel_actor`), and the queue, the plan, the claim and the sender's next step read LinkedIn's actions only, so nothing sends one before gamma-p4-010 (`tests/safety/versions.test.ts`). The campaigns' routes answer ids, codes, numbers, delays and the campaign's name, in the request's tenant run.
- **Reply actions (gamma-p4-050):** nothing lifts a suppression. An `opt_out` label, suggested or not, adds the opt-out on every channel and cancels every held step of the person in the caller's run; releasing an out-of-office hold reads the suppression first and exits a suppressed person, and the queue run exits a paused enrollment whose person is suppressed before it resumes any. A released step goes back `planned` with no item, so it sends only after a new item, its approval and the gate, and a step whose item was approved before the reply keeps the hold; the gate's reply check counts any reply after `resumed_at` (`tests/safety/reply-actions.test.ts`). The new columns hold ids, codes and instants (`remind_user_id`, a user's id). The notices carry ids in the event, the workflow's input and its history; the mail goes through the app-mail port to the user the event names, only while they are an enabled member of the workspace, with the person's first name (one line, 50 characters at most) and a link, never the reply's text or another name (`core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-reply-notice.test.ts`). The port takes no idempotency key: an attempt that fails after its send may mail twice, as inbox's reply mail.
- **The launch (gamma-p3-050):** a campaign leaves `draft` only through `launchCampaign`, which locks its row and reads every check again in that run, never the screen's answer: the owner's LinkedIn account connected, not paused and healthy, its sending disclosure accepted, and credits for a send; a failing one refuses the launch and writes nothing (`tests/safety/launch.test.ts`). The checks gate the launch only: linkedin's reservation, the gate and the credits' reserve still refuse each send on their own. No email goes from a launched campaign before gamma-p4-010, so a launch reads no mailbox until the email phase adds its check. The editor's save writes a new version only, never an enrollment, and its routes answer ids, codes, numbers, delays and the campaign's name, under `campaigns.write` for the writes, in the request's tenant run.
