# campaigns: concepts

- **Template:** the one fixed sequence of beta, `linkedin_default` version 1: a connection request, then three messages, each step with its key (`step_key`), position, action, channel and delay (README.md "Campaigns and versions"). Since gamma-p3-030 it is the seeded campaign's version 1.
- **Campaign:** a named sequence of a workspace (`cmp_…`), `draft`, `active`, `paused`, `completed` or `archived`, owned by the member whose accounts it sends from (`owner_user_id`), with its versions; its `key_ref` is what enrollments and step executions carry as `template_id`: `linkedin_default` for the seeded campaign, its own id for any other.
- **Campaign version:** one saved state of a campaign's steps (`cmv_…`), numbered from 1 (`version_no`, `initial` then `structural`), never changed once saved. A save with other steps makes the next version, which only enrollments made after it join; the campaign's `current_version_id` names the one new enrollments join.
- **Step:** one step of a version (`stp_…`): its position, its key (`STEP_KEYS`, given by action and order), its action (`invite`, `message` or `email`), its channel (`linkedin`, `email`), its delay and its hash. An enrollment writes a step execution per step of its version.
- **Seeded campaign:** the workspace's campaign made on first use from beta's template (`linkedin_default`, an empty name, `active`, owned by the user of the workspace's oldest `Owner` membership), with version 1 by `system`; every enrollment joins its current version. Beta's enrollments read as its version 1, with nothing rewritten.
- **Enrollment:** a person's way through a campaign's version, one row per approval delivered: `active` while it runs, `paused` while a member or an out-of-office reply holds it back (gamma-p3-060), then `replied`, `completed` or `exited` with an exit reason (`suppressed`, `removed`, `no_sender`, `not_accepted`, `send_refused`). A person has one live enrollment at most, `active` or `paused`; an ended one stays.
- **Step execution:** one step of an enrollment for its person, sent or not: `planned` when written; the queue moves it to `awaiting_approval` with its approval item, the owner's decision to `scheduled` or `skipped`; the sender's claim to `executing`, and its settle to `succeeded`, back to `scheduled`, or `failed` for a send LinkedIn refused for good, a person not found or a request off the port's schema (beta-p4-040), which ends the enrollment; a stop to `held` or `cancelled`. It keeps the enrollment's sender, campaign and version, and the step's hash; an email step has no sender account, and nothing sends one before gamma-p4-010.
- **Step template hash:** the SHA-256 of the step's canonical definition (`step_key`, `action`, `delay`, `channel`, JSON with sorted keys): a changed delay or action is a new hash.
- **Idempotency key:** the SHA-256 of the workspace, the person, the campaign's `key_ref` and the step's hash, joined by `:`. The same step content for the same person in one campaign has the same key, across its versions; at most one row with a key is `executing` or `succeeded`, so a step is never sent twice, while unsent rows with the key may stand.
- **Sender:** the campaign owner's own connected LinkedIn account (linkedin's `senderOf`; gamma-p6-040, beta's enrollments keeping the approver's), the account every LinkedIn step of the enrollment goes out from; its email steps go from the same member's mailbox.
- **Reason codes:** what a step's `skip_reason` and `error_code`, and an enrollment's `exit_detail`, may hold: the lists of `domain/steps/constants.ts`, each code a word the panel translates.
- **Gate:** the just-in-time check campaigns' own sender (beta-p4-020) runs on a step right before the call, in its own run, reading the database each time with no cache: the step `scheduled` (or `executing` under the sender's claim), the enrollment `active`, the person not suppressed, no inbound message since the enrollment began, no touch on today's quota day in the sender's zone, and, for every step but a withdrawal, its approval. The first check that fails decides; a block writes its outcome and `jit_checks` on the step, a pass writes nothing. A blocked step never reaches linkedin's reservation, so it takes no slot and no credit.
- **Hold:** what a reply does to the person, in the transaction that records it: every pending step (`planned`, `awaiting_approval`, `scheduled`) of the person, in every enrollment, becomes `held` with `skip_reason` `replied` and its pending approval item blocked, each active enrollment `replied`, and the lead `replied` unless it is suppressed. Held steps keep their rows, so gamma can resume them.
- **Stops:** the hold, a suppression (the enrollment `exited`, `suppressed`, its pending steps `cancelled` with the reason) and an ended enrollment (its step `held` after a reply, `cancelled` otherwise). None of them touches an `executing` step but the gate, which only the sender calls: the sender settles its own claim.
- **Enrollment panel:** the lead page's view of a person's enrollments, newest first (README.md "Screens"): each one's status and why it stopped, and its steps with their status, their time in the sender's zone, named, and why one was skipped, held or waits, every code in words.
- **Daily plan:** every 15 minutes, for each workspace, the times of each sender account's due `scheduled` steps, drawn in what is left of the account's window today ([flows.md](flows.md) "The daily plan").
- **Placed step:** a `scheduled` step with a `scheduled_for`, the time the plan drew in its sender account's window, and that time's `quota_day`. It keeps its time until the sender claims it; more than a gap behind, or on an earlier quota day, it is placed again.
- **Quota day:** the sender account's local date of a time, `YYYY-MM-DD`, as linkedin's quota day; the plan places on the current one only.
- **Gap:** `MIN_ACTION_GAP_MS`, 40 seconds: the least time between two actions of one account, placed or sent. The sender waits one before each claim too (beta-p4-020).
- **Queue window:** approvals' `queueWindowHours`, its setting `APPROVALS_QUEUE_WINDOW_HOURS`, 24 unset. Every 15 minutes the queue opens an item in "Up next" for each `planned` connection request or message of an active enrollment due within it, written as the sender account's owner ([flows.md](flows.md) "The queue"); a step due later waits for a later tick. An item not decided by its day stays, and "Up next" flags it stale.
- **Approval:** the sender account's owner's decision on a step's item. Approved, the step is `scheduled` with the draft; skipped, it is `skipped` (`skipped_by_owner`), with what follows ([flows.md](flows.md) "The decision"). The gate's last check finds the approval again and the text unchanged since (`not_approved`, `content_changed`); a withdrawal sends nothing to the person and needs none, and every other action needs one, an action added later included.
- **Detection time:** when Whytide learned that a person accepted the account's connection request, `detected_at` of linkedin's `linkedin.invite_accepted`: at the provider's event, or at linkedin's daily check for one the provider missed. An invite without a note can be reported hours after LinkedIn's own time of the acceptance, which linkedin keeps; the first message counts from the detection (docs/07 §5.3).
- **`accepted_at`:** on the `connect` step, the detection time, written once by the acceptance subscriber on a request that went out; its status stays. `message_1` then falls due 1 to 3 hours later, never under 10 minutes, inside the sender's working hours on a weekday ([flows.md](flows.md) "Acceptance → the first message").
- **Sender run:** the account's workflow, `campaigns_sender`, one run at a time per account, which the plan starts once it placed a step for today: the account's one writer of its sends, which takes its placed steps one at a time, a gap apart ([flows.md](flows.md) "The sender").
- **Claim:** the sender's move of a placed step from `scheduled` to `executing`, after the gate passes, conditional on the status and the time it read; in the same run, the step's credits and linkedin's slot are reserved. A claimed step is the sender's alone: the stops leave it, and only the settle moves it on.
- **Attempt:** `attempt`, the claims a step has had, raised at each one. The credits' key carries it (`<idempotency_key>:<attempt>`), so a step released and claimed again reserves new credits; linkedin's send activity checks before any call when it is above 1.
- **Request file:** `raw/campaigns/<stepExecutionId>/send.json`, in the workspace's files, in linkedin's `sendRequestFile` shape: the person's member id, the approved note or text and the step's key. It is the one place the text and the member ref travel to linkedin's activity; the workflow carries its key only, and it is deleted once the step is settled.
- **Counts:** the counts page's numbers per sender account (README.md "Routes", "Screens"): a Member's own accounts', an Owner's or an Admin's every member's (gamma-p6-070). Connection requests and messages sent: the steps of that action `succeeded`, by `executed_at`, the sender's settle's. Invitations accepted: the `connect` steps with an `accepted_at`, by it, the detection time. People who replied: the enrollments `replied`, by `ended_at`, the hold's. Every other step status and a withdrawal count in none, nor does a reply after the sequence completed, which leaves the enrollment `completed` (the inbox shows it).
- **Counted periods:** today, from the sender account's local midnight; the last 7 days, from the local midnight six days before it, so today and the six days before; since the start, every row. The days are the account's, from platform's `quotaDay` and `instantAt`, whatever the reader's zone: a send at 23:30 local yesterday is in the last 7 days and not today. An account linkedin no longer holds has its days in UTC.
- **The funnel:** the Results page's numbers per campaign (README.md "Routes", "Screens"; gamma-p6-080), each a count of the campaign's enrollments (`template_id`, its `key_ref`), each enrollment once per stage, however many steps it sent. Enrolled: those with `started_at`. Sent, per channel: those with a step `succeeded` on it (`invite` and `message` LinkedIn's, `email` email's; a withdrawal never). Accepted: those whose connection request has an `accepted_at`; email has none. Replied: those whose latest reply label is set and neither `out_of_office` nor `bounce`, on the channel of the last step sent before the reply's instant, on none when nothing was (the totals alone). Positive: of those, the latest label `interested`, so a member's correction counts. A share is a number over the stage above it with a number, on the same channel.
- **Withdraw step:** the step the withdrawal job adds to an `active` enrollment whose connection request is still not accepted 21 days after it went out (README.md "Withdrawals"): `step_key` `withdraw`, `position` 1, `action_type` `withdraw`, the request's hash, `scheduled` and due at once, with no approval item and no credits. It goes through the plan, the gate and linkedin's reservation as any send, and ends `succeeded`, `failed` when the invitation was already gone (`linkedin_not_found`, `linkedin_send_refused`), or `cancelled` (`invite_accepted`) when the request was accepted before the call. The first two end the enrollment `not_accepted` and finish the lead; the touch keeps the person from a new enrollment for 90 days ([flows.md](flows.md) "The withdrawal").
- **Withdrawal key:** the withdraw step's idempotency key, the connection request's followed by `:withdraw`: it names the request it takes back, and with the one step per key in an enrollment (`step_key`), an enrollment has one withdrawal at most, whatever the job's runs.
- **Member ref:** the person's member id at the LinkedIn provider, which a send's request file needs: contacts' `providerRefs` under `MEMBER_REF_KEY` (`unipile`), never a column of campaigns. A connection request whose person has none is preceded by one profile read (beta-p4-030), whose settle stores it through contacts at the person's stored URL, so a changed profile URL never makes a second person. It travels to linkedin only inside files under `raw/`, never in a workflow's input, result or history, a log line or an event.
- **Profile read:** that read, reserved in linkedin's quota after the step's credits and before its slot, logged as `read_profile` and counted in the day's reads and the 200, made by linkedin's `linkedin_read_profile` through two files, `raw/campaigns/<stepExecutionId>/profile.request.json` and `profile.json`. The invite follows it `MIN_MINUTES_AFTER_PROFILE_READ` (3 minutes) later at the earliest, after the gate again; a profile not found fails the step (`profile_unreadable`) and ends the enrollment (`send_refused`).
- **Check:** linkedin's `linkedin_check_sent` on a step left `executing`, run by the account's sender before its first claim (beta-p4-040; [flows.md](flows.md) "The check first"): sent, not sent, refused, or failed at the provider; while it fails, once a quota day.
- **Session's end:** when a sender run stops before its steps run out (`end` `refused` or `unsettled`): a refusal with no time or `rate_limited`, `insufficient_credits`, a port's error that touches the account (`SEND_ERROR_MAP`'s account effect), an unknown outcome, or a check that waits; the plan starts the next run at a later tick. A send refused for good, a person not found or a request off the schema fail that step only, and the run goes on.
- **Warm signal:** the default of a campaign made without steps (`WARM_SIGNAL_STEPS`): a connection request without a note and three LinkedIn messages, then the fallback's three emails (README.md "The warm-signal default").
- **Condition:** a step's `condition`, null for none; gamma has one, `invite_not_accepted_within`: the step goes while the connection request is not accepted.
- **Fallback:** the emails marked with the condition, the last steps of their version: the first due 7 calendar days after the connection request went out, the next ones after the step before; an acceptance skips every one still pending (`invite_accepted`), and a skipped one leaves the LinkedIn steps as they are (README.md "The fallback").
- **Paused:** an enrollment held back by a member (`user`) or an out-of-office reply (`out_of_office`), until `paused_until` or a resume; or a campaign paused by a member or its bounce rate, `paused_at`. Nothing of it is opened, placed or claimed meanwhile, its steps waiting with `enrollment_paused` or `campaign_not_active`; it stays live for every stop (README.md "Pausing").
- **Reply label:** what inbox's classification says a reply is (`REPLY_ACTION_LABELS`), and its default action on the person's held enrollments (README.md "Reply actions", gamma-p4-050): a notice, a reminder in `CAMPAIGNS_NOT_NOW_REMINDER_DAYS` (30 unset) days, a cool-down of `CAMPAIGNS_NOT_INTERESTED_COOL_DOWN_DAYS` (180 unset) days, an out-of-office pause, or an opt-out. An out-of-office label releases the hold (`resumed_at`): the held steps wait again as new, each through a new approval and the gate.
