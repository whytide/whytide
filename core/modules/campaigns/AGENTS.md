# campaigns: the brief for a coding agent

1. Read `README.md`, then `docs/concepts.md`, `docs/data.md`, `docs/flows.md`, `docs/security.md` and `docs/runbook.md`. They say what is true now and change in the same merge request as the behavior.
2. The rules: [coding](../../../docs/rules/coding.md), [security](../../../docs/rules/security.md), [delivery](../../../docs/rules/delivery.md). The shape of this folder is [architecture §4.2](../../../docs/architecture.md#42-inside-a-module): rules in `domain/` without a framework, Zod schemas in `schemas/`, NestJS in `api/`, Temporal in `workflows/`, agent tasks in `agents/` (importing `@whytide/ai`, never `@mastra/*`), Next.js in `ui/`, every screen string a key in `messages/en.json`, the registries in `permissions.ts`, `prices.ts`, `events.ts` and `settings.ts`, the tests in `tests/`.
3. Commands, from the repository root: `pnpm -F campaigns build`, `typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, `test:tenancy` and `test:safety`; then `pnpm -F worker test` for the subscriber, which loads this module's `dist/`.
4. Stay inside this folder and `campaigns.prisma`. A change to a shared package is its own task.
5. No foreign key to another module's table: `person_id`, `sender_account_id` and the other ids of another module are plain ids. The one foreign key, a step's `(workspace_id, enrollment_id)`, stays inside the module.
6. No message text in an event, a workflow input or this module's tables: ids and codes only. A step's text is messaging's draft, read by id.
7. Every new step starts `planned`. A code in `skip_reason`, `error_code` or `exit_detail` is one of `domain/steps/constants.ts`, checked with `checkedCode`; a new code goes into that list with its key in the panel's map.
8. Every send passes `checkGate` right before the call, in the sender's run, and reserves only after a pass. Nothing but the sender settles an `executing` step: the hold and the suppression subscriber leave it.
9. The enrollments route and the lead page's panel (README.md "Routes", "Screens"): their tests are the api's `tests/campaigns-enrollments.test.ts` and the web's `tests/campaigns-enrollment-panel.spec.ts`; a new reason code also gets its key in `ui/enrollment-panel/words.ts` and `messages/en.json`.
10. Only the plan writes a step's `scheduled_for` and `quota_day`, and only on a `scheduled` step. A delay of hours or days is a row a 15-minute tick reads, never a Temporal timer (architecture §8).
11. Every path that holds, cancels or skips a step with an open approval item blocks that item in the same transaction with approvals' `cancelItems`, as the hold, the suppression subscriber, the gate's suppression branch and the skip do. A step reaches `scheduled` only through the decision subscriber, on its approved item; `cancelItems` leaves an approved item approved, so the gate reads the step's own status before its approval check.
12. Only the acceptance subscriber writes the `connect` step's `accepted_at` and `message_1`'s first `due_at` (`domain/acceptance.ts`), each once and conditional on the row as read; the only steps whose status it changes are the pending fallback emails, `skipped` (`invite_accepted`, gamma-p3-060), which ends the enrollment when nothing of it is left open. The first message counts from the detection, never from LinkedIn's time of the acceptance. The fallback's first `due_at` is the connection request's settle's, from its `executed_at` (README.md "The fallback").
13. A step is sent only by its account's sender (`campaigns_sender`), through linkedin's send activities called by name, after the gate's pass right before the call and both reservations, credits first. The text and the member ref travel only in the request file under `raw/`, never in a workflow's input, result or history, a row or a log line. A step whose send may have gone out stays `executing`, never `failed`, which would free its key; the sender clears a step's time when the step waits again, and never sets one.
14. The counts route and page (README.md "Routes", "Screens"): their tests are `tests/counts.test.ts`, the api's `tests/campaigns-counts.test.ts` and the web's `tests/campaigns-counts.spec.ts`. A send counts once it is `succeeded`, by its `executed_at`, so a new step status or action counts in no number until `domain/counts.ts` names it.
15. Withdrawals (README.md "Withdrawals") are the one exception to 7, 11 and 13: only the withdrawal job writes a `withdraw` step, already `scheduled`, with no item and no credits, once per enrollment; the gate checks its request's `accepted_at` instead of an approval; and an answer after the call that the invitation is gone (`linkedin_not_found`, `linkedin_send_refused`) marks it `failed`, since nothing went out.
16. Only the sender settles an `executing` step, at its check before the first claim, by linkedin's answer and its error map (README.md "Worker", beta-p4-040): no Temporal retry of a send activity, no settle by hand or by another workflow, and a change to the check, the error map's settle or the gate's freshness comes with its case in `core/apps/worker/tests/safety/recovery.test.ts`.
17. Campaigns and versions (README.md "Campaigns and versions"): a version never changes once saved (`app` may not update or delete its rows), and only new enrollments join a new one; an enrollment's due times read its own version (`versionSteps(template_id, template_version)`). The step keys are the closed list `STEP_KEYS`, which messaging maps by their strings. An email step has no sender account and nothing sends it before gamma-p4-010: the queue's, the plan's and the sender's reads name LinkedIn's actions (`linkedinOnly()`), and LinkedIn's code narrows a step with `linkedinSenderAccount` and `linkedinActionType`. Besides 5's, the module's foreign keys are a campaign's current version, a version's campaign and a step's version, all inside the module.
18. Pausing (README.md "Pausing"): a paused enrollment is live (`LIVE_ENROLLMENT_STATUSES`) for every stop, which clears its pause (`NO_PAUSE`). A pause is a wait, never a skip: the queue, the plan and the claim pass over its steps, the claim's gate writes `enrollment_paused` or `campaign_not_active`, and a resume clears it under the lead's and the enrollment's locks.
19. An existing connection (README.md "Acceptance and the first message", connect-already-connected): a connection request whose person is a connection already, as LinkedIn's refusal (`already_connected`, linkedin's own error-map row) or the read before it says, is `skipped` (`already_connected`) by `skipConnected` (`domain/send/connected.ts`), never `succeeded`: no touch, no `campaigns.step_sent`, no `accepted_at`. It is the one other writer of `message_1`'s first `due_at`, by the acceptance's rule from its settle's time (`dueFirstMessage`, `domain/acceptance.ts`); its safety cases are the worker's `tests/safety/already-connected.test.ts`.
20. Reply actions (README.md "Reply actions", gamma-p4-050): `applyReplyLabel` runs in its caller's run, the lead then the enrollments locked. Nothing in `domain/replies/` lifts a suppression, and releasing a hold reads `suppressionOf` before it changes anything. A released step goes back `planned` with no item (a withdrawal `scheduled`), so it passes a new approval and the gate; a step whose item was approved keeps the hold. The gate's reply check counts a reply after the later of `started_at` and `resumed_at`; the safety cases are `tests/safety/reply-actions.test.ts`. The notices' event, workflow input and history carry ids only; the mail, the first name and a link, never the reply's text.
21. The launch (README.md "Launch checks", gamma-p3-050): a campaign leaves `draft` only through `launchCampaign`, which reads every check again under the campaign's row lock and writes nothing on a refusal; a new check goes into `LAUNCH_CHECKS` with its code in `LAUNCH_CHECK_CODES`, and its case into `tests/safety/launch.test.ts`. The editor saves through `saveVersion` only, never an enrollment.
22. The campaign screens (README.md "Screens", gamma-p3-051): their spec is the web's `tests/campaigns-sequence-editor.spec.ts`, on the e2e module's `POST …/e2e/campaigns`. A new sequence rule, launch check or check code also gets its words in `ui/campaign/words.ts` and `messages/en.json`; the api decides every rule: the editor sends what the member set and shows the refusal, and Add's limits only mirror `MAX_STEPS` and `STEP_KEYS`.
23. Agents' campaigns (README.md "Agents' campaigns", gamma-p3-065): campaigns imports playbooks, never the reverse. An agent's campaign is made only by the subscriber to `playbooks.playbook_created`, one per agent (`campaigns_playbook`, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`), and its leads wait in it until `launchCampaign`. Routing (`domain/enroll/route.ts`) reads under the lead's lock and changes nothing of the enrollment's order of checks; the sender is the campaign's owner's (25); its safety case is `tests/safety/routing.test.ts`. `setCampaignActive` (`testing/`) stands for the launch in tests and the e2e module only.
24. Who writes a campaign (README.md "Decided here", gamma-p6-015): every write of a campaign goes through `lockCampaign`, which asks platform's `canWriteOwned` with its `ownerUserId` and `campaigns.manage`; an enrollment's routes ask `assertEnrollmentWriter` first. A new write of a campaign locks it the same way.
25. Senders per member (README.md "A removed member", gamma-p6-040): a step goes only from its campaign owner's account or mailbox, never the approver's. `enrollApproved` takes `senderOf(ownerUserId)` of the routed or the seeded campaign; an owner who is not an active member (`isActiveMember`, tenancy's `findActiveMembership`, read with no cache) gets no enrollment, no item and no send, as the enrollment, the queue and the gate each check, and the gate refuses an email step's mailbox of another member (`sender_mismatch`). Its safety case is the worker's `tests/safety/senders-per-member.test.ts`.
26. The approval mode (README.md "The approval mode", gamma-p4-070): the mode's approval passes the gate only for a follow-up, a message or an email, in a campaign whose effective mode is `review_first_touches` at the check; anything unknown needs a person (`approvalRequired`, `isFirstTouch`, `effectiveMode`). Every `approvedContent` caller passes `allowMode`, true only for `person_or_mode` (the report: true, then `mode_not_allowed`). A follow-up is a first touch until another step of its action in its enrollment has `succeeded` (review-gaps): `stepRequirement` reads the enrollment's steps with no cache, from the step's and the enrollment's ids every caller passes. Its safety case is `tests/safety/approval-mode.test.ts`.
27. The report's gamma lines (README.md "Commands", gamma-p7-050): one file per figure in `domain/report/`, pure where it can be, and gamma's lines after beta's in `cli/gamma-lines.ts`; beta's lines print unchanged. A first touch is the gate's stored `jit_checks.approval.firstTouch`, never a position; the suppression read is on the `linkedin` channel; a log row naming no step (an Inbox reply) is no send of the activity lines. The exit code reads the three zeros; email's two join them with the email phase.
