# campaigns

Campaigns, their versions and their steps on LinkedIn and email, the sequence an approved lead goes through, and the record of each of its steps ([architecture §7.4](../../../docs/architecture.md#74-campaigns-senders-and-inbox)). Approve (contacts, beta-p2-030) publishes `contacts.lead_approved`; this module's subscriber enrolls the lead in its campaign's current version with the campaign owner's connected LinkedIn account (gamma-p6-040): one enrollment and a step execution per step of the version, all `planned`, each with its idempotency key ("Campaigns and versions"). Every 15 minutes the queue puts each step due within 24 hours into approvals' "Up next", as the sender account's owner's to approve, and the owner's decision schedules or skips the step ("The queue", "The decision"); the gate refuses a send whose approval is missing or whose text changed ("The approval check"). The daily plan gives each sender account's due `scheduled` steps their times, and the account's sender sends each at its time through linkedin's send activities, after the gate once more and both reservations, the credits and linkedin's slot, then settles it ("Worker"). An accepted connection request makes the first message due 1 to 3 hours after linkedin detects it, inside the sender's working hours on a weekday ("Acceptance and the first message").

## Campaigns and versions

A campaign (`campaigns`, `cmp_…`) has versions (`campaign_versions`, `cmv_…`), each an ordered list of steps (`steps`, `stp_…`) on LinkedIn and email with their delays (gamma-p3-030; [docs/data.md](docs/data.md)). A version never changes once saved: `app` may not update or delete its rows, and an edit is a new version, which only enrollments made after it join. Enrollments and step executions name the campaign by `template_id`, its `key_ref`, and the version by `template_version`, its `version_no`.

The seeded campaign: `key_ref` `linkedin_default` (`SEEDED_CAMPAIGN_KEY`, beta's `TEMPLATE_ID`), an empty name, `active`, owned by the user of the workspace's oldest `Owner` membership (tenancy's `workspaceOwner`), made on first use with version 1 (`initial`, by `system`) from beta's template (`domain/template/constants.ts`):

| `step_key` | `position` | action | delay |
|---|---|---|---|
| `connect` | 1 | `invite`, no note by default | none: due at enrollment |
| `message_1` | 2 | `message` | 1 to 3 hours after the acceptance is detected (`FIRST_MESSAGE_DELAY_HOURS`), never under 10 minutes (`MIN_MINUTES_AFTER_ACCEPTANCE`) |
| `follow_up_1` | 3 | `message` | 3 business days after `message_1` was sent |
| `follow_up_2` | 4 | `message` | 5 business days after `follow_up_1` was sent |

Beta's rows (`linkedin_default`, 1) read as that version, with nothing rewritten. A workspace without an `Owner` gets no seeded campaign, and its enrollments join the template itself as version 1 (`SEEDED_VERSION`).

Business days are Monday to Friday in the sender's time zone; holidays are not counted. Each step's `due_at` is set when its anchor happens: the connection request at the enrollment, the first message at the acceptance, a later step once the one before it is sent or skipped, by the delay of the enrollment's own version (`versionSteps(template_id, template_version)` in the settle, the skip and the acceptance subscriber, which finds the version's first LinkedIn message by its key). A version the workspace does not hold gives no due time.

The rules (`validateSequence`, `domain/campaigns/sequence.ts`), checked in this order, each failure 400 `campaign_sequence_invalid` with `details.rule`: `step_count`, 1 to `MAX_STEPS` (7) steps; `step_action`, each an `invite`, a `message` or an `email`; `connect_first`, the first the connection request, due at the enrollment; `connect_once`, the only one; `channel_limit`, at most 3 LinkedIn messages and 3 emails; `first_message`, a LinkedIn message, if any, second, with beta's draw after the acceptance, which no one edits; `step_delay`, every other later step `MIN_STEP_DELAY_BUSINESS_DAYS` (1) to `CAMPAIGNS_MAX_STEP_DELAY_BUSINESS_DAYS` (30 unset; "Settings") whole business days after the step before it; `fallback` (gamma-p3-060), "The fallback".

The keys (`STEP_KEYS`, `domain/campaigns/constants.ts`), a closed list that messaging's `STEP_KINDS` maps by their strings, given at save by action and order: `connect`; `message_1`, `follow_up_1`, `follow_up_2`; `email_1`, `email_2`, `email_3`. A step's `step_template_hash` is the SHA-256, in hex, of its `step_key`, `action`, `delay`, `channel` and, only when set, `condition` as JSON with sorted keys, at every depth; the position is not in it. Its idempotency key is the SHA-256, in hex, of `workspace_id`, `person_id`, `template_id` and the hash joined by `:` (`domain/template/keys.ts`). A step unchanged across versions keeps its hash, and so its key: `step_executions_key_claimed` refuses a second claim of the same content for the same person in the same campaign. A changed delay or action changes it.

The commands (`domain/campaigns/`), each in the caller's tenant run:

- `seedCampaign()`: the seeded campaign, inserted with `ON CONFLICT (workspace_id, key_ref) DO NOTHING`; only the run that inserted it writes its version and steps, so two first reads at once leave one. Null without an `Owner`.
- `createCampaign({ name, steps?, ownerUserId, createdBy, playbookId? })`: a `draft`, `key_ref` its own id, its name trimmed, 1 to `CAMPAIGN_NAME_MAX` (80) characters, and the steps, else `WARM_SIGNAL_STEPS`, as version 1 (`initial`). With `playbookId`, the agent's (gamma-p3-065): inserted with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, so an agent that has one answers it, its current version `written: false` ("Agents' campaigns").
- `saveVersion(campaignId, steps, { createdBy })`, under a lock on the campaign's row and nothing else: the rules; steps equal to the current version's (the same keys, actions and hashes, in order) answer it, `written: false`; otherwise version `max + 1` (`structural`), its steps, and `current_version_id` moved. 404 `campaign_not_found`; 409 `campaign_archived`.
- `campaignOf(campaignId)` (404 `campaign_not_found`) and `versionSteps(keyRef, versionNo)` (null for a version the workspace does not hold): reads, with no cache.

Email steps exist from here, but nothing sends them before gamma-p4-010: an email step has no `sender_account_id` and, until then, no `mailbox_id`. The queue, the plan, the sender and `sentStep` name LinkedIn's actions in their filters (`linkedinOnly()`, `domain/steps/actions.ts`), so they pass over an email step, and the steps after it wait behind it. Code that handles a LinkedIn step narrows its sender with `linkedinSenderAccount(step)` and its action with `linkedinActionType(step)`, which throw on an email step.

Tests: `tests/sequence.test.ts` (in `test`), each rule and the keys; `tests/campaign-versions.test.ts` (in `test`), two first reads at once, `saveVersion` and `createCampaign`, an approval delivered before and after a new version, beta's rows through the migrations and the database's refusals; `tests/safety/versions.test.ts` (in `test:safety`): an edit never changes an enrollment in flight, the seeded keys are beta's, the same content twice is refused at the claim, and an email step goes to no queue run, no plan and no LinkedIn sender.

## Enrolling

The subscriber (`events.ts`, its body `domain/enroll/enroll.ts`) writes in the delivery's transaction ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md)), the lead locked first (contacts' `lockLeadStatus`). Routing then reads the campaign the lead joins (`routedCampaign`, `domain/enroll/route.ts`; gamma-p3-065), before beta's checks, which it changes none of: the lead's oldest source of kind `signal`, the agent holding it (playbooks' `playbookOfSource`) and that agent's campaign unless it is `archived`; else the seeded campaign ("Agents' campaigns"). Then, in this order:

1. an enrollment with the event's id as `source_event_id` exists: nothing, a redelivery;
2. the person has a live enrollment, `active` or `paused`: nothing;
3. no lead, or `suppressionOf(personId, { newEnrollment: true })` answers `person_not_found`: nothing, the person is gone;
4. any other suppression: an `exited` enrollment, `suppressed`, the reason in `exit_detail`, no step;
5. the lead is no longer `approved` (suppressed, in a campaign or done since Approve): nothing;
6. the campaign's owner is not an active member of the workspace (tenancy's `findActiveMembership`; `exit_detail` `owner_removed`), or linkedin's `senderOf(ownerUserId)` answers null: an `exited` enrollment, `no_sender`, no step; the lead stays `approved`;
7. otherwise an `active` enrollment with the sender, the version's `key_ref` and `version_no` and `started_at`; a step per step of the version, `planned`, with its key, position, action and hash, `connect` due now and the others' `due_at` null until their anchor happens; and `recordLeadStatus(personId, "in_campaign", { templateId: key_ref, actor: system })`.

The version is the routed campaign's current one, else the seeded campaign's, read after step 3 (`seededEnrollment`, `domain/campaigns/commands.ts`, which makes the seeded campaign on first use); the exits of steps 4 and 6 carry it too. The sender is the campaign owner's account, never the approver's (gamma-p6-040): an agent's campaign's `owner_user_id`, the agent's owner, and the seeded campaign's, the workspace's oldest active Owner when it was made; a workspace with no Owner, and so no seeded campaign, has no owner to send from (`no_sender`). An enrollment made before keeps the account it started with. An enrollment in a `draft` or `paused` agent's campaign waits until its launch or resume ("Pausing"). An email step is written without a sender account.

## Reason codes

`replied`, `enrollment_ended` and `touched_today`. p3's (beta-p3-050): `skipped_by_owner`, a step skipped in "Up next" and the steps a skipped connection request cancels, and the gate's `not_approved` and `content_changed`. p4's (beta-p4-020), the sender's waits: `insufficient_credits`, `member_ref_unknown`, and linkedin's refusals of a send before its call (`SEND_REFUSAL_CODES`): `reserveSend`'s, the send activity's re-read of them, `reservation_not_open` (a pause releases the slot), `read_cap_reached` for the chat's read, the health's `account_paused` and `invites_paused`, and the pending invitations' two. p4's recovery (beta-p4-040, `RECOVERY_CODES`): the LinkedIn port's codes a send's answer carries (`linkedin_rate_limited`, `linkedin_provider_error`, `linkedin_output_invalid`, `linkedin_error_unmapped`, `linkedin_limit_reached`, `linkedin_input_invalid`, and beta-p4-060's `linkedin_send_refused` and `linkedin_not_found`, in `WITHDRAWAL_CODES`), `account_replaced`, and `send_in_flight`, a person's step waiting on another in flight; ## Decided here

- The key: the template's id stands where a campaign's will (RB-9.3.3), and the separator `:` is in none of its parts.
- The partial unique key `(workspace_id, idempotency_key) WHERE status IN ('executing', 'succeeded')`, not the data model's full one: the same step content is never sent twice to a person, while an unsent row never blocks another. A retry reuses its row and raises `attempt`, which later tasks put in the credit key (`<idempotency_key>:<attempt>`). Approve is refused from `in_campaign`, so a person is enrolled again only after an exit that sent nothing, and a sent key never meets a new row.
- `source_event_id` (not in the data model): the `contacts.lead_approved` event an enrollment came from, unique per workspace, so a redelivered event changes nothing. Kept on exited enrollments too.
- `exit_detail` (not in the data model): the suppression reason behind `suppressed`, a code of `EXIT_DETAILS`, for the panel.
- The sender is the campaign owner's connected account (`senderOf(ownerUserId)`, gamma-p6-040); beta's was the approver's own (`senderOf(approvedBy)`), which its enrollments keep. Without one, the enrollment exits `no_sender` and the lead stays `approved`.
- An enrollment that exits at its creation (`suppressed`, `no_sender`) has no `started_at`: it never started; `created_at` and `ended_at` say when.
- The enrollment is inserted with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (`createMany` with `skipDuplicates`): a delivery that meets, at once, another delivery's active enrollment of the person enrolls nothing and writes no step, rather than failing its delivery.
- `connect` is due when the event is delivered, not when Approve was pressed; the two differ by seconds.
- No message key or error code for a reason code: an unknown one is a bug, thrown as an `Error`, as other modules throw their invariants.
- Enrollment and step rows keep their end state and are never deleted but by `erasePerson`; the lead's changes are contacts' records (RB-1.3.2, reduced). A status-change table for enrollments is left to gamma.
- `held` (architecture §14.2 and §14.3) is in the step statuses so gamma can resume what a reply held.
- The gate's order (beta-p2-050): the step, the enrollment, the suppression, the reply, the touch. A replied person's enrollment ends first, so their steps are held, not skipped; suppression before the reply, so an opted-out person's step records `opted_out`. The first failing check decides.
- The gate's outcomes: `step_not_ready` writes nothing and is never stored; `enrollment_ended` holds the step of a `replied` enrollment and cancels any other's; a suppression skips the step and cancels the person's other pending steps; `replied` holds; `touched_today` sets `error_code` and keeps the status. Each block writes `jit_checks` on the step, if it still has the status the gate read.
- No cache: every check reads the primary in the caller's run, through contacts' and linkedin's interfaces, each time.
- The gate may settle an `executing` step, since only the sender calls it; the hold and the subscriber never touch one.
- A suppressed lead stays `suppressed` through a reply: the hold holds the steps and records no status. An opted-out person whose enrollment already `replied` keeps their held steps held, and a later resume runs the gate again (the task's open question).
- The reply check fails closed: an enrollment without `started_at` counts every inbound message, and a touch dated after the gate's quota day counts as today's.
- The lead is locked at the start of enrolling (contacts' `lockLeadStatus`, a public read of the lock `recordLeadStatus` takes), and only an `approved` lead is enrolled; the gate and the subscriber stay the backstop.
- Only the plan writes `scheduled_for` and `quota_day`, each write conditional on the step still `scheduled` (and, for a new time, unplaced); the sender only clears them, when a step it read or claimed waits again (beta-p4-020). `campaigns_sender` `{ workspaceId, accountId }` (beta-p4-020; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The sender"), the account's one writer of its sends, one step at a time. Its activities are kind `db`, each in a tenant run (`domain/send/`, `workflows/sender.ts`), with a start-to-close of `SENDER_TIMEOUT` (1 minute):

3. `campaigns_sender_claim`: the gate, then the claim, `executing` with `attempt` raised and `error_code` cleared, conditional on `scheduled` and the time read; for an invite or a message, `credits.reserve` of `campaigns.linkedin_send` × 1 under `<idempotency_key>:<attempt>`, `subjectRef` the step's id, lease `async`, its usage in `credit_usage_id`; then linkedin's `reserveSend` under the step's own key, `withNote` for an invite whose approved note is not empty. `{ claimed, reservationId, usageId, attempt }`, `{ blocked }` or `{ refused, retryAt }`.

A send's port error after the call is settled by linkedin's `SEND_ERROR_MAP` (`domain/send/recover.ts`; the table in docs/flows.md), but for a withdrawal's invitation already gone, which `settleGone` takes first ("Withdrawals"). A waiting step's code and `due_at` are noted on its approved item for "Up next" (approvals' `noteItemWait`) and cleared at the next claim. The plan also starts the sender of an account with an `executing` step due for its check, unless it skips the account. The subscriber to `linkedin.account_replaced` (`domain/replaced.ts`) ends the account's `active` enrollments `no_sender` and leaves `executing` steps to the check.

Tests: `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-recovery.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test`): each row of the error map, a note over 300 characters, `invites_paused`, the check first and its answers, the note budget's wait, the day's limits after an outage, the replaced account through the outbox, twice, and the read before an invite's settle retried after its files are gone, a stored URL off the schema and the read's three attempts. `core/apps/worker/tests/safety/recovery.test.ts` (`test:safety`): a run terminated between the send and the settle, a 5xx and no call before `retry_at`, one check per quota day, no Temporal retry, a stale gate, a late step at the gate, after a profile read too, and the gap after a step in flight; `tests/safety/check-sent-attempts.test.ts`: a check failing on every attempt, three attempts and three reads, the run `unsettled` with no send, and the next run's check before its send. `workflows/__histories__/campaigns_sender_check_executing.json` is replayed too.

## The queue

`workflows/` and `domain/queue/` ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The queue"). The global schedule `campaigns.queue`, every `TICK_MINUTES`, overlap skip, runs `campaigns_queue_tick`: its activity `campaigns_start_queue_runs` lists the workspaces through tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds()` and starts each one's `campaigns_queue` with `startWorkflow`'s `oneAtATime`, as `campaigns.queue:<workspaceId>:<slot>`, the slot the tick's 15 minutes in UTC (`20261029T1000Z`, `workflows/queue.ts`); it answers `{ workspaces, started }`.

`campaigns_queue` `{ workspaceId }`: `campaigns_due_steps` lists the ids of the `planned` `invite` and `message` steps of `active` enrollments due by now plus approvals' `queueWindowHours` (`APPROVALS_QUEUE_WINDOW_HOURS`, 24 unset; `dueStepIds`), a page at a time ("The pages" below); then `campaigns_queue_step` `{ workspaceId, stepExecutionId }` each, one tenant run per step (`queueStep`, `domain/queue/open.ts`). It answers `{ opened, passedOver, noSender, failed }`. A step not approved by its day stays `awaiting_approval` with its item: the queue lists `planned` steps only, and "Up next" shows the item stale.

`queueStep(stepExecutionId)`: `passed_over` unless the step is still `planned` with a `due_at`, an `invite` or a `message`, its enrollment `active` and its person not suppressed (`suppressionOf(personId, { newEnrollment: false })`: no draft is paid for, and the suppression subscriber or the gate ends the step); `no_sender` when linkedin no longer holds its account, or its owner is not an active member of the workspace (tenancy's `findActiveMembership`, gamma-p6-040): the step stays `planned` and no draft is paid for. Otherwise the step becomes `awaiting_approval` under a condition on `planned`, approvals' `openItem` opens its item with the step's ids, action and key, `scheduledFor` its `due_at`, `senderUserId` the account's owner and `earlierDraftIds` the drafts of the enrollment's `succeeded` steps before it, oldest first, and the step keeps the item's id in `approval_item_id`: `opened`. A withdrawal never gets an item.

The pages (gamma-p0-085), behind `patched("queue-pages")` (`QUEUE_PAGES_PATCH`), asked once at the run's start: `campaigns_due_steps` `{ workspaceId, afterId? }` answers `QUEUE_PAGE_SIZE` (200, `workflows/constants.ts`) due step ids at most, after the cursor `afterId`, in id order, with the next page's cursor (`duePage`, `workflows/queue.ts`); after a page that is not the last, the run continues as new with `{ workspaceId, afterId, counts }`, the page's last id and its counts so far, under its fixed id, so a tick while the chain runs still starts none, and the last run answers the totals. A step opened in one page is no longer due in the next; one whose open fails stays `planned`, the cursor moves past it, and the next tick asks it again. The activity's other work, the enrollments resumed or exited, the reminders and the first messages given a due time, runs on a tick's first page only. A run begun before the patch handles the steps its list answered: all of them, or the first page when the list answers after the deploy, the next tick handling the rest. `useQueuePageSize(size)` (`workflows/queue.ts`) sets the page under `APP_ENV=test` and throws in any other environment. Tests: `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-queue.test.ts` and `tests/safety/queue-pages.test.ts` (`test:safety`): every step asked once, in id order, across three runs, none opened twice, and a failed open left `planned` for the next tick; `workflows/__histories__/campaigns_queue_pages.json`, a chain's first run, replays beside `campaigns_queue.json`.

## The decision

`approvals.item_decided` (`events.ts`), in its delivery's transaction (`applyDecision`, `domain/decisions.ts`): only a step still `awaiting_approval` whose `approval_item_id` is the event's item, and only on the item's stored status, read with `decisionsFor`, with who decided and when; anything else, a redelivery, a decision after a hold or a cancel, changes nothing. Approved: the step `scheduled` with the item's draft in `draft_id`. Skipped (`skipStep`, `domain/steps/skip.ts`, the lead locked first, then the enrollment): the step `skipped` (`skipped_by_owner`); for the connection request, the enrollment's other pending steps `cancelled` (`skipped_by_owner`) with their items blocked, the enrollment `exited` (`removed`) with `ended_at`, and the lead `finished` (`removed`, the owner its actor); for a message, the next step due its delay in business days after the decision, Monday to Friday in the sender's zone (`nextDueAt`), or no due time once linkedin no longer holds the account, and after the last step the enrollment `completed` and the lead `finished`. A suppressed lead stays suppressed.

Every path that holds, cancels or skips a step with an open item blocks that item in the same transaction (approvals' `cancelItems`): the hold, the suppression subscriber, the gate's suppression branch and the skip. A late press in "Up next" then answers 409 `approval_item_not_pending`.

## The approval check

The gate's last check, for every step but a withdrawal (`checkApproval`, `domain/gate/approval.ts`; `needsApproval`, `domain/steps/actions.ts`, fails closed: an action added later needs an approval until it is exempted by name), in the sender's run with no cache: the step's `approval_item_id` must be its latest item in approvals' `decisionsFor`, `approved`, else `not_approved`; then its requirement (`stepRequirement`, "The approval mode"): the mode's approval (origin `approval_mode`) under any requirement but `person_or_mode` is `not_approved`; approvals' `approvedContent(stepExecutionId, { allowMode })`, `allowMode` true only for `person_or_mode`, must answer for that item, its frozen text still hashing to the approval's hash and approved by the account's owner or the mode, else `content_changed`. A block writes the step `skipped` with the reason and `jit_checks`, with the owner's skip's follow-on ("The decision"); a pass adds `approval: { approvalItemId, status, contentHash, mode, firstTouch, origin }` to `checks` (`origin` `app` or `approval_mode`), which the sender stores with its send and the report reads. The step's own status is read first (`step_not_ready`): a held or cancelled step whose item was approved before the stop passes nothing. A withdrawal skips the check: it sends nothing to the person.

Tests: `tests/queue.test.ts` (in `test`): the due steps and their window, `queueStep`'s cases, the decision's cases and `nextDueAt` across a weekend and a zone; `tests/gate.test.ts`, the check last in the order and a pass's `approval`. `tests/safety/approval-check.test.ts` (in `test:safety`): no step `scheduled` without its approved item, `not_approved` and `content_changed` reserve nothing, the stops block the items, a withdrawal needs none, and any other action does; `tests/safety/approval-mode.test.ts`, the mode's ("The approval mode"). `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-queue.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test`): the tick, the first run's item, the next day's run, approve and skip through approvals' commands, and a late Approve's 409 after a hold; `pnpm -F worker test:replay` replays `campaigns_queue_tick` and `campaigns_queue`.

## Acceptance and the first message

linkedin publishes `linkedin.invite_accepted` `{ sender_account_id, person_id, detected_at }` when it detects that a person accepted the account's connection request: its receiver, at the provider's event (beta-p0-060), and its daily check, for an acceptance the provider missed (beta-p4-070). `detected_at` is when Whytide learned of it, which for an invite without a note can be hours after the acceptance (docs/07 §5.3); the first message counts from it. The subscriber (`events.ts`, its body `recordAcceptance`, `domain/acceptance.ts`), in the delivery's transaction ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "Acceptance → the first message"):

1. the lead locked (contacts' `lockLeadStatus`), then the person's live enrollment with that account, `active` or `paused`; none: nothing;
2. its `connect` step: nothing unless it went out (`succeeded`, or `executing` under the sender's claim) and its `accepted_at` is null;
3. `accepted_at` = `detected_at`, and no other column: the step's status stays, as only the sender settles its steps;
4. `message_1`, when it is `planned` without a due time: `due_at` = `firstMessageDueAt(detectedAt, account, random)`, the account linkedin's (`findSenderAccount`) and the draw campaigns' `random()`.

`firstMessageDueAt` (pure): the detection plus a uniform draw in `FIRST_MESSAGE_DELAY_HOURS` (1 to 3 hours), never under `MIN_MINUTES_AFTER_ACCEPTANCE` (10 minutes) after it; outside the account's window, or on a Saturday or Sunday in its zone, the next window start on a weekday (platform's `nextWindowAt`, the account's hours with its days cut to Monday to Friday) where the window is open. Null when the account's days hold no weekday. Nothing else starts the step: due within the queue's 24 hours, it gets its item at the queue's next run, at most `TICK_MINUTES` later ("The queue"), and once approved the plan draws its time in the window, never before `due_at`.

An existing connection (connect-already-connected): a connection request to a person who is a 1st-degree connection already is never sent. The read before an invite finds the profile `connected` (the LinkedIn port's flag, from the same answer), or, for a person whose member id is known and so not read, LinkedIn refuses the invite: `linkedin_send_refused` with `reason` `already_connected`, linkedin's own row in `SEND_ERROR_MAP` (its slot committed with no invitation pending and no outreach row, so neither the pending count, the daily check nor the acceptance rule reads it), the reason a code beside `refused` in the answer. Either settle (`skipConnected`, `domain/send/connected.ts`), in its run, frees the credits first, then locks the lead and the enrollment: the `connect` step `skipped` (`already_connected`), with no touch, no `campaigns.step_sent`, no withdrawal and no `accepted_at`, so it counts as neither an invite sent nor an acceptance; the enrollment stays `active` or `paused` and the lead `in_campaign`; the fallback's pending emails are skipped with the same reason, the enrollment completed if nothing is left of it, and `message_1`, when `planned` without a due time, gets `firstMessageDueAt(<the settle's time>, account, random)` (`dueFirstMessage`, shared with the acceptance). From there it is the path after an acceptance, the queue's recovery of a first message with no due time included. An enrollment ended meanwhile gets nothing more. No workflow's commands change: the refusal settles in `campaigns_sender_settle`, and the read's settle answers `{ blocked: "already_connected" }`, as `profile_unreadable`'s does, so no sleep and no slot claim follow.

Tests: `tests/acceptance.test.ts` (in `test`): inside the window, near its end, at night, on a Friday evening and across daylight-saving changes, no weekday, a broken source. `tests/safety/first-message-timing.test.ts` (in `test:safety`): seeded detections and working hours in three zones, never under 10 minutes after the detection, never outside the hours or on a Saturday or Sunday, 1 to 3 hours after it inside the window; through `recordAcceptance`, the same on `message_1`'s row, and a second acceptance moving nothing. `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-acceptance.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test`): the event through the outbox, a redelivery and a later acceptance, an ended enrollment, a request never sent and another account's or workspace's acceptance, and the queue's run opening the item. An existing connection: `core/apps/worker/tests/safety/already-connected.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test:safety`): the refusal's one call, skip and due time, `message_1` only approved and through the gate after it, other refusals ending the enrollment as before, a pause and an end during the call, and the read finding a connection; `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-sender.test.ts`: the reason a code in the history; `tests/queue.test.ts`: its first message recovered.

## Settings

`CAMPAIGNS_CREDITS_PER_LINKEDIN_SEND` (1, an integer from 0 to 100; `settings.ts`, the `modules/campaigns` block of `deploy/.env.example`): the credits of one send, a connection request or a message, `campaigns.linkedin_send`'s price as the module declares it. credits' `PRICE_CAMPAIGNS_LINKEDIN_SEND` overrides it when set, and a change is a new price version at the api's next start (credits' README "Declaring a price"). A withdrawal and a check cost nothing.

In the same block (env-settings-a), each a whole number with no maximum, the defaults in `domain/campaigns/constants.ts` and `domain/replies/constants.ts`:
- `CAMPAIGNS_MAX_STEP_DELAY_BUSINESS_DAYS` (30, 5 or more): a later step's longest delay after the step before, in business days (the `step_delay` rule). Its least is the longest delay of the built-in sequences, beta's template (the seeded campaign's version 1, read through the rules as the module loads) and the warm-signal default, so both always pass. `GET …/campaigns/{campaignId}` answers it as `limits.maxStepDelayBusinessDays`, which the sequence editor shows; the OpenAPI document names the default only.
- `CAMPAIGNS_NOT_INTERESTED_COOL_DOWN_DAYS` (180, 1 or more): after a "not interested" reply, the days the person cannot be enrolled again.
- `CAMPAIGNS_NOT_NOW_REMINDER_DAYS` (30, 1 or more): after a "not now" reply, the days until the owner's reminder mail, which names them.
- `CAMPAIGNS_OOO_DEFAULT_PAUSE_DAYS` (7, 1 or more): how long an out-of-office reply without a return date pauses the person's campaign.
- `CAMPAIGNS_OOO_RESUME_BUSINESS_DAYS` (1, 0 or more): the business days after an out-of-office return date the campaign resumes; 0 resumes on the return date.

Unset or empty, the default applies; a value off its range stops the api's and the worker's start with `config_invalid` naming it. They are read once, as the module loads: **a changed value needs an api and worker restart**. A changed value applies to what happens next: a reminder, a cool-down or a pause already set keeps its time. approvals' `APPROVALS_QUEUE_WINDOW_HOURS` sets the queue's window ("The queue"). `tests/settings.test.ts` sets each, and approvals' window, and tries the values off their ranges; the api host's `campaigns-sequence.test.ts` reads the default's `limits`.

## Withdrawals

`domain/withdraw/` and `workflows/withdraw.ts` (beta-p4-060; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The withdrawal"). A connection request not accepted `INVITE_WITHDRAW_AFTER_DAYS` (21) days after it went out is withdrawn once, through the plan, the gate and linkedin's reservation as any send, but with no approval and no credits; the enrollment then ends, and contacts' touch refuses the person a new enrollment for 90 days.

The job ("Worker"), daily at 05:00 UTC, runs `openWithdrawals(now)` in each workspace: for each `active` enrollment (a paused one waits for its resume) whose `connect` step is `succeeded`, with `executed_at` at least 21 × 24 hours ago and `accepted_at` null, one step `withdraw` (`WITHDRAW_STEP_KEY`), `position` 1, `action_type` `withdraw`, with the `connect` step's sender, template and `step_template_hash` and its key followed by `:withdraw`, `scheduled` and due now, with no approval item. The unique `(workspace_id, enrollment_id, step_key)` skips one that exists, so a second run, or the next day's, opens none.

The sender ("Worker") sends it as any step but for this: the gate's last check is the request's `accepted_at`, not an approval (`invite_accepted`: the step `cancelled`, the enrollment going on; `jit_checks.invite`); the claim reserves no credits; linkedin's `reserveSend` takes `actionType: "withdraw"` (`WITHDRAWALS_PER_DAY_MAX`, 15, and the 200); the request file holds no text; the call is `linkedin_withdraw_invite`. The settle (`domain/withdraw/settle.ts`) locks the lead, then the enrollment, then the step:

- the withdrawal went out: the step `succeeded` with `executed_at`; contacts' `recordTouch`; `campaigns.step_sent` with `actionType: "withdraw"`; then the end;
- `linkedin_not_found` or `linkedin_send_refused` after the call, the invitation already gone (declined, or withdrawn by hand): the step `failed` with the code and `executed_at`, the touch recorded all the same, no event; then the end, and the run goes on;
- the end, unless the request was accepted meanwhile: its pending LinkedIn steps `cancelled` (`not_accepted`) with their items blocked, a pending fallback email kept ("The fallback"); then, with no step of it open, the enrollment `exited` (`not_accepted`) with `ended_at` and the lead `finished`, else when its last fallback email ends.

A person erased meanwhile has no touch to record. The codes, in `STEP_REASON_CODES` and the panel's words: `invite_accepted` and `not_accepted` (`skip_reason`), `linkedin_not_found` and `linkedin_send_refused` (`error_code`).

Tests: `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-withdraw.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test`): the schedule and the tick's starts, the run's rows and what it passes over, the withdrawal end to end, an invitation already gone, and an acceptance on the way. `core/apps/worker/tests/safety/withdrawal.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test:safety`): day 20 kept and day 21 withdrawn once, 15 a day, the 200, the window, the weekdays and a kill switch, no approval and no credits, an accepted request, and 90 days without a new enrollment; each asserts the fake's calls. `pnpm -F worker test:replay` replays `campaigns_withdraw_tick` and `campaigns_withdraw`.

## The fallback

The only condition gamma has, `invite_not_accepted_within` (`steps.condition`, `FALLBACK_CONDITION`): the step goes while the connection request is not accepted. The rule `fallback` of `validateSequence`: the condition on emails only, in a sequence with a connection request; when one email carries it, every email does and they are the last steps; the first anchored `{ anchor: "invite_sent", unit: "days", value: 7 }` (`FALLBACK_DELAY`), fixed, the others on the step before as `step_delay` says. A condition enters a step's hash only when set, so every hash of beta's template stays.

`domain/fallback/`, read by the settle, the acceptance, the gate and the skip ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The fallback"):

- the connection request's settle, once it went out, sets the first fallback email's `due_at` to its `executed_at` plus 7 days, while it is `planned` without one: a column the 15-minute jobs read, never a timer;
- the acceptance (`recordAcceptance`), after recording it, skips every pending fallback step with `invite_accepted`, its pending item blocked (approvals' `cancelItems`), in the delivery's transaction, and ends the enrollment if nothing of it is open;
- the gate, before a fallback step's claim: `accepted_at` set skips it with `invite_accepted` (`jit_checks.invite`), for an acceptance between the queue and the claim;
- a skipped fallback email, for any reason, leaves the LinkedIn steps as they are, and the next fallback email falls due;
- the withdrawal keeps pending fallback steps ("Withdrawals"), and the enrollment ends after its last one.

Nothing sends an email before gamma-p4-010: a due email step waits `planned`, so a warm-signal enrollment never accepted stays live after its withdrawal until its fallback emails end ([docs/runbook.md](docs/runbook.md)).

Tests: `tests/safety/fallback.test.ts` (in `test:safety`): the 7 days from a fixed instant, no fallback email claimed after an acceptance before the fallback, between the queue and the claim, or after `email_1` went out, and a skipped email leaving every LinkedIn step as it was; `tests/sequence.test.ts`, the rule and the hashes; `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-fallback.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test`): the send through the sender, the acceptance's skip, the day-21 withdrawal with a pending fallback, and the end.

## Pausing

`domain/pause/`, each command in the caller's tenant run, the lead then the enrollment locked (a campaign: its row, then, for its resume, each waiting step's lead and enrollment), and each behind a route ("Routes"); the panel's Pause and Resume call the enrollment's ("Screens"):

- `pauseEnrollment(enrollmentId, { until?, reason })` and `resumeEnrollment(enrollmentId)` ("Public interface"): `active` to `paused` with `paused_reason` (`user`, `out_of_office`) and `paused_until`, and back, clearing the code its steps waited on;
- `pauseCampaign(campaignId, { reason })` and `resumeCampaign(campaignId)`: `active` to `paused` with `paused_reason` (`user`, `bounce_rate`) and `paused_at`, and back; a `draft` is not paused (409 `campaign_not_active`), and a resume of one not paused is 409 `campaign_not_paused`. The resume then clears `campaign_not_active` on the campaign's waiting steps one enrollment at a time, in id order, under the lead's lock, then the enrollment's (gamma-p3-061).

A step of an enrollment `paused`, or of one whose campaign is not `active` (`draft`, `paused`), is opened by no queue run (`dueStepIds`, `queueStep`) and placed by no plan; the gate blocks its claim (`enrollment_paused`, `campaign_not_active`) and the claim puts it back to wait, unplaced. Read on every run with no cache, a pause stops new claims at once (RB-9.3.1's 60 seconds); a resume needs the next runs. A step already `executing` ends as the sender settles it. `campaigns_due_steps` first resumes the workspace's enrollments whose `paused_until` has passed (`resumeDueEnrollments`).

Tests: `tests/safety/pause.test.ts` (in `test:safety`): nothing opened, placed or claimed for a paused enrollment or a paused or draft campaign, a pause between the plan and the claim, a resume, and a reply and a suppression still stopping a paused enrollment; `tests/pause.test.ts` (in `test`): the transitions and their refusals, the database's refusals, the resume at the queue run's start, the acceptance and the withdrawal on a paused enrollment, a campaign made without steps, and the campaign's resume waiting for a held lead, then a held enrollment, before it clears a step's code. The routes: "Routes".

## Reply actions

`domain/replies/` (gamma-p4-050): what each label of a reply does to the person's campaigns. `applyReplyLabel(personId, { label, suggested, returnDate, repliedAt, threadId, ownerUserId })` runs in the caller's tenant run, inbox's classification (gamma-p4-040), so the label's action commits with its row or not at all. It acts on the person's `replied` enrollments that still have `held` steps, the newest first, after locking the lead, then each enrollment (the stops' one order), and counts every time from `repliedAt`: no clock.

- A `suggested` label changes nothing, unless it is `opt_out`; the same label for the same reply again changes nothing.
- Any other call first undoes what an earlier label scheduled: a reminder not yet sent is cleared, and an enrollment still `paused` for `out_of_office` is held again by `holdForReply(personId, { at: repliedAt })`, as the reply left it; a cool-down and a notice already published stay. Then it records `reply_label` and `reply_label_at`, and acts:
  - `interested`: one `campaigns.reply_notice_due` (`interested`) for the newest enrollment, so a person in two campaigns gets one notice;
  - `not_now`: `remind_at` = `repliedAt` + `CAMPAIGNS_NOT_NOW_REMINDER_DAYS` (30 unset) and `remind_user_id` on the newest; the first queue run after it sets `reminded_at` and publishes `campaigns.reply_notice_due` (`not_now`), once;
  - `not_interested`: every held step `cancelled` (`not_interested`), its items blocked, and `cool_down_until` = `repliedAt` + `CAMPAIGNS_NOT_INTERESTED_COOL_DOWN_DAYS` (180 unset) days, until which the enrollment (`enrollApproved`) refuses the person (`cool_down`, no row, as `enrolled_already`);
  - `out_of_office`: the newest enrollment's hold released, its first step due at the pause's end, then gamma-p3-060's `pauseEnrollment(enrollmentId, { until, reason: "out_of_office" })` in the same run; `until` is `CAMPAIGNS_OOO_RESUME_BUSINESS_DAYS` business days after the return date (1 unset, the next business day; 0, the return date itself; Monday to Friday, holidays not counted) at 09:00 in the sender's zone, or `repliedAt` + `CAMPAIGNS_OOO_DEFAULT_PAUSE_DAYS` (7 unset) days without one;
  - `opt_out`, suggested or not: contacts' `addOptOut(personId, { source: "system", reason: "reply_opt_out" })`, on every channel, and every held step of the person `cancelled` (`opted_out`), its items blocked;
  - `bounce` (its action is the email phase's) and `other`: the label alone; the person stays held.

Releasing the hold (`domain/replies/release-hold.ts`, not exported) reads the suppression first: a person suppressed now is exited (`suppressed`) with their held steps `cancelled`, and nothing is released; nothing here lifts a suppression. The hold also stays when the release could not end well ("Decided here"). Otherwise the enrollment is `active` again with `resumed_at`, its held steps `planned` with no item (a withdrawal `scheduled`), each due no earlier than the pause's end, and the lead `in_campaign`.

The resume is gamma-p3-060's: `campaigns_due_steps` resumes an enrollment whose `paused_until` has passed (`resumeDueEnrollments`), and the same queue run opens the first released step, which sends only after a new item, its approval and the gate before the call. Just before, in the same run, a paused enrollment whose person is suppressed is exited (`exitSuppressedDue`). The gate's reply check counts an inbound message after the later of `started_at` and `resumed_at`, so the out-of-office reply does not hold the enrollment again and a new reply does. A real reply during the pause holds the paused enrollment again (`holdForReply`), and its label decides.

The notices: campaigns' own subscriber to `campaigns.reply_notice_due` starts `campaigns_reply_notice` as `campaigns.reply_notice:<workspaceId>:<event id>`, so a redelivered event starts no second run. Its one activity, `campaigns_reply_notice_mail` (kind `provider`), mails the user the event names through the app-mail port: `campaigns.mail.interested` with the link `<APP_PUBLIC_ORIGIN>/w/<workspaceId>/inbox/<threadId>`, or `campaigns.mail.not_now` with `…/leads/<personId>`. A mail names the person by first name only, on one line and 50 characters at most, else "a lead", and never carries the reply's text. A user who is not one any more, is disabled or is no longer a member of the workspace (tenancy's `resolveMembership`, read outside any run) gets none, and the run ends with the ids logged ([docs/security.md](docs/security.md)).

Tests: `tests/safety/reply-actions.test.ts` (in `test:safety`): an opt-out, suggested or not, in two campaigns; a person suppressed before the label, or during the pause before the suppression's delivery, never resumed; a held step approved before the reply kept held; a resumed step through a new item, its approval and the gate before any reservation; a reply during the pause, and after the resume. `tests/reply-actions.test.ts` (in `test`): each label, suggested and not, the business day, the undo, a repeat, the cool-down at 179 and 181 days. `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-reply-notice.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test`): the mails on the app-mail fake, a redelivery, an owner disabled, removed or no user, the out-of-office resume and the reminder through `campaigns_queue`, and the replay of `workflows/__histories__/campaigns_reply_notice.json`.

## Launch checks

`domain/launch/` (gamma-p3-050; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "Saving and launching"): a campaign leaves `draft` only through `launchCampaign(campaignId)`, in the caller's tenant run: the campaign's row locked (`lockCampaign`), `draft` or 409 `campaign_not_draft`, then every check read again under the lock, never the screen's answer, and with every one passing the campaign `active` with `launched_at`, else 409 `campaign_launch_blocked` with `details.checks` and nothing written. `launchChecks(campaignId)` reads the same checks for the screen. Each is read live, with no cache, for the campaign's owner (`owner_user_id`), in order (`LAUNCH_CHECKS`, `domain/launch/constants.ts`):

| `check` | passes when | `code` when it fails |
|---|---|---|
| `linkedin_sender` | linkedin's `senderOf(ownerUserId)` answers a connected account, not paused (`killSwitch`), whose health is not `restricted`, `checkpoint` or `disconnected` | `no_linkedin_sender`, `linkedin_sender_unhealthy` |

## Agents' campaigns

gamma-p6-040 ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "A removed member"; RB-1.1.1, RB-1.1.2 reduced): each campaign sends from its owner's own senders, an agent's campaign from the agent's owner's and the seeded one from the workspace Owner's ("Enrolling"), whoever approved the lead, and nothing goes from the senders of a member removed from the workspace (tenancy's `removed_at`, gamma-p6-030). ## The approval mode

gamma-p4-070 (F13 §3.3; RB-14.2.1 with two modes of its four, RB-20.6.1, RB-25.3.3; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The approval mode"): each campaign has an approval mode (`domain/approval-mode/`).

- The modes (`campaign_approval_mode`): `review_everything`, the default, where a person approves every step; `review_first_touches`, opt-in, where a person approves each first touch and the campaign's mode approves the follow-ups, each with its channel's AI disclosure line (messaging's `approveStepDraftByMode`, gamma-p4-080, through approvals, gamma-p4-090).
- The lock: `review_locked_until`, the campaign's creation plus `REVIEW_EVERYTHING_DAYS` (7), set by `createCampaign` and `seedCampaign` (the migration's time plus 7 days for older campaigns). An agent's campaign is made at the agent's creation ("Agents' campaigns"), so its days are the agent's. Until then `effectiveMode(campaign, at)` (`mode.ts`) is `review_everything` whatever is stored; nothing moves the lock.
- First touches (`isFirstTouch`, `domain/steps/actions.ts`): the first message of its action the lead receives from the enrollment (review-gaps). A step is a follow-up only when both hold: an earlier step of its version has its action (by position), and another step of that action in its enrollment has `succeeded`. So the connection request, the first message and the first email are first touches always, and a follow-up stays one until a message of its action went out: after a first message skipped, failed, held or cancelled, the next one is the lead's first and waits for a person. `stepRequirement` reads the enrollment's steps beside the version's, in the caller's run with no cache, from the step's and the enrollment's ids its three callers pass (the queue, the gate's `checkApproval`, the sender's `prepareRequest`); an enrollment, a version or a step not found is a first touch, and a read that fails throws.
- Whose approval (`approvalRequired(step, mode)`, beside `needsApproval`, which stays): `none` for a withdrawal; `person_or_mode` for a message or email follow-up in `review_first_touches`; `person` otherwise, an unknown action or mode included. `stepRequirement` (`requirement.ts`) reads it with no cache; a campaign not found is `review_everything`.
- The queue (`queueStep`) opens the item with `approvalBy` `mode` for a `person_or_mode` step, `person` otherwise. The mode's approval names no person; the decision schedules the step by its origin (`domain/decisions.ts`).
- The change (`set.ts`, "Routes"): `review_first_touches` only after the lock; the mode written with `approval_mode_set_by` and `approval_mode_set_at` unless it is the campaign's already. Back to `review_everything`, each step `awaiting_approval` or `scheduled` whose latest item is the mode's, pending or approved by it, goes back to `planned` with no item, draft or place, in a run of its own under the person's locks; approvals' `returnModeItems` blocks its items and makes the approved draft `ready` again without the line, so the queue opens an item for a person.
- The card (`ui/approval-mode/picker.tsx`) on the campaign's page: the two modes, "Review everything until <date>" while the lock holds, "Review first touches" off then, and each channel's AI disclosure line, messaging's panel in the page's `disclosure` slot (`ui/campaign/slots.ts`), shown before the switch. A member without `campaigns.approval_mode` sees the mode without the control.
