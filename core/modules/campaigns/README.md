# campaigns

The one fixed sequence an approved lead goes through, and the record of each of its steps ([architecture §7.4](../../../docs/architecture.md#74-campaigns-senders-and-inbox)). Approve (contacts, beta-p2-030) publishes `contacts.lead_approved`; this module's subscriber enrolls the lead in the template with the approver's own connected LinkedIn account: one enrollment and four step executions, all `planned`, each with its idempotency key. Every 15 minutes the queue puts each step due within 24 hours into approvals' "Up next", as the sender account's owner's to approve, and the owner's decision schedules or skips the step ("The queue", "The decision"); the gate refuses a send whose approval is missing or whose text changed ("The approval check"). The daily plan gives each sender account's due `scheduled` steps their times, and the account's sender sends each at its time through linkedin's send activities, after the gate once more and both reservations, the credits and linkedin's slot, then settles it ("Worker"). An accepted connection request makes the first message due 1 to 3 hours after linkedin detects it, inside the sender's working hours on a weekday ("Acceptance and the first message").

- **Owns:** `enrollments` and `step_executions` in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/campaigns.prisma` ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)), the template (`domain/template/`), the reason codes (`domain/steps/constants.ts`), the gate (`domain/gate/`), the hold (`domain/hold.ts`), the event `campaigns.step_sent` and the subscribers to `contacts.lead_approved`, `contacts.suppression_added`, `approvals.item_decided` and `linkedin.invite_accepted` (`events.ts`), the enrollments route (`api/`, "Routes"), the lead page's panel (`ui/enrollment-panel/`, "Screens"), the daily plan (`domain/plan/`, `workflows/`, "Worker"), the sender (`domain/send/`, `workflows/sender.ts`, "Worker"), the price of a send and its setting (`prices.ts`, `settings.ts`, "Settings"), the queue (`domain/queue/`, `workflows/`, "The queue"), the decision (`domain/decisions.ts`, `domain/steps/skip.ts`, "The decision"), the approval check (`domain/gate/approval.ts`, "The approval check"), the acceptance (`domain/acceptance.ts`, "Acceptance and the first message"), the counts route `GET /v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/campaigns/counts` (`api/counts.controller.ts`, `domain/counts.ts`, "Routes") and its screen (`ui/counts/`, "Screens"), and the "Done when" report (`cli/report.ts`, `domain/report/`, "Commands").
- **Public interface:** `index.ts`, below.
- **Depends on:** `platform` (its `credits` client, which credits implements, among the rest), `db`, `ui`, `providers` (the files port, for the request file), `approvals` (`openItem`, `cancelItems`, `decisionsFor`, `approvedContent`, `editStats`, `decisionDays`, `QUEUE_WINDOW_HOURS`, its `events`), `contacts` (`suppressionOf`, `suppressedAt`, `recordLeadStatus`, `lockLeadStatus`, `lastTouchAt`, `recordTouch`, `getPerson`, its `events`), `linkedin` (`senderOf`, `lastInboundAt`, `findSenderAccount`, `remainingToday`, `reserveSend`, `releaseSend`, `commitSend`, `findSendReservation`, `sendRequestFile`, `sendLogRows`, `healthEventsSince`, its send activities by name, its `events`), `tenancy` (`listWorkspaceIds`, `findWorkspace`, `findWorkspaceBySlug`).
- **Reviewers:** the owners of this module; it is on the send path ([docs/security.md](docs/security.md)).

## The template

`TEMPLATE_ID` `linkedin_default`, `TEMPLATE_VERSION` 1, on LinkedIn (`domain/template/constants.ts`):

| `step_key` | `position` | action | delay |
|---|---|---|---|
| `connect` | 1 | `invite`, no note by default | none: due at enrollment |
| `message_1` | 2 | `message` | 1 to 3 hours after the acceptance is detected (`FIRST_MESSAGE_DELAY_HOURS`), never under 10 minutes (`MIN_MINUTES_AFTER_ACCEPTANCE`) |
| `follow_up_1` | 3 | `message` | 3 business days after `message_1` was sent |
| `follow_up_2` | 4 | `message` | 5 business days after `follow_up_1` was sent |

Business days are Monday to Friday in the sender's time zone; holidays are not counted. The delays are declared here; the tasks that schedule apply them (beta-p4-010, beta-p4-050).

A step's `step_template_hash` is the SHA-256, in hex, of its `step_key`, `action`, `delay` and `channel` as JSON with sorted keys, at every depth; the position is not in it. Its idempotency key is the SHA-256, in hex, of `workspace_id`, `person_id`, `template_id` and the hash joined by `:` (`domain/template/keys.ts`). A changed delay or action changes the hash and so the keys: that is a new template version.

## Enrolling

The subscriber (`events.ts`, its body `domain/enroll/enroll.ts`) writes in the delivery's transaction ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md)), the lead locked first (contacts' `lockLeadStatus`), in this order:

1. an enrollment with the event's id as `source_event_id` exists: nothing, a redelivery;
2. the person has an `active` enrollment: nothing;
3. no lead, or `suppressionOf(personId, { newEnrollment: true })` answers `person_not_found`: nothing, the person is gone;
4. any other suppression: an `exited` enrollment, `suppressed`, the reason in `exit_detail`, no step;
5. the lead is no longer `approved` (suppressed, in a campaign or done since Approve): nothing;
6. `senderOf(approvedBy)` answers null: an `exited` enrollment, `no_sender`, no step; the lead stays `approved`;
7. otherwise an `active` enrollment with the sender, the template's id and version and `started_at`; the four steps, `planned`, `connect` due now and the others' `due_at` null until their anchor happens; and `recordLeadStatus(personId, "in_campaign", { templateId, actor: system })`.

## Reason codes

`STEP_REASON_CODES` lists what a step's `skip_reason` and `error_code` may hold, `EXIT_DETAILS` what an enrollment's `exit_detail` may hold (`domain/steps/constants.ts`). Every writer checks its code with `checkedCode` (`domain/steps/codes.ts`), which throws on a code its list does not hold, naming the column and never the value; the panel (beta-p2-060) maps each code to a message key. p2's: the suppression reasons `never_contact_person`, `never_contact_company`, `opted_out` and `person_not_found`, with `recently_contacted` an exit detail only; `replied`, `enrollment_ended` and `touched_today`. p3's (beta-p3-050): `skipped_by_owner`, a step skipped in "Up next" and the steps a skipped connection request cancels, and the gate's `not_approved` and `content_changed`. p4's (beta-p4-020), the sender's waits: `insufficient_credits`, `member_ref_unknown`, and linkedin's refusals of a send before its call (`SEND_REFUSAL_CODES`): `reserveSend`'s, the send activity's re-read of them, `reservation_not_open` (a pause releases the slot), `read_cap_reached` for the chat's read, the health's `account_paused` and `invites_paused`, and the pending invitations' two. p4's recovery (beta-p4-040, `RECOVERY_CODES`): the LinkedIn port's codes a send's answer carries (`linkedin_rate_limited`, `linkedin_provider_error`, `linkedin_output_invalid`, `linkedin_error_unmapped`, `linkedin_limit_reached`, `linkedin_input_invalid`, and beta-p4-060's `linkedin_send_refused` and `linkedin_not_found`, in `WITHDRAWAL_CODES`), `account_replaced`, and `send_in_flight`, a person's step waiting on another in flight; the exit details `account_replaced` (`no_sender`) and `linkedin_input_invalid` (`send_refused`). Each later task adds its own.

## Decided here

- The key: the template's id stands where a campaign's will (RB-9.3.3), and the separator `:` is in none of its parts.
- The partial unique key `(workspace_id, idempotency_key) WHERE status IN ('executing', 'succeeded')`, not the data model's full one: the same step content is never sent twice to a person, while an unsent row never blocks another. A retry reuses its row and raises `attempt`, which later tasks put in the credit key (`<idempotency_key>:<attempt>`). Approve is refused from `in_campaign`, so a person is enrolled again only after an exit that sent nothing, and a sent key never meets a new row.
- `source_event_id` (not in the data model): the `contacts.lead_approved` event an enrollment came from, unique per workspace, so a redelivered event changes nothing. Kept on exited enrollments too.
- `exit_detail` (not in the data model): the suppression reason behind `suppressed`, a code of `EXIT_DETAILS`, for the panel.
- The sender is the approver's own connected account (`senderOf(approvedBy)`); in beta's setup, the workspace's one sender. Without one, the enrollment exits `no_sender` and the lead stays `approved`.
- An enrollment that exits at its creation (`suppressed`, `no_sender`) has no `started_at`: it never started; `created_at` and `ended_at` say when.
- The enrollment is inserted with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (`createMany` with `skipDuplicates`): a delivery that meets, at once, another delivery's active enrollment of the person enrolls nothing and writes no step, rather than failing its delivery.
- `connect` is due when the event is delivered, not when Approve was pressed; the two differ by seconds.
- No message key or error code yet: an unknown reason code is a bug, thrown as an `Error`, as other modules throw their invariants.
- Enrollment and step rows keep their end state and are never deleted but by `erasePerson`; the lead's changes are contacts' records (RB-1.3.2, reduced). A status-change table for enrollments is left to gamma.
- `held` (architecture §14.2 and §14.3) is in the step statuses so gamma can resume what a reply held.
- The gate's order (beta-p2-050): the step, the enrollment, the suppression, the reply, the touch. A replied person's enrollment ends first, so their steps are held, not skipped; suppression before the reply, so an opted-out person's step records `opted_out`. The first failing check decides.
- The gate's outcomes: `step_not_ready` writes nothing and is never stored; `enrollment_ended` holds the step of a `replied` enrollment and cancels any other's; a suppression skips the step and cancels the person's other pending steps; `replied` holds; `touched_today` sets `error_code` and keeps the status. Each block writes `jit_checks` on the step, if it still has the status the gate read.
- No cache: every check reads the primary in the caller's run, through contacts' and linkedin's interfaces, each time.
- The gate may settle an `executing` step, since only the sender calls it; the hold and the subscriber never touch one.
- A suppressed lead stays `suppressed` through a reply: the hold holds the steps and records no status. An opted-out person whose enrollment already `replied` keeps their held steps held, and a later resume runs the gate again (the task's open question).
- The reply check fails closed: an enrollment without `started_at` counts every inbound message, and a touch dated after the gate's quota day counts as today's.
- The lead is locked at the start of enrolling (contacts' `lockLeadStatus`, a public read of the lock `recordLeadStatus` takes), and only an `approved` lead is enrolled; the gate and the subscriber stay the backstop.
- Only the plan writes `scheduled_for` and `quota_day`, each write conditional on the step still `scheduled` (and, for a new time, unplaced); the sender only clears them, when a step it read or claimed waits again (beta-p4-020). The plan never reads or writes an `executing` step: the sender alone settles its steps (beta-p4-020, beta-p4-040), and its own wait of one gap covers a send in flight.
- Only due steps are placed: a step due later today gets its time at the first tick after its `due_at`, at most `TICK_MINUTES` late, so a drawn time never meets a `due_at`.
- Messages first, then withdrawals, then invites, each by `due_at`, `position` and id: follow-ups above new invites (RB-9.2.1); a withdrawal lowers the pending count that can refuse invites (not in the sources).
- `MIN_ACTION_GAP_MS`, 40 seconds, RB-11.2.4's smallest gap inside a session, between any two times of an account, kept or new, and after its last `executed_at`, whatever the step's status now.
- A time is drawn once and kept, so a step does not move at every tick. One more than a gap behind, or on an earlier quota day, loses its time and is placed again in what is left of the window: no catch-up burst (architecture §20).
- The three types together stay within `remainingToday`'s `actions`, the actions left of the 200: each type's number is within it, but their sum may pass it. linkedin answers `actions` for this, a field added outside the module.
- Kept steps past a type's number or the actions left: the latest lose their time, and wait for room on a later run.
- A drawn time where `isWindowOpen` says the window is closed, in a spring-forward's missing hour, is dropped rather than moved: one candidate fewer is placed, and it waits for the next run.
- A candidate whose person contacts no longer knows (`lastTouchAt` answers `person_not_found`) gets no time; the plan goes on with the others.
- One plan per workspace at a time, by its `:current` id; every workspace gets a run every tick, as signals' and qualification's ticks do.
- The queue's runs are named by the tick's slot, `campaigns.queue:<workspaceId>:<slot>`, one at a time: the tick's activity tried again in its slot starts no second run while the first is open, and two slots' runs may overlap, which each step's conditional move and `openItem`'s answer for a step's open item make harmless.
- The queue claims the step (`planned` to `awaiting_approval`) before `openItem` asks for the draft, in one transaction: a hold or a cancel that meets the claimed step waits for its commit, then blocks the item it opened.
- A queue run counts a step that fails past its attempts as `failed` and goes on: one bad step never starves the others, and it stays `planned` for the next tick.
- Skipping the connection request ends the enrollment (`exited`, `removed`) and finishes the lead: nothing else can follow it, so the lead cannot be approved again in beta (the task's open question).
- A blocked approval (`not_approved`, `content_changed`) skips the step as the owner's skip does, rather than asking for a fresh approval: both mean a defect or a tampered row (the task's open question). A connection request so skipped ends its enrollment; a message makes the next step due.
- The decision acts on the item's stored status (`decisionsFor`), never on the event's, and only on a step still `awaiting_approval` with the event's item.
- A skip counts the next step's delay from the decision's `decided_at`, the gate's block from its `at`, in business days in the sender's zone (`nextDueAt`, `domain/template/due.ts`).
- The approval check fails closed: every action needs its approved item but a withdrawal, exempted by name (`needsApproval`); the queue opens items for connection requests and messages only (`opensItem`), so a step of an action added later is blocked `not_approved`.
- Withdrawals (beta-p4-060): 21 days fixed (RB-11.2.5 reduced: not 14 to 30 to choose), 21 × 24 hours after the request's `executed_at`. No approval, as a withdrawal reaches nobody's inbox (a Finding), and no credits, as only sends and engagers cost credits (the owner's decision of 2026-10-29); every other rule holds: the plan, the gate, and linkedin's `reserveSend` with its 15 a day, the 200, the window, the weekdays and the switches.
- An accepted request is never withdrawn: the job reads `accepted_at`, and the gate reads it again for a withdrawal, last, in place of the approval (`invite_accepted`: the step `cancelled`, before any slot), for an acceptance detected after the job. A settle that finds `accepted_at` set meanwhile ends nothing, and a call then answered `linkedin_not_found` records no touch: the person is connected, and the conversation goes on.
- An invitation already gone: `linkedin_not_found` or `linkedin_send_refused` with no `retryAt` and the slot `committed` (`findSendReservation`, action `withdraw`); a check's refusal before a retry keeps its slot `reserved` and stays the sender's. The step `failed` with the code and `executed_at` (the settle's time, which the touch and `ended_at` share), and no `campaigns.step_sent`: nothing went out.
- The end cancels every pending step, not only the `planned` ones, with `skip_reason` `not_accepted`, and blocks their items, as every stop does; the lead `finished` with the template's id, as the system.
- The job's runs are named by the UTC date, one at a time; the rows, not the run's id, keep a withdrawal single: the unique `(workspace_id, enrollment_id, step_key)` and the key `<connect key>:withdraw`. 05:00 UTC is not in the sources: the plan places each step in its account's window.
- A skip whose sender account linkedin no longer holds leaves the next step without a due time: its delivery succeeds once rather than failing at every redelivery, and nothing could go from that account anyway.
- The anchor is the detection: the first message counts from the event's `detected_at`, when Whytide learned of the acceptance, never from LinkedIn's own time of it, which linkedin keeps on its outreach row (docs/07 §5.3).
- The draw: uniform over 1 to 3 hours after the detection, to the millisecond, through platform's `randomTimesInWindow` with campaigns' `random()`. `due_at` is the earliest time; the plan draws the send's own time in the window, so acceptances detected overnight do not all go at the window's start.
- A draw outside the window moves to the next window start where `isWindowOpen` agrees: a start whose whole window a spring-forward skips is passed over, within `WINDOW_STARTS_TRIED` (8) starts.
- An account linkedin no longer holds, or whose working days hold no weekday: the acceptance is recorded and `message_1` gets no due time, so the delivery succeeds once; nothing could go from that account. The account's status is not read: a disconnected account's first message falls due all the same, and the plan places nothing until it is connected.
- The subscriber locks the enrollment (`FOR UPDATE`), then its steps, never the lead or an item: the stops' one order, as the suppression subscriber takes it ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The suppression subscriber"). `accepted_at` is written while null and while the request is still out (`succeeded` or `executing`, not the status read, which the sender may settle meanwhile), `due_at` only on a `planned` `message_1` without one: two acceptances at once, the receiver's and the daily check's, record one.
- The queue's next run opens `message_1`'s item, and the subscriber starts nothing (the task's open question). A due time more than 24 hours ahead, a Friday evening's acceptance due on Monday, opens when it enters the queue's window.
- The sender's two keys (beta-p4-020): linkedin's slot takes the step's own idempotency key, which a released key may reserve again; the credits take `<idempotency_key>:<attempt>`, since credits answers a used key with its old row whatever its status. The claim raises `attempt`, so a step released and claimed again reserves anew, and a send commits once.
- The order: the claim's run takes the gate, then the claim, `credits.reserve`, then `reserveSend`; the settle's commits the credits, then the slot. credits takes the organization's lock before any row, so one order everywhere. The gate's own writes come before the claim takes the step's row, and the settle locks the lead (after the last step only), then the enrollment, then the step: the stops' one order, so the sender never deadlocks with a hold or a suppression.
- The gate twice: at the claim, before any hold is taken, and in `campaigns_sender_gate`, right before the call, after the chat's read and the request file. The second compares the approval's `contentHash` with the hash of the text in the file: a mismatch, or no file, is `content_changed`, skipped as the gate's approval block skips. A pass writes `jit_checks` with the item's id and the hash sent.
- The reply read: before a message only, linkedin's `linkedin_check_reply` (a `send_check` read), before the claim and its holds. A reply found sets the outreach row's `last_inbound_at`, so the claim's gate holds the person. A refused read waits as a refused slot does; an answer that is no refusal (a port's error) ends the run with no write.
- The gap: `campaigns_sender_next` answers the wait until the later of the step's time and the account's last `executed_at` plus `MIN_ACTION_GAP_MS`, and one sleep lasts a tick at most, so a step the plan places meanwhile ahead of it is read in time. A step more than a gap past its time is the plan's to place again: `next` passes it over, and the claim refuses it after a slow read (`step_not_ready`, no write). So the sender spaces its own sends, whatever two overlapping plan runs placed.
- A person with another step `executing` waits as `send_in_flight` (beta-p4-040; `touched_today` before it), unplaced: that send's outcome, and so its touch, are not known yet, and the plan's touch rule reads no `executing` step. `touched_today` at either gate unplaces the step too; the claim and a sent step clear `error_code`.
- The sender marks a step `failed` only when nothing went out, never while its send may have gone out, which would free its key: a send LinkedIn refused for good, a person not found or a request off the port's schema (beta-p4-040); a withdrawal whose call answered that the invitation is gone (`linkedin_not_found`, `linkedin_send_refused`; "Withdrawals", beta-p4-060); and a connection request whose profile read found no profile, before any send call (`profile_unreadable`, beta-p4-030). It takes up an `executing` step only at its check. A refused check that kept its slot (told from a refusal before the call by the reservation's status, linkedin's `findSendReservation`), a port's error whose outcome is unknown or a lost answer leave it `executing` with its holds, and the run ends `unsettled`; the next run's check settles it. A step put back and claimed again carries `stepAttempt` 2 or more, so linkedin checks before any call.
- `member_ref_unknown`, and `person_not_found` for a person erased meanwhile, free the holds and put the step back unplaced; the plan places it again at the next tick, and a connection request's next placement reads the profile first (beta-p4-030). `insufficient_credits` rolls the claim's run back, is written in a run of its own, and ends the run: the balance is the workspace's.
- A refusal ends the run when it names no `retryAt` (sending closed, a switch, a paused account, a disconnected account) or is `rate_limited`; one with a time moves `due_at` to it when later, and the run goes on with the next step: an invite refused for the pending invitations (`pending_invites_unknown`, `pending_invites_over_limit`, beta-p4-070), or `invites_paused`, one action's refusal with no time (beta-p4-040, with the patch), leaves the account's messages going.
- The read before an invite (beta-p4-030): the credits first, so an empty balance reads nothing, and the slot only after the wait, so the 3 minutes hold none of the day's slots. The claim's one activity takes a `mode`, typed by it in the workflow; each claim keeps its key `<idempotency_key>:<attempt>`, so a read refused and an invite later release `:1` and commit `:2`.
- The flag comes from `campaigns_sender_next`, and the patch is asked only for such an invite, so a run recorded before beta-p4-030 replays its claim. The settle answers the wait in milliseconds (`waitMs`), from its own time on campaigns' clock, after the read's answer: the workflow's clock is not the module's, and `useProfileReadWait` reaches the activity alone.
- The read's reservation names the step as its object, as linkedin's check before a send does; linkedin's `ok` row of the read names none. A refusal commits the read, one linkedin answers before its call too (`account_paused`, `linkedin_rate_limited`, beta-p6-020), as signals settles its reads (the safe side, as the task has it); no answer releases it.
- `linkedin_not_found` fails the step (`profile_unreadable`), cancels the enrollment's pending steps under the same code with their items, exits the enrollment (`send_refused`) and finishes the lead, as beta-p4-040 settles a send's `linkedin_not_found`. The run counts it `blocked` and goes on: it ends that step as a gate's block does, and says nothing of the account. Another refusal, or no answer, makes the step wait with the refusal's code when `STEP_REASON_CODES` lists it, as it lists `linkedin_provider_error` since beta-p4-040, else `member_ref_unknown`, and ends the run.
- The member id is stored only through contacts' upsert, at the person's stored URL with its provenance; a person erased meanwhile stores nothing, and the gate after the wait ends the step (`person_not_found`).
- Only the sender settles its steps (beta-p4-040): the plan, the queue, a hold and a stop never touch an `executing` step, and the sender takes one up only at its check, before its first claim, one run per account. `due_at` on an `executing` step is when its check may run: null or past, due; a check refused waits for its `retryAt`, and one that failed at the provider for the next quota day's start in the sender's zone: one check per quota day during an outage, never a loop. A step whose request cannot be written again is checked with the file its send left, or, with none, put back unsent: no send goes without its file.
- The run ends on an account's error: a port's error the error map gives an account effect (`linkedin_rate_limited`, the unknown outcomes, `linkedin_limit_reached`, `linkedin_account_disconnected`), and every check that defers, end it at once; a send refused for good, a person not found or a request off the schema fail that step and the run goes on. `linkedin_limit_reached` and `linkedin_account_disconnected` wait for the next quota day's start, `linkedin_rate_limited` for its `retryAfterSec` (with none, `due_at` as it was: linkedin's `retry_at` holds the account).
- No Temporal retry of a send activity (`maximumAttempts: 1`, a minute to start at most): an activity that fails ends the run `unsettled`, the step `executing`, and the next run's check, not a retry, decides. The gate before the call is asked again while its pass is older than `GATE_FRESH_MS` (60 s, of the workflow's time) when the call is scheduled, and refuses a step more than `MIN_ACTION_GAP_MS` past the later of its time and its slot's reservation, its holds freed and the step back for the plan; `campaigns_sender_next` counts a step left `executing` in the gap from its gate's pass (`jit_checks.at`).
- One patch, `check-executing`, gates the check, the gate asked again and `invites_paused` going on, so a run started before it replays as it ran; the send activity's options are not in a history.
- A failed step ends its enrollment `send_refused` (`exit_detail` the code for a defect, `linkedin_input_invalid`), cancels its pending steps with the code and their items, and finishes the lead, as a stop does; a request the port refuses as off its schema is a defect, so the enrollment ends rather than sending it again (the task's open question).
- The error map leaves a withdrawal's invitation already gone to `settleGone` (beta-p4-060, `domain/withdraw/settle.ts`), which the settle asks first: `linkedin_not_found` or `linkedin_send_refused` after a withdrawal's call, its slot `committed`, ends the enrollment `not_accepted`; after a connection request's or a message's call the two codes fail the step by the map, the enrollment `send_refused`.
- A withdrawal answered `linkedin_not_found` because its request was accepted just before, the acceptance detected only after the settle, ends the enrollment `not_accepted`, with the touch's 90 days (kept, from beta-p4-060's review): rare, and on the safe side, as nothing more goes to a person who stays connected; a relation read before that end is the task's open question.
- A withdrawal's check, linkedin's before a retried call (`stepAttempt` above 1) or the sender's of one left `executing`, counts no invitation pending as sent (linkedin's `wentOut`), so a request declined before it ends `succeeded` with `campaigns.step_sent` rather than `failed` (kept, from beta-p4-060's review): the enrollment and the person end the same, `not_accepted` and the touch, and inbox stores nothing for a withdrawal; only the step's status and code differ.
- A replaced account (`linkedin.account_replaced`): its `active` enrollments exit `no_sender` (`account_replaced`) and their leads are `finished`, not `approved` again (the task's open question); an `executing` step is left to the sender's check, which settles it on the account it went from.
- The read before an invite, from beta-p4-030's review: a connection request is late at the gate before the call from the later of its time and its slot's reservation, taken the read's wait after its time; a stored URL off linkedin's `profileRequestFile` is a defect that reserves no read and fails the step (`linkedin_input_invalid`), as a send's request off the schema does; a settle retried once its files are gone answers from the person's member id; and linkedin's read gets `PROFILE_READ_ATTEMPTS` (3) attempts, its own, not the worker's five.
- `erasePerson` locks the lead, then the person's enrollments in id order, before its deletes: the stops' one order. A `message_1` left without a due time gets one at the queue's next run once its account allows it, drawn from that run's time (`domain/queue/recover.ts`).

## The gate

`checkGate(stepExecutionId, { at })` (`domain/gate/gate.ts`), for this module's sender (beta-p4-020) right before each call, in its tenant run: the step `scheduled` or `executing` (`step_not_ready`), the enrollment `active` (`enrollment_ended`), `suppressionOf(personId, { newEnrollment: false })` (its reason), no inbound message on linkedin's outreach row since `started_at` (`replied`), no touch on today's quota day in the sender's zone (`touched_today`), and, last, for every step but a withdrawal, its approval ("The approval check"). The answer is `{ pass: true, checks }` or `{ pass: false, reason, checks }`; a block writes its outcome and `checks` (`jit_checks`), a pass nothing. The sender reserves (linkedin's `reserveSend`) only after a pass. The order, the writes and `checks`: [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The gate".

## The hold on reply

`holdForReply` (`domain/hold.ts`, "Public interface") locks the lead, then the enrollments, the steps and their items, the one order the enrollment and the skip take too: a reply racing an approval's delivery holds the enrollment it makes, and a skip racing a reply waits for the hold, then finds the step held. The gate's reply check calls it too. [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The hold on reply".

## Worker

`workflows/` (beta-p4-010; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The daily plan"). The global schedule `campaigns.plan`, every `TICK_MINUTES` (15, `workflows/constants.ts`), overlap skip, paused while `WORKER_SCHEDULES=off`, runs `campaigns_plan_tick`: its activity `campaigns_start_plans` lists the workspaces through tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds()` and starts each one's `campaigns_plan` with `planStart`, one run of `campaigns.plan:<workspaceId>:current` at a time (`startWorkflow`'s `oneAtATime`), so a tick while one runs starts none for that workspace, and two runs never place times for one account together. A workspace whose start fails leaves the others started, and the tick then fails with its code; it answers `{ workspaces, started }`.

`campaigns_plan` `{ workspaceId }`: its activity `campaigns_plan_accounts` lists the sender accounts of the workspace's `scheduled` steps and plans each in a tenant run of its own with `planAccount(accountId, now)` (`domain/plan/account.ts`), one that fails leaving the others planned; it answers `{ accounts, placed }` and logs `campaigns: account planned` per account, with its id, the skip, the counts and `sender_started`. Once an account has a step placed on today's quota day, the activity starts its sender with `senderStart(workspaceId, accountId)`: `campaigns.sender:<workspaceId>:<accountId>`, one run at a time (`oneAtATime`), so a running one answers `{ started: false }`. The replay histories are in `workflows/__histories__/`.

The global schedule `campaigns.withdraw` (beta-p4-060; "Withdrawals"), daily at 05:00 UTC (`WITHDRAW_CRON`, `WITHDRAW_ZONE`), overlap skip, runs `campaigns_withdraw_tick`: its activity `campaigns_start_withdrawals` starts each workspace's `campaigns_withdraw` with `withdrawStart`, one run of `campaigns.withdraw:<workspaceId>:<UTC date>` at a time, a failed start leaving the others started, and answers `{ workspaces, started }`. `campaigns_withdraw` `{ workspaceId }`: its activity `campaigns_open_withdrawals`, in a tenant run, opens the withdraw steps (`openWithdrawals`, `domain/withdraw/open.ts`) and answers `{ opened }`. `campaigns_sender` `{ workspaceId, accountId }` (beta-p4-020; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The sender"), the account's one writer of its sends, one step at a time. Its activities are kind `db`, each in a tenant run (`domain/send/`, `workflows/sender.ts`), with a start-to-close of `SENDER_TIMEOUT` (1 minute):

1. `campaigns_sender_next`: the account's earliest `scheduled` step placed on today's quota day, no more than `MIN_ACTION_GAP_MS` past its time, its action and how long to wait: until the later of its time and the account's last `executed_at` plus the gap. None: the run ends `no_step`. A wait sleeps at most `SENDER_SLEEP_MAX_MS` (a tick), then asks again.
2. Before a message, linkedin's `linkedin_check_reply` `{ workspaceId, accountId, personId }`.
3. `campaigns_sender_claim`: the gate, then the claim, `executing` with `attempt` raised and `error_code` cleared, conditional on `scheduled` and the time read; for an invite or a message, `credits.reserve` of `campaigns.linkedin_send` × 1 under `<idempotency_key>:<attempt>`, `subjectRef` the step's id, lease `async`, its usage in `credit_usage_id`; then linkedin's `reserveSend` under the step's own key, `withNote` for an invite whose approved note is not empty. `{ claimed, reservationId, usageId, attempt }`, `{ blocked }` or `{ refused, retryAt }`.
4. `campaigns_sender_prepare`: the request file `raw/campaigns/<stepExecutionId>/send.json` in linkedin's `sendRequestFile` shape, written through the files port after the run: the member id from contacts' `providerRefs` under `MEMBER_REF_KEY` (`unipile`), the approved text from approvals' `approvedContent` (an invite's note, left out when empty, or a message's text), the step's key. `{ requestKey, contentHash }`, or `{ waiting }` with the holds freed.
5. `campaigns_sender_gate`: the gate again, against the hash of the text in the file; a block deletes the file.
6. linkedin's `linkedin_send_invite`, `linkedin_send_message` or `linkedin_withdraw_invite` by name, `{ workspaceId, accountId, reservationId, requestKey, stepAttempt }`, with `SEND_ACTIVITY_TIMEOUT` (2 minutes, equal to linkedin's `SEND_CALL_MARGIN_MS`; `workflows/constants.ts`) and `WAIT_CANCELLATION_COMPLETED`, one attempt (`SEND_ATTEMPTS`, beta-p4-040) started within `SEND_START_TIMEOUT` (1 minute). An activity that fails ends the run `unsettled`, the step `executing`.
7. `campaigns_sender_settle`, on the answer; then the file is deleted, but for a step left `executing`.

The run answers `{ sent, blocked, refused, end }`, `end` `no_step`, `refused` or `unsettled`. Inputs, results and the history hold ids, codes, booleans, hashes and instants: never a text, a note or a member ref, which travel only in the request file ([docs/security.md](docs/security.md)). The settle of a send that went out: `credits.commit`, then linkedin's `commitSend`; the step `succeeded` with `executed_at` and `error_code` cleared; the next step due its delay in business days after it in the sender's zone (`nextDueAt`), `message_1` waiting for the acceptance; contacts' `recordTouch`; `campaigns.step_sent`; after `follow_up_2`, the enrollment `completed` and the lead `finished`. A commit that answers `usage_not_open` (past the 24-hour lease) leaves the step `succeeded` and logs the warn line `campaigns: send_uncharged` with `step_execution_id` and `credit_usage_id`. Each other answer: [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The sender".

## The queue

`workflows/` and `domain/queue/` ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The queue"). The global schedule `campaigns.queue`, every `TICK_MINUTES`, overlap skip, runs `campaigns_queue_tick`: its activity `campaigns_start_queue_runs` lists the workspaces through tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds()` and starts each one's `campaigns_queue` with `startWorkflow`'s `oneAtATime`, as `campaigns.queue:<workspaceId>:<slot>`, the slot the tick's 15 minutes in UTC (`20261029T1000Z`, `workflows/queue.ts`); it answers `{ workspaces, started }`.

`campaigns_queue` `{ workspaceId }`: `campaigns_due_steps` lists the ids of the `planned` `invite` and `message` steps of `active` enrollments due by now plus approvals' `QUEUE_WINDOW_HOURS` (24), oldest first (`dueStepIds`); then `campaigns_queue_step` `{ workspaceId, stepExecutionId }` each, one tenant run per step (`queueStep`, `domain/queue/open.ts`). It answers `{ opened, passedOver, noSender, failed }`. A step not approved by its day stays `awaiting_approval` with its item: the queue lists `planned` steps only, and "Up next" shows the item stale.

`queueStep(stepExecutionId)`: `passed_over` unless the step is still `planned` with a `due_at`, an `invite` or a `message`, its enrollment `active` and its person not suppressed (`suppressionOf(personId, { newEnrollment: false })`: no draft is paid for, and the suppression subscriber or the gate ends the step); `no_sender` when linkedin no longer holds its account. Otherwise the step becomes `awaiting_approval` under a condition on `planned`, approvals' `openItem` opens its item with the step's ids, action and key, `scheduledFor` its `due_at`, `senderUserId` the account's owner and `earlierDraftIds` the drafts of the enrollment's `succeeded` steps before it, oldest first, and the step keeps the item's id in `approval_item_id`: `opened`. A withdrawal never gets an item.

## The decision

`approvals.item_decided` (`events.ts`), in its delivery's transaction (`applyDecision`, `domain/decisions.ts`): only a step still `awaiting_approval` whose `approval_item_id` is the event's item, and only on the item's stored status, read with `decisionsFor`, with who decided and when; anything else, a redelivery, a decision after a hold or a cancel, changes nothing. Approved: the step `scheduled` with the item's draft in `draft_id`. Skipped (`skipStep`, `domain/steps/skip.ts`, the lead locked first, then the enrollment): the step `skipped` (`skipped_by_owner`); for the connection request, the enrollment's other pending steps `cancelled` (`skipped_by_owner`) with their items blocked, the enrollment `exited` (`removed`) with `ended_at`, and the lead `finished` (`removed`, the owner its actor); for a message, the next step due its delay in business days after the decision, Monday to Friday in the sender's zone (`nextDueAt`), or no due time once linkedin no longer holds the account, and after the last step the enrollment `completed` and the lead `finished`. A suppressed lead stays suppressed.

Every path that holds, cancels or skips a step with an open item blocks that item in the same transaction (approvals' `cancelItems`): the hold, the suppression subscriber, the gate's suppression branch and the skip. A late press in "Up next" then answers 409 `approval_item_not_pending`.

## The approval check

The gate's last check, for every step but a withdrawal (`checkApproval`, `domain/gate/approval.ts`; `needsApproval`, `domain/steps/actions.ts`, fails closed: an action added later needs an approval until it is exempted by name), in the sender's run with no cache: the step's `approval_item_id` must be its latest item in approvals' `decisionsFor`, `approved`, else `not_approved`; approvals' `approvedContent(stepExecutionId)` must answer for that item, its frozen text still hashing to the approval's hash and approved by the account's owner, else `content_changed`. A block writes the step `skipped` with the reason and `jit_checks`, with the owner's skip's follow-on ("The decision"); a pass adds `approval: { approvalItemId, status, contentHash }` to `checks`, which the sender stores with its send. The step's own status is read first (`step_not_ready`): a held or cancelled step whose item was approved before the stop passes nothing. A withdrawal skips the check: it sends nothing to the person.

Tests: `tests/queue.test.ts` (in `test`): the due steps and their window, `queueStep`'s cases, the decision's cases and `nextDueAt` across a weekend and a zone; `tests/gate.test.ts`, the check last in the order and a pass's `approval`. `tests/safety/approval-check.test.ts` (in `test:safety`): no step `scheduled` without its approved item, `not_approved` and `content_changed` reserve nothing, the stops block the items, a withdrawal needs none, and any other action does. `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-queue.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test`): the tick, the first run's item, the next day's run, approve and skip through approvals' commands, and a late Approve's 409 after a hold; `pnpm -F worker test:replay` replays `campaigns_queue_tick` and `campaigns_queue`.

## Acceptance and the first message

linkedin publishes `linkedin.invite_accepted` `{ sender_account_id, person_id, detected_at }` when it detects that a person accepted the account's connection request: its receiver, at the provider's event (beta-p0-060), and its daily check, for an acceptance the provider missed (beta-p4-070). `detected_at` is when Whytide learned of it, which for an invite without a note can be hours after the acceptance (docs/07 §5.3); the first message counts from it. The subscriber (`events.ts`, its body `recordAcceptance`, `domain/acceptance.ts`), in the delivery's transaction ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "Acceptance → the first message"):

1. the person's `active` enrollment with that account, locked; none: nothing;
2. its `connect` step: nothing unless it went out (`succeeded`, or `executing` under the sender's claim) and its `accepted_at` is null;
3. `accepted_at` = `detected_at`, and no other column: the step's status stays, as only the sender settles its steps;
4. `message_1`, when it is `planned` without a due time: `due_at` = `firstMessageDueAt(detectedAt, account, random)`, the account linkedin's (`findSenderAccount`) and the draw campaigns' `random()`.

`firstMessageDueAt` (pure): the detection plus a uniform draw in `FIRST_MESSAGE_DELAY_HOURS` (1 to 3 hours), never under `MIN_MINUTES_AFTER_ACCEPTANCE` (10 minutes) after it; outside the account's window, or on a Saturday or Sunday in its zone, the next window start on a weekday (platform's `nextWindowAt`, the account's hours with its days cut to Monday to Friday) where the window is open. Null when the account's days hold no weekday. Nothing else starts the step: due within the queue's 24 hours, it gets its item at the queue's next run, at most `TICK_MINUTES` later ("The queue"), and once approved the plan draws its time in the window, never before `due_at`.

Tests: `tests/acceptance.test.ts` (in `test`): inside the window, near its end, at night, on a Friday evening and across daylight-saving changes, no weekday, a broken source. `tests/safety/first-message-timing.test.ts` (in `test:safety`): seeded detections and working hours in three zones, never under 10 minutes after the detection, never outside the hours or on a Saturday or Sunday, 1 to 3 hours after it inside the window; through `recordAcceptance`, the same on `message_1`'s row, and a second acceptance moving nothing. `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-acceptance.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test`): the event through the outbox, a redelivery and a later acceptance, an ended enrollment, a request never sent and another account's or workspace's acceptance, and the queue's run opening the item.

## Settings

`CAMPAIGNS_CREDITS_PER_LINKEDIN_SEND` (1, an integer from 0 to 100; `settings.ts`, the `modules/campaigns` block of `deploy/.env.example`): the credits of one send, a connection request or a message, `campaigns.linkedin_send`'s price as the module declares it. credits' `PRICE_CAMPAIGNS_LINKEDIN_SEND` overrides it when set, and a change is a new price version at the api's next start (credits' README "Declaring a price"). A withdrawal and a check cost nothing.

## Commands

`pnpm campaigns:report [--workspace <id or slug>] [--since <YYYY-MM-DD>]` (`cli/report.ts`), from the repository root, builds the module and prints every figure of beta's "Done when" that code can measure, read from the logs, on the worktree's database (`.env.local`); on the production machine, from `deploy/`, `docker compose exec api node ../../modules/campaigns/dist/cli/report.js [options]`. Without `--workspace`, every workspace (tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds`), each in its own tenant run as the service `campaigns`; `--workspace` is looked up first (an id has an underscore, a slug none). `--since` starts the period at that day's midnight in UTC; without it, every row. It loads campaigns, approvals, contacts, linkedin and tenancy through their `index.ts`, calls no provider and writes nothing. Each line is `<code>: <detail>` with ids, codes, counts, days and instants only, never a name, a text, a note or a provider reference; the figures are `domain/report/`, one file each, and [docs/runbook.md](docs/runbook.md) "The report" says how to read them. In order:

- `sends_without_approval: <n>`: of linkedin's `ok` invites and messages (`sendLogRows`), those whose step approvals' `approvedContent` does not cover: `no_approval`, `item_mismatch` (another item or hash than the step's `jit_checks`) or `approved_after_send`; each `violation: sends_without_approval <workspaceId> <sx_id> <reason> <at>`.
- `send_step_missing: <n>`: `ok` rows, withdrawals included, whose step no longer exists or that name none; each `missing_step: <workspaceId> <sal_id> <sx_id or none> <sac_id> <kind> <at>`. Not a zero.
- `actions_against_suppressed: <n>`: invites and messages to a person contacts' `suppressedAt` answers `opted_out`, `never_contact_person` or `never_contact_company` at the row's instant; each `violation: actions_against_suppressed …` as above. `person_not_found`, a person erased since, is not counted.
- `duplicate_sends: <n>`: a step with more than one `ok` invite or message row, or an idempotency key more than one `succeeded` step holds, one of them in the period; each `violation: duplicate_sends <workspaceId> <sx_id> <its rows, or the other steps>`.
- `drafts_small_edits: <share> approved=<n> unedited=<n> small_edits=<n> target=0.5`: approvals' `editStats`, summed; `none` when nothing was approved.
- `acceptance_rate: <share> sent=<n> accepted=<n> target=0.25`, then `acceptance_rate_sender: <workspaceId> <sac_id> <share>`: the connection requests `succeeded` in the period at least 7 days before the run (`REPORT_ACCEPTANCE_MIN_AGE_DAYS`), with `accepted_at` set.
- `senders_three_weeks: <n> target=3`, then `sender_span: <workspaceId> <sac_id> <first day> <last day> <days>`: the UTC days of each sender's first and last `ok` invite or message; a sender counts at 21 days (`SENDER_SPAN_DAYS`).
- `restrictions: <n>` and `checkpoints: <n>`, each event `health_event: <workspaceId> <sac_id> <she_id> <state> <trigger> <at>` (linkedin's `healthEventsSince`).
- `decision_days: <workspaceId> <n> of <m>`: of the weekdays among the 7 local days before the run's day in each sender's zone, those with a decision in "Up next" (approvals' `decisionDays`); always the last week, whatever `--since`.

Exit 0 the three zeros hold; 1 a zero is broken, or `workspace_not_found: <id or slug>`; 2 the usage, for an unknown option, a positional or a malformed day. Through pnpm, any failure is 1. Tests: `tests/report.test.ts` (each figure across two workspaces and its boundaries) and `tests/report-cli.test.ts` (the lines, the exit codes and no personal data), in `test`.

## Withdrawals

`domain/withdraw/` and `workflows/withdraw.ts` (beta-p4-060; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The withdrawal"). A connection request not accepted `INVITE_WITHDRAW_AFTER_DAYS` (21) days after it went out is withdrawn once, through the plan, the gate and linkedin's reservation as any send, but with no approval and no credits; the enrollment then ends, and contacts' touch refuses the person a new enrollment for 90 days.

The job ("Worker"), daily at 05:00 UTC, runs `openWithdrawals(now)` in each workspace: for each `active` enrollment whose `connect` step is `succeeded`, with `executed_at` at least 21 × 24 hours ago and `accepted_at` null, one step `withdraw` (`WITHDRAW_STEP_KEY`), `position` 1, `action_type` `withdraw`, with the `connect` step's sender, template and `step_template_hash` and its key followed by `:withdraw`, `scheduled` and due now, with no approval item. The unique `(workspace_id, enrollment_id, step_key)` skips one that exists, so a second run, or the next day's, opens none.

The sender ("Worker") sends it as any step but for this: the gate's last check is the request's `accepted_at`, not an approval (`invite_accepted`: the step `cancelled`, the enrollment going on; `jit_checks.invite`); the claim reserves no credits; linkedin's `reserveSend` takes `actionType: "withdraw"` (`WITHDRAWALS_PER_DAY_MAX`, 15, and the 200); the request file holds no text; the call is `linkedin_withdraw_invite`. The settle (`domain/withdraw/settle.ts`) locks the lead, then the enrollment, then the step:

- the withdrawal went out: the step `succeeded` with `executed_at`; contacts' `recordTouch`; `campaigns.step_sent` with `actionType: "withdraw"`; then the end;
- `linkedin_not_found` or `linkedin_send_refused` after the call, the invitation already gone (declined, or withdrawn by hand): the step `failed` with the code and `executed_at`, the touch recorded all the same, no event; then the end, and the run goes on;
- the end, unless the request was accepted meanwhile: the enrollment `exited` (`not_accepted`) with `ended_at`, its pending steps `cancelled` (`not_accepted`) with their items blocked, and the lead `finished`.

A person erased meanwhile has no touch to record. The codes, in `STEP_REASON_CODES` and the panel's words: `invite_accepted` and `not_accepted` (`skip_reason`), `linkedin_not_found` and `linkedin_send_refused` (`error_code`).

Tests: `core/apps/worker/tests/campaigns-withdraw.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test`): the schedule and the tick's starts, the run's rows and what it passes over, the withdrawal end to end, an invitation already gone, and an acceptance on the way. `core/apps/worker/tests/safety/withdrawal.test.ts` (`pnpm -F worker test:safety`): day 20 kept and day 21 withdrawn once, 15 a day, the 200, the window, the weekdays and a kill switch, no approval and no credits, an accepted request, and 90 days without a new enrollment; each asserts the fake's calls. `pnpm -F worker test:replay` replays `campaigns_withdraw_tick` and `campaigns_withdraw`.
