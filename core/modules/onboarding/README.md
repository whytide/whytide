# onboarding

The workspace's one Company Knowledge record: what the company sells, to whom, its customers' pain points, its proof points, its competitors and its tone, with the website it comes from. The tester reads and edits it on the screen at `/w/{ws}/company`, or has it built from the website by the prompt on the workspace home (onboarding-setup); the website fetch (alpha-p4-020) and the drafter (alpha-p4-030) fill it, also from the company's documents, picked on the screen (onboarding-setup) or given to the script `pnpm knowledge:draft` (knowledge-draft); the writer and the own-employee filter read it. Every content field remembers who wrote it last, an AI draft or a person, so that the drafter can leave a person's edit alone (alpha-p4-030).

- **Owns:** `company_knowledge`, `website_pages` and `company_documents` in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/onboarding.prisma` ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)), the Draft's workflow under `workflows/`, the drafter under `agents/draft-company-knowledge/`, the screen and the prompt under `ui/` and the script under `cli/`.
- **Public interface:** `index.ts`, below.
- **Depends on:** `platform` (its egress client among the rest), `db`, `ai` (the drafter's task, `callAgent`); the workflow on `@temporalio/workflow` and `@temporalio/activity`; the screen on `@whytide/ui`, `@whytide/web` (its api client), `next-intl`, `react` and `unpdf` (a PDF's text, imported only when a PDF is picked); the prompt on icp's `GET …/icp` and `GET …/icp/draft`, through the web's generated client, never an import; the script on `tenancy` (`findWorkspaceBySlug`) and `unpdf`.
- **Reviewers:** the owners of this module.

## Public interface

| | |
|---|---|
| `readCompanyKnowledge(workspaceId)` | the `CompanyKnowledge` record, as `GET` answers it, in the caller's tenant run (`withTenant`): the drafter's and the writer's read; the first read creates it empty; another workspace than the run's is `tenant_mismatch` |
| `getCompanyDomain()` | the run's company domain, the host of `sourceUrl` without a leading `www.` (`acme.test`), or `null` without a website: the own-employee filter's (alpha-p8) |
| `api` | the routes below |
| `events` | `onboarding.company_knowledge_updated` `{ workspaceId, version }`, on every save that changes the record; `onboarding.draft_requested` `{ workspaceId, companyKnowledgeId, draftRunId }`, on every Draft |

The registries: `ids` (`ck`, `page`, `cdoc`, `drun`), `errors` (`company_knowledge_invalid_url` and `documents_too_many`, 400; `company_knowledge_no_website` and `company_knowledge_no_draft`, 409; `draft_agent_not_loaded`, 500; `fetch_failed`, a run's code, never an answer; each with its key in `messages/en.json`; the drafter's run codes `ai_failed`, `draft_failed` and `draft_empty`, and the documents' other refusal codes, are not registered, their keys are the screen's), `permissions` (`company_knowledge.read`: Owner, Admin, Member; `company_knowledge.write`: Owner, Admin; kept, not evaluated, in alpha), `events` and `settings` (`ONBOARDING_FETCH_MAX_PAGES`, 1 to 10, 5 by default; `AI_MODEL_ONBOARDING_DRAFT_COMPANY_KNOWLEDGE`, the drafter's model, the LLM port's first when empty; `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILES` and `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILE_BYTES`, "Settings"). The types `CompanyKnowledge`, `ContentField`, `FetchStatus`, `FieldSource` and `ProofPoint` come with them.

## Routes

The api host mounts them under `/v1/workspaces/{ws}/onboarding`; their requests are in `api-calls/alpha-p10-090-onboarding.http`, one per route. Each answers the record with `pages`, those the last fetch read, in its order: `{ url, title, status, fetchedAt }`, and `documentLimits`, `{ files, bytes }`, the two settings the documents' card reads files by ("Settings").

| Route | Permission | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /v1/workspaces/{ws}/onboarding/company-knowledge` | `company_knowledge.read` | the record; the first read creates it empty, `fetchStatus` `none`, version 1 |
| `PUT /v1/workspaces/{ws}/onboarding/company-knowledge` | `company_knowledge.write` | saves the website and every content field (a full replacement) and answers the saved record |
| `POST /v1/workspaces/{ws}/onboarding/company-knowledge/draft` | `company_knowledge.write` | `@Idempotent()`, 202, body optional `{ documents?, autoAccept? }`: drafts from the saved website and the documents given ("The documents"; more than `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILES` 400 `documents_too_many`, `details.files` the setting), `fetchStatus` `fetching` (below), the run accepting its own draft with `autoAccept` ("The automatic accept"); with neither a website nor a document 409 `company_knowledge_no_website` |
| `POST …/company-knowledge/draft/accept` | `company_knowledge.write` | `@Idempotent()`, body `{ replaceEdited?: boolean }`: accepts the pending draft ("The drafter"); without one 409 `company_knowledge_no_draft` |
| `POST …/company-knowledge/draft/discard` | `company_knowledge.write` | `@Idempotent()`: drops the pending draft, every field kept; without one 409 `company_knowledge_no_draft` |

A length or a count off the limits is the host's 400 `validation_failed`, `details.fields` naming each path (`longDescription`, `products`, `products.3`). A website or a proof point's page that is not an absolute `http` or `https` URL without a user name or password (a proof point may also cite a document, `document:<name>`, "The documents") is 400 `company_knowledge_invalid_url`, `details.fields` naming each (`sourceUrl`, `proofPoints.1.sourceUrl`), never the value; nothing is written.

## The record

- `sourceUrl` (2,048 characters; empty or `null` for none), `oneLiner` (200), `longDescription` (2,000), `tone` (500); `products`, `painPoints`, `featuresBenefits` and `competitors`, 20 items of 1 to 200 characters each; `proofPoints`, 20 of `{ text, sourceUrl? }`, a text of 1 to 500 characters. The limits are `LIMITS` in `domain/fields.ts`.
- Stored trimmed, each list without duplicates (a proof point by its text), in the order given.
- `fetchStatus` (`none`, `fetching`, `fetched`, `drafted`, `failed`), `fetchedAt` and `fetchError` belong to the Draft's run, below; a save never moves them. `draftRunId`, the latest Draft's run, is never answered.
- `pendingDraft`: the drafter's draft, `{ model, promptVersion, decisionRecordId, fields }`, each field's value with `sourceUrl` and `quote` (`schemas/draft-company-knowledge.ts`), until it is accepted or discarded, or a new Draft starts; `null` otherwise.
- `fieldSources`: per content field, `{ kind: "ai_draft" | "human", at, sourceUrl? }`, who wrote it last. A save marks each content field whose value changed `human`, a list's order included; the others keep theirs.
- `version` is 1 when created and moves by one with every save that changes a field, the website included; the event carries it. A save that changes nothing writes nothing and publishes nothing.

## The website fetch

A Draft (`requestDraft`, `domain/draft.ts`) locks the record, gives it a new `draftRunId` (`drun_…`), sets `fetching`, clears the error and publishes `onboarding.draft_requested` in the same transaction; a repeated key answers the first answer and publishes nothing. The worker's subscriber starts `onboarding_draft_company_knowledge` with the ids, by the id `onboarding.draft:<workspaceId>:<draftRunId>` through the platform's `startWorkflow` (`draftStart`, `events.ts`), so an event delivered twice starts one run. The workspace is the delivery's tenant run's (`currentTenant()`), never the payload's: the starter does not compare them.

The workflow's first activity, `onboarding_fetch_website` (`workflows/fetch-website.ts`, kind `provider`), reads the website through the platform's `fetchSafe` with no tenant run open: `robots.txt` first (500 KiB; a 4xx answer is no rule, another answer but a 2xx allows nothing; the `whytide` group, else `*`), then the home page, then up to `ONBOARDING_FETCH_MAX_PAGES - 1` of its same-host links, in this order: the first about, product, pricing and customers page (`FETCH_PREFERRED`, `workflows/constants.ts`), then navigation links, then the rest; never a path `robots.txt` disallows. One megabyte, ten seconds and five redirects a page, sixty seconds in all, a heartbeat per page, HTML only, never inflated. The text keeps the title, headings (`#` per level) and paragraphs, drops scripts, styles, navigation, footers and comments, at most 50,000 characters; a page cut at either bound is `truncated`.

The reader is the platform's (`readSite`, core/packages/platform/README.md "The site reader", gamma-en-065), shared with enrichment's `website` source; onboarding gives it its preference and keeps what is its own: the content hash, `fetchedAt` and the Draft's records. A home page robots.txt disallows is `fetch_failed`, as before.

One tenant run then writes the outcome, only while `draftRunId` is still the run's (a later Draft supersedes it: `superseded`, nothing written): the pages replace the last run's, with `url` after redirects and `fetchedAt`, and the record becomes `fetched`; or it becomes `failed` with the code of `robots.txt` or the home page (`egress_*`), or `fetch_failed` (not a readable HTML page, or disallowed), and keeps no page. Another page that fails is left out. A site's failure is a value, never retried; a fault (the database) retries, and once the attempts are spent `onboarding_fetch_failed` marks the run `fetch_failed`.

## The drafter

Once the fetch wrote its pages, the workflow's second activity, `onboarding_draft_from_pages` (kind `ai`, `domain/drafter.ts`), drafts the record from them with the agent task `onboarding.draft-company-knowledge` ([agents/draft-company-knowledge/](agents/draft-company-knowledge/README.md)), which the worker registers at start; unregistered, the activity fails with `draft_agent_not_loaded`. One tenant run reads the pages' `url`, `title` and `text`, in the order read, then the Draft's documents ("The documents"), each text whole as stored (50,000 characters at most), while the run is the latest. `callAgent` then asks the model outside any run, the record's id as its subject: the pages in one `<pages>` data block, the answer checked against the output schema and the gates (`domain/draft-gates.ts`: every `sourceUrl` a page read, every `quote` in that page's text, case and whitespace aside, every list and text within the record's limits), retried once with the issues in a `<validation_error>` block, then `ai_failed`. The wrapper writes one decision record and one cost event per call; unchanged pages give the same input, so a second Draft reuses the decision record and asks no model. One tenant run then writes, the row locked and only while the run is the latest (else `superseded`, nothing written): the draft as `pendingDraft` and `drafted`, or `failed` with `ai_failed`. The content fields never change there. An attempt of the activity has fifteen minutes (`DRAFT_TIMEOUTS`, `workflows/constants.ts`): one model call on every text, up to the task's 800,000 input and 16,000 output tokens, and the gates' one retry. A fault past the activity's attempts (the model's port, the database) marks the run `failed`, `draft_failed`, through `onboarding_draft_failed`.

The review: an accept (`acceptDraft`, `domain/review.ts`) writes each drafted field, a text or a list with an item, whose source is not `human`, or every one with `replaceEdited`, stored as a save stores it (`applyDraft`, `domain/rules.ts`); each field that changed is marked `ai_draft` with its value's page, a list with its first item's; then the version moves and `onboarding.company_knowledge_updated` carries it, the draft cleared, in one transaction under the row lock. When no field changed, only the draft is cleared. A discard (`discardDraft`) clears the draft and nothing else.

## The script

`pnpm knowledge:draft --workspace <id or slug> [--url <https://…>] [--file <path>]... [--accept [--replace-edited]]` (`cli/draft.ts`), for an engineer: drafts a workspace's record from its website and up to `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILES` of its documents (five unset, "Settings"). It never calls a model. It reads the files first ("The documents"); then one tenant run saves the website with `--url` (the form's rule, the content fields unchanged) and starts the screen's Draft, `requestDraft` with the documents; the worker fetches and drafts. It reads the record every two seconds, each read its own run, until the run is `drafted` or `failed`, twenty minutes at most (`WAIT`, `cli/constants.ts`, the screens' stuck rule), then prints the pages (address and status), the documents (name and status) and each drafted field on a line (a text's first 60 characters, a list's count), or the run's code. `--accept` then accepts the draft as the screen does (`acceptDraft`; `--replace-edited` for `replaceEdited`), while the run is still the latest; without it, the script prints the Company screen's path. Every line starts with a code and carries names, codes and field values, never a page's or a document's text. Exit codes (`EXIT`): 0 done, 1 refused before any write, 2 usage, 3 the run failed, 4 a later Draft superseded the run, 5 the wait ran out: the worker goes on and the draft shows in the Company screen ("is the worker running?" while still `fetching`), 6 `workspace_not_found`: the workspace is found first (tenancy's `findWorkspace` for an id, which has an underscore, else `findWorkspaceBySlug`), so a mistyped id writes nothing and starts no Draft. Through pnpm, any code but 0 shows as 1; the printed code says which.

## The documents

The script's `--file` and the Company screen's documents' card take the same files by the same rules (`domain/documents.ts`, `domain/constants.ts`): at most `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILES`, each a `.pdf`, `.txt` or `.md` of at most `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILE_BYTES` (five and 10 MB unset, "Settings"), checked before a byte is read; a PDF's text through `unpdf` (PDF.js; no OCR), a text file's as UTF-8; U+0000 removed, another control character but a tab or a line break a space, a lone surrogate U+FFFD; cut at 50,000 characters like a page's, never inside a character (`truncated`, else `read`). The codes: `document_type_unsupported`, `document_not_found` (the script's alone), `document_too_large`, `document_no_text` (a scan), `document_encrypted`, `document_unreadable`, `documents_too_many`, `document_name_repeated`, `document_name_invalid`. The script reads in its own process before anything is written (`cli/documents.ts`), and its first refusal stops it with its code and the path, printed, never an answer. The screen (`ui/company/documents-card.tsx`, below the website's card) reads the two limits from the record's answer (`documentLimits`) and each file in the browser, `unpdf` through a dynamic `import()`, so that its PDF.js chunk loads only once a PDF is picked; it lists each file with its status or its refusal in words, each removable, and Draft from documents sends the texts read, the refused ones left out, with the Draft: `POST …/draft` with `{ documents: [{ name, mediaType, status, text }] }`, at least one, a name `isDocumentName` takes, each once, a text of 1 to 50,000 characters without U+0000, refused by its path (`documents.2.text`), never its value; more than `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILES` is `requestDraft`'s 400 `documents_too_many`, since no route schema reads the environment. The progress and the review follow as for a Draft from website. No file is stored, only the texts.

They are the documents of the record's latest Draft, in `company_documents`: `requestDraft(workspaceId, documents)` replaces them in the transaction that starts the run; a Draft without any removes them. A record with no website drafts from its documents alone: the fetch activity reads nothing and writes `fetched` with no page, and the drafter reads the documents (with neither, 409 `company_knowledge_no_website`, the script's too); a run left with neither a page nor a document (the website removed while it fetched) ends `fetch_failed` and asks no model. The drafter reads them after the pages, each one an entry `{ url: "document:<name>", title: <name>, text }`; a drafted value cites `document:<name>`, which the gates check like a page's address. The screen shows such a source as "Source: <name>", never as a link; an accept keeps it with its proof point, and a save takes it back.

## The automatic accept

A Draft with `autoAccept: true` (the prompt at sign-in's) marks its run (`draft_auto_accept`, the latest Draft's, cleared by the next Draft without it). When its drafter's write lands (`recordDraft`), under the row lock and in the same transaction, the run accepts its own draft as Accept does without "Also replace the fields I edited" (`acceptDraft(false)`): a field a person wrote, before the run or during it, is kept; the version moves and `onboarding.company_knowledge_updated` is published when a field changed; the record stays `drafted` with no pending draft. A draft with no value (no text, no list item: `draftHasValue`) saves nothing and ends `failed` with the run code `draft_empty`, worded on the Company screen and the prompt. A run without the flag waits for review as before.

## The screen

`/w/{ws}/company` (`ui/nav.ts`, the `company` group): `page.tsx` renders the title and what the record is for; `company-screen.tsx` reads the record in the browser through `browserApi()`, with the kit's `LoadingState` and the cards as skeletons, then on a failure its `ErrorState`, the code in words with a retry. `company-form.tsx` holds the form: the website's card, with the empty state while the record has no website and no content; the fields in four cards (the company, what it sells, who it sells to, how it sounds), two side by side from `xl`: the one-liner, the long description and the tone with their counters, the lists as chips, a badge per field ("AI draft" with a link to its page or its document's name, or "Edited"), the trigger of the kit's `Tooltip`, whose hint (`onboarding.company.source.hint`) shows on hover and on keyboard focus; then the kit's `SaveBar`, which stays in view while the form differs from the record, with "Last changed {time} by an AI draft you accepted" (or "by a person"), the time and the kind of the latest `fieldSources` entry, no line before a field was written, and Save, which PUTs every field through `browserApi()` and shows the saved record, the error under each field the api names, or another failure in words with a retry. A proof point keeps its page while its text stays; one typed on the screen has none.

Under the website's field, `website-fetch.tsx`: Draft from website (the form saved first when its website changed, a new `Idempotency-Key` per press, the same one on a retry), which asks first in the kit's `Dialog` while a draft waits for review, since a Draft drops it (Cancel keeps it); the progress while `fetching`, then while the drafter reads the pages (`fetched`), a bar for each step, asking for the record every two seconds until it ends `drafted` or `failed`. A failed read stops the asking and shows its code with a retry, which resumes it. A run `fetching` or `fetched` for more than twenty minutes by the record's `updatedAt` is stuck (`STUCK_MS`, `DRAFT_RUN_MS.stuck`: more than the fetch's 75 seconds and a drafter's 15-minute attempt): the asking stops, the screen says so, and Draft, which a run never disables, starts a new one ([docs/runbook.md](docs/runbook.md)). Then the pages read, by title (the address without one), each `http` or `https` page linked in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, "Partly read" on a cut one, "No page was read" for none; or the failure in words: one text for `egress_blocked_address` and `egress_dns_failed`, so that the screen never tells which names resolve to private addresses; the drafter's `ai_failed`, `draft_failed` and `draft_empty` under "The draft from your website failed". Below the website's card, the documents' card ("The documents"), whose Draft starts as the website's does (`draft-start.tsx`). With a pending draft, `draft-review.tsx` shows each drafted field, the record's value ("Empty" when it has none) beside the draft's from `md`, the "Edited" badge on a person's, every drafted value with a link to its page or its document's name, "Also replace the fields I edited", Accept, whose answer replaces the form's fields, and Discard, which leaves the form as it is; a draft with no value says so, with Discard alone. A failed Accept or Discard shows its code in words with a retry, which sends the press's key and body again; none for `company_knowledge_no_draft`, nor for the Draft's `company_knowledge_no_website`. The text is `onboarding.company.*` and `onboarding.errors.*`; Save, Cancel, Try again, the loading word and the generic failure are the shared `ui.*` and `platform.error.generic`.

## The prompt at sign-in

The workspace home's `home` slot (`ui/nav.ts`; the web host's docs/screens.md). `ui/get-started/slot.tsx`, a server component, reads `GET /v1/me` and the record as the member: the card shows to an Owner or an Admin (`COMPANY_KNOWLEDGE_WRITE_ROLES`, `company_knowledge.write`'s) while the record has no content field and no draft waiting for review; else, or on a failed read, nothing. The card (`get-started.tsx`): a title and a line on what happens, the website's field (the form's limit, and the PUT's refusal under it in the form's words), **Build**, and **Skip, set it up myself**, a link to the Company screen that hides the card in this tab until the next sign-in (sessionStorage, a key per workspace, in try/catch). ## Decided here

- The routes sit under the module's name, `/v1/workspaces/{ws}/onboarding/company-knowledge`; the task's `…/company-knowledge` follows it.
- The event is `onboarding.company_knowledge_updated`, the platform's `module.event_name`, for the task's `company_knowledge.updated`.
- The message keys are snake_case (`onboarding.company.one_liner`, `onboarding.company.source.ai_draft`): a key's segments are lower-case letters, digits and underscores, so the task's camelCase keys would fail the message check.
- `PUT` replaces every field, as icp's does; the version starts at 1 and moves on any change, the website included; the website is not a content field, so it has no source.
- A list's order counts as a change; whitespace around a text does not.
- Item lengths the task leaves open: 200 characters per list item, 500 per proof point, 2,048 per URL.
- A URL with a user name or a password is refused, as one the fetch would send them with.
- `getCompanyDomain()` drops a leading `www.`, so the own-employee filter compares `acme.test` with `acme.test`.
- No foreign key to `workspaces` (Prisma would need the opposite relation in `tenancy.prisma`), as icp.
- The commands run in the caller's run, so the module keeps no database client of its own.
- The workflow is `onboarding_draft_company_knowledge`, its activity `onboarding_fetch_website`: the worker takes names that start with the module's, for the task's `draft_company_knowledge` and `fetchWebsite`. The route is `…/onboarding/company-knowledge/draft`, under the module's name; the keys `onboarding.company.pages_read` and `fetch_failed`, snake_case.
- The text extraction is a small tokenizer of our own, linear in the page, so no HTML parser is a dependency.
- Only `robots.txt` and the home page fail a run; the home page disallowed is `fetch_failed`. A 5xx `robots.txt` allows nothing (RFC 9309).
- A Draft while one is fetching is allowed and supersedes it: the latest Draft wins, by one compare in the final write's transaction.
- The pages keep their text after the draft: the company's own public site, which the writer may cite.
- alpha-p4-030: the task is `onboarding.draft-company-knowledge` (the registry's names take hyphens), its activity `onboarding_draft_from_pages`, the keys snake_case (`onboarding.company.draft_ready`). The pages reach the model as one `<pages>` block of JSON `{ url, title, text }`, since the ai package writes one block per input field, for the task's `<page url="…">` blocks; each text cut at 6,000 characters and the budget 40,000 input and 6,000 output tokens (draft-full-text changed both, below), priced at no credit in alpha (the cost event records it).
- The cache's subject is the record and its input the pages' `url`, `title` and `text` only, never a time or an id, so unchanged pages reuse the decision record.
- `ai_failed` and `draft_failed` are run codes in `fetchError`, not registered errors: no route answers them, and `ai_failed` is every agent's.
- An accept marks only the fields it changed and moves the version only on a change; a new Draft clears the pending draft; the answer carries `pendingDraft` with its `decisionRecordId`.
- `fetched` is now the step between the fetch and the drafter; a record an older run left `fetched` or `fetching` is stuck after ten minutes (alpha-p10-090).
- alpha-p10-090: the record is read in the browser, so that its loading and error states show and Playwright can hold or fail the read; `page.tsx` keeps the `<title>`.

## Settings

Two values that were constants until env-settings-b (the owner's request of 2026-11-15), `settings.ts`, each a whole number of 1 or more with no maximum, its default in `domain/constants.ts` (`DOCUMENT_FILES`, `DOCUMENT_BYTES`). Unset or empty, the default applies; a value that is not a whole number of 1 or more stops the api's and the worker's start, and the script's, with `config_invalid` naming it. Each is read once, as the module loads: **a changed value needs an api and worker restart**, and a reload of the Company screen.

- `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILES` (5): the documents one Draft takes, from the script (`cli/documents.ts`, `documents_too_many` naming the first file past it) or the screen's documents' card (the file refused on the list); `requestDraft` refuses more with 400 `documents_too_many`, `details.files` the setting. No route schema reads it, so the OpenAPI document is the same everywhere; the answer's `documentLimits.files` carries it to the card.
- `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILE_BYTES` (10485760, 10 MB): the bytes of one file, checked before a byte is read (`document_too_large`); the card reads it from `documentLimits.bytes` and says it in megabytes, to two decimals at most.

The Draft's body is 10 MB, no setting (`DRAFT_BODY_BYTES`, domain/constants.ts): the Draft route alone sets it, with the api's `whytide:body-limit` metadata, as signals' source imports do, and every other route keeps the api's 1 MB (`JSON_BODY_LIMIT`, `core/apps/api/src/protection/body.ts`). Only the texts reach the api, never the files, so `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILE_BYTES` never meets it, but `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILES` can. A document keeps 50,000 characters at most, each at most three bytes in the Draft's JSON (about 150 KB a document): 69 full documents of three-byte text (Chinese, say) fit, a 70th passes the 10 MB; of English text about 200 fit. Past it, the api answers 413 `payload_too_large` before the route runs: no Draft starts and nothing is written; the card shows the generic error (onboarding has no message for that code) with Try again, which fails the same way until a document is removed. The script calls `requestDraft` in its own process, with no body limit. The web's own rewrite to the api in development (`next dev`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL`) passes up to 10 MB of a body too (Next.js's `proxyClientMaxBodySize`, 10 MB by default); in production the reverse proxy sends `/api/*` to the api, and a proxy with its own body limit (Caddy has none by default, nginx's `client_max_body_size` is 1 MB) needs 10 MB on that path. The api's `core/apps/api/tests/onboarding-company-knowledge.test.ts` sends 69 and 70 full three-byte documents with the default five allowed, and gets documents_too_many and the 413; `core/apps/api/tests/settings-openapi.test.ts` starts a Draft of seven with ten allowed.

Raising `ONBOARDING_MAX_FILES` also adds to the drafter's input: with `ONBOARDING_FETCH_MAX_PAGES` at its 10 and every page and document at 50,000 characters of three-byte text, a sixth document passes the drafter's 800,000-token budget (five give 766,214, the agent's README "Input"); the drafter then asks no model (`ai_budget_exceeded`) and the run ends `draft_failed`. The drafter's budget comes first, long before the body: it holds about 15 full three-byte documents (each counts as 50,000 tokens) and no page, where the 10 MB body takes 69.

The tests: `tests/document-limits.test.ts` sets 10 files of 20 MB (the script reads ten, one of exactly 20 MB, and refuses an eleventh and a byte more; `requestDraft` takes ten and refuses eleven) and tries values that are not whole numbers of 1 or more in processes of their own; `tests/documents.test.ts` and `tests/cli.test.ts` run the defaults; the api's `onboarding-company-knowledge.test.ts` checks the answer's `documentLimits` and a sixth document's `documents_too_many`, and `settings-openapi.test.ts` the answer with ten files of 20 MB set and the OpenAPI document unchanged.
