# onboarding: data

Three tenant tables in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/onboarding.prisma`, created by the migrations `20261022181000_onboarding_company_knowledge`, `20261022192000_onboarding_website_pages` and `20261029014500_onboarding_company_documents`; `20261022194000_onboarding_pending_draft` adds `pending_draft`. The ids are `ck_<ULID>`, `page_<ULID>` and `cdoc_<ULID>` (`ids.ts`); the times are `timestamptz`.

| Table | Id | Columns | Keys | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `company_knowledge` | `ck_` | `workspace_id`, `source_url`, `fetch_status` (text, `none` by default: `none`, `fetching`, `fetched`, `drafted`, `failed`), `fetched_at`, `fetch_error` (the fetch's code, or the drafter's `ai_failed` or `draft_failed`), `one_liner`, `long_description`, `products`, `pain_points`, `features_benefits` and `competitors` (text arrays), `proof_points` (JSON, `[{ text, sourceUrl? }]`), `tone`, `field_sources` (JSON, per content field `{ kind, at, sourceUrl? }`), `version` (from 1), `updated_at`, `draft_run_id` (the latest Draft's `drun_…`), `pending_draft` (JSON or null, `{ model, promptVersion, decisionRecordId, fields }`) | `(workspace_id)` unique | tenant |
| `website_pages` | `page_` | `workspace_id`, `company_knowledge_id`, `url` (after redirects), `status` (`fetched`, `truncated`), `fetched_at`, `title`, `text`, `content_hash` (SHA-256 of the text) | `(workspace_id, company_knowledge_id, url)` unique | tenant |
| `company_documents` | `cdoc_` | `workspace_id`, `company_knowledge_id`, `name` (the file's base name), `media_type` (`application/pdf`, `text/plain`, `text/markdown`), `status` (`read`, `truncated`), `text` (at most 50,000 characters), `content_hash` (SHA-256 of the text), `added_at`: the documents of the latest Draft, from the script | `(workspace_id, company_knowledge_id, name)` unique | tenant |

No foreign key to `workspaces`: Prisma would need the opposite relation in `tenancy.prisma`. The limits, the URLs and the fetch statuses are checked by the commands and the schema, the only writers, not by the database.

Personal data: not by design, but present. The record and the pages describe a company, yet the fetch reads its public site, the about, team and customer pages among them, so people's names and words (team members, customers quoted in testimonials) can be in `website_pages` (`title`, `text`), in the drafter's prompt sent through the LLM port, in the decision record's raw prompt (the pages' text, purged after `AI_RAW_RETENTION_DAYS`, 30 by default), in `pending_draft`, and once a person accepts the draft, in the record: a proof point may quote a customer by name, kept as the company's own marketing text. Every page keeps its `url` and `fetched_at`, its provenance. Each run replaces the pages; they are kept with the workspace after the draft. The documents an engineer gives the script (README "The documents") are the company's own, yet may hold the same, and more (a deck may name customers and staff): their text is in `company_documents`, in the drafter's prompt and its raw record, and in `pending_draft`; each Draft replaces them, and a Draft from the screen removes them. The drafter's call writes one `decision_records` row and one `cost_events` row (the ai package's and the platform's). Retention: configuration, kept with the workspace; nothing is deleted in alpha.

The screen derives two things and stores neither: the last-changed line from `field_sources` (the latest `at` and its `kind`), and a stuck run from `fetch_status` and `updated_at`.
