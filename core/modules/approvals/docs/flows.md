# approvals: flows

- **An item opens:** campaigns calls `openItem` in its run. A step's pending or approved item is answered as it is; else a `pending` item is written and messaging's `requestStepDraft` asks for the draft, written as the sender's owner, an email's from its mailbox.
- **The draft attached:** messaging publishes `messaging.step_draft_ready` when the draft ends; the subscriber, in the delivery's transaction, sets `draft_id` on the step's pending item without one. A redelivery or a blocked item changes nothing.
- **The mode's approval:** an item opened for the mode, once its draft is attached at the open or by the subscriber, in that same run: the item is claimed while `pending`; a connection request or a draft not `ready` is left to a person (`mode_declined`); else messaging's `approveStepDraftByMode` adds the disclosure line and freezes the text, or refuses it (`mode_declined` too); the item becomes `approved`, origin `approval_mode`, no `decided_by`, with the hash; `approvals.item_decided`. All commit together or not at all.
- **An approval:** `POST …/items/{id}/approve` in the request's run: the item is claimed while `pending`, the caller must own the sender, the account or the mailbox, the draft must be attached and be the `draftId` given; messaging's `approveStepDraft` checks and freezes the text, a first email's subject with it; the item becomes `approved` with the hash, its wait cleared; `approvals.item_decided` is written to the outbox. All commit together or not at all.
- **A skip:** the same checks; the item becomes `skipped`; the draft stays as it is; `approvals.item_decided`.
- **A cancel:** campaigns calls `cancelItems` when it holds, cancels or skips a step: its pending item becomes `blocked`, so a late press is 409.
- **The send's check:** campaigns reads `approvedContent` before a send and compares the hash with what it carries (beta-p3-050): a person's approval only, until gamma-p4-070 asks with `allowMode`. inbox's record of a sent message asks with `allowMode: true`: the step was sent.
- **The queue's read, per member (gamma-p6-050):** in the request's run, the senders of the items it could show or count (pending, waiting, decided by a person in the last `LOCAL_DAY_MAX_HOURS`), with their counts per status in one grouped read, and the caller's connected LinkedIn account; each sender read once, linkedin's `findSenderAccount` or email's `findMailbox`; those the caller owns kept; then those senders' items, at most `ITEMS_LIST_MAX`, and their recent decisions, counted per sender from today's start in its zone. A caller who owns none gets an empty queue and no sender.
- **"Up next" loads:** the page reads `GET …/approvals/items` as the member, on the server; the browser reads it again after each decision, every `POLL_MS` while a row is drafting (until `POLL_MAX_MS` after its step opened), and on Check again or Try again. The last read wins.
- **"Up next" presses:** Approve posts `POST …/items/{id}/approve` with the shown `{ draftId }`, and `text` and `subject` once edited; Skip posts `POST …/items/{id}/skip` once its dialog is confirmed. Each press has its own `Idempotency-Key`, which its Try again sends again; a refusal stays on its row, a refused text with the gates' issues. Show facts reads messaging's `GET …/messaging/leads/{personId}/dossier`, once per row, on the first open.
