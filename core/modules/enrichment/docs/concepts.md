# enrichment: concepts

- **Source:** one way to fill fields, named in a list: `company-data/<adapter>` for each adapter of `PROVIDER_COMPANY_DATA`, or a module's own (`defineSource`). It has a kind (`company` or `person`), the fields it fills, the inputs it needs (any one of the company's `linkedinUrl`, `domain`, `name`, the person's `locationText`) and an activity kind: `provider` (a vendor's API, 15 seconds unless it sets its own, one attempt), `ai` (its own timeout, one attempt) or `db`.
- **Source class:** the provenance class a source's facts carry in contacts, the one the lead page shows (RB-7.3.2): `open_data` for an adapter that declares open data, else `data_vendor`; `web_search` for the source `web-search`; `ai_inferred` for `country-ai` (gamma-en-075). The provider beside it is the adapter's or the source's name.
- **Run:** one lead's enrichment, a row of `enrichment_runs`: its trigger (`new_lead` from the batch, `request` from a member), its key (`new_lead:<personId>`, `request:<key>`), its status (`queued`, `running`, `done`, `failed`), the fields it wanted and filled, the sources it asked and why it skipped the company (`no_company`, `enriched_recently`, `company_running`).
- **Wanted field:** a field the run looks for: the company's `domain`, `linkedinUrl`, `industry`, `sizeRange`, `country` and `description` while empty, and the person's `person.country` while empty and a person source fills it. A field a member set or cleared is never wanted.
- **Pass:** the walk over the run's sources in their order, from the top again each time a source adds an input (a domain, a LinkedIn page). A source runs at most once a run, when it has an input and a wanted field to fill is still open.
- **Outcome:** what asking a source came to: `found`, `not_found`, `no_company_key` (facts with neither a page nor a domain for a person with only a company name), `allowance_spent`, `failed` with the error's code, or a source's own word for a skip (`no_account`, `ambiguous`), a code. `found`, `not_found`, `no_company_key`, and web-search's `not_a_company` and `ambiguous` (`ANSWERED`) count as the company tried; a run where every source failed, was out of calls or skipped in its own word tried nothing.
- **Allowance:** a source's free calls per UTC day or month, its adapter's. The calls are counted for the whole instance in `source_usage`; a vendor's word that they are gone marks the period spent.
- **Hold:** contacts' wait of a new lead before its first judgement (`CONTACTS_ENRICHMENT_HOLD_MINUTES`, gamma-en-040). A run's finish releases it; contacts' own limit releases it anyway.
- **A company known only by name:** a person whose company is a name, with no LinkedIn page and no website. The source `web-search` (gamma-en-070) looks for its page and its website with at most two web searches and keeps a result only when it matches the name in code; a new page or domain makes the company record, links the person, and restarts the pass for the sources that need it.
- **Not a company:** an employer name that names no company (self-employed, freelance, stealth, retired, student, open to work, n/a and the like, `NOT_A_COMPANY_NAMES`), or one shorter than 2 characters: web-search makes no search for it, `not_a_company`.
- **Ambiguous:** two results that match the name and name different pages or hosts. Nothing is written: a wrong company would give the lead a wrong size and industry, which is worse than none.
- **Named country** (gamma-en-075): a person's country the source `country-ai` got from the AI, from the location text alone, when contacts' place list could not read it. Written only when the model is sure and the code is a country contacts knows; a location naming no place ("Remote") asks nothing, and an ambiguous one ("Cambridge") stays empty.
- **Email lookup** (gamma-en-080): one person's work email found and verified, a row of `enrichment_requests`: its trigger (`jit_step`, `request`, `reverify`), its key, its attempt and its status (`queued`, `running`, `found`, `not_found`, `failed`, `skipped_suppressed`, `waiting_budget`, `waiting_allowance`), with the reason it has no address.
- **Email source:** proposes candidate addresses: `email-finder/<adapter>` for each adapter of `PROVIDER_EMAIL_FINDER`, or a module's own (`kind: "email"`). Only one with `anyDomain` (the lead's own addresses) may propose one off the company's domain: a personal address.
- **Candidate:** an address a source proposed, numbered in the lookup, held in its `raw/` file until the settle, never in the workflow. Dropped before any verifier when off the address's form (`address_invalid`), off the domain (`other_domain`), seen already (`seen`), judged invalid by its finder (`vendor_invalid`), or personal for a lead whose country needs consent (`personal_needs_consent`).
- **Verdict:** a verifier's word on an address: `valid`, `catch_all`, `risky`, `invalid` or `unknown`. Only `valid` keeps it.
- **Contact point:** a verified address on a person, a row of `contact_points`, `work_email` or `personal_email`, with its verification, provider, source class, acquisition time and legal basis; `active`, `bounced` or `opted_out`. One address sits on one person of a workspace.
- **Cache entry:** a person's key (first name, last name, domain, hashed) with its answer: the address for `ENRICHMENT_EMAIL_VALID_DAYS` (120 unset), or a negative reason for `ENRICHMENT_EMAIL_NOT_FOUND_RETRY_DAYS` (30 unset).
