# icp: runbook

- **Settings**: `AI_MODEL_ICP_DRAFT_PROFILE`, the drafter's model; empty, the LLM port's first model.
- **Failure modes**: 400 `icp_unknown_seniority`, `icp_unknown_size_range` or `icp_unknown_country`: `details.fields` names the values off the list by their path. 400 `icp_weights_must_sum_100`: `details.sum` is what the six added up to. 500 `tenant_mismatch` from `readIcpForScoring`: the caller passed another workspace than its run's. 404 `icp_not_found`: the audience's id is not one of the workspace's (`?audience=` on the screen, `--icp` for the script). 409 `icp_audience_limit`: the workspace holds `ICP_MAX_AUDIENCES_PER_WORKSPACE` audiences, `details.limit` (10 unset); raise the setting and restart the api, or reuse an audience (README "Settings").
- **The draft** (`pnpm icp:draft`): `icp_draft_timed_out`: the worker is not running, or not on the stack's Temporal; start it and run the script again. `icp_draft_failed: ai_failed`: the model's answer failed its schema or gate twice, see the decision record; `draft_failed`: the drafter failed three times (the model's port, the database), see the worker's log; `icp_draft_no_source`: the workspace has no Company Knowledge, pass `--brief`. `icp_not_empty`: the ICP has targeting, pass `--replace`. `icp_draft_ended`: the draft was used or discarded on the ICP screen while the script waited; nothing for it to save. 500 `icp_draft_agent_not_loaded`: the worker started without the drafter's task.
- **The draft on the screen**: "No draft yet: is the worker running?" after ten minutes is the script's `icp_draft_timed_out`: start the worker, then Try again. 409 `icp_draft_none`: the proposal was used or discarded meanwhile, in another tab or by the script, or a later request replaced it; the card shows the draft as it is now. 409 `icp_not_empty`: the ICP got targeting since the page loaded; Use again asks first. 422 `icp_draft_invalid`: the proposal is off the PUT body's limits, `details.fields` its paths; discard it and draft again.
- **Events**: `icp.updated`, `icp.audience_updated` and `icp.draft_requested` rows in `outbox`, delivered by the worker; a dead one is replayed with the platform's `replayDeadLetter`.
