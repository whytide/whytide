# credits: runbook

- **Settings**: `CREDITS_MONTHLY_ALLOWANCE` and `CREDITS_PLAN_CODE` (README "Settings"); a value off the rules stops the api's start with `config_invalid`, `details.block` `modules/credits`, `details.variables` naming the variable.
- **Change the allowance with `.env`**: set `CREDITS_MONTHLY_ALLOWANCE` and restart the api, or run `pnpm credits:seed`. The plan changes at once; the current period keeps its grant, and the next period is granted the new value.
- **Change the allowance with SQL**, as `billing_writer` (`psql "$DATABASE_URL_BILLING_WRITER"`): `UPDATE plans SET monthly_allowance = 20000000 WHERE is_fixed;`, effective from the next period. The next start writes `.env`'s value again: change `.env` too.
- **Read the prices**: the costs page's prices card, or `GET /v1/workspaces/<workspace>/credits/prices` (`api-calls/gamma-p2-060-visible-prices.http`): each action's price in effect, its version, and `next` during a notice.
- **Raise a price**: set `PRICE_<ACTION>` in `.env` (README "Declaring a price") and restart the api. The start logs `credits: a price rise is announced` with `price_list_version` and `effective_from`, `CREDITS_PRICE_CHANGE_NOTICE_DAYS` days on (30 unset); the old price holds until then, and the screens show the coming one with its date. A malformed value stops the start, `config_invalid` naming the variable.
- **Lower a price**: the same; a drop applies at the start, logged `credits: prices written`.
- **Withdraw a pending rise**: before its date, set the price back (remove its `PRICE_<ACTION>`, or set the old value) and restart: the start logs `credits: a pending price change is withdrawn` with the version, and `next` is null again. Any other change during a notice withdraws it too, and announces the declared list with a new notice.
- **Fix a wrong price**: one too high, lower it: it applies at once. One too low can only rise with its notice. The usage rows already charged keep their credits and their `price_list_version`. Never edit `price_list` by hand: `billing_writer` may only set `withdrawn_at` of a pending version, and the CHECK `price_list_withdrawn_before_effect` (SQLSTATE `23514`) refuses a version in effect.
- **Read the week's cost for the alpha review**: open `/w/<workspace>/credits` (or click the balance in the header) and pick the week, Monday to Sunday in UTC; or `GET /v1/workspaces/<workspace>/credits/costs?week=2026-W44` (`api-calls/alpha-p10-010-cost-counter.http`). Note `credits`, `money`, `qualified` and `costPer100Qualified`; `byAction` and `byVendor` say where they went. `money` is the price list's currency and leaves out LinkedIn reads. A lead's own cost is the line on its page, or `GET …/credits/leads/<personId>/cost`.
- **A credit alert** (README "Alerts"): the Owners and Admins of an organization's workspaces got a mail at a threshold of `CREDITS_ALERT_THRESHOLDS` (80, 95 or 100 percent unset; README "Settings") of the period's granted credits. After a 100 percent alert, raise `CREDITS_MONTHLY_ALLOWANCE` and restart the api, as "The seeder" says: the plan changes at once, but the current period keeps its grant, so the new allowance counts from the next period. Until then work that spends credits gets `insufficient_credits` once the balance, carry-over included, is spent; the mail says how many credits are left. Read the alerts as `app`: `SELECT organization_id, period_start, threshold, used, granted, notified_at, tries FROM credit_alerts ORDER BY created_at DESC LIMIT 20;`.
- **An alert not mailed**: a row with `notified_at` null and `tries` under 3 goes at the next check, every 15 minutes, while its period lasts; one at 3 never goes. Look for `credits: an alert's mail failed its attempts` (`alert_id`, `row_count`) and the worker's `worker: an activity failed` with `activity: "credits_mail_alert"` and the app-mail port's code (`mail_unreachable`, `mail_rejected`): check `PROVIDER_APP_MAIL` and the SMTP settings (core/packages/providers/app-mail/README.md). `credits: an alert has no Owner to mail` means no Owner or Admin was active and enabled in any workspace of the organization: the alert counts as mailed.
- **A mail that keeps failing for one recipient**: the others are mailed once and recorded in `credit_alert_mails`; each later attempt and check sends to the failing one only, until `tries` reaches 3. A timeout counts as a failed try too, so a hanging SMTP server also stops after 3 checks. See who got it, as `app`: `SELECT user_id, mailed_at FROM credit_alert_mails WHERE alert_id = '<calert_…>';`. A recipient may get it twice only when their row failed to write after the send. Once the cause is fixed, an alert at 3 tries never goes again; the next threshold's alert still does.
- **Metrics**: `credits_usage_total` by `outcome` (reserved, insufficient, committed, released, expired), on the dashboard `deploy/observability/dashboards/credits.json`; a rise of `insufficient` means wallets running dry.
- **Logs** of the alerts: `credits: the alerts are checked` with `organization_count`, `recorded_count` and `alert_count` at every check; `credits: an alert is mailed` with `alert_id`, `organization_id`, `threshold` and `recipient_count`.
- **Logs**: `credits: the plan and the periods are seeded` at every start, with `plan_id`, `granted_count` and `period_start_at`; `credits: prices written` with `price_list_version` and `price_count` when a version takes effect at once; `credits: a price rise is announced` with `price_list_version` and `effective_from`; `credits: a pending price change is withdrawn` with `price_list_version`; `tenant: running without a tenant context` with `reason: "credits.balance"` on every balance read.
- **Failure modes**:
  - the api does not start, `module_invalid` for `credits` with `field: "onStart"`: the seeder failed; the cause is in the log line. Check `DATABASE_URL_BILLING_WRITER` and its login role (`pnpm env:status`), then run `pnpm credits:seed`;
  - `credits_seed_self_hosted: no fixed plan, run pnpm credits:seed` (SQLSTATE `P0002`) when an event is delivered: the seeder never ran on this database; run it, and the event's retry grants;
  - after a change of `CREDITS_PLAN_CODE`, the new plan is fixed for organizations subscribed later, and a subscription keeps its plan: move it as `billing_writer` with `UPDATE subscriptions SET plan_id = (SELECT id FROM plans WHERE is_fixed);`;
  - `insufficient_credits` (402): the organization's wallet has less than price × quantity left; a reservation of a crashed caller holds until its lease ends and the next reserve expires it;
  - a balance that looks wrong: compare it with the sums over `credit_grants` and `credit_usage` (the query of `tests/balance-trigger.test.ts`); only the triggers write it.
