# credits

The wallet, one per organization: the plan it is on, the monthly grants and the usage reserved or committed against it ([architecture §10](../../../docs/architecture.md#10-credits)). In Core the plan is fixed, `self_hosted`, written from `.env` at the api's start by the seeder; an organization gets its subscription and its first grant when tenancy creates it. Modules spend through the platform's `credits` client, which this module implements (reserve, commit, release), at the prices they declare in their `prices.ts`.

- **Owns:** `plans`, `subscriptions`, `credit_grants`, `credit_usage`, `credit_balances`, `price_list` and `credit_alerts` in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/credits.prisma`, with the grants, the balance triggers and `credits_seed_self_hosted` in the migration `credits_initial`, the CHECKs, `app`'s column grant and the settle-once trigger in `credits_reserve`, `credit_alerts` with its CHECKs and `app`'s grants in `credits_alerts`, and `credit_alert_mails` with `app`'s grants in `credits_alert_mails` ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)).
- **Public interface:** `index.ts`, below.
- **Depends on:** `platform`, `db`, `tenancy` (`findWorkspace` and its event); for the cost counter, `contacts` (`getLead`), `qualification` (`countQualified`) and `signals` (`personReads`, its prices' actions).
- **Reviewers:** the owners of this module; two reviews ([security §8](../../../docs/rules/security.md#8-conduct)).

## Public interface

| | |
|---|---|
| `readBalance(workspaceId)` | the balance of the organization the workspace belongs to: `{ organizationId, granted, reserved, committed, updatedAt }`, the amounts as decimal strings; `null` for an unknown workspace or before the first grant. Outside a tenant run only |
| `periodFor(date)` | the period holding the instant: `{ start, end }`, the calendar month in UTC, `end` excluded |

| `api` | `GET /v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/credits/balance`: `{ available, granted, reserved, committed, periodEnd }` of the workspace's organization; `GET …/credits/usage?from&to&cursor&limit`: the workspace's usage rows, newest first, `limit` 1 to 100 (50), with `nextCursor`, each with the `priceListVersion` its reserve charged (null for a row the migration could not fill); `GET …/credits/prices`: `{ prices: [{ action, credits, unit, version, effectiveFrom, next }] }`, every action of the version in effect by action, `next` (`{ credits, unit, version, effectiveFrom, announcedAt }`) when a pending version changes its price, else null (Showing a price); `GET …/credits/costs?week` and `GET …/credits/leads/{personId}/cost`, the cost counter, below. The registries: `ids` (`plan`, `sub`, `grant`, `usage`, `bal`, `pl`, `calert`), `settings`, `metrics` (`credits.usage`, by outcome: reserved, insufficient, committed, released, expired; `deploy/observability/dashboards/credits.json`), `permissions` (`credits.read`), `errors` (`insufficient_credits` 402, `usage_not_open` 409, `credits_not_bound` 500, `credits_input_invalid` 400), and `events`: none published; `events.ts` subscribes to tenancy's `tenancy.organization_created` and calls `credits_seed_self_hosted` for the new organization.

## The cost counter

What a week and a lead cost (alpha-p10-010; [docs/concepts.md](docs/concepts.md) "Costs"), in `domain/costs/`, read in the request's run:
- `weeklyCosts({ workspaceId, week })`, `GET …/credits/costs?week=2026-W44` (the current week without `week`): the ISO week in UTC, Monday 00:00 to the next Monday excluded; `credits`, the usage committed among the rows created in the week, `byAction`; `money`, the `unit_cost` of the cost events created in it, `byVendor`; `qualified`, qualification's `countQualified`; `costPer100Qualified`, both × 100 ÷ `qualified`, null for none. A week the year lacks is `credits_input_invalid` (400), `details.fields` `["week"]`.
- `leadCost(personId)`, `GET …/credits/leads/{personId}/cost`: the committed usage and the cost events whose subject is the person, and what the signal runs that found it charged, signals' `personReads`, both under `signals`: its share of each run's committed `signals.engager_read`, a run's before beta-p1-090, its credits × the person's candidates ÷ the candidates the run staged; and the committed `signals.engager` whose subject is one of the person's candidates, the profile reads of a run since; `byFeature` by module. A lead found by runs of both kinds shows both. A person with no lead in the workspace, another workspace's too, is contacts' `lead_not_found` (404), never a zero.

Amounts are decimal strings, summed exactly (`domain/costs/decimal.ts`), a share cut at the 30th decimal. LinkedIn reads write no cost event: their money is not counted.

## Screens

`ui/nav.ts` declares the shell's `balance` slot (core/apps/web/docs/screens.md): `ui/balance/slot.tsx` reads `GET …/credits/balance` as the member when a workspace page loads and shows `available` as `credits.balance.label`, a link to the costs page; `ui/balance/balance.tsx` reads it again from the browser at each navigation, since the shell's layout stays while its pages change. A failed read shows nothing, or keeps the last value: the header never takes a page down.

The costs page, `/w/{ws}/credits` (a `pages` entry: no navigation group holds it), reads the balance, the prices, the week's costs and its usage in the browser: the balance, large, and the period's end, with a bar of the period's grant, spent then reserved; the prices card (Showing a price); a week picker of the last twelve weeks; the week's numbers as tiles, by action and by vendor, each amount with a bar of its share of the week, and its usage a page at a time, with the loading state, the error state with the code in words and a retry, and the empty state of a week with nothing spent. `ui/lead-cost/panel.tsx` is the cost line in the `cost` slot of contacts' lead page, a server component; the slot shows its loading and error states.

## Reserving

A module calls `credits` of `@whytide/platform` (core/packages/platform README "Credits") inside its own tenant run, a request's or an activity's `context.tenant`, never the tables ([architecture §4.3](../../../docs/architecture.md#43-how-modules-talk), rule 5):
- `reserve({ workspaceId, action, quantity, idempotencyKey, subjectRef, lease })` takes the organization's lock (`pg_advisory_xact_lock`, held to the run's end), expires the organization's reservations past their lease, checks `granted − reserved − committed` against price × quantity, at the price in effect at the reserve's instant, and inserts the row `reserved` with that price list's version in `price_list_version`: `{ usageId, credits }`, or `insufficient_credits` with no row. A key already used in the workspace returns its row. `lease` is `sync`, 15 minutes, for work in a request, or `async`, 24 hours, for a workflow's.
- `commit(usageId, actualQuantity?)` settles it at the credits of the actual quantity, pro rata and never above the reservation; `release(usageId)` frees it. A repeat returns the same; an unknown usage, the other settlement or a commit past the lease is `usage_not_open`.
- `balance(workspaceId)` and `priceOf(action)` read in the run; an action without a price is `credits_input_invalid`.

## Declaring a price

A module prices its actions in its `prices.ts`, and exports `prices` from its `index.ts` so the api loads it:

```ts
export const prices = definePrices([{ action: "qualification.assessment", credits: 1, unit: "lead" }], import.meta.url);
```

At the api's start, `onStart` writes the declared prices into `price_list` as `billing_writer`, at the start's instant (`syncPrices(client, entries, log, now)`), against the version in effect then:
- a new action, a removed action or a drop (fewer credits for the same unit, 0 included) is in effect at once, in a new version at `now` that keeps the current price of any rise;
- a rise, more credits or another unit, waits `CREDITS_PRICE_CHANGE_NOTICE_DAYS` days (30 unset, "Settings"): the declared list becomes the pending version, in effect at `now` plus those days, `announced_at` `now`, logged `credits: a price rise is announced`, unless the pending version holds exactly the declared list already;
- a pending version that no longer matches the declared list is withdrawn, `withdrawn_at` `now`, logged `credits: a pending price change is withdrawn`: so the operator withdraws a pending rise by setting the price back, removing its `PRICE_<ACTION>` say, and restarting. A change during a notice starts a new notice;
- the same list, during a notice too, writes nothing.

The version in effect at an instant is, among the versions not withdrawn whose `effective_from` is at or before it, the latest `effective_from`, then the highest version: reserve and `priceOf` read it at their instant (`currentPrice(tx, action, at)`). `PRICE_<ACTION>` in `.env`, the action in upper case with `.` as `_` (`PRICE_QUALIFICATION_ASSESSMENT=0.5`), overrides a price: a decimal of 0 or more, else the start stops with `config_invalid` naming the variable. An action priced by two modules stops the start with `duplicate_price_action` naming both files. A price changes with a restart only.

## The seeder

`onStart`, run by the api at every start and by `pnpm credits:seed`, as `billing_writer` (`DATABASE_URL_BILLING_WRITER`):
1. upserts the plan `CREDITS_PLAN_CODE` with `monthly_allowance` set to `CREDITS_MONTHLY_ALLOWANCE` and `is_fixed`; any other plan stops being the fixed one;
2. calls `credits_seed_self_hosted` for every organization with a subscription, so each has its grant for the current period;
3. logs `credits: the plan and the periods are seeded` with `plan_id`, `granted_count` and `period_start_at`.

A failure stops the api's start (`module_invalid` for `credits`, `details.field` `onStart`); `pnpm credits:seed` exits 1. Changing the allowance: [docs/runbook.md](docs/runbook.md).

## Periods

A period is the calendar month in UTC (`periodFor`, and the same rule in SQL). `credits_seed_self_hosted(organization_id)` subscribes the organization to the fixed plan when it has no subscription, moves the subscription to the current period and grants its plan's allowance for that period, once: a second call in the same period changes nothing and returns `false`. A subscription that is not `active` gets nothing.

The worker grants a new period: the schedule `credits.period` starts the global workflow `credits_period` every day at 00:05 UTC. Its activity `credits_grant_periods` (kind `db`) lists the organizations with a subscription in `withoutTenant("credits.period")` and calls `credits_seed_self_hosted` as `app` for each, one call each, so a failure keeps the grants made before it and the activity's retry runs the pass again. It logs `credits: the periods are granted` with `organization_count` and `granted_count`. The api's start grants the current period too (The seeder).

## Settings

The `modules/credits` block of `deploy/.env.example`: `CREDITS_MONTHLY_ALLOWANCE`, a whole number of credits from 0 to 2^53 − 1 (default `10000000`), and `CREDITS_PLAN_CODE`, 2 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and underscores, starting with a letter (default `self_hosted`). Then two values that were constants until env-settings-b (the owner's request of 2026-11-15), each read once, as the module loads; unset or empty, the default applies, and a value off its form stops the api's and the worker's start with `config_invalid` naming it. **A changed value needs an api and worker restart**:

- `CREDITS_ALERT_THRESHOLDS` (`80,95,100`): the shares of a period's granted credits, in percent, that each send one alert mail a period ("Alerts"): whole numbers from 1 to 100 (a share of the period's credits, so 100 is the top), separated by commas, at least one, each once, in any order (the check reads them lowest first). Its default is `ALERT_THRESHOLDS` in `domain/alerts/constants.ts`; the CHECK `credit_alerts_threshold` takes any whole percent from 1 to 100 since the migration `20261115121624_credits_alert_thresholds`. The mail's subject and body name the threshold as `{threshold}%`; only 100 has words of its own (the subject says all of the period's credits are used, and the body that work stops when the balance runs out), so a list without 100 sends no such mail. Rows already written keep their thresholds. A threshold added below a period's share is recorded at the next check and mailed with the period's figures then, even when a higher one was mailed already; a threshold removed records nothing more.
- `CREDITS_PRICE_CHANGE_NOTICE_DAYS` (30): the days a price rise is announced before it applies ("Declaring a price"), a whole number of 1 or more with no maximum; its default is `PRICE_NOTICE_DAYS` in `domain/constants.ts`. A pending rise keeps the date it was written with; a later start that withdraws and announces it again takes the new days.

The tests: `tests/settings.test.ts` sets thresholds `50,80,100` and a 14-day notice, applies the migration on a table holding 80, 95 and 100, and tries the forms in processes of their own; `tests/alerts.test.ts` and `tests/prices.test.ts` run the defaults. The api host's `settings-openapi.test.ts` builds the OpenAPI document with them unset, set small and set large: the same.

## Decided here

- The organization-level tables are global models, read through `withoutTenant("credits.<reason>")` for the organization tenancy gives for the workspace, never one a caller names; `credit_usage` is the tenant table ([docs/security.md](docs/security.md)).
- `credit_usage`'s idempotency key is unique per `(workspace_id, organization_id, idempotency_key)`: a tenant table's unique keys start with `workspace_id`.
- `billing_writer` inserts and updates; nobody deletes. `credit_balances` is written by the triggers alone.
- Amounts are `numeric`, read as decimal strings.
- tenancy's event is `tenancy.organization_created`, named `module.event_name` as the platform's events are.
- reserve runs in the caller's run, on its transaction: the lock, the sweep, the check and the insert commit or roll back with the caller's work. It reads the workspace's `organization_id` in that transaction, since tenancy's `findWorkspace` opens a `withoutTenant`, which a run refuses (`nested_run`). The sums stay Postgres `numeric`: Prisma's `Decimal` computes with 20 digits.
- An advisory lock, not `SELECT … FOR UPDATE`: `app` may not lock `credit_balances`. Commit and release take it too, before any row, so a run that settles, then reserves, cannot deadlock with another reserve of the organization. reserve reads the price after the repeated key, so a repeat returns its usage even when the action lost its price. The sweep is lazy, by the next reserve of the organization; `balance` shows an expired lease as reserved until then.
- A commit's credits are the reservation's pro rata of the actual quantity, at the reservation's price; a release frees a reservation past its lease too.
- The price list's version is global: each version holds the whole list.
- Only a rise waits: a drop, a new action or a removal never makes a workspace pay more, so it applies at once. Another unit counts as a rise, since its credits cannot be compared with the old ones.
- A change during a notice, a drop of another action included, withdraws the pending version and announces the declared list with a new notice: a version has one date, not one per action.
- A withdrawn version stays in `price_list` for the record and never takes effect, so no usage row names it; a new version's number follows the highest one, withdrawn ones included. A start keeps the pending version only when it equals the list it would announce.
- `syncPrices` writes in one transaction, so a start never leaves a withdrawal without its new version. `now` defaults to the clock, as the worker's tests call it; `onStart` and credits' tests pass it.
- `GET …/credits/prices` reads the clock of the request, and its `next` is the earliest pending version's price where it changes the action's; the screens show the unit as declared, a code, since the units have no words of their own yet.
- `credits_period` runs every day, not on the 1st alone: the function grants a period once, so a day with nothing new grants nothing, and a worker down on the 1st grants the month the next day.
- The cost counter's week is the ISO week in UTC, as contacts' weekly summary and qualification's golden set name one. A usage row counts in the week it was created, committed only; a lead counts as qualified in the week its assessment's own snapshot (no re-score's criteria hash) was created with `qualified`, once. A run's candidates are its counts' `staged`, whose share of the run's reads each candidate carries, so the shares of a run's persons never add up to more than the run's credits.
- review-gaps-2 (2026-11-13): an alert's mail records each recipient in `credit_alert_mails` right after their send, so a retry or a later check mails only the others; at most the one recipient whose record fails after the send gets it twice. An advisory lock of the alert and the user, held across that one send in a transaction of at most `ALERT_SEND_HOLD_MS`, keeps two checks at once from mailing anyone twice, rather than a claim row inserted before the send, which a crash after it would leave unmailed for good. The workflow raises the tries through `credits_fail_alert` once the mail activity fails for good, rather than the activity's last attempt, which a start-to-close timeout never reaches. No `patched()`: `credits_alerts` had run on no deployed worker (merged as gamma-p7-020 the same day, the owner's app on an earlier commit), so no history holds the old commands; `__histories__/credits_alerts.json` is recorded again, a run with a sent mail and a failed one.
- The api host references the module in its tsconfig and loads its build by path, with no dev dependency (its tests' `pnpm run build` builds it first): credits depends on qualification, which depends on icp, which lists the web, which depends on the api: a cycle `pnpm -r` refuses (`ERR_PNPM_TASK_CYCLE`).

## Alerts

When an organization has used a threshold of `CREDITS_ALERT_THRESHOLDS` (80, 95 and 100 percent unset; "Settings") of the credits granted for its current period, the Owners of its workspaces get one mail per threshold per period (gamma-p7-020; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The credit alerts"). The operator sets the thresholds in the env file; no screen, action or permission sets one.

- **The share** (`domain/alerts/share.ts`), for each organization whose subscription's period (`period_start` to `period_end`) holds the check's instant: `granted`, the sum of the grants whose `period_start` is the period's; `used`, the sum of the usage `reserved` or `committed` created in the period; the share, used ÷ granted in percent, exact and cut, never rounded, so 79.9 reaches nothing. A period with no grant, or a grant of 0, raises no alert. Credits carried over from earlier periods count in neither figure, so the 100 percent alert can go while credits are left; the mail says how many.
- **The workflow** `credits_alerts`, global, from the schedule `credits.alerts` every `CREDIT_ALERT_CHECK_MINUTES` (15), Temporal's default overlap policy, skip, paused under `WORKER_SCHEDULES=off`. It passes its start time to `credits_check_alerts` (kind `db`, global), which inserts a row of `credit_alerts` for each threshold reached, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` on `(organization_id, period_start, threshold)`, so two checks at once insert one row per threshold, and answers by organization the highest of the current period's rows whose `notified_at` is null and whose `tries` are under `CREDIT_ALERT_MAX_TRIES` (3). Then `credits_mail_alert` (kind `provider`, global, 5 attempts on Temporal's backoff, a `retryAfterSec` honored) for each, one at a time; a mail that failed its attempts, whatever ended them, a start-to-close timeout included, counts as `failed`, gets its `tries` raised by `credits_fail_alert` (kind `db`, global), and the others still go. The run answers `{ organizations, recorded, mailed, failed }`.
- **The mail** (`domain/alerts/mail.ts`), as linkedin's health mail: the recipients through tenancy, the organization's workspaces from `listWorkspaceIds()` and `findWorkspace(id)`, in each one's run (as the service `credits`) its active members from `listMembers()`, every `Owner` and `Admin` (declared beside Owner for credit alerts, F18 §4; no gamma path gives the role), each user once, a user whom `findUser` shows disabled left out. Each is mailed through the app-mail port in their locale, outside any run: `credits.mail.alert.subject` and `.body` with the threshold, `used` and `granted` at the crossing, `left` (the organization's balance, carry-over included) and the period's end, and while `APP_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` is set the link to the costs page of the first of the organization's workspaces where they are Owner or Admin. It names the organization and nothing else of a person, a lead or a workspace. Each recipient's send runs under an advisory lock of the alert and the user (`ALERT_MAIL_LOCK`), in a transaction of its own, and is recorded in `credit_alert_mails` right after it: a retry, in the same check or a later one, and another check at the same time mail only the recipients not recorded. A recipient whose record fails after their send, the one case, is mailed again by the next attempt. A failed send leaves the others to go, then fails the attempt. Once every recipient is recorded, `notified_at` is set on the row and on the organization's lower rows of the period not mailed yet: a check that crosses several thresholds sends one mail, for the highest, and records the lower ones with it. A mail that failed its attempts gets `tries` raised on those rows (`failAlert`, through `credits_fail_alert`), so the next check tries again, up to `CREDIT_ALERT_MAX_TRIES`, and a long outage still ends in one mail per recipient. An alert mailed already answers `already_sent`; one whose period is no longer the subscription's, `past_period`: a past period's rows are never mailed. An organization with no Owner or Admin to mail answers `no_recipient` and its rows count as mailed.
- **Logs**, ids and counts only: `credits: the alerts are checked` (`organization_count`, `recorded_count`, `alert_count`), `credits: an alert is mailed` (`alert_id`, `organization_id`, `threshold`, `recipient_count`), `credits: an alert has no Owner to mail`, `credits: an alert's mail failed its attempts` (`row_count`).
- **Tests**: `tests/alerts.test.ts` (`pnpm -F credits test`): the share and the thresholds as a table, then the check on fixed instants and periods, two organizations, two checks at once, a new period, no grant, a grant of 0 and carry-over, and the alerts a check no longer answers, then a partial send and a later check that mails only the recipients not recorded, and two mails of one alert at once; `core/apps/worker/tests/credits-alerts.test.ts`: the schedule, then the check and the mails end to end on the app-mail fake, Owners, an Admin, Members, an Owner of two workspaces, a removed member, a disabled Owner, the jump to 95 and the 100, a failing mail then success, the cap on tries, a recipient whose send keeps failing, a mail that times out on every attempt, and two checks at once; its recorded history, `workflows/__histories__/credits_alerts.json`, replays in `pnpm -F worker test:replay`; db's `test:roles` proves the grants on `credit_alerts` and `credit_alert_mails`.

After a 100 percent alert: [docs/runbook.md](docs/runbook.md) "A credit alert".
