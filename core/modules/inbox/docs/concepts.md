# inbox: concepts

- **Thread**: one conversation of a sender account with a person on one provider chat (`provider_thread_ref`), which the platform started: the receiver keeps no other (RB-13.1.3). Its account's owner reads it.
- **Message**: one message of a thread, the person's (`in`) or the account's (`out`, beta-p5-020), its text in `body` as the provider gave it. The account's is the campaign's (`sent_by = campaign`): a step sent it, `body` is the text its owner approved, with `approved_by` and `approved_at`; or the user's (`sent_by = user`): the tester typed it on LinkedIn, on a platform thread.
- **Touch**: contacts' `last_touch_at`, which the gate's one touch a day reads (`touched_today`). A step's send records one; so does a message the tester typed, at its time (RB-9.2.2).
- **Reply**: an inbound message the store kept. Any reply stops the sequence for that person (F12 §11): every `planned`, `awaiting_approval` and `scheduled` step of theirs, in every enrollment, is held in the transaction that stores it.
- **Opt-out phrase**: one of `OPT_OUT_PHRASES` (`domain/opt-out/constants.ts`): "remove me", "unsubscribe", "opt out", "do not contact", "don't contact me", "not interested, remove", "stop messaging me", "stop contacting me", "stop sending me", anywhere in a reply; one of `OPT_OUT_REPLIES`, "please stop", as the whole reply; or `STOP_WORD`, "stop", as its first word. A reply holding one suppresses the person in the workspace: contacts' opt-out (`source = system`, `reason = reply_opt_out`), never removed, and `opt_out_matched` on the message.
- **The match**: `matchesOptOut(text)` (`domain/opt-out/match.ts`): the text folded (curly apostrophes to straight ones, lower case, every run of characters other than letters, digits and inner apostrophes to one space), then its first word compared with "stop", the whole of it with each whole-reply phrase, and each phrase, folded the same way, sought as whole words.
- **Reply mail**: the one email the sender account's owner gets for a reply (beta-p5-030): who replied, by first name, and the thread's link, never the text. `owner_notified_at` on the message marks it sent.
