# qualification: flows

- **Hard filters** (alpha-p6-010): a function call; no request, workflow or event; the country and the size, the title being the assessment's (title-check-ai). The signal run (alpha-p8-030) prepares them once per run (`prepareHardFilters`) and applies them to each candidate after the profile read, before anything is stored. The batch (alpha-p6-040) prepares them once per batch and applies them to each lead it selected before the credit reservation and the assessment; it writes the reason on a miss.
- **The assessment** (alpha-p6-020): `assessPerson` runs inside the batch's activity (alpha-p6-040), for a person who passed the hard filters. One tenant run reads the person (contacts' `getPerson`), its company (`getCompany`) and the ICP (`readIcpForScoring`), checks the ICP's version and builds the anonymized blocks; `callAgent` then asks the model outside any run, through the result cache, the gate and one retry, and writes the decision record and the cost event in runs of its own; a second run writes the snapshot and its six verdicts. A call the cache answers returns the stored snapshot. `erasePerson` is called by the deletion script (alpha-p10-030).
- **The score** (alpha-p6-030): `scoreSnapshot` runs inside the batch activity's tenant run, after `assessPerson` stored a snapshot, and opens none. It reads the snapshot, the ICP and its criteria (`readIcpForScoring`; a snapshot of another ICP version is `icp_version_stale`) and the verdicts; it computes the fit, the status, the tier and the why; it writes the snapshot's scores, the verdicts' weights and points, then the lead's decision through contacts' `getLead` and `applyQualification`. All of it commits with the run, or nothing does. A retried activity finds the snapshot scored and gets the stored result; a run late enough that the lead is already on a newer snapshot scores its own and leaves the lead. A criteria save starts nothing here (decisions.md); the re-score is alpha-p6-040's.
- **The re-score** (alpha-p6-040; [README](../README.md#the-re-score)): `POST …/qualification/rescore` or `pnpm rescore` counts the leads it marks and writes `qualification.rescore_requested` in its tenant run; the worker's outbox loop delivers it and its subscriber starts `qualification_rescore` with the id `qualification.rescore:<workspaceId>:<requestId>`, so a redelivery starts none. The workflow runs `qualification_rescore_page` per page of 50 persons: one run, the ICP read, then per lead a copy of its snapshot scored with the current criteria, or an earlier copy given back, and the lead's `qualification.lead_qualified`. No model call, no credits.
- **The batch** (alpha-p6-040; [README](../README.md#the-batch)): the schedule `qualification.batch_tick` runs the global `qualification_batch_tick` every `QUALIFICATION_SCHEDULE_MINUTES`; its activity lists the workspaces' ids through tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds` and, in each workspace's own run, reads the audiences and one lead to assess, the batch's own selection; it starts `qualification_batch` where an audience has targeting and a lead of it waits, unless one runs, and fails after the last workspace if one failed. `discovery.people_imported`, `icp.updated` and `icp.audience_updated` (gamma-p5-050), delivered in the worker's outbox loop, start the delivery's workspace's batch the same way. The batch: `qualification_filter_leads` (one run: the audiences, each lead's own (playbooks' agent of its first signal source, else the default; gamma-p5-050), the selection per audience, its own pending leads only by a filter on their first signal source (review-gaps-2), with the leads a retired rule decided ([README](../README.md#titles-and-disqualifiers)), each audience's hard filters, each miss written against its audience with its event, a `title_mismatch` lead that passes `pending` again; the persons who passed, grouped by audience), then per lead `qualification_reserve` (one run, credits' lock, the lead's attempt against its audience read), `qualification_assess` (the person's excerpts read from the signal reader in a run of their own, then `assessPerson` against the audience, no run), and `qualification_commit` (one run: `scoreSnapshot`, `credits.commit`, the event; past the lease, the release and a second reserve and commit) or `qualification_release` (one run: `credits.release`, the attempt moved, the lead `pending` or `ai_failed`, the event). It stops at `insufficient_credits`, a provider failure or an ICP that moved, and fails at any other failure; the next tick goes on. A cancelled batch waits for its step and releases an assessment it stopped.
- **The lead page's read** (alpha-p6-060): `GET …/qualification/leads/{personId}/snapshot`, in the request's run: contacts' `getLead`, then the snapshot it points at and its verdicts, then the person's `intent_scores` row (gamma-p5-065). Nothing is written. The intent panel (gamma-p5-065) is a server component of the page's `evidence` slot that reads this route as the member.
- **The lead page's Re-score** (alpha-p10-100, lead-rescore-ai; [README](../README.md#a-new-assessment)): the page renders the panel on the server, which reads the lead through contacts' route as the member. In the browser, Re-score posts `…/qualification/leads/{personId}/reassess`: in the request's run, under the person's lock, a decided lead goes back to `pending` with `member_requested`, its snapshot kept and its attempt moved to the next round, and `qualification.reassess_requested` is written; the worker's outbox loop delivers it and its subscriber starts the workspace's batch (`batchStart`). The batch filters, reserves, assesses and commits the lead as any pending one; `scoreSnapshot` first gives the new snapshot the members' verdicts of the one the lead waited with. Given a snapshot scored already, the answer cache's when nothing changed, `qualification_commit` decides the lead again from it and commits the reservation at quantity 0, no credits. The panel reads the lead every three seconds, sixty times at most, while it waits with `member_requested`, then has the page read its data again.
- **The golden set** (alpha-p9-050; [README](../README.md#the-golden-set)): `GET …/qualification/exports/golden-set.jsonl` or `pnpm export:golden`. One run reads the reviewed leads (contacts' `latestReviews`); a run per hundred reads their snapshots, verdicts and raw prompts (the ai package's `rawPrompts`) and, for an input to rebuild, the person, the company and the ICP; outside any run, the files port writes the file. No row is written, but the empty ICP that icp's first read creates in a workspace without one.
- **The intent** (gamma-p5-060; [README](../README.md#intent-and-priority)): with a signal reader, `scoreSnapshot` takes the person's advisory lock before it reads the ICP and the lead, asks the reader for the person's items of the last 90 days, computes the intent, the priority and the tier, and writes the lead's tier and why through `applyQualification`, then the person's `intent_scores` row, all in the caller's run. The re-score does so for every person of a page; a lead it skips gets `updateIntent`: the lock, contacts' `getLead`, the lead's current snapshot and verdicts and the ICP's criteria, then the same writes with the status and the fit as they are, and no event.
- **The nightly decay** (gamma-p5-060): the global schedule `qualification.intent_decay`, daily at 02:30 UTC, runs `qualification_intent_decay_tick`, whose time is its run's (or its input's `at`). Its activity `qualification_start_intent_decays` lists the workspaces' ids through tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds` and starts each one's `qualification_intent_decay` with that time, unless one runs (`qualification.intent_decay:<workspaceId>:current`); a failed start leaves the others started and fails the tick after the last. The decay runs `qualification_intent_decay_page` until a page is not full: one run reads up to 50 rows computed before that time whose intent is above 0 or that hold a boost, by person id, then each person gets `updateIntent` as of that time in a run of its own. Its row's `computed_at` becomes that time, which takes it out of the next page; the row of a lead without a decision is deleted. With no reader, a page reads nothing.
- **The Hot alert** (gamma-p5-065; [README](../README.md#the-hot-alert)): a decision or an update whose intent write takes the lead into Hot (Hot over no row or another tier) publishes `qualification.lead_hot` `{ workspaceId, personId }` in its run, so the event commits with the row; a write of another tier clears `hot_alerted_at`. The worker's outbox loop delivers the event; its subscriber, in the delivery's run, sets `hot_alerted_at` where it is null and the row is Hot, then starts `qualification_hot_alert` with the id `qualification.hot_alert:<workspaceId>:<eventId>`; with no claim it starts nothing. The workflow runs `qualification_mail_hot_lead` once (no retry): one run reads the row, still Hot, and its why-now part; outside any run, tenancy's `resolveMembership` and `findUser`; then the mail, rendered in the member's locale, through the app-mail port. A claim whose mail went nowhere stays: the lead page still shows the tier.
