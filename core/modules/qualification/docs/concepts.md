# qualification: concepts

- **Hard filters**: the first stage of qualification (RB-3.2.1): code compares a person's country and company size with the ICP before any model sees the person, so a miss costs no credit ([architecture §14.1](../../../../docs/architecture.md#141-signal-run-to-qualified-lead)). Industry is the assessment's in alpha, and the title too since title-check-ai: people combine and shorten titles, which the model reads better than a token rule ([README](../README.md#titles-and-disqualifiers)). The order is country, then size; the first miss is the answer, with its reason and the values compared.
- **The balanced rule**, alpha's only mode: a value the person or the company lacks passes to the assessment: no country, no company row, a size that reads as no count. An ICP list left empty filters nothing. Only a known value outside the ICP's list fails.
- **Titles** (RB-3.1.2, reduced, English only), a hard filter until title-check-ai and since then signals' read order only (`titleFits`): a title becomes tokens. Lower case; accents gone; punctuation (`&`, `/`, `-`, `.`, `,`, `|`) a space; the stop words (`a`, `an`, `and`, `at`, `for`, `in`, `of`, `on`, `the`, `to`) and the seniority noise (`senior`, `sr`, `snr`, `junior`, `jr`, `interim`, `acting`) dropped; then the synonyms read:
  - vice president, v.p., vp, svp and evp are `head`, and so are director and dir: one role since gamma-p5-050, so a VP of sales, a director of sales and a head of sales fit one another;
  - a chief officer is its letters: `ceo`, `cto`, `cfo`, `coo`, `cmo`, `cro`, `cio`, `cpo`;
  - co-founder and cofounder are `founder`;
  - a function's letters are its words: `sdr`, `bdr`, `ae`, `bd`, `hr`, `gm`, `revops`, `ops`, `mgr`, `rep`.

  A person's title matches an ICP title when it holds every one of that title's tokens, in any order and among others, so a title of several roles passes when one of them matches. A missing token is a miss: functions are never merged, nor levels other than vp, director and head (a sales development representative is no head of sales, nor a VP of marketing). [tests/fixtures/titles.json](../tests/fixtures/titles.json) shows the rule in fifty-four pairs. The read order is all it orders: seniority stays the assessment's criterion, so a VP judged against "Head of" can still miss on it. No title, or one of noise only, fits.
- **Company size**: contacts keeps the size as its source wrote it. The filter reads a count or a range of employees (`11-50`, `11 to 50`, `11-50 employees`, `10,001+`, `10k+`, `10000 or more`, `35`) and takes the ranges of icp's list it falls in: `5,001-10,000` is `5001+`, `1-50` is `1-10` and `11-50`. It passes when one of them is in the ICP's. Text that reads as no count (`Self-employed`) is unknown. [tests/fixtures/sizes.json](../tests/fixtures/sizes.json) shows the parser.
- **Country**: the person's `location_country`, the ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code contacts reads from the location, against the ICP's codes, case aside.
- **Prepared once**: `prepareHardFilters(icp)` reads the ICP's countries and sizes once and returns the filters for that ICP, a function of the person and the company. The batch (alpha-p6-040) and the signal run (alpha-p8-030) prepare once per batch or run, so the prepared value lives with the caller and the module keeps no state. `applyHardFilters(person, company, icp)` gives the same answer and prepares on every call.
- **The lead's audience** (gamma-p5-050, RB-5.1.1): a lead is judged against the audience of the agent that found it, its first signal source's agent, whatever the agent's status; a lead with no signal source, or whose source is in no agent, against the workspace's default audience. The snapshot, the attempt and the lead's decision name it (`icp_id`, `icp_id_scored`); its criteria score the snapshot, a re-score's copy too. A lead whose source later moves to another agent keeps its decision until its audience's targeting changes or it is assessed again; a lead found by two agents is judged by its first source's.
- **The assessment** (alpha-p6-020, RB-5.1.1 reduced): the agent `qualification.assess-fit` reads one anonymized person who passed the hard filters, with the lead's audience, and answers a verdict for each of the six criteria (`title_function`, `seniority`, `company_size`, `industry`, `location`, `must_haves`): `met`, `partially_met`, `not_met` or `unknown`, each with its evidence, a few words quoted from the person's data; the disqualifiers it sees, from F04 §4.2's closed list of eleven (any one of them means not qualified, alpha-p6-030); and one neutral sentence. The prompt and the schema are the [agent's README](../agents/assess-fit/README.md).
- **Grounding**: after the call, a verdict whose evidence is empty or shares no word with the person's data (the profile, the company, the excerpts; never the ICP's texts) is stored `unknown` with no evidence: a verdict the data does not support counts for nothing. Words are compared in lower case, without accents, placeholders and common words.
- **Fail safe**: an answer off the schema or the gate (each criterion once) is retried once, then the call is `ai_failed` and nothing is stored but the decision record; alpha-p6-040 decides what the lead becomes then.
- **The snapshot**: the verdicts are stored as a snapshot of the person at the ICP's version, with the model, the prompt version and the decision record of the call; the score, the confidence and the tier fill it later (alpha-p6-030). An ICP that moved since the caller read its version is refused (`icp_version_stale`) before the call: the batch reads the version again and assesses with the new one.
- **The score** (alpha-p6-030, RB-5.1.3; scorer `fit-v1`): code computes the fit from the stored verdicts, never the model. Each verdict earns points, `met` 1, `partially_met` 0.5, `unknown` 0.4 (balanced, alpha's only mode), `not_met` 0, and fit = 100 × Σ(weight × points) / Σ weight, over the criteria the ICP has enabled and the assessment answered, rounded half up. The weights are the ICP's criteria at scoring time (defaults: title_function 30, seniority 15, industry 15, company_size 10, location 10, must_haves 20). A must criterion (the ICP's `isMust`; title_function and location by default) at `not_met` caps the fit at 40. A disabled criterion counts for nothing: no weight, no cap, no review, no unknown, no bullet. No criterion counting is a fit of 0.
- **Confidence**: from the unknowns among the criteria that count: `high` with at most one, `medium` with two, `low` with three or more.
- **The gate and the status** (RB-5.1.4, reduced): any disqualifier makes the lead `not_qualified`, its code the reason, whatever the fit (of several, the first in the closed list's order). Otherwise below 60 is `not_qualified`; at 60 or above the lead is `qualified`, or `needs_review` when a must is `unknown` or the confidence is `low`, for a human to decide.
- **The tier**: from the fit alone: `hot` from 75, `warm` from 60 to 74, `cool` below 60. The thresholds are a guess for alpha (intent is out), a value to revisit with the golden set. The snapshot keeps this tier; with a signal reader, the lead's tier is its priority's (gamma-p5-060, "Tiers" below).
- **The why** (RB-5.2.1, reduced; F04 §8): up to three bullets, built in code from the verdicts, the criteria and the disqualifiers, never from the model's summary: first what stops the lead (a hard filter's miss, a disqualifier, or the heaviest must at `not_met` with its quote); then the fit (the heaviest criterion met, its quote, and how many of those that count are met); then the data gap, only when a criterion that counts is `unknown`. A bullet is a message key with its params, codes, numbers and quotes only, so a second language needs no re-scoring.
- **Attempts** (alpha-p10-100, lead-rescore-ai): a lead is assessed at most three times per round of attempts at an ICP version when the model's provider fails; each attempt reserves its credits under a key of its own, and a released key is never reserved again. Before a round's third, the lead waits `pending` for a later batch; at the third it is `ai_failed`. A member's press starts the next round, three attempts of its own. A failure that is not the provider's is the batch's, not the lead's: the batch fails and the lead keeps its reservation.
- **Marked** (alpha-p10-100): the leads a re-score moves now, those whose current snapshot is scored at the ICP's version by criteria other than the saved ones. The route answers their count; a lead with no such snapshot waits for the batch, which assesses it.
- **What comes next** (alpha-p10-100, lead-rescore-ai): the lead page's panel says it for a pending lead: the next batch assesses it, after a top-up when credits ran out, in a few minutes when a member asked for it. Every decided lead has Re-score, which asks the AI again; `ai_failed` says why the AI failed, `needs_review` to check the verdicts, change the criteria if they are off, then re-score.
- **A scorer version**: `fit-v1` is fixed. [tests/fixtures/golden/fit-v1.json](../tests/fixtures/golden/fit-v1.json) holds ten verdict sets with their criteria and what `fit-v1` gives them, and the test fails if a result changes. Changing the formula, a weight rule, the cap, the gate, the tiers or the confidence means a new scorer version (`fit-v2`) with a golden file of its own, never an edit of this one.
- **Intent** (gamma-p5-060, RB-5.3.1; scorer `intent-v1`): how strongly the person's recent signals point to a need now, 0 to 100, from the signal reader's items of the last 90 days. Each item's term is its base weight × its relevance (clamped to 0.3 to 1; 0.65 while unrated) × its intent class's multiplier (1 while unrated) × 0.5^(age in days ÷ 14). S is the sum of the terms × the stacking bonus, +15% for each distinct kind after the first among the items of the last 30 days, +60% at most; intent = round(100 × (1 − e^(−S ÷ 8))). One competitor comment of relevance 1 gives 53 on its day and 29 sixteen days later.
- **Base weights**, by kind and role (F03 §6): `topic_engagement` author 7, comment 5, reaction 3; `competitor_engagement` comment 6, reaction 4; `creator_engagement` comment 4, reaction 2; `my_post_engagement` comment 6, reaction 4; `post_engagement` comment 5, reaction 3 (a guess). A repost counts as a reaction, and every reaction weighs the same whatever its type (Unipile reports each as a like); a role the kind does not list counts for nothing.
- **Intent classes** (the AI's rating, gamma-p5-030): `seeking_recommendation` 1.5, `pain_or_complaint` 1.4, `evaluating_or_comparing` 1.3, `praising_competitor` and `other` 1, `generic_praise` 0.8, `self_promotion` and `job_seeking` 0.6.
- **Priority** (RB-5.3.2): round(0.55 × fit + 0.45 × intent), plus 10 while boosted, 100 at most. The boost lasts 48 hours from an item that is first-party (`my_post_engagement`) or weighs 6 or more. With no signal a lead's priority is at most 55: an imported lead is Warm at best.
- **Tiers**: from the priority, `hot` from 75, `warm` from 55 to 74, `cool` below 55. A `qualified` or `needs_review` lead has one; a `not_qualified`, `hard_filtered` or pending lead has none.
- **Why now**: the item with the largest term becomes the bullet `qualification.why.why_now` `{ kind, role, days }` ("Commented on a competitor’s post · 2 days ago"), right after the fit's bullet; the why keeps three bullets at most.
- **The decay**: the terms fall as the items age, so every night recomputes each lead whose intent is above 0 or whose boost holds ([flows.md](flows.md)); a tier may move, the status never.
- **intent-v1** is fixed as `fit-v1` is: [tests/fixtures/golden/intent-v1.json](../tests/fixtures/golden/intent-v1.json) holds twenty item sets with what it gives them. A changed weight, multiplier or bound is `intent-v2`, with a golden file of its own.
