# qualification

Decides which persons become qualified leads. Three stages are here: the hard filters, pure code that stops a person whose country or company size cannot match the ICP before any model sees them (alpha-p6-010; the title is the assessment's since title-check-ai, [Titles and disqualifiers](#titles-and-disqualifiers)); the assessment, where the agent `qualification.assess-fit` judges an anonymized person against the ICP's six criteria and the verdicts are stored as a snapshot (alpha-p6-020); and the score, where code computes the fit, the status, the tier and the why from those verdicts and gives the lead its decision (alpha-p6-030). The batch runs the three per lead, with the credits, in the worker, and the re-score scores the scored leads again after a criteria save (alpha-p6-040). On the lead page, the module's panel re-scores a lead and says what comes next for one not scored (alpha-p10-100). The golden set exports the reviewed leads without names, for the evals (alpha-p9-050). A lead's intent and priority come from its signals ([Intent and priority](#intent-and-priority), gamma-p5-060); the lead page shows them, and a lead that turns Hot mails the member whose source found it ([The Hot alert](#the-hot-alert), gamma-p5-065).

- **Owns:** `score_snapshots`, `criterion_verdicts`, `assessment_attempts` and `intent_scores` in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/qualification.prisma` ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)); the rules in `domain/hard-filters/`; the anonymizer in `domain/anonymize/`; the agent in `agents/assess-fit/` ([its README](agents/assess-fit/README.md)); the scorer in `domain/score/` and the why in `domain/why/`, whose message keys it owns (`ui/labels.ts`); the intent in `domain/intent/` and the signal reader's registry in `domain/signal-reader.ts` ([Intent and priority](#intent-and-priority)); the Hot alert in `domain/alert/` ([The Hot alert](#the-hot-alert)); the batch, its tick and the nightly decay in `workflows/`; the re-score in `domain/rescore.ts`, its route in `api/` and its command in `cli/`; the snapshot route (alpha-p6-060); the golden set in `domain/export/`, its route and its command, and the why bullets' source of the grounding sample there; the Re-score panel in `ui/rescore-action/` and the intent panel in `ui/intent-panel/` ([Screen](#screen)).
- **Public interface:** `index.ts`, below.
- **Depends on:** `ai` (callAgent, `rawPrompts`, `defineGroundingSource`), `contacts` (`getPerson`, `getCompany`, `getLead`, `listLeads`, `applyQualification`, `leadsPendingQualification`, `latestReviews`), `icp` (`readIcpForScoring`, `listAudiences`, `findAudience`, `icp.updated`, `icp.audience_updated`), `playbooks` (`playbookOfSource`, `getPlaybook`: the lead's audience, gamma-p5-050), `discovery` (`discovery.people_imported`), `tenancy` (`listWorkspaceIds`, `findWorkspaceBySlug`, and `resolveMembership` and `findUser` for the Hot alert), `db`, `platform` (the credits client, `startWorkflow`, `renderMail`), `providers` (the files port, the app-mail port); `@nestjs/common` in `api/` only, whose snapshot route takes contacts' `leads.read` and `lead_not_found`, and the golden set's `exports.read`; `@whytide/ui`, the web's API client, `next`, `next-intl` and `react` in `ui/`, which the web host compiles.
- **Reviewers:** the owners of this module; two tables hold personal data, and the anonymizer decides what a model sees ([docs/security.md](docs/security.md)).

## The batch

`workflows/` (alpha-p6-040; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md)). The global schedule `qualification.batch_tick`, every `QUALIFICATION_SCHEDULE_MINUTES`, overlap skip, runs `qualification_batch_tick`, whose activity lists the workspaces through tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds` and starts the `qualification_batch` of each with an audience that has targeting (one never saved has none) and a lead of it to assess, checked in the workspace's own run with the batch's own selection; a workspace that fails leaves the others started, and the tick then fails with its code. The subscribers of `discovery.people_imported`, `icp.updated` (the default audience) and `icp.audience_updated` (any audience, the default's too: saving the default publishes both, which the one run at a time below makes one batch) start it at once, for the workspace of the delivery. Every start is `batchStart`: one run of `qualification.batch:<workspaceId>:current` at a time (`startWorkflow`'s `oneAtATime`), so a start while one runs starts none and the next tick picks up what it would have read.

Each lead is judged against its own audience (gamma-p5-050; `domain/audience.ts`): the audience of the agent that found it, its first `signal` source's (contacts' `firstSignalSource`; the agent holding that source among playbooks' `listPlaybooks` and `sourcesOf`, and its `icpId`, whatever the agent's status), else the workspace's default, for a lead with no signal source, whose source is in no agent, or whose agent's audience the workspace lacks. A batch reads the audiences and the agents' sources once and up to `QUALIFICATION_BATCH_SIZE` leads, oldest first: per audience with targeting, contacts' `leadsPendingQualification` gives the `pending` leads of that audience, by the same rule as a filter on their first signal source (`pendingSources`), and the leads decided against it at an older version (for the default, also those decided before audiences and those decided by a rule title-check-ai retired, [Titles and disqualifiers](#titles-and-disqualifiers)) whose audience has targeting (`decidedSources`), `QUALIFICATION_BATCH_SIZE` at most each; each lead goes to its own audience. A lead whose audience has no targeting is never read: it stays as it is, however many wait, holds back no other audience's, and is selected once its audience is saved (review-gaps-2). The tick's check is the selection of one lead: one lead at most per audience with targeting. Each step is an activity of its own, and no tenant run is held across the model call:

1. `qualification_filter_leads`, one run: the selection above, each audience's filters, the country and the size, prepared once; a lead `hard_filtered` for `title_mismatch` that passes is `pending` again; a miss is `hard_filtered` against its audience, with the reason, the why bullet `qualification.why.hard_filtered` (the compared values), the audience and its version, no credits. It answers the persons who passed, grouped by audience.
2. `qualification_reserve`: `credits.reserve` of `qualification.assessment`, key `qual:{personId}:{icpId}:{icpVersion}:{model}:{promptVersion}`, with `:{attempt}` from the lead's second attempt against that audience at that version (`assessment_attempts`), lease `async`. `insufficient_credits` leaves a `pending` lead with that `status_reason`, and a lead decided at an older ICP version as it was, and stops the batch; the next batch tries it again.
3. `qualification_assess` (kind `ai`): `assessPerson({ personId, icpId, icpVersion, evidence })`, outside any run, against the lead's audience, `icp_version_stale` when it moved. The evidence is up to five (`EXCERPTS`) of the person's own words from the signal reader's `excerpts`, read in a run of its own inside the activity and handed to `assessPerson` only: never in the activity's input or answer, so never in the workflow's history; anonymized as every input is, each cut, names, links and addresses replaced; none without a reader, as for an imported person. It answers ids and codes only.
4. `qualification_commit`, one run: `scoreSnapshot` with the audience's criteria, the snapshot and the lead (`icp_id_scored`) naming the audience, then `credits.commit`. A reservation past its 24-hour lease is released and the lead's attempt moved; the batch reserves again under the next attempt's key and commits. On `ai_failed`, `qualification_release` releases and writes the lead `ai_failed` with the reason (`invalid`, `gates`, `empty`, `length`, `content_filter`).

Every release moves the lead's attempt, so a released key is never reserved again. A provider failure (an `llm_*` code) past the activity's attempts releases and stops the batch: the lead waits `pending` with `provider_failed` for a later batch, and its third attempt at the version, or of a member's press ([A new assessment](#a-new-assessment)), is `ai_failed` with `provider_failed`. Any other failure of the assessment (`assessment_agent_not_loaded`, a lasting database error) fails the batch and keeps the reservation, which the next batch's reserve finds by its key. An ICP that moved during the batch releases, leaves the lead and stops it. A failed commit fails the batch and keeps the reservation too. A cancelled batch waits for its running step's outcome and releases an assessment it stopped. Every decided lead publishes `qualification.lead_qualified` in the run that wrote it.

The key changed shape with the audiences (gamma-p5-050): a batch in flight at the deploy replays on its old path behind `patched("audience")`, whose steps read the default audience and keep the old key, and whose first step, run anew, answers no lead: the next batch reads them. A reservation an old batch kept under the old key ends with its 24-hour lease, charging nothing.

A new lead waits for enrichment before its first judgement (gamma-en-040): contacts' selection leaves out a `pending` lead held past now, so neither the batch nor the tick's `hasWork` sees it until its hold ends (`CONTACTS_ENRICHMENT_HOLD_MINUTES`, 60 by default, 0 for none; contacts' README "Enrichment's hold") or enrichment releases it; a decided lead, a member's new assessment and the re-score never wait.

## The re-score

A criteria save (weights, must, enabled) moves no ICP version, so the batch selects nothing (decisions.md, 2026-10-22). `POST …/qualification/rescore` (`@Idempotent()`, `qualification.rescore`; body `{}` or `{ personIds }`, 1 to 500) and `pnpm rescore --workspace <id or slug> [--person <id>]` write `qualification.rescore_requested`; its subscriber starts `qualification_rescore`, one run per request. The route answers 202 `{ requestId, marked }`: `marked` counts the leads the run would move now, by the rule below, read in the request's run. The run takes the named persons, or every person with an assessment at its audience's current version, by id, in pages of 50, each page one activity and one run (`rescorePage`). A lead whose current snapshot is scored at the current version by other criteria (`scored_criteria_hash`, [docs/data.md](docs/data.md)) is scored again from its verdicts with the current criteria of the snapshot's own audience (gamma-p5-050): a copy of the snapshot, of the same audience, keyed with the criteria's hash (`criteria_hash`), scored by `scoreSnapshot`, takes the lead; the snapshot these criteria scored before, a copy or the assessment's own, takes it back. Each publishes `qualification.lead_qualified`. No model call and no credits. Skipped: no lead, no scored snapshot at the current version (the batch assesses it), or criteria that scored it already. The snapshot that takes the lead carries the members' verdicts ([A member's verdict](#a-members-verdict)). A lead that waits as `pending` is skipped, whatever snapshot it keeps. The lead page no longer calls the re-score: its Re-score asks the AI again ([A new assessment](#a-new-assessment)).

With a signal reader, every person of a page also gets the intent of now ([Intent and priority](#intent-and-priority)): a lead re-scored with its decision, a skipped one through `updateIntent`, which gives the lead its tier and why again with no event. So a re-score of named persons, which signals asks for a known person's new event, makes a new or newly rated signal count at once (RB-5.3.1).

## The golden set

The reviewed leads as the evals read them, without names (alpha-p9-050; RB-25.4.1). `GET …/qualification/exports/golden-set.jsonl[?week]`, under contacts' `exports.read`, and `pnpm export:golden --workspace <id or slug> [--week <ISO week>]` build it alike: a line per lead with a decision, or with `week` per lead whose latest decision falls in that ISO week (contacts' `latestReviews`), by person id. The file goes through the files port to `exports/golden-set-<date>.jsonl` (UTC; kept 7 days at most; a second export that day replaces it; 25 MiB at most, `files_too_large`). The route answers it (`application/jsonl`); the command prints `golden_set_written: w/<workspaceId>/exports/golden-set-<date>.jsonl, <n> lines`.

A line ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)) holds the snapshot the lead had at its decision: the assessment's input, the verdicts, the disqualifiers, the status, the fit and the tier; then the decision, its reason code and the tester's texts; the person is the sha256 of its id. The input is read back from the decision record's `raw_prompt` until its `raw_purge_after` (`input_from: "decision_record"`); after that time, purged or not, or for a lead decided before any assessment, the anonymizer rebuilds it from the person, the company and the ICP as they are now, without evidence (`"rebuilt"`).

## Screen

A panel in the `actions` slot of contacts' lead page (`ui/nav.ts` `panels`; `ui/rescore-action/`): `panel.tsx` reads the lead on the server, as the member, through contacts' lead route; `rescore-action.tsx` shows it in the browser. Every decided lead (`qualified`, `not_qualified`, `needs_review`, `hard_filtered`, `ai_failed`) has Re-score (`qualification.action.rescore`) and one line under it, `qualification.action.rescore_hint`: it asks the AI again with the lead's current details, for one assessment's credits ([A new assessment](#a-new-assessment)). `qualification.explain.needs_review` follows when the lead needs review, `qualification.explain.ai_failed` (`provider_failed`, or the model's answer) when the AI failed. A pending lead shows a short note instead, `qualification.explain.pending`, by its `status_reason`: none, `insufficient_credits`, `provider_failed`, `member_requested`. Re-score posts `…/qualification/leads/{personId}/reassess` with an idempotency key per press; while the request runs and while the lead waits with `member_requested` (the lead read every three seconds, sixty times at most: `ui/rescore-action/constants.ts`), the button is busy and the kit's loading state shows. Then `qualification.action.rescored` with the new status, or, for a lead that waits for something else now (credits, the provider), its note, and the page reads its data again; `qualification.action.waiting` after the last read. A failed read or request is the kit's error state, the code in words, whose retry reads the page again or asks again. Its specs are the web host's `tests/qualification-states.spec.ts` and `tests/rescore-action.spec.ts`.

A panel in the `evidence` slot (gamma-p5-065; `ui/intent-panel/`): `panel.tsx` reads the snapshot route on the server, as the member, through the web's API client, and `intent-view.tsx` shows the lead's intent, in a section titled `qualification.intent.title`: the priority, the tier and the intent (`qualification.intent.priority`, `.tier`, `.intent`); the tier rule applied, `qualification.intent.tier_rule`, from intent-v1's numbers (F04 §8); why now, the strongest signal named by its kind and role, with its date (`qualification.intent.why_now`, `.signal`); each signal's share of the intent, its contribution over the sum, newest first (`qualification.intent.signals`, `.contribution`). A lead with no signal that counts says `qualification.intent.no_signal` instead of the last two. Above it, `audience-note.tsx` says which audience the lead was judged against (`qualification.snapshot.audience`: its name, else the default or an unnamed audience; gamma-p5-050). A lead without a snapshot shows no panel, one without an intent row the audience alone; a failed read is the slot's error state. No signal's words show: the row holds none. The spec is the web host's `tests/lead-page.spec.ts`, axe included.

## Routes

- `POST /v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/qualification/rescore`: [The re-score](#the-re-score); the request is `api-calls/alpha-p10-100-qualification.http`.
- `GET /v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/qualification/exports/golden-set.jsonl`: [The golden set](#the-golden-set); contacts' `exports.read`; 400 `contacts_input_invalid` for a week the year does not have. The request is in `api-calls/alpha-p9-050-exports.http`.
- `GET /v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/qualification/leads/{personId}/snapshot`: a member's session and contacts' `leads.read`, since a snapshot is a lead's; `getSnapshot`'s answer, with `audience`, `{ id, name, isDefault }`, the audience the lead was judged against, by icp's `findAudience` (gamma-p5-050), null when icp no longer holds it, and `intent`: `{ intentScore, priority, tier, whyNow: { kind, role, at }, boostUntil, breakdown: [{ eventId, kind, role, contribution, at }], scorerVersion, computedAt }`, or null without an intent row (gamma-p5-065). The snapshot's `tier` stays the fit's; the intent's is the lead's. `whyNow` is the strongest signal, the breakdown newest first; `at` is null for a part written before gamma-p5-065, which comes last. The source's creator stays out. 404 `score_snapshot_not_found` for a lead without a snapshot or one that waits as `pending`, `lead_not_found` for a person without a lead, also another workspace's. The schemas are `schemas/snapshot.ts`, the request `api-calls/alpha-p6-060-lead-page.http`.
- `PUT /v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/qualification/leads/{personId}/verdicts/{criterion}`, body `{ verdict, note? }`, and `DELETE` on the same path: [A member's verdict](#a-members-verdict); a member's session and contacts' `reviews.write`; each answers as the snapshot read. 400 `validation_failed` for a criterion or a verdict off the lists or a note past 500 characters; 404 `score_snapshot_not_found` and `lead_not_found` as the read. The schemas are `schemas/member-verdict.ts`, the requests `api-calls/lead-verdict-edit.http`.
- `POST /v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/qualification/leads/{personId}/reassess`, no body: [A new assessment](#a-new-assessment); `@Idempotent()`, `qualification.rescore` (Owner and Admin). 202 `{ status: "pending", requested }`, `requested` false for a lead that waits already, which stays as it is; 404 `lead_not_found` for a person without a lead, also another workspace's. The schemas are `schemas/reassess.ts`, the request `api-calls/lead-rescore-ai.http`.

## Settings

In the `modules/qualification` block of `deploy/.env.example`: `AI_MODEL_QUALIFICATION_ASSESS_FIT`, the assessment agent's model as the LLM port names it, empty for the port's first; `QUALIFICATION_BATCH_SIZE` (50, 1 to 500) and `QUALIFICATION_SCHEDULE_MINUTES` (2, 1 to 60), read at the worker's start.

## Decided here

- An ICP title matches when the person's title holds every one of its tokens (the read order's rule since title-check-ai); a role collapses to one token (vice president is `vp`, so a vp is no president), a function's abbreviation expands to its words (`sdr`). The task's `head of` = `director of` is read as director = head wherever it stands; since gamma-p5-050 vp, svp and evp are `head` too, so "VP of Sales" fits "Head of Sales" and the reverse, in signals' read order only.
- Unknown, so passing: no title or one of noise only, no country, no company row (the task's Open question), a size that reads as no count. An ICP list left empty filters nothing, and an ICP title of noise only is skipped.
- The order is country, then size; the first miss is the result. The title came first until title-check-ai.
- A size spanning several of icp's ranges (`1-50`) passes when one of them is in the ICP's.
- `domain/hard-filters/size.ts` repeats icp's closed list of ranges with their bounds; the type-level test fails if icp's list gains a range this one lacks.
- The ICP's criteria (enabled, must) do not switch a filter off: its lists alone decide.
- After review: the ICP's titles are read once per ICP (`icpTitleTokens`, which `prepareHardFilters` called until title-check-ai), since reading them on every call missed the 50 ms at fifty titles. No memo either: it would be state shared by every workspace of the process.
- The assessment (alpha-p6-020) takes the batch activity's context rather than opening runs of its own: callAgent opens its own runs and must run outside any, and a run inside the activity's would be `nested_run`.
- A snapshot per decision record, audience and ICP version (a unique key): a retried activity, which the record's cache answers without a model call, gets the stored snapshot back; two audiences alike, whose input is the same, share the record and get a snapshot each (gamma-p5-050).
- The table names the task gives; the snapshot also keeps `disqualifiers`, which the score (alpha-p6-030) and the lead page (alpha-p6-060) read, and its status, confidence and tier are enums now, so alpha-p6-030 needs no migration.
- The success result carries `status: "assessed"`, so that a caller tells it from `ai_failed` by one field.
- The score (alpha-p6-030) reads the ICP's criteria from `readIcpForScoring`, never constants: the weights and the musts are the ICP's rows. So `decide` and `buildWhy` take the criteria too, and `buildWhy` one object, with the hard filters' reason beside their detail.
- A disabled criterion counts for nothing: out of the normalization, no cap and no review as a must, no unknown in the confidence, no bullet; its verdict row keeps weight 0.
- The fit is rounded half up to the column's integer; the cap, the gate and the tier read the rounded fit. When no criterion counts, the fit is 0.
- `needs_review` holds a lead at or above the gate only; below it the lead is `not_qualified`, a must unknown or not. The tier comes from the fit alone, a disqualified lead's too, as the task says.
- Of several disqualifiers, the reason and the bullet name the first in the closed list's order, whatever the model's.
- The why: what stops the lead first (the hard filters' miss, a disqualifier or a must at `not_met`), then the fit (the heaviest criterion met, its quote, how many met), then the gap (the heaviest unknown, how many more). Params are codes, numbers and quotes, a quote cut to 300 code points ending with "…"; a message words a code through an ICU `select`.
- A lead the hard filters stopped gets `qualification.why.hard_filtered`, not the must-missed key: a filter is no criterion, and the ICP's flags never switch one off.
- `scoreSnapshot` runs in the caller's run, so the batch can commit its credits in the same transaction. A scored snapshot is final: a retry gets the stored result. A lead never moves back to a snapshot created before its current one (ties by id). A snapshot of another ICP version is `icp_version_stale`. A criteria save re-scores nothing (decisions.md): the re-score from stored verdicts is alpha-p6-040's.
- A verdict row's `weight` is its criterion's weight in this fit and `points` the verdict's (1, 0.5, 0.4, 0), so the rows give the fit before the cap.
- `ui/labels.ts` writes each key out in a `t()` call, so that the message check reads it; contacts' screens read a why bullet's key as data.
- From the review of alpha-p6-020: an activity whose snapshot insert loses to a concurrent one on the same decision record (P2002, which aborts its run) reads the stored snapshot in a run of its own.
- The batch (alpha-p6-040): one global tick that starts a batch per workspace (decisions.md), no `rescore_pending` status (the batch reads the leads decided at an older ICP version), and `qualification.lead_qualified` for the task's `lead.qualified`.
- The hard filters, once per batch, run in one activity with the selection; the reservation, the assessment and the commit or release are three, since credits' lock lasts the reserving run.
- A lead `hard_filtered` or `ai_failed` keeps the ICP version, so a targeting change reads it again. A provider failure stops the batch, as the provider is likely down for every lead.
- The event carries the lead's status and tier as stored, read after the write; none for a lead left `pending`. A retried activity may publish twice (at least once).
- A `not_qualified` lead gets no tier (decisions.md); the snapshot keeps the computed one.
- The tick lists the workspaces through tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds`: no module reads another's tables, and every global fan-out goes through it (decisions.md, 2026-10-22).
- The re-score (decisions.md, 2026-10-22): `criteria_hash` joins the snapshot's unique key, and the assessment's own snapshot keeps it empty. Each lead re-scored publishes `qualification.lead_qualified`, as the batch does: its decision may have changed.
- alpha-p10-100: every snapshot stores the hash of the criteria that scored it, `scored_criteria_hash`, outside the unique key, so a re-score with unchanged criteria writes no copy, and the criteria that scored the assessment give its own snapshot back. The migration fills it for the copies; an assessment's own snapshot scored before it stays empty, so its first re-score still writes one copy.
- alpha-p10-100: the lead's attempt (`assessment_attempts`, one row per person and ICP version, none for the first) moves at every release: a provider failure, an `ai_failed` answer, an ICP that moved, a cancelled step or a lease run out. So a released key is never reserved again, and the first attempt's key keeps its old form, which a reservation held across the deploy is found by. A failure that is not the provider's keeps the reservation, so a lasting one holds a lead's credits until the lease frees them, then the next batch reserves again.
- alpha-p10-100, from the coordinator: the batch's and the re-score's activities wait for their cancellation to complete (`ActivityCancellationType.WAIT_CANCELLATION_COMPLETED`, as signals' run), so a cancelled batch's step finishes and the batch settles or releases what it reserved.
- alpha-p10-100: an ICP "never saved" is one with no targeting (no list, must-haves or disqualifiers): icp keeps no saved flag, and such an ICP filters nothing and tells the model nothing. `startBatches` tries every workspace, then fails with the first failure, so the tick shows it.
- alpha-p10-100: `marked` applies the run's rule in the request's run, each named person through contacts' `getLead`, or every lead through `listLeads` by pages of 100; the run, later, may differ if the criteria or the leads change between. The panel reads the lead through contacts' route over HTTP, as the page does, and polls the snapshot route; its keys are snake_case as the module's others (`explain.needs_review`, not the task's `needsReview`), and its specs are the web host's, as the other screens'.
- The snapshot route (alpha-p6-060) is a read of the lead's `current_score_snapshot_id` through contacts' `getLead`, so a person without a lead, another workspace's included, is contacts' `lead_not_found`, and a lead never scored `score_snapshot_not_found`. It answers no `modelId`, `promptVersion` or decision record: the page shows none, and the record is read in the database. `scoredAt` is the snapshot's `created_at`, written just before it is scored, since the table keeps no other time. After a re-score the lead points at the copy that took it, scored with the current criteria, and the page shows that copy, its `scoredAt` the copy's `created_at`.
- alpha-p9-050: the golden set is qualification's, which imports contacts (contacts never imports qualification): contacts' `latestReviews` gives each lead's latest decision and its snapshot at that time, and the ai package, which owns the decision records, reads their raw prompts (`rawPrompts`).
- A line's `input` is the assessment's input object, the eval cases' `input` (core/packages/ai/evals/), read back from the raw prompt's blocks rather than kept as text, so a kept and a rebuilt input have one shape. A rebuilt one is `assessmentInput`, the function `assessPerson` sends.
- Keys are snake_case, as the input's. The line holds no id: `person_sha256` ties one person's lines across exports; the workspace can recompute it, nobody outside can.
- The route and the command open their own runs (the reviews in one, the lines a hundred leads a run) and call the files port outside any; the file is built in memory, as the port holds a body so.
- gamma-p5-060: intent-v1's numbers are constants in `domain/intent/constants.ts`, no setting. Those not in the sources are guesses to calibrate: `post_engagement`'s weights (5, 3), the multipliers outside F03 §6, the unrated value, the 90 days, the boost's threshold and 02:30 UTC. A change is a new scorer version with its own golden file.
- gamma-p5-060: an unrated item (relevance or class null) counts at relevance 0.65 and a multiplier of 1, so a signal counts before gamma-p5-030 rates it; a relevance that is no finite number counts as unrated, and an item dated after the computation's time counts as of that time.
- gamma-p5-060: the boost needs an item younger than 48 hours that is first-party (`my_post_engagement`, any engagement with the seller's post) or weighs 6 or more (a post on a tracked topic, a comment on a competitor's post); `boost_until` is 48 hours after the newest such item.
- gamma-p5-060: the lead's tier is the priority's for a `qualified` or `needs_review` lead, none for a `not_qualified` one, whose row still holds its intent and priority with no tier. The snapshot and `scoreSnapshot`'s result keep the fit's tier (fit-v1 unchanged); with no reader, the lead keeps it too.

- gamma-p5-060: the why-now bullet goes right after the fit's, or before the gap's when the why has no fit bullet; with three bullets already, the gap's goes. `days` counts whole days since the item; `role` is the counted one, so a repost reads as a reaction.
- gamma-p5-060: the re-score computes the intent of every person of its pages, named or not, through `updateIntent` for a lead it skips.
- gamma-p5-060: the person's lock is a transaction-level advisory lock on `qualification.person:<workspaceId>:<personId>`, taken by `scoreSnapshot` before it reads the ICP and the lead, by `reapplySnapshot`, by `updateIntent` and by the batch's `hard_filtered` and `ai_failed` writes. The eraser takes none: the deletion script holds the lead's row lock first, and an intent update takes the advisory lock before it writes the lead, so the other order would deadlock.

The time is the tick's, from the workflow's clock as its history holds it, or the tick's optional `at`, which the tests give.

The claim is in the delivery's run, before the start, and the workflow's id is the event's: a redelivery after a lost commit claims again and starts no second run.

Refused before reserving (`unchanged`) was the other way: it would build and hash the assessment's input in the request. ## Intent and priority

gamma-p5-060 (RB-5.3.1, RB-5.3.2; F04 §5, §7, §8; [docs/concepts.md](docs/concepts.md)). A decided lead gets an intent from its signals, a priority from its fit and intent, a tier from the priority, and a why-now bullet, under the scorer version `intent-v1`: pure code in `domain/intent/`, its numbers in `domain/intent/constants.ts`, its golden file `tests/fixtures/golden/intent-v1.json`. The signals come from the reader signals registers (`defineSignalReader`, gamma-p5-030); qualification imports nothing of signals. With no reader, nothing here runs and every lead keeps its fit tier.

- **When**: `scoreSnapshot`, for every decision (the batch's and the re-score's), before the lead is written; the re-score, for every person of its pages ([The re-score](#the-re-score)); and every night, the global schedule `qualification.intent_decay`, at `INTENT_DECAY_AT_UTC` (02:30 UTC), runs `qualification_intent_decay_tick`, whose activity lists the workspaces through tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds` and starts each one's `qualification_intent_decay` (`qualification.intent_decay:<workspaceId>:current`, one run at a time) with the tick's time. A workspace that fails leaves the others started, and the tick then fails with its code. The decay recomputes the rows with an intent above 0 or a boost, 50 a page, each page an activity and each person a run of its own ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md)).
- **What is written**: the lead's tier and why through contacts' `applyQualification`, the decision's other fields as they are, then the person's `intent_scores` row ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)). An update (`updateIntent`: the re-score's skipped leads and the decay) changes no status and publishes no `qualification.lead_qualified`: a score change never takes a lead out of a sequence (RB-5.3.2).
- **The lock**: a decision and an update take the person's advisory lock in their run before they read the lead, so the second waits for the first and computes from what it wrote: the lead keeps the decision's fields and the newest intent.
- **The data**: the reader's items are ids, codes, numbers and dates, and the row holds ids and numbers; no words reach the intent. Each part of the breakdown keeps its signal's date and its source's creator, a member's id (gamma-p5-065). The decay's inputs, outputs and histories hold workspace ids, the time and counts.

`tests/intent.test.ts` checks intent-v1 against its golden file, with the stacking, the unrated value, the priority, the tiers and the why-now bullet in words; `tests/signal-reader.test.ts` the registry and the case with no reader; `tests/rescore.test.ts` the rows a decision and a re-score write and the lock, both ways; `tests/lead-priorities.test.ts` `leadPriorities` and `setLeadPriority`; `tests/tenancy.test.ts` and `tests/assess.test.ts` the table's isolation and its erasure. The worker's `tests/qualification-intent-decay.test.ts` runs two nights, the boost's end, a failing workspace and the schedule; its histories are `workflows/__histories__/qualification_intent_decay_tick.json` and `qualification_intent_decay.json`.

## The Hot alert

gamma-p5-065 (RB-5.3.2, reduced to one mail to the source's creator; F04 §7). When an intent write takes a lead into Hot, the member who created the source of its why-now signal gets one mail with the signal's kind and age and the link to the lead's page ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md)).

- **The entry**: `writeIntent` reads the row's tier before it writes; a write of Hot over no row or another tier is an entry, and the decision or update that wrote it (`domain/score/score-snapshot.ts`) publishes `qualification.lead_hot` `{ workspaceId, personId }` in the same run. A write of another tier clears `hot_alerted_at`, so a later entry alerts again; a write that keeps the lead Hot publishes nothing.
- **The claim**: the subscriber (`events.ts`), in the delivery's run, sets `hot_alerted_at` where it is null and the row is still Hot (`domain/alert/claim.ts`), then starts `qualification_hot_alert`, `qualification.hot_alert:<workspaceId>:<eventId>`. A redelivery finds the claim and starts nothing; a start that fails rolls the claim back, and the outbox delivers again.
- **The mail**: the workflow's one activity, `qualification_mail_hot_lead` (kind `provider`, one attempt; `domain/alert/mail.ts`). A run reads the row, still Hot, and its why-now part; outside any run, tenancy's `resolveMembership` and `findUser`; then `qualification.mail.hot_lead` through `renderMail` in the member's locale, sent through the app-mail port. The result is `{ sent: true }`, or `{ sent: false, reason }` with `not_hot`, `no_signal`, `owner_unknown`, `member_left` or `owner_unavailable`; the claim stays whatever it is.
- **What it holds**: the signal's kind and role in words, its age in days, and the link `<APP_PUBLIC_ORIGIN>/w/<workspaceId>/leads/<personId>`, none while the origin is empty. Never the person's name, headline, company, words or URL ([docs/security.md](docs/security.md)). It is the app's own mail to a member, not outreach: no suppression list and no sending cap apply.
- **The log**: one line per alert, `qualification: the Hot alert`, with `person_id`, `user_id` and `outcome` only, through the worker host's logger (`onWorkerStart`).

`tests/hot-alert.test.ts` runs it on the worktree's database and the app-mail fake: the entry, a second update, the exit and the re-entry, a member who left, an unknown creator and no origin. The worker's `tests/qualification-hot-alert.test.ts` runs it through the host's outbox and Temporal, with the history `workflows/__histories__/qualification_hot_alert.json`.

## A member's verdict

lead-verdict-edit (the owner's request, 2026-11-03). On the lead page a member sets the verdict of a criterion the model got wrong or could not judge (contacts' `ui/lead/verdicts.tsx`).

- **The data**: each `criterion_verdicts` row keeps the model's `verdict` and `evidence` as they were and may hold a member's beside them: `member_verdict`, `member_note` (trimmed, 500 characters at most, empty is none), `member_set_by` (the user's id) and `member_set_at` (migration `qualification_member_verdicts`, whose CHECKs keep them together). The effective verdict is the member's when set, else the model's (`domain/score/member.ts`). A criterion of the ICP with no row on the snapshot gets a row of the member's verdict alone (`verdict` null), which a clear deletes.
- **The edit**: `setMemberVerdict` and `clearMemberVerdict` (`domain/member-verdict.ts`), behind `PUT` and `DELETE …/qualification/leads/{personId}/verdicts/{criterion}` ([Routes](#routes)), in the request's run, under the person's lock. The criterion must be one of the ICP's. A lead scored by other criteria than the current ones is re-scored first; then the lead's current snapshot is decided again in place from its effective verdicts (`redecideSnapshot`: fit, confidence, tier, status, each row's weight and points), and the lead gets the decision, the why and the intent's tier as a decision gives them. No model call and no credits. `qualification.lead_qualified` is published when the lead's status changed. An enrolled lead stays enrolled whatever its new status.
- **Every score reads it**: the batch's, the re-score's, the intent's and the read's. In the why a member's verdict quotes nothing: `qualification.why.fit_member` and `qualification.why.must_missed_member` say it is a member's ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)).
- **The re-score keeps it**: a copy takes the members' verdicts of the criteria the ICP still has, and a snapshot given back takes the current ones, decided again when they differ. The golden set exports the model's verdicts only.
- **The read**: per criterion `verdict` (the effective one), `modelVerdict`, and `member`, `{ verdict, note, setBy, setAt, mine }` or null.

`tests/member-verdict.test.ts` sets, changes and clears verdicts against fit-v1's `decide`, with the event, the refusals, a criterion without a row and the re-score; `core/apps/api/tests/qualification-member-verdicts.test.ts` runs the routes; the web host's `tests/lead-verdict-edit.spec.ts` runs the page.

## Titles and disqualifiers

title-check-ai (the owner, 2026-11-04): people combine and shorten titles ("RevOps & GTM Lead"), so the AI judges the title, not a filter, and a people-type disqualifier follows the ICP.

- **No title filter**: `prepareHardFilters` checks the country, then the size. A person with any title goes to the assessment, whose `title_function` judges it; a clear mismatch is a must at `not_met` by default, so the lead ends `not_qualified` with its why. Signals stores a person whose country fits as a pending lead, whatever the title (signals' README "Processing").
- **Prompt v2** of `qualification.assess-fit` (`agents/assess-fit/prompt.v2.md`; v1 stays): `title_function` reads each role of a combined or shortened title, the headline helping; `met` when one of them does the work of one of the ICP's titles, `partially_met` for an adjacent function; the level word is seniority's. `agency_or_consultant`, `freelancer`, `recruiter`, `student_or_intern` and `job_seeker` apply only when the data clearly shows them and the ICP's titles, seniorities, industries or must-haves do not target that kind of person; the six other codes as before. The batch's key holds the prompt version, so a v2 assessment is a reservation of its own.
- **Assessed again once** (`domain/reassess/`): the batch's own rule, not a data migration, because the owner's deploy migrates while the previous worker still runs, and its batch would filter a lead reset by a migration again as `title_mismatch` at the ICP's current version, which nothing selects then. The selection adds the leads `hard_filtered` for `title_mismatch`, `pending` again once they pass the filters, and the leads whose current snapshot, at the ICP's version, was made before prompt v2 and scored `not_qualified` for people-type disqualifiers only; such a lead keeps its decision until the new one. Once: the new snapshot is newer, and nothing writes `title_mismatch`. A lead disqualified for another code, alone or beside a people-type one, or `hard_filtered` for the country or the size, stays as it is. contacts' migration `contacts_title_hashes_reset` deletes the signal hashes kept for the title alone (contacts' README).
- **Cost**: each person the title filter used to stop, and each lead assessed again, is one assessment, its credits and one model call.

## A new assessment

lead-rescore-ai (the owner, 2026-11-04): a member who edited a lead's fields, or doubts its decision, asks the AI to assess it again with the lead page's Re-score, whatever its status ([Screen](#screen)).

- **The press**: `requestReassess` (`domain/member-reassess/`), behind `POST …/qualification/leads/{personId}/reassess` ([Routes](#routes)), in the request's run, under the person's lock. A decided lead goes back to `pending` with `status_reason` `member_requested`, its snapshot kept; its attempt at the ICP's current version moves to the next round (`assessment_attempts`); `qualification.reassess_requested` `{ workspaceId, personId }` is published, whose subscriber starts the workspace's batch at once (`batchStart`; while one runs, the next tick takes the lead). A pending lead stays as it is.
- **The batch**: the lead is pending, so the batch filters and assesses it with the person's fields of now, a member's edits included; an edited location can pass the filters now. One assessment is reserved under the round's key, never used before; the provider failing the round's third attempt makes the lead `ai_failed`, before it the lead waits.
- **Nothing changed**: callAgent's cache answers the same input with the snapshot stored before, scored already. `qualification_commit` then decides the lead again from it, re-scored when other criteria scored it, and commits the reservation at quantity 0: no model call and no credits.
- **What stays**: the members' verdicts of the snapshot the lead waited with carry to its new snapshot at the same ICP version, for the criteria the ICP still has ([A member's verdict](#a-members-verdict)); the review status, the enrollment, the approval items and the intent row stay as they are. While the lead waits, the snapshot read answers none, a member's verdict is refused and the criteria re-score skips it.
- **Cost**: each press on a changed lead is one assessment, its credits and one model call.

`tests/member-reassess.test.ts` runs the rounds, the press, a pending lead and the readers of a waiting lead; the api's `tests/qualification-reassess.test.ts` the route, a Member refused and another workspace's lead not found; the worker's `tests/qualification-reassess.test.ts` the batch on the LLM fake: an edited title assessed with the member's verdict carried, a press with nothing changed free, a filtered lead edited into the ICP's countries, the provider's failures after a press, a retried assessment charged once; the web host's `tests/rescore-action.spec.ts` and `tests/qualification-states.spec.ts` the panel.
- review-gaps-2 (2026-11-13): each audience's query answers its own pending leads only, a filter on the lead's first signal source in contacts' query, rather than a cursor reading on past the leads it skips: the tick's check then reads one lead at most per audience, where a cursor would read every waiting lead on every tick. The rule is one: the agents' sources, read once per batch, each with its agent's audience, give both a lead's audience (`of`) and the query's filter (`leadsOf`), and contacts reads the first signal source in SQL as `firstSignalSource` does in code. The leads decided again keep their audience's rule (its `icp_id_scored`) and are narrowed to those whose audience has targeting, so a lead decided against one audience but whose agent's audience is now an unsaved one holds back nobody either. A member's press reads the same rule (`audienceOfLead`).
