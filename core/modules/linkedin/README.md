# linkedin

The tester's own LinkedIn account, connected through the provider's hosted authentication after a risk disclosure: a sender account per user and workspace, with its status, time zone, working hours and daily read cap. The provider's account reference is stored sealed and opened in the worker only. Every read through the account passes the read quota (a reservation inside the cap and the working hours) and the action log; the signal run (alpha-p8-020) reads through them.

- **Owns:** `sender_accounts`, `sender_quota_usage`, `sender_quota_reservations` and `sender_action_log` in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/linkedin.prisma` ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)), the screen `ui/accounts`, the workflows `linkedin_refresh_account` and `linkedin_status_refresh` with the schedule `linkedin.status_refresh`, the activities `linkedin_refresh_status`, `linkedin_list_workspaces`, `linkedin_list_referenced_accounts`, `linkedin_read_post_engagers`, `linkedin_read_profile` and `linkedin_read_posts`, the metric `linkedin.actions` (`metrics.ts`: kind, outcome).
- **Public interface:** `index.ts`: `api`, which the api host mounts; `webhooks`, which it mounts at `POST /v1/linkedin/webhooks/{provider}`; `reserveReads`, `commitReads`, `releaseReads`, `readsLeftToday` and `logAction` ("Read quota"); `findSenderAccount`; `senderOf` and `remainingToday`, campaigns' queries ("Read quota"); `sendingDisclosureRequired(account)`, true while the account's owner has not accepted the current sending disclosure (`disclosureVersion`, `personalAccountConfirmedAt`; "Risk disclosure" in docs/concepts.md), campaigns' launch check (gamma-p3-050); the engager, profile and posts reads' types, `engagersFile`, `profileRequestFile`, `profileFile`, `postsRequestFile` and `postsFile` ("Worker"); `sendLogRows({ since })`, the `ok` rows of the sends (`invite`, `message`, `withdraw`) at or after `since`, oldest first, as `{ id, stepExecutionId, senderAccountId, kind, at }` (`stepExecutionId` null for a row naming no `sx_` id), and `healthEventsSince({ since })`, the health events into `restricted` or `checkpoint` at or after `since`, oldest first, as `{ id, senderAccountId, toState, trigger, createdAt }`: campaigns' "Done when" report (beta-p7-020), each in the caller's tenant run, `read_input_invalid` for no instant (`tests/report-reads.test.ts`); `events`; the registries `errors`, `ids` (`sac`, `squ`, `sqr`, `sal`) and `permissions`. The worker loads `events.ts`, with its subscriber of `linkedin.status_refresh_requested`, and `workflows/` itself.
- **Depends on:** `platform`, `db`, `providers` (the LinkedIn port), `tenancy` (a workspace's organization, and every workspace's id for the hourly refresh); the screen on `@whytide/ui` and the web's API client.
- **Reviewers:** the owners of this module.

## Read quota

The quota and its reservations, reads and sends. Each account has a budget per day for connection requests, messages, withdrawals and reads, bounded by hard maximums that no setting, route or script raises: `domain/quota/constants.ts` and the CHECKs of the migration `linkedin_send_quotas` ([docs/concepts.md](docs/concepts.md), [docs/data.md](docs/data.md)).
- The day's row is made with its limits drawn once, in the insert that makes it, by the first reservation, `remainingToday` or the usage route of the quota day: connection requests and messages are the account's targets (`invite_target` 5 to 15, `message_target` 10 to 40, raised by the ramp, beta-p1-080) times a uniform draw from 80 to 100 percent, rounded down, never above 25 and 80; the read allowance is the account's cap, at most 100 and at most 200 less the two limits and the 15 withdrawals; `check_after` a random instant in what is left of today's window, after which the day's check of the pending invitations runs ("Worker").
- A time-zone change takes effect from the next local day: the row keeps the zone it was made in, and while its date is not over there and in the account's zone now (`dayEndsAt`), the reservations, `remainingToday` and the usage route keep using it. A date already used keeps its row.
- What is left today (`limitsFor`, pure): connection requests within the day's limit and 100 over the last seven quota days; messages within their limit; withdrawals within 15; reads within the smaller of the allowance and the cap as it stands; each within the 200 actions left, counted over the four, reserved and made.
- `remainingToday(accountId)` → `{ invites, messages, withdrawals, actions, windowEnd }`, in the caller's tenant run: that answer and the end of today's window, null when none is left today; no send and no window on Saturday and Sunday in the account's zone, nor while sending is closed, a kill switch holds, the account is paused ("Health"), its disclosure for sending is not accepted or its member id is unknown (`actions` 0 too; Finding 56); no invite while the instance's invites are paused, the account is `at_risk` or its pending invitations' count refuses one ("Pending invitations"). Each of the three is within `actions`, the actions left of the 200, but their sum may pass it: campaigns' plan caps the sum with `actions` (beta-p4-010). `senderOf(userId)`: the user's `connected` account in the run's workspace, or null.

Every read through an account is reserved first, then committed or released, each call in the caller's own tenant run (an activity's `context.tenant`), as credits' reserve ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md)):
- `reserveReads({ accountId, count, kind, objectRef? })`, `kind` `read_engagers` (an engager-list page counts 1), `read_profile`, `send_check` (a check before a send's retry or a message, beta-p1-050), `pending_invites` (a page of the account's pending invitations) `relation_check` (an invite the vendor no longer lists; both the day's check's, "Worker") `read_posts` (a page of a source's posts counts 1, gamma-p5-015) or `read_company` (a company page counts 1, gamma-en-060; "Company pages"), `count` 1 to 1000: `{ reservationId, granted }`, all of `count` or none; or a refusal, `{ refused, retryAt }`: a kill switch, `platform_stopped`, `workspace_stopped` or `account_stopped` (`retryAt` null, "Kill switches"), `linkedin_account_disconnected` for an account not `connected` (`retryAt` null), `rate_limited` while its `retry_at` lies ahead (that time), `outside_working_hours` with the next window's start, in the account's own working days, `read_cap_reached` with the next quota day's start, when the day's reads reserved and committed plus `count` pass the smaller of the day's read allowance and `dailyReadCap`, then `daily_actions_cap_reached`, with the same start, when they pass the 200 actions; for `read_company`, `read_cap_reached` with the same start when the day's company reads pass their share, `LINKEDIN_COMPANY_READS_PER_DAY`; with `keep` (0 to `READS_PER_DAY_MAX`, gamma-p5-015), `read_cap_reached` with the next window's start when the reads left after `count`, within the 200, would be fewer than `keep`, checked under the day's lock as the cap is, so two reservations at once cannot both pass. Signals passes `keep`, the reads it leaves for the send path's checks; the send path's own reads (`send_check`, `pending_invites`, `relation_check`) pass none. A refusal is returned, not thrown, so the run commits its log row. `sender_account_not_found` for an account not in the run's workspace.
- `readsLeftToday(accountId)` → `{ quotaDay, left, windowOpen, nextWindowAt }`, in the caller's tenant run, for signals' scheduler (gamma-p5-015): `left` the reads `reserveReads` would still grant in the quota day, the smaller of the allowance and the cap less the reads reserved and made, within the 200, as the usage route counts them (a hold past its hour counts until the next reservation sweeps it); 0 while a kill switch, an account not `connected`, its health's pause or its `retry_at` holds; outside the window the same reads, with `windowOpen` false and the next start. It reserves nothing, so a caller that shares the reads out still reserves each one, and it makes today's row when missing, as the usage route does.
- A page counts one read, whatever it costs the vendor, as the engagers' page does: in the Unipile adapter one page may cost up to `MAX_LOADS` (10) vendor loads, plus one author lookup on an author's first page (a member's profile, or a company's slug). Unipile's search pages hold at most 49 (`SEARCH_POSTS_PAGE_MAX`), so a phrase page above 49 posts costs two loads or more: signals (gamma-p5-020) asks for at most 49 a page.
- `commitReads(reservationId, actual)`: `{ reservationId, committed }`, the reads made, counted as made, also after the reservation's hour or above its count; `releaseReads(reservationId)`: the reads freed. A repeat answers as the first; the other settlement, a send's reservation, or an unknown id, is `reservation_not_open` (409).
- A lead's "Contact info" read (gamma-en-087, "Contact info") is a `read_profile` read: one reserved per read, counted in the account's daily reads and its 200 actions, inside its window, pauses and switches, with the person's id as its object, as a profile read's is.
- A read's reservation holds an hour, a send's two (`SEND_RESERVATION_MS`); the next reservation of its account and day frees those past it, of every type, each off its type's counter (the lazy sweep).
- Each reservation reads the account's cap as it stands, so a cap lowered on the screen holds from the next reservation; a raised one holds from the next quota day, since the day's allowance was drawn with its row. A run already waiting for a refusal's `retryAt` keeps sleeping until then.
- `logAction({ accountId, kind, objectRef?, count, outcome, withNote? })`: a read made, a send (`invite`, `message`, `withdraw`; `withNote` on an invite only) or a status check. `outcome` is one of `ACTION_OUTCOMES` (`schemas/quota.ts`, the CHECK's list): `ok`, `reserved`, every refusal of the reservations, the later tasks' included, `reservation_not_open`, the port's codes and the statuses a status check records. `objectRef` is a person's id (`per_…`), a post's URL without query, or, alone on a send's row, its step execution (`sx_…`) or inbox's reply (`msg_…`, gamma-p4-060); never a profile's URL; anything else is `read_input_invalid` (`details.fields`). The reservations log each answer themselves. `linkedin.actions` counts the rows of linkedin's own activities once their run has returned, so a run that rolls back counts none; the rows written in another module's run, its reservations, are counted in the table only.

Every connection request, message and withdrawal is reserved first, under the step's idempotency key, in the caller's tenant run with the day's row locked, as a read is ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "Sends"):
- `reserveSend({ accountId, actionType, idempotencyKey, withNote? })`, `actionType` `invite`, `message` or `withdraw`, the key campaigns' (1 to 128 characters; never built here), `withNote` on an invite only: `{ reservationId, status: "reserved" }`, or a refusal `{ refused, retryAt }`, in this order: `sending_closed`, `platform_stopped`, `workspace_stopped`, `account_stopped` (null, "Kill switches"); `linkedin_account_disconnected` (null); `account_paused` (null, "Health"); `invites_paused`, an invite while the instance's invites are paused or the account is `at_risk` (null); `disclosure_required` (null); for an invite, `pending_invites_unknown` while the account's pending invitations' count is unknown or was checked more than 48 hours before (an hour after today's `check_after`, the hourly run after it, while today's check is still to come, else the next quota day's start) and `pending_invites_over_limit` while it, with the account's invites in flight, is above 400 (the next quota day's start) ("Pending invitations"); `member_id_unknown` (null); `rate_limited` (`retry_at`); `outside_working_hours`, outside the window and on Saturday and Sunday in the account's zone whatever days it lists (the next weekday window's start; null when it lists the weekend alone); `invite_note_budget`, a fourth invitation with a note in 30 days (when the oldest counted leaves them); `invite_cap_reached`, `message_cap_reached`, `withdrawal_cap_reached` (the next quota day's start); `weekly_invite_cap_reached` (the first quota day whose seven allow one); `daily_actions_cap_reached` (the next quota day's start). One log row each. The state refusals are `sendStateRefusal(account, actionType, at, own?)` (`domain/send/state.ts`), where the later tasks add theirs and which beta-p1-050 asks again before the call, `own` its reservation, which the invites in flight leave out.
- A repeat of a key answers the reservation that holds it, `reserved` within its hold or `committed` (a late commit's too), with no second slot and no log row; a released or expired key reserves again, as a new row.
- `commitSend(reservationId)` → `{ reservationId, status }`: the send counted once, on its reservation's own day; a repeat answers as the first. A committed invite adds one to its account's pending invitations' count, unless it is unknown, until the next check. A released or expired reservation counts too, as the send went out; when another reservation holds its key meanwhile, it becomes `committed_late`. `releaseSend(reservationId)`: the slot freed; a repeat or an expired one changes nothing; a committed one, or a read's, is `reservation_not_open`.
- The note budget counts the `ok` invite rows with a note of the last 30 days, which beta-p1-050 writes with each commit, and the held invite reservations with one.
- `SEND_ERROR_MAP` (`domain/send/error-map.ts`, frozen): what each answer of a send does to the slot and `retry_at` (beta-p1-050 applies them), the credits and the step (campaigns', beta-p4-020 and beta-p4-040) and the account's health (beta-p6-010's), [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The error map". `domain/send/readers.ts`, for beta-p1-050 inside this module: a send reservation by id, and the reservations of one key, oldest first.

`index.ts` adds `reserveSend`, `commitSend`, `releaseSend`, `findSendReservation` (the reservation by id, in the caller's run, or null: campaigns' sender tells a refused check, whose slot stays `reserved`, from a refusal before the call; beta-p4-020), `SendReservation`, `SendRefusal`, `SEND_ERROR_MAP` and `SendErrorMap`. Tests: `tests/send-reservations.test.ts`, `tests/error-map.test.ts` and `tests/action-log.test.ts`; `tests/safety/no-double-reservation.test.ts`, `send-hours.test.ts` and the send sequences of `quota-invariants.test.ts`, which `test:safety` runs and `test` leaves out.

## Kill switches

Three kill switches stop every LinkedIn action of their scope, reads included, from the next reservation: the instance's stop, the workspace's "Stop everything" and the account's pause (`kill_switch`). Two sending gates stop sends only: the instance's `sending_open`, closed on a fresh database until the operator opens it (beta-p1-060), and `invites_paused`, connection requests only (the breaker's, beta-p6-030) ([docs/concepts.md](docs/concepts.md)). Their tables: `linkedin_platform_controls` (`lpc`, global, one row at most), `linkedin_workspace_controls` (`lwc`), `linkedin_control_events` (`lce`, global, append-only) and `sender_accounts`' `kill_switch` and `kill_switch_source` ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)).
- `domain/controls/`, in the caller's tenant run, each change taking the caller's principal, whose rights `domain/access.ts` answers (gamma-p6-070): `readControls()`; `pauseAccount(accountId, principal)`, the account's owner or a holder of `linkedin.manage` (`not_account_owner`); `unpauseAccount(accountId, principal)`, a tester's pause its owner or a holder of `linkedin.manage`, a removed member's its owner alone (`not_account_owner`), never the operator's pause (`stopped_by_operator`); `setWorkspaceStop(stopped, principal)`, both ways, a holder of `linkedin.manage` alone (`forbidden`), never lifting the operator's stop. Each change is a compare-and-set that writes one control event in the same run; a call that changes nothing writes none.
- The operator's, module-internal for the control command ("The control command"): `pauseAccountAsOperator(workspaceId, accountId, name)`, `unpauseAccountAsOperator(…)` and `setWorkspaceStopAsOperator(workspaceId, stopped, name)`, each in the named workspace's run, taking a tester's or a removed member's stop over and leaving one to lift (`paused_by_tester`, `paused_by_member_removed`); `setPlatformSwitch("sending" | "stop", on, name)`, outside any run, the change and its event in one transaction on the global client; `readPlatformControls()` and `readWorkspaceControls(workspaceId)`, the status's reads.
- A member's removal (gamma-p6-070; `domain/removed.ts`, docs/flows.md "A member's removal"): tenancy's `tenancy.member_removed`, delivered through the outbox, runs linkedin's subscriber (`events.ts`) in the workspace's run, which pauses each account the removed user owns there with the source `member_removed`, one control event each (`actor_type` `system`, actor `tenancy.member_removed`): from off, or taking a tester's pause over; the operator's stays; a redelivery finds the account paused and writes nothing. Only the account's owner lifts it, once invited again.
- The refusals carry `retryAt` null: `sendStateRefusal` and `readStateRefusal` (`domain/send/state.ts`) read the switches from Postgres in the reservation's own run, with no cache ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "A switch and the next reservation"). A repeat of a reserved key answers its hold before them: beta-p1-050's activity asks `sendStateRefusal` again just before the port call.
- Each member's own account (gamma-p6-070): `tests/removed.test.ts` (the removal's subscriber through the outbox, a redelivery), the worker's `tests/safety/linkedin-per-member.test.ts` (who may pause and lift, then the next reservation and the fake's sends), `core/apps/api/tests/linkedin-controls.test.ts`, `linkedin-accounts`, `linkedin-usage`, `linkedin-health` and `linkedin-disclosure` (the routes' owners), and the screen's teammates in `linkedin-switches.spec.ts`, in `wsp_e2e_teammates`; `setAccountPaused` (`testing/`) takes `member_removed` too.

## The webhook

`POST /v1/linkedin/webhooks/{provider}`, the api host's route for a module's `webhooks` (core/apps/api/README.md "Modules"): no session, no workspace, `RATE_LIMIT_WEBHOOK_PER_IP_PER_MIN` per address (429; a Redis that does not answer lets the call through), the raw body at most 64 KiB (413), no `Content-Encoding` (415). A `provider` other than `PROVIDER_LINKEDIN`, or a signature or body the adapter refuses, is `linkedin_webhook_invalid` (401) and changes nothing. Then by kind (providers' linkedin/README.md "Events"):

- **Account events**, alpha's three kinds and `account.status`, read as one status. `connected` with the link's `providerRef`, the vendor's notify of the owner's connect, pairs the pending account the link names and seals the reference: only the webhook pairs, and only the pairing writes the reference and `connected_at`. A pending account whose LinkedIn account another user connected in the workspace already ends `disconnected`, its link cleared, and the answer is `sender_account_in_use` (409); the vendor's retry finds no pending account (204). Every other status, `connected` without the link's `providerRef` too (Unipile's `OK` and `SYNC_SUCCESS`), finds the accounts by the reference's hash and is stored as a refresh stores it, in the order the statuses arrive: `status_checked_at`, and the event when it crosses into `connected` or `disconnected`; a pending account waits for its link. `checkpoint` and `restricted` change nothing yet but `linkedin.account_warnings` (`status`): beta-p6-010 pauses the account.
- **`ignored`**: `linkedin.events_dropped` (`kind`), nothing else.
- **Person events**, `invite.accepted` and `message.received`: the receiver (`domain/receiver/`, [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The receiver") keeps one only when an outreach row of the account the reference's hash finds concerns the person: an acceptance by `provider_member_ref`; a message by `provider_chat_ref`, or by `provider_member_ref` against its sender (inbound) or its attendees (the account's own). Anything else is dropped before it is stored: no row, no file, no log line, `linkedin.events_dropped` alone. A kept message's payload goes to `raw/linkedin/messages/<event_key_hash>.json` (72 hours); then one transaction writes the receipt (a repeat ends there), an inbound message's `last_inbound_at` and `linkedin.message_received { sender_account_id, person_id, direction, file_key }`. A kept acceptance sets `accepted_at` and `accepted_detected_at` once and publishes `linkedin.invite_accepted { sender_account_id, person_id, detected_at }`.

204 once that resolves, also for an account none of ours: the subscribers run in the worker, from the outbox. ## Worker

Two workflows, both through the activity `linkedin_refresh_status`. `linkedin_status_refresh`, global, every hour by the schedule `linkedin.status_refresh` (paused while `WORKER_SCHEDULES=off`): `linkedin_list_workspaces`, global, takes every workspace's id from tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds()`, and nothing else, and answers a page of them, `STATUS_REFRESH_PAGE_SIZE` (50, `workflows/constants.ts`) after the run's cursor, in id order, with the next page's cursor (`workflows/pages.ts`); then, workspace by workspace, `linkedin_list_referenced_accounts` lists the workspace's accounts with a reference, ids only, in its run, and each account is refreshed in turn, an account whose refresh fails past its attempts counted and the others refreshed (`{ accounts, failed }`). `linkedin_refresh_account` `{ workspaceId, accountId }`: one account, started by `events.ts`'s subscriber of `linkedin.status_refresh_requested` with the id `linkedin.status_refresh:<workspaceId>:<event id>`, the workspace the delivery's run's: a second delivery starts none. Their replay histories are in `workflows/__histories__/`.

The pages (gamma-p0-085), behind the patch `status-refresh-pages` (`STATUS_REFRESH_PAGES_PATCH`), asked once at the run's start as `PENDING_INVITES_PATCH` is: after a page that is not the last, the run continues as new with `{ afterId, counts }`, the page's last workspace id and its `{ accounts, failed }` so far, under the same workflow id, so a history holds one page and the last run answers the totals. A run begun before the patch handles the workspaces its list answered: all of them, or the first page when the list answers after the deploy, the next hourly run handling the rest. `useStatusRefreshPageSize(size)` (`workflows/pages.ts`) sets the page under `APP_ENV=test` and throws in any other environment. Tests: `core/apps/worker/tests/linkedin-status-refresh.test.ts`; the history `workflows/__histories__/linkedin_status_refresh_pages.json`, a chain's first run.

`linkedin_refresh_status` `{ workspaceId, accountId }`: the provider's status for an account with a reference, stored, the event when it crosses into `connected` or `disconnected`, and a `status_check` row in the action log; an account the provider no longer knows is `disconnected`. Another typed port error is answered, never thrown (beta-p6-020): a `status_check` row with the error's code, `retry_at` and the health as a send's error sets them (`SEND_ERROR_MAP`, "Health"), and `{ status, failed }`, the status unchanged; so the call is made once, and the next hourly run checks again. While the account's `retry_at` lies ahead (a rate limit's wait, an hour after a provider error, the third error's pause), no call and no row: `{ status, held: "rate_limited" }` (beta-p6-040). A refresh that reads `connected` while the health is `disconnected` heals it, the status unchanged ("Health"). An error that is not the port's still throws. `workflows/decrypt.ts` `withDecryptedRef(context, accountId, fn)` opens the reference for `fn` and zeroes it after; the lint's `decrypt-in-worker-only` keeps `workflows/` out of the api.

`linkedin_read_post_engagers` `{ workspaceId, accountId, postUrl, cursor?, limit, rawKey }` (alpha-p8-020, signals' reader): one page of the post's engagers, newest first, through the opened reference, written to the files port at `rawKey` (under `raw/`, so the workspace's prefix and the 72-hour lifecycle) as `{ engagers }` (`engagersFile`; each engager's `dateKnown`, the port's since gamma-p5-010, may be absent in a page written before it), answered as `{ key, count, nextCursor }`: the engagers never cross the workflow's history. A disconnected account or an unknown post answers `{ refused }` with the code; other errors fail the attempt (`linkedin_rate_limited` retried after its `retryAfterSec`). Each call writes a `read_engagers` row in the action log, `rawKey` its `call_ref`. The caller reserves and settles the read; this activity does not.

`linkedin_read_profile` `{ workspaceId, accountId, requestKey, rawKey }` (alpha-p8-030, the engager read's twin): the profile whose URL the request file at `requestKey` holds (`profileRequestFile`, `{ profileUrl }`), read through the opened reference and written at `rawKey` (`profileFile`, `fetchedAt` in ISO), answered as `{ key }`; both files under `raw/`, so no URL crosses the history. `{ refused }` as above, and `linkedin_provider_error` on the third attempt (`PROFILE_READ_ATTEMPTS`, `workflows/constants.ts`; the two before fail and are retried), so that the run skips a profile the provider cannot read; a rate limit still fails the attempt. A `read_profile` row in the action log, with no object, `rawKey` its `call_ref`. The caller reserves `read_profile` and settles it.

`linkedin_read_posts` `{ workspaceId, accountId, requestKey, rawKey }` (gamma-p5-015, `workflows/posts.ts`): one page of a source's posts, newest first, through the opened reference. The request file at `requestKey` (`postsRequestFile`) holds `{ source, postedSince, after?, limit }`: `source` `{ by: "phrase", phrase }`, `{ by: "author", authorUrl }` or `{ by: "own" }` (the port's `searchPosts`, `listAuthorPosts`, `listOwnPosts`), `postedSince` in ISO, `limit` 1 to 50. The page goes to `rawKey` as `{ posts, nextCursor }` (`postsFile`, `postedAt` in ISO), answered as `{ key, count, more }`. The vendor's cursor stays in the page file, never in the workflow's history, since it may carry the author's vendor id or the phrase (!213's review): for the next page the caller writes a request with `after`, the previous page's `rawKey` as the caller passed it (relative, under `raw/`, not the answer's whole `key`), and the activity reads the cursor from that file; an `after` file that is no page, or the last one (`more` false), is `read_input_invalid` naming `after`, as a request file off the schema names its fields. A disconnected account or an unknown author answers `{ refused }` with the code; any other error fails the attempt and counts towards the third error ("Health"). Each call writes a `read_posts` row, no object, `rawKey` its `call_ref`. The caller reserves `read_posts` and settles it. Tests: `core/apps/worker/tests/linkedin-read-posts.test.ts`; the reservation's, `keep`'s and `readsLeftToday`'s in `tests/quota.test.ts`.

The engagers read and the posts read run at most `POST_READ_ATTEMPTS` (3, `workflows/constants.ts`) times, the definitions' own bound (gamma-p0-080's `attempts`), so every caller gets it; past it the activity fails, and what the run does then is signals'. The profile read runs at most `PROFILE_READ_ATTEMPTS` (3) times the same way (gamma-p0-085), for signals' read as for campaigns' sender, whose proxy keeps its own bound: a rate limit on every attempt fails the activity after the third, and a provider error is the refusal above on the third.

The send activities (beta-p1-050, `workflows/send.ts`; docs/flows.md "The send activities"), campaigns' sender's: `linkedin_send_invite`, `linkedin_send_message` and `linkedin_withdraw_invite` `{ workspaceId, accountId, reservationId, requestKey, stepAttempt }`, the caller's `reserveSend` for the step's key, the request file it wrote under `raw/` (`sendRequestFile`: `{ stepExecutionId, personId, actionType, memberRef, note?, text?, idempotencyKey }`, no chat or invitation ref) and `step_executions.attempt`. After a connect (beta-p1-070): `events.ts`'s subscriber of `linkedin.sender_account_connected` starts `linkedin_refresh_account` as above, by the event's id, and `linkedin_refresh_status` stores the hash of the member id `getAccountStatus` answers, the one-row check ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) "The member id after a connect"): `{ status: "disconnected", refused: "sender_account_in_use" }` when the LinkedIn account is connected on another row, a result, not a failure.

## The control command

`pnpm linkedin:control <subcommand>` (`cli/control.ts`), from the repository root, builds the module and runs the operator's command on the worktree's database (`.env.local`); on the production machine, whose image has no pnpm, `docker compose exec api node ../../modules/linkedin/dist/cli/control.js <subcommand>` ([docs/runbook.md](docs/runbook.md)). A thin layer over the operator's variants in `domain/controls/` ("Kill switches"), with no SQL of its own, so the routes, the screen and the command apply the same rules and write the same control events. It loads linkedin, tenancy, db and platform, calls no provider and never opens a reference.
- `sending open|close --operator <name>`: the platform row's `sending_open`, the row made when missing; `stop|start --platform --operator <name>`: its `stopped`, every LinkedIn action, reads included. On the global client outside any run, the change and its event in one transaction.
- `pause|unpause --workspace <id or slug> [--account <sac_id>] --operator <name>`: the workspace's stop, or the account's `kill_switch`, source `operator`, in that workspace's run as the service principal `linkedin`, with its event. The operator's pause takes a tester's over, so the screen can no longer lift it (`stopped_by_operator`); the operator's unpause lifts the operator's only: a tester's answers `paused_by_tester` and stays the tester's to lift. A removed member's pause (gamma-p6-070) is treated as a tester's: the operator's pause takes it over, and the operator's unpause answers `paused_by_member_removed`, the pause its owner's to lift.
- `breaker reopen --operator <name>`: the connection requests the fleet breaker paused open again ("Health"), the only way to lift it: `reopenInvites(name)` sets `invites_paused` false, its reason null and `changed_by` the name, with one event (`invites`, `on` to `off`), on the global client outside any run; not paused, `unchanged` and no event.
- `status [--workspace <id or slug>]`: `platform: sending=open|closed stop=on|off invites_paused=on|off reason=<trigger>|none`, then each workspace, every one tenancy's `listWorkspaceIds()` gives or the one named, each read in its own run: `workspace: <wsp_id> slug=<slug> stop=on|off source=tester|operator|none`, followed by its accounts, oldest first, `account: <sac_id> workspace=<wsp_id> owner=<usr_id> status=<status> switch=on|off source=tester|operator|member_removed|none`. Ids and codes only.
- A change prints `changed: <what>` and appends one `linkedin_control_events` row, `actor_type` `operator` and `actor` the name; or `unchanged: <what>` and no row when the state was so already. `--operator` is a name, `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9._-]{0,63}$` (`cli/constants.ts`), never free text. Exit 0 done; 1 refused: `workspace_not_found` (no workspace with the id, or no single one with the slug), `sender_account_not_found` (not in that workspace), `paused_by_tester`, `paused_by_member_removed`; 2 the usage: an unknown subcommand or option, a change without `--operator` or with a name outside the pattern, `--platform` with `--workspace`, `breaker` without `reopen` or with a scope. Exit 1 or 2 changes nothing; through pnpm, any failure is 1.

Tests: `tests/control-command.test.ts`, and `tests/safety/operator-stop.test.ts` and `fleet-breaker.test.ts`, which `test:safety` runs.

## Settings

`LINKEDIN_DAILY_READ_CAP_MAX` (100, 1 to 100; `config.ts`, the `modules/linkedin` block of `deploy/.env.example`): the highest daily read cap `PATCH /accounts/{id}` takes. Its own bound is `READS_PER_DAY_MAX`, a hard maximum: a value above 100 stops the start with `config_invalid`, so an `.env` from before beta-p1-020 (250 by default) comes down to 100 before the upgrade. Lowering it changes no account's cap, only the next change. The adapter is `PROVIDER_LINKEDIN` (`fake` or `unipile`, providers' block), the return address the api's `APP_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` (`WEB_DEV_ORIGIN` on a laptop), the key `APP_MASTER_KEY`. On Unipile, `UNIPILE_NOTIFY_URL` is the public URL of the webhook route.

`LINKEDIN_HASH_KEY` (a secret of 32 characters or more, optional; `config.ts`, the `modules/linkedin` block): the key of the member id's hash and of the receipts' event key hash, the same value in the api, whose receiver hashes the event keys, and in the worker, whose refresh hashes the member id. Empty, the hosts start, no member id is stored, and no account can send (`member_id_unknown`), one whose hash was stored before included, and a kept person event answers `linkedin_hash_key_missing` (500). Set once and never changed: under another key every account looks replaced at its next refresh, which asks its disclosure again and publishes `linkedin.account_replaced`, on which campaigns ends its enrollments (beta-p4-040), and the receipts kept under the old key no longer match a redelivery ([docs/runbook.md](docs/runbook.md)).

`LINKEDIN_ALERT_EMAIL` (an email address, empty by default; `config.ts`, the `modules/linkedin` block): the operator's address for the fleet breaker's mail ("Health"). Empty, the breaker trips all the same, no mail goes and the worker logs `linkedin_alert_email_missing` once per trip.

`LINKEDIN_INVITE_NOTES_PER_MONTH` (3, an integer of 1 or more with no maximum; `settings.ts`'s `noteSettings`, the default `INVITE_NOTES_PER_MONTH` in `domain/send/constants.ts`, the `modules/linkedin` block; env-settings-a): the invitations with a note one account sends in 30 days, the note budget (docs/concepts.md). **3 is LinkedIn's limit for a free account: raise it only for accounts on a paid LinkedIn plan**, as with messaging's `LINKEDIN_NOTE_LIMIT`. Unset or empty, 3; a value that is not an integer of 1 or more stops the api's and the worker's start with `config_invalid` naming it. It is read once, as the module loads: **a changed value needs an api and worker restart**. `tests/settings.test.ts` sets 5 and tries the values off its range.

## Decided here

- Connect is a `whytide:own-runs` route without `@Idempotent()`: the provider call must sit outside any run, and own-runs does not combine with `@Idempotent()`. It is idempotent in the domain instead: one row per user and workspace, upserted; a repeat replaces the pending link.
- One account per user and workspace (`(workspace_id, owner_user_id)` unique). The reference's hash is SHA-256 of `<provider>:<reference>`, unique per workspace.
- A user's disconnect clears the reference and the hash; a webhook's keeps them, so the vendor's status finds the account again.
- `linkedin.manage` is Owner and Admin (`LINKEDIN_MANAGE_ROLES`). Since gamma-p6-070 the own-account routes check the account's owner in the domain (`domain/access.ts`): a Member reaches their own account alone; a holder of `linkedin.manage` reads every account and pauses it, while renaming, the working hours, the read cap, Refresh status, Resume, the disclosure and Disconnect stay the owner's.
- Events `linkedin.sender_account_connected` and `linkedin.sender_account_disconnected`, `{ sender_account_id }`, the platform's `module.event_name` form.
- The read quota (alpha-p7-040): a refusal is an answer, not a throw, so the caller's run keeps its log row and its workflow waits for `retryAt`; reserveReads takes a `kind` (and an `objectRef`) for that row. All or none: no partial grant. A reservation is a row of its own (`sender_quota_reservations`); the day's counters change in the same run. The sweep covers the day being reserved: a reservation of an earlier day holds nothing of today's cap. A commit counts every read made, after the hour or above the reservation too: they happened, and the cap guards the account, not the caller. An account not `connected`, a restricted one included once its status says so, is refused with `linkedin_account_disconnected` and logged.
- Refresh status (alpha-p10-130) is a route that writes an event through the outbox, and the module's own subscriber in the worker starts the run: the api holds no Temporal client (platform README "Workflows"). The screen polls the account for its new status rather than waiting on the run. The hourly refresh reads no tenant table across workspaces: its global activity takes the workspaces' ids from tenancy, and each workspace's accounts are listed and refreshed in that workspace's run.
- The screen loads the account and the usage in the browser, for the kit's loading and error states with a retry in place.
- `quota_day` is `text` `YYYY-MM-DD` with a CHECK: the schema lint takes no `@db.Date`. The window is `[start, end)` in local minutes.
- The daily read cap (fix-read-cap) is the owner's to set, a whole number from 1 to `LINKEDIN_DAILY_READ_CAP_MAX`, which is 100 by default and at most since beta-p1-020 (`READS_PER_DAY_MAX`): reads count in the 200 actions a day and stop at 100 a day, the owner's decision of 2026-10-29. A new account's cap stays 100.
- The screen on the design system (ui-tailwind), with no change of behaviour, request or key but the header's description (`linkedin.page.description`): the empty state is the connect card headed by its sentence, rather than the kit's empty state above the card; the card's Disconnect is a secondary button, its confirmation the danger one; the account card's facts and the working hours' two times follow the card's width (container queries), as the card's column does not follow the screen's. The days stay the kit's chip input.
- The send quotas (beta-p1-020): the draw is uniform over the whole numbers from 80 percent of the target, rounded down, to the target, so the target itself is drawn: a continuous draw rounded down would never reach it, and would give 4 every day for a target of 5. The usage route and `remainingToday` make today's row when missing, so the screen and the plan see the limits the reservations check; a raised read cap waits for the next quota day, a lowered one holds at once. The weekly sum counts the connection requests reserved and made on the six quota days before, so an open reservation of an earlier day still counts.
- The receiver (beta-p0-060) works in the order verify, filter, file, one transaction: a dropped event reaches no table and no file, a receipt exists only for a kept event, a failed transaction leaves at most an orphan file that raw/'s 72 hours expire, and a redelivery stops at the receipt, read before the file, so a file inbox's store deleted is never written again (beta-p5-010); two deliveries at once both write the same file, and the second run stops at the receipt. The file is written before the run: no provider or files call inside one.
- The receipts are global, keyed by the HMAC-SHA-256 of `event:<adapter>:<eventKey>` under `LINKEDIN_HASH_KEY` (beta-p1-070), and inserted on the account's run's own transaction (a global table runs as written there): the receipt, the outreach row's change and the event commit together or not at all. A delivery at the same time waits on the key, then finds the receipt.
- `invite_accepted` and `message_received` carry snake_case payloads, as linkedin's other events; privacy's platform eraser matches `person_id` as well as `personId`. `last_inbound_at` only moves forward: an earlier message delivered late leaves the later time.
- A second user's pairing of a LinkedIn account connected in the workspace already is checked in the pairing's run, before the update, rather than caught from the unique `(workspace_id, provider_account_ref_hash)`.
- `connected` pairs only with the link's `providerRef`, which beta-p0-040's notify carries; without it, as Unipile's account statuses send it (`OK`, `SYNC_SUCCESS`), it is a status, stored as a refresh stores it. The reference is not sealed again and `connected_at` stays the owner's last connect, which beta-p6-040 reads after a checkpoint; a pending account waits for its link; from `credentials_required`, `error` or the vendor's `disconnected`, the account is `connected` again and announced.
- The send reservations (beta-p1-030): the key is the step's idempotency key, taken as given, and one reservation per key and workspace is `reserved` or `committed` (the partial unique index), so a retry takes no second slot and a released or expired key reserves again. A repeat is answered before any refusal. A commit after a release or the hold's end while another reservation holds the key is `committed_late`, the simplest mark the index leaves out; clearing the key would lose the step. A key's stale reservation is expired on its own day's row, locked first, before today's row.
- The hold is one lease of two hours, longer than a send activity's worst retry span (5 attempts up to 15 minutes apart, plus 5 timeouts of 2 minutes; `tests/error-map.test.ts` pins it), rather than a lease renewed at each attempt. The sweep and the settles move each action type's own counters, by increments in the statement.
- The error map is a frozen constant keyed by the answer: `linkedin_account_disconnected` by its `details.reason` (`checkpoint`, `restricted`, anything else as disconnected), `linkedin_send_refused` by `already_connected` for an invite only (connect-already-connected: the slot committed with no invitation pending, the credits released, the step skipped, the answer `{ refused, reason }`, `reason` one of `SEND_REFUSED_REASONS`), a code it lacks as `linkedin_error_unmapped`, and `linkedin_webhook_invalid`, which no send answers, as an unknown outcome.
- A day's row records its zone and stays the account's day until its date is over there and in the account's zone now: a move east opens no second day's budgets within the same 24 hours, and a move west goes back to no earlier date. No send on Saturday or Sunday in the account's zone, whatever days it lists; reads keep the account's days.
- The action log takes no UPDATE from `app` and keeps DELETE for `erasePerson` and the 90-day purge, as `review_decisions`; its outcomes are a closed list, `ACTION_OUTCOMES`, which the CHECK mirrors; a send's row names its step execution or inbox's reply, never a person.
- The kill switches (beta-p1-040): a stop's state is `off`, `tester` or `operator`, one column pair per scope with a CHECK that the source is set exactly when it is on, and a stop on without a source reads as the operator's, fail closed. The tester never lifts the operator's stop, and the operator's press takes a tester's over. `app` may not delete the instance's row, since a deleted row would lift its stop, nor update or delete a control event. The instance's switch runs outside any run, in a transaction of its own on the global client, so its change and its event commit together (beta-p1-060).
- The control command (beta-p1-060): each subcommand takes its own options only, anything else being the usage, so `status` takes no `--operator`; a `--workspace` with an underscore is an id, else a slug, and one that is neither, or a slug two organizations share, is `workspace_not_found`; `status` reads each workspace's slug from tenancy's `findWorkspace`, and the platform's row on the global client. `inGlobal` casts `globalClient()`, typed as a transaction's client, to reach its `$transaction`; the global guard checks every query inside it.
- The risk disclosure for sending (beta-p1-070): alpha's acknowledgement stays for reads, and `disclosure_version` with `personal_account_confirmed_at` gates sends, compared with `DISCLOSURE_VERSION`, never a literal. Accepting moves `disclosure_acknowledged_at` to its time too. Connect asks for the current version and both boxes: alpha's body, without the version, is `disclosure_required`. A disconnected card shows no notice: Connect's boxes accept the disclosure.
- The member id is read by the worker after the pairing, as the notify carries none, and kept only as a keyed hash under a secret of its own, `LINKEDIN_HASH_KEY`, as contacts' `HASH_SALT`; the receipts' event key hash takes the same key (`event:` before the adapter, `linkedin:` before a member id). Without the key a kept event is refused before any write, so the vendor delivers it again.
- One LinkedIn account per connected row is the database's check, a partial unique index no run's scope hides; a violation ends the run's transaction, so a second run ends the account, also when a status write meets it. A pairing whose kept member id another connected row holds is paired as replaced, its id cleared, and the refresh decides. Every pairing clears `member_id_checked_at`, which only a refresh that read a member id sets: a reconnected row keeps the previous LinkedIn account's hash, so it sends nothing until then rather than under that account's targets or from two rows. The activity answers `sender_account_in_use` as a result, so neither the worker nor the vendor retries.
- `createConnectedAccount` makes an account ready to send (version 2, confirmed, a random member id hash) unless told otherwise, so the other modules' send tests change nothing; a refresh under a key then replaces it unless `memberRef` names its member.
- The account's health (beta-p6-010): a cool-down survives a disconnect and a reconnect (C26), which change only the session `status`; LinkedIn's weekly invitation limit (`linkedin_limit_reached`) pauses the whole account for 72 hours, as a restriction does, and a checkpoint gets the same 72 hours; the third rate limit or provider error in an hour pauses sends and reads until the next working day through `retry_at`, with no cool-down, no owner's resume and no breaker (the owner's decision of 2026-10-29).
- `healthSignalOf` decides a typed error's health, and `SEND_ERROR_MAP` still decides the slot and the step. A warning pauses every account the reference's hash finds, a pending one too. `linkedin.health_changes` counts by `status` (the new state) and `trigger`: the metric labels' allow-list has no `from` or `to`. A health event keeps `paused_until`, the end of the pause it set, for the mail and the screen.
- The screen's keys `linkedin.health.state.*` and `linkedin.health.trigger.*` came with beta-p6-040, which shows them: `messages:check` refuses a key nothing uses. The mail picks its words by state and trigger inside its own message.
- The ramp asks the idle restart (14 quota days without a send) before anything else and lets it only lower the targets, so an account that could not act for weeks (closed, stopped, paused, not connected, not healthy) reopens at half its targets; the safety case reads "no rise while it cannot act".
- The pending invitations (beta-p4-070) fail closed: a count never made, one checked more than 48 hours before (`PENDING_INVITES_FRESH_HOURS`: one missed check leaves a day's margin, two refuse) or one above 400 refuses an invite, and a check refused or failed writes no count, so it ages until it refuses. The two refusals come right after `disclosure_required` and before `member_id_unknown`, an invite's only; their statuses are 409 (`pending_invites_unknown`) and 429 (`pending_invites_over_limit`).
- One check a day, inside the hourly `linkedin_status_refresh` rather than a schedule of its own: due after the day row's `check_after`, drawn at random with the row in the first two hours of what is left of the window, never in its last hour (`CHECK_DRAW_MS`, `CHECK_END_MARGIN_MS`; beta-p6-020), so an account of weekdays only waits an hour or two on Monday and no day goes unchecked, and done once the row's `checked_at` is set. It shares the refresh's try, so a failure of either counts the account in `failed`, and the next hourly run tries again.
- Every committed invite adds one to a known count, a late commit's included, and the count written adds the invites committed since the check began, of any day. The 400 counts the account's invites in flight with the count (reserved, their hold not over; the gate's own aside), so invites in flight at 400 cannot each pass. An invite's commit and the count's write lock the account's row before the day's, the order applyHealth keeps; the write also names the pairing the check began with (`connected_at`) and the `connected` status, so a check that outlasts a reconnect writes nothing. The list stops past 400, enough to refuse, and the missed acceptances wait for a whole list.
- A declined invite is asked once: `relation_checked_at`, which the sources lack, marks each invite asked that the vendor neither lists nor shows connected, so it costs one read rather than one a day until its withdrawal. A row without a member ref is never asked; the read names the person. A missed acceptance publishes the receiver's `linkedin.invite_accepted`, so campaigns has one path, and `accepted_at` set in that run makes a later webhook publish nothing. A port error during the check is the account's as a read's: its row, `retry_at`, the health, the read committed.
- `remainingToday` answers no send while the disclosure or the member id would refuse one (Finding 56), and no invite while the count would, so campaigns' plan places no step the reservation refuses. `memberIdKnown` asks for `LINKEDIN_HASH_KEY` too: without it, an account whose hash was stored before is `member_id_unknown` as well. The refresh writes the member id only for the pairing it read (`connected_at`), so a reconnect during its call leaves `member_id_checked_at` to the next refresh.
- `@whytide/linkedin/testing` sets a test hash key on import, and `createConnectedAccount` a fresh count of 0, so the other modules' send tests change nothing.
- Who may pause and lift (gamma-p6-070) lives in `domain/access.ts`, and the kill switches take the caller's principal, so the routes, the list's flags and the safety cases ask one place; `setWorkspaceStop` asks `linkedin.manage` in the domain as the route does. `canPause` and `canUnpause` say what the caller may press now, `canEdit` that the account is theirs; `ownerName` comes from one `listMembers` read per request. The screen reads the role from `GET /v1/me` for "Stop everything", which has no account to carry a flag.
- A removed member's pause is a source of its own, `member_removed`, added alone in its migration (a value added in a transaction cannot be used in it); `WORKSPACE_STOP` names it for the shared enum's sake, never written for a workspace. The operator's pause takes it over as a tester's, and the operator's unpause leaves it (`paused_by_member_removed`): only its owner lifts it, once invited again.

## Open questions

- Statuses carry no time (Unipile's account statuses have none, nor the port's account events), so they apply in the order they arrive: a late `OK` can undo a newer `CREDENTIALS` until the next status or the hourly refresh. A `connected` for an account not connected could ask the worker's refresh, the vendor's status now, instead.
- An event about a LinkedIn account connected in two workspaces goes to the first whose outreach row it concerns, the receipt being global; beta-p1-070 (one LinkedIn account per row) prevents the case.
- Whether the reference's hash should be keyed (an HMAC), as the member id's and the receipts' are since beta-p1-070, rather than a plain SHA-256.
- Engager-list pages count one read each in alpha; the vendor's own accounting may say otherwise.
- Whether Refresh status needs a limit of its own: each press asks the provider once, under the api's limit per workspace (`RATE_LIMIT_WORKSPACE_PER_MIN`) only.
- The send hold is one lease of two hours; a lease renewed at each attempt, if a later change makes a send's retry span longer.
- `PROVIDER_ERROR_PAUSE_MS` (1 hour) applies to all three unknown outcomes, not only a vendor 5xx and a timeout; `ACCOUNT_COOLDOWN_MS` (72 hours) is the map's until beta-p6-010 keeps its own.
- The note budget reads `with_note` on the reservation as well as on the log row, so held notes count; the sources name the log only, and the `ok` rows are beta-p1-050's to write.
- `rate_limited` has no `linkedin.errors` key: the api's request protection registers that code, and a code is registered once.
- A send that goes out after its hold is still counted, and can pass the day's limit by one: the hold outlasts the retries so that it does not. A stale send reservation of an earlier day counts in the week until a sweep of that day frees it, and a day's row of a zone moved west can last more than 24 hours: both err on fewer sends.
- `weekly_invite_cap_reached`'s `retryAt` can fall on a weekend, when the next reservation answers `outside_working_hours`.
- The switches' state is read from Postgres at each reservation, with no cache (the beta prompt says "cached for seconds"); a cache, if ever added, must keep the 60 seconds of RB-23.1.3.
- Who may lift a pause, answered by gamma-p6-070: a tester's its owner or a holder of `linkedin.manage`, a removed member's its owner alone, the operator's the operator alone. `PUT /controls` takes no `Idempotency-Key`: it sets a state.
- A signals run stopped by a switch ends `failed` with the switch's code rather than waiting for the switch to lift, which may take days and would hold a workflow open.
- The operator's pause takes a tester's over, so after the operator's unpause the account runs although its tester had paused it: the runbook says to read `status` and ask the tester first; keeping both sources on the row is the alternative. The operator's unpause refuses a tester's pause; a `--force` would let the operator lift anything.
- `@whytide/db` could offer a transaction on the global client, so that `inGlobal`'s cast goes.
- The operator's pause, then the operator's unpause, of a removed member's account leaves it running, as with a tester's pause, since the row keeps one source: the runbook says to read `status` first. A removed member's account stays paused until its owner, invited again, resumes it: no Owner disconnects or lifts it.
- A changed `LINKEDIN_HASH_KEY` makes every account look replaced at its next refresh and lets a redelivered event be kept twice; a rotation would need the previous key too. On Unipile the member id comes with beta-p0-040: until then its accounts are refused `member_id_unknown`.
- A send's or a read's lost session (`linkedin_account_disconnected`, no warning reason) sets the health `disconnected` and leaves the session `status` `connected`; since beta-p6-040 a refresh that reads `connected` heals it. If the vendor keeps saying `connected` while the sends keep finding the session lost, each hourly refresh heals the account and the next send pauses it again, with a mail each time.
- A new account, and a replaced one, sends no invite before its first check, an hour or two after its first reservation (beta-p4-070); after a weekend an account of weekdays only waits for Monday's check likewise.
- An invite sent before the account's latest pairing is never asked about: after a reconnect of the same LinkedIn account, an acceptance the webhook missed before it stays unknown, and the acceptance rule counts it as not accepted.
- The acceptance rule counts every invite of the row, a replaced LinkedIn account's too, so a new LinkedIn account on the row inherits the rate until 100 invites of its own are 7 days old; reading the pairing would let a reconnect evade the rule.

## Health

The account's health (beta-p6-010; `domain/health/`, docs/concepts.md "Health", docs/flows.md "Health") sits beside its session `status`: `healthy`, `at_risk`, `restricted`, `checkpoint` or `disconnected`. `applyHealth` alone changes it, in the transaction of its cause, the account's row locked first: the state, one row of `sender_health_events` and the metric `linkedin.health_changes`.

- **The signals**: the webhook's `restricted` (trigger `limit_reached`) and `checkpoint` statuses; a send's, a check's or a read's typed error (`healthSignalOf`, A.19's account column: `linkedin_limit_reached` restricts, `linkedin_account_disconnected` gives `checkpoint`, `restricted` or a lost session by its reason); a status crossing into `credentials_required` or `disconnected` (a lost session, `session_invalid`), or into `connected`, a pairing, and a refresh that reads `connected` (a reconnect, `manual`: `disconnected` to `healthy`, beta-p6-040); the owner's resume. The user's own disconnect changes no health.
- **The cool-down**: `restricted` and `checkpoint` stop every action until `paused_until`, 72 hours after the warning (`COOLDOWN_HOURS`); a later warning moves it later, never earlier; a disconnect and a reconnect leave it. Only the owner's resume lifts it (beta-p6-040).
- **The owner's resume** (`resume.ts`, beta-p6-040; RB-11.3.1 R5): `POST /accounts/{id}/resume` ("API"), the account's owner alone, refused in this order: `not_account_owner`; `account_not_paused` unless `restricted` or `checkpoint`; `cooldown_active` before `paused_until`, or with none; `linkedin_account_disconnected` unless the status is `connected`; after a checkpoint, `reconnect_required` until a pairing since the latest warning (`connected_at` after `paused_until` less the 72 hours: a warning on a cool-down moves its end, not `health_changed_at`, so each checkpoint wants its own reconnect); `status_check_required` unless the worker read the status after the cool-down's end and at most 10 minutes ago (`RESUME_STATUS_MAX_AGE_MINUTES`; the api never opens the reference, so "Check status" asks the worker). Then, in one run, the account's row locked before the day's: `healthy` through `applyHealth` (trigger `manual`, the event acknowledged by the owner, no mail, the metric), every pausing event still open acknowledged by the owner, the targets halved as the idle restart halves them (`halvedTargets`: 15 and 40 become 7 and 20, 5 and 10 stay) with `ramp_updated_on` today, so the next rise waits its days, and today's send limits lowered to the smaller of the stored ones and a draw from the new targets, the read allowance kept. The fleet breaker stays as it is: one account's resume reopens no other's invites. `GET /accounts/{id}/health` answers the card's view, `resumeRefusal`'s code as `resumeBlockedBy`.
- **A lost session and a reconnect**: a reconnect, or a refresh that reads `connected`, returns a `disconnected` account to `healthy`, an `at_risk` one included: it stays `healthy` until the next day's check applies the acceptance rule again.
- **The third error**: a `linkedin_rate_limited` or `linkedin_provider_error` of a third distinct call of an account within 60 minutes, sends', checks' and reads' alike, sets `retry_at` to the start of its next working day (Monday to Friday, in its zone) with one health event, `rate_limit_errors` with `{ errors, windowMinutes }`, `errors` the calls; the health state, a cool-down and the platform's switches stay as they are. A call is its rows' `call_ref` (a signals read's `rawKey`, a send's step execution), so the attempts and retries of one read or one send count once, and one profile the provider cannot read, tried three times, pauses nothing; a row without one, a status check's or a check's read, is its own call (the coordinator's decision for the owner, 2026-10-30).
- **The acceptance rule** (`acceptance.ts`, beta-p6-020; RB-11.3.1, reduced): the last step of the day's check ("Worker"), so once a day, with no call and no read of its own. `acceptanceOf` takes the account's newest 100 invites (`ACCEPTANCE_SAMPLE`) at least 7 days old (`ACCEPTANCE_MIN_AGE_DAYS`), the platform's own (`sender_outreach`), and those of them accepted, a withdrawn one never; under 100 there is no rate and nothing changes. Under 20 percent (`ACCEPTANCE_MIN`, `constants.ts`) a `healthy` account goes `at_risk` (trigger `acceptance_rate`, metrics `{ sample, accepted, rate }`, `paused_invites`, the owner's mail): its connection requests are refused `invites_paused`, its messages, withdrawals and reads go on, and the fleet breaker stays as it is. At 20 percent or more, at a later day's check, an `at_risk` account goes back to `healthy` (`resumed`, no mail). Any other state stays whatever the rate: a paused or disconnected account's check is refused its reads before the rule. An acceptance the same check finds counts that day.
- **The refusals**: `sendStateRefusal` and `readStateRefusal` answer `account_paused` (`retryAt` null) right after the status for `restricted`, `checkpoint` and `disconnected`; an `at_risk` account's invites are `invites_paused`. `remainingToday` answers nothing while the account is paused or its `retry_at` lies ahead (Finding 56). A pause releases the account's open send reservations, but one held past its two hours for a send whose outcome is unknown, which its check settles (else a reconnect the same day could take the slot again before that send's late commit), and one settled since; the ramp moves no account that is not `healthy`.
- **The owner's mail**: a change into `restricted`, `checkpoint`, `disconnected` or `at_risk`, and the third error, publish `linkedin.account_paused { sender_account_id, health_event_id }`; its subscriber starts `linkedin_notify` by the id `linkedin.notify:<workspaceId>:<healthEventId>:owner`, whose activity `linkedin_mail_health` mails the account's owner through the app-mail port, in their locale: what happened, what stops (the subject by the trigger), until when, what to do, and the LinkedIn page's link while `APP_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` is set. `owner_notified_at` stops a retry once the mail went; a missing or disabled owner, or one no longer an active member of the workspace (gamma-p6-070, tenancy's `resolveMembership`), gets none (`owner_unavailable`), with no error.
- **The fleet breaker** (`breaker.ts`; RB-11.3.2, reduced for beta): a change into `restricted` or `checkpoint`, whatever its trigger, trips it in the same run. Where invites are not paused, the platform's row, made when missing, gets `invites_paused` true, `invites_paused_reason` the trigger and `changed_by` `system`; only then come one control event (`platform`, `invites`, `off` to `on`, `actor_type` `system`, actor `fleet_breaker`, the trigger, the account's workspace and id) and `linkedin.invites_paused { sender_account_id, health_event_id }`. Every account of the instance is refused its next connection request (`invites_paused`); messages, withdrawals and reads go on. `at_risk`, a lost session and the third error trip nothing; a second warning, or two at once, change nothing more. Only the operator's `breaker reopen` lifts it ("The control command"), never the tester's switches, the operator's others, a reconnect or the owner's resume.
- **The operator's mail**: `linkedin.invites_paused`'s subscriber starts `linkedin_notify` by the id `linkedin.notify:<workspaceId>:<healthEventId>:operator`, whose activity mails `LINKEDIN_ALERT_EMAIL` in the instance's default locale: the workspace's and the account's ids, the trigger, the time in UTC and the reopen command, never the account's label, its reference or a person's data. `operator_notified_at` stops a retry once it went. With the address empty, the run answers `no_address` and the worker logs `linkedin_alert_email_missing` (warn, with `health_event_id`).

## Company pages

`readCompany({ companyUrl }, { workspaceId, tenant })` (gamma-en-060, `domain/company/`), exported for enrichment's `linkedin` source and called from its activity in the worker: a company page's business facts through one of the workspace's accounts, whoever's lead it is, since a page read sends nothing and shows nothing to the company. It answers `{ status: "read", company }` (the port's `LinkedinCompany`) or `{ status }`: `no_account` (no connected account), `no_reads_left` (none with company reads left and its window open now) or `not_found` (no such page, or none the account can see).

1. **The account:** among the connected ones, those whose `readsLeftToday` leaves reads with the window open, the most company reads left first (the smaller of those reads and the share's rest); a kill switch, a health pause or `retry_at` ahead leave none.
2. **The reservation:** one `read_company` read (`reserveReads`), counted in the account's daily reads and its 200 actions as `read_profile` is, and refused past the share; a refused reservation moves on to the next account.
3. **The call:** the port's `getCompany`, outside any run, the reference opened by the worker's decrypt entry (`withDecryptedRef`), which `workflows/index.ts` registers as the worker loads it (`useRefOpener`). The api never loads it: there `readCompany` is `company_read_unavailable`.
4. **The settlement:** a call made, `ok` or its port code, is logged as a `read_company` row with no object, its error's health checked ("Health"), and the read committed; a failure before any call releases the read and is thrown. `linkedin_not_found` answers `not_found`; any other port error is thrown.

**The share:** `LINKEDIN_COMPANY_READS_PER_DAY` (20, 0 to 100; `config.ts`, the `modules/linkedin` block of `deploy/.env.example`), the most `read_company` reads an account makes in a quota day, inside its daily reads, never beside them: signals' reads keep the rest. 0 turns company reads off. What is used is the day's `read_company` reservations, from the action log's `reserved` rows since the quota day began in its zone, read under the day's lock; a reservation released without a call still counts, the safe side. `useCompanyReadShare(n)` sets it in a test.

The page's link and its facts go to no log line, span, metric label or action-log row. Tests: `tests/read-company.test.ts`; the source in a run, `core/apps/worker/tests/enrichment-run.test.ts`.

## Contact info

`readContactEmails({ personId }, { workspaceId, tenant })` (gamma-en-087, `domain/contact/`), exported for enrichment's email source `linkedin-contact-info` and called from its lookup activity in the worker: the email addresses a lead shows their 1st-degree connections in LinkedIn's "Contact info". It answers `{ status: "read", emails }` (lower case, three at most, none for a lead who shows none) or `{ status }`:

1. **The switch:** `LINKEDIN_CONTACT_INFO_ENABLED` (`false` by default; `config.ts`, the `modules/linkedin` block of `deploy/.env.example`), read through `contactInfoEnabled()`, exported. Off, `disabled`, before anything else: no read reserved, no row, no call.
2. **The account:** among the workspace's connected accounts, those whose invitation the person accepted (`sender_outreach.accepted_at`, with the member's ref on the row): none, `not_connected`, with no call. Of those, the ones whose `readsLeftToday` leaves reads with the window open, the most reads left first; a kill switch, a health pause or `retry_at` ahead leave none: `no_reads_left`.
3. **The reservation:** one `read_profile` read (`reserveReads`, the person's id as its object), in the account's daily reads ("Read quota"); a refused reservation moves on to the next account.
4. **The call:** the port's one exception, outside any run, the reference opened by the worker's decrypt entry, as for company pages ("Company pages"). Outside the worker, `contact_read_unavailable`. This file is the method's only caller: the providers' `tests/safety/port-never-list.test.ts` fails on any other.

The addresses reach no log line, span, metric label, action-log row or event here: enrichment keeps one only once a verifier calls it valid. Switching it off stops new reads only; nothing found earlier is deleted. `useContactInfoEnabled(on)` sets the switch in a test. Tests: `tests/read-contact-emails.test.ts`; the source in a lookup, `core/apps/worker/tests/enrichment-lookup.test.ts`.
