# linkedin: data

Four tenant tables in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/linkedin.prisma`, created by the migrations `20261022182000_linkedin_sender_accounts` and `20261022191500_linkedin_quota_and_log` (its CHECKs: no negative count, `quota_day` a date). Ids are `sac_`, `squ_`, `sqr_` and `sal_` `<ULID>` (`ids.ts`); times are `timestamptz`.

| Table | Columns | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| `sender_accounts` | `workspace_id`, `owner_user_id`, `label`, `provider` (the adapter's name), `provider_account_ref` (bytea, sealed, null until connected and after a user's disconnect), `provider_account_ref_hash` (hex SHA-256 of `<provider>:<reference>`), `provider_link_ref` (the pending hosted link's id), `status` (`sender_account_status`), `status_checked_at`, `time_zone`, `working_hours` (jsonb), `daily_read_cap` (100; 1 to `LINKEDIN_DAILY_READ_CAP_MAX` through PATCH), `disclosure_acknowledged_at`, `connected_at`, `disconnected_at` | unique `(workspace_id, owner_user_id)` and `(workspace_id, provider_account_ref_hash)`; indexes on the hash and on `provider_link_ref`, the webhook's lookups across workspaces |
| `sender_quota_usage` | `workspace_id`, `sender_account_id`, `quota_day` (text `YYYY-MM-DD`, the account's zone), `reads_reserved`, `reads_committed` | unique `(workspace_id, sender_account_id, quota_day)`: the row reservations lock |
| `sender_quota_reservations` | `workspace_id`, `sender_account_id`, `usage_id` (the day's row), `count`, `committed` (the reads made), `status` (`reserved`, `committed`, `released`, `expired`), `expires_at` (an hour on), `created_at`, `settled_at` | index `(workspace_id, usage_id, status, expires_at)`, the sweep's |
| `sender_action_log` | `workspace_id`, `sender_account_id`, `kind` (`sender_action_kind`), `object_ref` (pii: a post's URL names its author; or a person's id), `count`, `outcome`, `at` | index `(workspace_id, sender_account_id, at)` |

No foreign key to `workspaces` or `users`. Personal data: none of a lead's. The reference names the user's own LinkedIn account at the provider (PII class personal, provenance: the provider's webhook), sealed under `APP_MASTER_KEY` with the organization and the purpose `linkedin_account`. The label is the user's. `status_checked_at` is the last time the webhook or a refresh stored the provider's status. The events, in the platform's outbox, carry `{ sender_account_id }` only: `linkedin.sender_account_connected`, `linkedin.sender_account_disconnected` and `linkedin.status_refresh_requested`. A user's disconnect deletes the reference and its hash and keeps the row; nothing is deleted otherwise in alpha, the action log included (its 90 days come later).
