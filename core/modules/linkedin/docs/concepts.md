# linkedin: concepts

- **Sender account**: a user's own LinkedIn account in one workspace, a row of `sender_accounts`, connected through the provider's hosted page. One per user and workspace. Never a shared, rented or fake account, never a password or a cookie.
- **Risk disclosure**: what the tester reads and accepts before Connect, with two boxes: Whytide reads LinkedIn through their own account and sends from it only the connection requests and messages they approve, one by one, within fixed daily limits and working hours; the account must be their own personal one, used by them alone; LinkedIn may still restrict an account it finds automated. The text (`linkedin.connect.disclosure`) is a draft until counsel's replaces it before gamma. Two acknowledgements, kept apart: `disclosure_acknowledged_at`, alpha's, covers reads; `disclosure_version` and `personal_account_confirmed_at` cover sends. The api refuses a connect without both boxes (`disclosure_required`, `personal_account_required`).
- **Disclosure version**: `DISCLOSURE_VERSION` (2, `domain/disclosure/constants.ts`). An account below it, as every account connected in alpha is, or without the personal-account confirmation, reads but is refused every send `disclosure_required` until its owner accepts the current text on the screen; only the owner accepts (`not_account_owner`). A new text raises the constant, and every owner accepts again.
- **One LinkedIn account, one row**: the account's own LinkedIn member id, which the worker's refresh reads after each connect, is kept only as its keyed hash, `member_id_hash`, unique among connected rows in every workspace; until it is stored, and after each connect until the refresh has read it again (`member_id_checked_at`), the account sends nothing (`member_id_unknown`): a reconnected row still holds the previous LinkedIn account's hash. The same LinkedIn account on a second row ends that row `disconnected` (`sender_account_in_use`); a reconnect that brings another LinkedIn account replaces the row's: its targets back to 5 and 10, the disclosure to accept again, `linkedin.account_replaced`.
- **Status**: `pending` (waiting for the provider's webhook), `connected`, `credentials_required` (the user signs in again at the provider), `error`, `disconnected`. Only the provider's webhook, or the worker's refresh, sets `connected`.
- **Status refresh**: the worker asks the provider for an account's status: every hour for each account with a reference, the catch-up for a webhook that never came, and at once for one account when its user presses Refresh status.
- **Reference**: the provider's id of the account, what every read names. Sealed at rest for the workspace's organization, opened in the worker only; its hash finds the account when a webhook names it.
- **Working hours**: local `HH:MM` start and end in the account's IANA time zone and the ISO days (1 is Monday) the reads may run on. The start is before the end, the days distinct, one at least.
- **Daily read cap**: the reads a day at most, 100 by default: a profile counts one, a page of a post's engagers one. A member with `linkedin.manage` changes it on the screen, as the "Daily read limit", from 1 to `LINKEDIN_DAILY_READ_CAP_MAX` (100 by default and at most), under a warning; a lowered cap holds from the next reservation, a raised one from the next quota day (the read allowance).
- **Quota day**: the account's date in its time zone, `YYYY-MM-DD`; it turns at local midnight. Its usage row holds the day's limits, drawn when it is made, the zone it was made in, and per action type the count reserved and the count made (committed) that day.
- **Zone change**: takes effect from the next local day. The day's row stays the account's day until its date is over in the zone it was made in and in the account's zone now, so a move east opens no second day's budgets within 24 hours and a move west goes back to no earlier date; a date already used keeps its row.
- **Window**: the working hours in the account's zone, open from `start` until before `end` on the working days. Reads and sends run only inside it; outside, a reservation is refused with the next start.
- **Weekend rule**: nothing is sent on Saturday or Sunday in the account's zone, whatever days it lists (`WEEKEND_DAYS`): a send's window is the working hours' weekdays, and `remainingToday` answers no send then. Reads keep the account's own days.
- **`retry_at`**: on the account, the time before which nothing is sent or read: the provider's rate limit, or the pause after a send whose outcome is unknown (`PROVIDER_ERROR_PAUSE_MS`, an hour); beta-p1-050 sets it.
- **Budgets**: per account and quota day, one per action type: connection requests (invites), messages, withdrawals and reads. What is left of each is its limit less what is reserved and made, within the week's connection requests and the day's 200 actions (`limitsFor`; `remainingToday` for campaigns). The reservations spend them.
- **Targets**: `invite_target` (5 to 15) and `message_target` (10 to 40), the account's connection requests and messages a day before the draw; they start at 5 and 10, and the ramp raises them.
- **Ramp**: once a quota day, the daily job raises an account's targets by its sending days, not the calendar's: a step every 2 quota days at least (`RAMP_EVERY_DAYS`), `invite_target` by 2 and `message_target` by 5 (`RAMP_INVITE_STEP`, `RAMP_MESSAGE_STEP`, `domain/ramp/constants.ts`), each only when a send of its type was committed on a quota day since the last step (`ramp_updated_on`, today's included), up to 15 and 40: 10 sending days from 5 to 15, 12 from 10 to 40. Nothing rises while sending is closed, a kill switch holds, the account is not `connected` or its health is not `healthy`; one that sends only messages keeps its connection requests' target. A second run the same day changes nothing.
- **Idle restart**: an account with no connection request or message committed in the 14 quota days before today (`RAMP_IDLE_DAYS`) restarts at half its final targets, rounded down, 7 and 20 (`RAMP_RESTART_SHARE`), never above where it was nor below 5 and 10, and its last step becomes today; the ramp goes on from there once it sends again. It is asked first and only ever lowers, so an account that could not act for 14 quota days or more (closed, stopped, paused, not connected, not healthy) reopens at half its targets.
- **Daily draw**: when the day's row is made, by the first reservation, `remainingToday` or the usage route of the day, its limits are drawn once: each target times a uniform draw from 80 to 100 percent, rounded down, each whole number of that range as likely, never above the hard maximum. A second insert of the same day keeps the first draw. `check_after`, a random instant in the first two hours of what is left of the day's window, never in its last hour, is drawn with them.
- **Read allowance**: the day's reads at most, drawn with the row: the account's read cap, at most 100 and at most 200 less the two limits and the 15 withdrawals, so sends get their share first. Reads stop at the smaller of the allowance and the cap as it stands.
- **Week**: connection requests over the last seven quota days, the day's included: at most 100 (`INVITES_PER_WEEK.cap`).
- **The 200**: every action of a day, reserved and made, reads included, at most 200 (`ACTIONS_PER_DAY_MAX`); past it, a reservation is refused `daily_actions_cap_reached` until the next quota day.
- **Hard maximums**: 25 connection requests and 80 messages a day, 200 connection requests a week, 200 actions, 100 reads and 15 withdrawals a day. They live in `domain/quota/constants.ts`, frozen, and in the database's CHECKs, never in a setting: no setting, route, script or operator raises them.
- **Reservation**: `count` reads held against the day's read limit and the 200 before they run, all or none, for an hour; committed with the reads made, or released. Past its hold, the next reservation of the day frees it (the lazy sweep).
- **Send reservation**: one connection request, message or withdrawal held against the day's budgets before the port call, under the step's idempotency key: one reservation per key is reserved or committed at a time, so a retry takes no second slot. Committed once the send went out, after a release or past its hold too; released when it did not. The account's state refuses first (`sendStateRefusal`: the sending gates and the kill switches, not connected, the health, the disclosure, an invite's pending invitations, the member id, `retry_at`, the window), then the note budget, the day's limit of the type, the week and the 200.
- **Hold**: how long a reservation keeps its slot: an hour for reads, two hours for a send (`SEND_RESERVATION_MS`), longer than a send activity's retries, so the last attempt still finds its slot.
- **Committed late**: a send reservation committed after its release or its hold's end while another reservation holds its key: counted as made, and outside the one live reservation per key.
- **Note budget**: three invitations with a note in 30 days (`INVITE_NOTES_PER_MONTH`, `INVITE_NOTE_WINDOW_DAYS`): the `ok` invite rows with a note and the held invite reservations with one; a fourth waits until the oldest leaves the 30 days. An invite without a note does not count.
- **Error map**: `SEND_ERROR_MAP`, what each answer of a send does to its slot, its credits, its step and its account (docs/flows.md "The error map").
- **Action log**: a row per reservation (its answer), per read made, per send and per status check: the kind (`read_engagers`, `read_profile`, `send_check`, `pending_invites`, `relation_check`, `invite`, `message`, `withdraw`, `status_check`), the object (a post's URL or a person's id; a send's step execution; never a profile's URL), the count, the outcome, one of `ACTION_OUTCOMES`, and the note of an invite. Rows are never updated. The metric `linkedin.actions` counts linkedin's own activities' rows by kind and outcome, never the object.
- **Outreach row**: a person an account invited or messaged, a row of `sender_outreach` per account and person: the person's member id and chat at the vendor, and the times of the invitation, the acceptance, the last message sent and the last one received, and of the day's check's question about an invite the vendor no longer lists (`relation_checked_at`). Sends write it (beta-p1-050); the receiver keeps only the events an outreach row concerns. `last_inbound_at` is the reply signal campaigns' gate reads (`lastInboundAt`).
- **Event receipt**: a kept person event's row in `linkedin_event_receipts`, by its key's keyed hash, written in the transaction that publishes the event: a redelivery finds it and stops.
- **Kill switches**: each stops every LinkedIn action of its scope, reads included, from the next reservation, with `retryAt` null: the instance's stop (`platform_stopped`, the operator's alone), a workspace's "Stop everything" (`workspace_stopped`) and an account's pause (`account_stopped`, its `kill_switch`). A workspace's or an account's is off, or held by the tester or by the operator. A switch is not a health state: the account keeps its status.
- **Sending gates**: each stops sends only, never a read: the instance's `sending_open`, closed on a fresh instance, which has no row, until the operator opens it (`sending_closed`, beta-p1-060), and `invites_paused`, which refuses connection requests and lets messages and withdrawals through (the breaker's, beta-p6-030). The disclosure, the member id, the health and the pending invitations add theirs.
- **Who lifts what**: any member with `linkedin.manage` pauses an account, and only its owner resumes it (`not_account_owner`); any member with `linkedin.manage` stops the workspace and lifts the stop. A stop the operator holds, an account's, a workspace's or the instance's, only the operator lifts: a tester's press answers `stopped_by_operator` and changes nothing. The operator's stop takes a tester's over. The fleet breaker's pause only the operator's `breaker reopen` lifts.
- **Control event**: a row of `linkedin_control_events` per change of a switch or a gate: its scope, its target, the state before and after, who changed it (a user, the operator by name, the system) and when. A call that changes nothing writes none; no row is ever changed or deleted.
- **Health**: the account's state beside its session `status` (`domain/health/`): `healthy`; `at_risk`, its connection requests paused while fewer than 20 percent of its last 100 invites at least 7 days old were accepted (the acceptance rule, at the day's check, which lifts it by itself at 20 percent or more); `restricted` and `checkpoint`, every action stopped until the cool-down's end and the owner's resume; `disconnected`, every action stopped until a reconnect. Changed only by `applyHealth`, in the transaction of its cause, with a health event.
- **Fleet breaker**: a restriction or a checkpoint on any account pauses the connection requests of every account of the instance (`invites_paused`, its reason the trigger) until the operator reopens them; the operator gets one mail per trip.
- **Health trigger**: why the health changed: `limit_reached` (LinkedIn restricted the account or its weekly invitation limit was reached), `checkpoint`, `session_invalid` (the session ended), `manual` (a reconnect, later the owner's resume), `rate_limit_errors` (the third error), `acceptance_rate` (beta-p6-020).
- **Cool-down**: `paused_until`, 72 hours after a restriction or a checkpoint (`COOLDOWN_HOURS`), moved later by a later warning, never earlier; a disconnect and a reconnect keep it (C26).
- **The third error**: a rate limit or provider error of the third distinct call of an account within 60 minutes (`PROVIDER_ERRORS_PER_HOUR_MAX`, `PROVIDER_ERROR_WINDOW_MINUTES`; the attempts of one read or one send are one call, `call_ref`) moves its `retry_at` to the start of its next working day, with a health event and the owner's mail; the health state stays.
- **Health event**: a row of `sender_health_events` per change, and per third error: the state before and after, the trigger, the numbers (`metrics`, never a person's data), the action (`paused_all_actions`, `paused_invites`, `resumed`), the pause's end, whether the owner must acknowledge it, and when the owner's mail went (`owner_notified_at`).
- **Pending invitations**: the account's connection requests LinkedIn still lists as pending, those its owner sends by hand included: `pending_invites`, counted by the day's check and raised by one with each committed invite until the next check; null until the first check, and again after a replacement. Above 400 with the invites in flight (`PENDING_INVITES_MAX`, RB-11.2.5), and while the count is null or older than 48 hours (`PENDING_INVITES_FRESH_HOURS`), the account sends no connection request (`pending_invites_over_limit`, `pending_invites_unknown`): failing closed. Its messages and withdrawals go on.
- **The day's check**: once a quota day, inside the hourly status refresh, after the day row's `check_after`, so at another time each day: the pending invitations counted, a read per page, then each invite since the latest pairing that the vendor no longer lists asked once (`relation_check`): connected, an acceptance the webhook missed, published as the receiver publishes one; neither connected nor pending, an invite declined or withdrawn on LinkedIn, never asked again. Last, the acceptance rule, with no call of its own.
