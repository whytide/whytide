# signals

Where leads come from: a workspace's signal sources, each of a kind, the runs that read them into staged candidates, and the processing that makes those people, leads, events and evidence, or a salted hash. A kind is a folder under `sources/` that declares its config, its schedule, its reader, its event and its evidence: `post_engagers`; `topic_engagement` and `my_post_engagement` (gamma-p5-020); `competitor_engagement` and `creator_engagement` (gamma-p5-025); `web_research` (gamma-wr-030), a source with no LinkedIn account.

- **Owns:** `signal_sources`, `signal_source_imports`, `signal_runs`, `signal_candidates`, `signal_events` and `evidence` in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/signals.prisma` ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)), the registry of source kinds, the workflows `signals_run` and `signals_research_run` (gamma-wr-030) and each source's schedule `signals-<sourceId>`, the daily runs (`signals_daily_runs_tick`, its step and the schedule `signals.daily_runs`), the evidence reduction (`signals_evidence_reduction`, its daily tick and the schedule `signals.evidence_reduction`) and `pnpm evidence:reduce`, the screen `ui/sources` and the lead page's evidence panel `ui/lead-evidence`.
- **Public interface:** `index.ts`: `api`, which the api host mounts; `events`; `erasePerson(personId)`, in the caller's tenant run, which deletes the person's events and evidence, scrubs where signals names them (Reduction), and returns the rows deleted by table, the deletion script's step (core/modules/privacy); `listEvidence(personId)`, in the caller's tenant run, the person's events, newest first, each with its evidence row or null, or contacts' `person_not_found` (the evidence route's read, and messaging's dossier's); `personReads(personId)`, in the caller's tenant run, the runs the person has events from, by run id, each `{ runId, candidates, personCandidates, candidateIds }`: the candidates the run staged (its counts' `staged`, never fewer than the person's), the person's events from it, and their candidates' ids, an event's id carrying its candidate's ULID since beta-p1-090 (credits' cost counter, alpha-p10-010); the registries `errors`, `ids` (`src`, `run`, `cand`, `sev`, `evd`), `permissions` (`signals.read`, `signals.write`, `signals.run`), `prices` (`signals.engager`, `signals.engager_read`) and `settings`; `workflows/` for the worker.
- **Depends on:** `platform`, `db`, `providers` (files); `linkedin` (its accounts, its read quota and its activities `linkedin_read_posts`, `linkedin_read_post_engagers` and `linkedin_read_profile`); `contacts` (persons, companies, leads, hashes, `countryOf`), `icp`, `playbooks` (its agents' links to sources, read for the limit, the share, a run's audience and the list, `detachSource` at a delete, and the source checker signals registers; gamma-p5-045), `qualification` (its hard filters, its title rule, its batch and its re-score request), `onboarding` (the company's domain) and `tenancy` (its names); credits through the platform's port; the screen on `@whytide/ui` and the web's API client, through which it reads linkedin's accounts and an account's usage.
- **Reviewers:** the owners of this module.

## API

Under `/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/signals`; requests in `api-calls/alpha-p8-010-source-kind-registry.http`, `api-calls/alpha-p8-020-signal-run.http` (Run now and Stop), `api-calls/alpha-p8-030-evidence.http` and `api-calls/sources-csv-import.http`, `api-calls/gamma-wr-110-found-companies.http` (the found companies) (the source imports). A source is answered as its row, without `workspace_id` and the reader's `watermark`.

- The kind is checked against the registry on every write, the config against the kind's schema at the create, and the stored config is what the schema returns.
- Every source answered carries `fireTimes`, its kind's fire times from the save that come after its `lastRunAt` (all of them before its first run), oldest first, and `nextRunAt`, the first of them or null; a disabled source has `[]` and null (`domain/fire-times.ts`). alpha-p8-020 sets `lastRunAt` when a run starts.
- Every source answered carries `runOpen`: one of its runs is `running` or `waiting`, and run now answers `signal_run_open` until it ends; a `scheduled` run is not open. And `openRun`, that run as `{ id, status, stopRequestedAt }`, null when none is open; a scheduled run starts whatever is open, so of two, the running one, else the newest. A run answered carries `stopRequestedAt`, null until a stop is asked.
- `senderAccountId` must name a sender account of the workspace (linkedin's `findSenderAccount`), connected or not; a run checks that it is connected. It must be the caller's own unless she holds `signals.manage` (gamma-p6-015): another member's is 403 `forbidden`, before anything is written, on a create, an import and the import's check (`assertSenderAccount`).
- Who writes a source (gamma-p6-015): every role holds `signals.write` and `signals.run`, and `PATCH`, `DELETE`, `POST …/run` and `POST …/runs/{runId}/stop` then ask platform's `canWriteOwned` about the source (`writableSource`): its creator, `createdBy`, writes it, a holder of `signals.manage` (Owner, Admin) writes every one, and anyone else is 403 `forbidden`, before anything is written. A source not found is 404 first. The create and the import make the caller the creator.
- Every source answered carries `playbook`, its agent (playbooks' link) as `{ id, name }`, or null for a source in no agent; a new source is in no agent, and is linked on the agent's page (gamma-p5-045).
- At most `SIGNALS_MAX_ENABLED_SOURCES` are enabled in each group (gamma-p5-045): an agent's sources, and the sources in no agent; and at most `SIGNALS_MAX_TRACKED_PER_ACCOUNT` competitor and creator sources per sender account (RB-4.2.1), counted first, across the groups. A create counts the sources in no agent, an enable its source's group, `signal_sources_limit` with `details.limit` past it. A create or an enable takes a transaction-level advisory lock on the workspace before it counts, so two at once cannot both pass.
- Events, ids only, in the transaction of the change: `signals.source_created`, `signals.source_updated` and `signals.source_deleted`, `{ source_id }`, the deleted one with `workflow_ids` when it had open runs; `signals.run_requested`, `signals.run_stop_requested` and `signals.source_blocked`, `{ source_id, run_id }`; `signals.person_found`, `{ workspaceId, personIds }` (below).
- A Web research source (gamma-wr-030): `POST /sources` takes no `senderAccountId` for it, and answers it with `senderAccountId` null; one given is `signal_sender_account_invalid` (400, `details.fields: ["senderAccountId"]`), and a LinkedIn kind's missing one is the same refusal. `config` of this kind is `focus`, `region`, `languages` and `startPages` (Kinds), `signal_config_invalid` naming `config.focus`, `config.region`, `config.languages` or `config.startPages`. `PATCH /sources/{id}` takes `{ config }` for a kind that allows it, this one: the kind's schema reads it, the result is stored and answered, and the same refusals apply; for any other kind `config` is `signal_config_invalid` naming `config`. Who may write a source does not change. Every source answered carries `cadence` (`daily`, `research` or null) and `everyDays` (`SIGNALS_RESEARCH_EVERY_DAYS` for `research`, else null). `GET /sources/{id}/research-plan` (`signals.read`; gamma-wr-041) answers `{ plan }`: the source's newest plan, with its `id`, `runId`, `madeAt`, `words` (by language) and `placeIds`, or `{ plan: null }` before any; another workspace's source is `signal_source_not_found`. Requests: `api-calls/alpha-p8-010-source-kind-registry.http`.

## Kinds

`post_engagers`: the people who reacted to, commented on or reposted one LinkedIn post. Config `{ postUrl }`: https on `linkedin.com` or `www.linkedin.com`, `/posts/<author>_<words>-activity-<id>-<code>` or `/feed/update/urn:li:activity:<id>`, with `share` or `ugcPost` in place of `activity` too; stored as `https://www.linkedin.com<path>`, without the query or the fragment. A short link from the Share button, `https://lnkd.in/p/<code>` or `https://lnkd.in/<code>`, is resolved at the create: one GET to it through `fetchSafe`, whose redirect is answered unfollowed, and its `Location`, when it is one of those post links, is stored the same way (`sources/post_engagers/post-url.ts`, its bounds in `constants.ts`). It runs at save, then three and seven days later, reading back `SIGNALS_POST_ENGAGERS_LOOKBACK_DAYS`, and only within that lookback of its save: a fire time past it is dropped, and Run now after it is `signal_source_expired`. Its reader is linkedin's `linkedin_read_post_engagers`, 25 engagers a page.

`topic_engagement`: the posts that hold an exact phrase, and their author, commenters and reactors. Config `{ phrase, roles }`: 1 to 5 words, 100 characters at most, stored with its words one space apart; `roles` a non-empty subset of `author`, `commenter` (a comment) and `reactor` (a like or a repost), all three by default, stored in that order. A phrase that holds a term of `BLOCKED_TOPIC_TERMS` (`sources/topic_engagement/constants.ts`: health, religion, politics, sexual orientation, ethnicity, union membership; whole words, in any case, a plural `s` too) is `signal_topic_blocked`: nothing is stored or searched. It reads back `SIGNALS_TOPIC_LOOKBACK_DAYS`.

`my_post_engagement`: the engagers of the sender account's own posts, config `{}`, reading back `SIGNALS_MY_POST_LOOKBACK_DAYS`; their author is not kept.

`competitor_engagement`: the engagers of a company page's posts, a competitor's. Config `{ companyUrl, companyName }`: the page, https on `linkedin.com` or `www.linkedin.com`, `/company/<id>` (providers' `AUTHOR_PATHS`), stored as `https://www.linkedin.com/company/<id>`, without the query, the fragment or a trailing slash (`sources/_authors.ts`); the name the tester gives, 2 to 100 characters, stored with its words one space apart (`sources/competitor_engagement/constants.ts`), since the port reads no company; gamma-p5-030 reads both fields by these names. It reads back `SIGNALS_COMPETITOR_LOOKBACK_DAYS`; a company's post has no author candidate. On the screen, the form asks for the page's URL and the name, and the card shows the name, linking the page.

`creator_engagement`: the engagers of one member's posts, a creator's. Config `{ profileUrl }`: `/in/<id>` on the same hosts, stored as `https://www.linkedin.com/in/<id>` the same way, its id percent-encoded. It reads back `SIGNALS_CREATOR_LOOKBACK_DAYS`; the creator's own posts are not leads, so their author is not kept. Erasing the creator disables the source (Reduction). On the screen, the form asks for the profile's URL, and the card links it, or says its target was erased.

The four are posts kinds: they run at their save, then once a day inside their share of the account's reads (Runs), read the posts with linkedin's `linkedin_read_posts`, 10 a page, then each post's engagers, 25 a page (Runs); the competitor and creator kinds read their author's posts, `{ by: "author", authorUrl }`, and keep every reaction. At their create, `signal_config_invalid` names `config.companyUrl`, `config.companyName` or `config.profileUrl`, which the form shows under the field; past `SIGNALS_MAX_TRACKED_PER_ACCOUNT` enabled ones on the sender account, a create or an enable is `signal_tracked_limit` (Settings), which the form shows in words. A page or a profile that is gone, or that the account cannot see, is a posts read refused `linkedin_not_found`: the run ends `failed` with that code and keeps the watermark, and the runs panel says so. An engager whose `dateKnown` is false (Unipile's reactions), or a reaction without it (a page before gamma-p5-010), is dated at the later of its post's date (post engagers: the source's save) and the start of the source's last done run, never later than the listing.

`web_research` (gamma-wr-030): what to look for on the web, with no LinkedIn account (the kind's `readsThroughAccount` is `false`, its reader `{ research: true }`, its cadence `research`). Config `{ focus, region?, languages?, startPages? }`: `focus` 3 to 300 characters, stored with its words one space apart; `region` up to 100 characters, left out when empty; `languages` up to 3 distinct ISO 639-1 codes from the kind's own list (`sources/web_research/constants.ts`, every code of the standard, so that it names every language and favours none; the screen names each one with `Intl.DisplayNames`), lower-cased, left out when empty; `startPages` up to 5 distinct https addresses, with no user, password or port, never `linkedin.com`, `lnkd.in`, a social network (`NEVER_FETCHED_HOSTS`) or a subdomain of one, stored as the URL without its fragment. Each refusal is `signal_config_invalid` naming its field. Its run plans since gamma-wr-040, searches since gamma-wr-041 and picks and reads pages since gamma-wr-045 and says what each page is and keeps the candidates since gamma-wr-046 ("Web research"); reading a candidate's own site, the verdict and the found company since gamma-wr-050, the price and the people after it; a run ends `done`, or `failed` with `research_no_country`, `research_plan_failed` or `research_search_unavailable`.

## Adding a kind

1. A folder `sources/<kind>/` whose `index.ts` calls `defineSourceKind({ kind, configSchema, labelKey, lookbackDays, schedule, reader, event, evidenceOf }, import.meta.url)` (`sources/_contract.ts`); its settings in a `config.ts` of the folder, in the `modules/signals` block. The `reader` names a provider activity that writes a page to the files port and answers `{ key, count, nextCursor }`, the activity's input from the config, the page size, and `page(file, config, cut)`, the candidates of a page file; the `event` is `{ kind, objectOf?(config) }`, the event's kind and what the candidates engaged with. Or a posts reader, `{ source(config), keepsAuthors(config), keepsReactions(config), pageSize }`: the source of linkedin's `linkedin_read_posts`, whether each post's author is a candidate, the engagers' reactions kept (none: no engager is read), the posts a page; its candidates name their own post. Optional: `endsAt(savedAt)`, after which Run now refuses; `scrub(config, slug)`, the config without the URL that names an erased person (Reduction); and `cadence: "daily"`, a run a day from the daily tick (Runs).
2. `pnpm -F @whytide/signals build`: `scripts/kinds-generate.ts` writes `sources/registry.generated.ts` (git-ignored), which imports every `sources/*/index.ts`; `build`, `typecheck` and `test` run it first, and so do the api's and the worker's `generate`. No other server file changes.
3. Loading the module registers each kind. A name off snake_case fails the start with `signal_kind_invalid`; a name two folders declare with `signal_kind_duplicate`, `details.sources` naming both files.
4. To add it from the screen: its name in `KINDS` and its fields' paths in `FIELDS` (`ui/sources/constants.ts`), which the form shows a refusal under, its fields in the add form, its target on the card and its label `signals.kinds.<kind>.label`; the form names each kind's keys as literals, which messages:check reads.
5. A kind that reads through no LinkedIn account (gamma-wr-030): `readsThroughAccount: false`, a `reader` of `{ research: true }`, `cadence: "research"` and `schedule: () => []`, so that it gets no Temporal schedule and the daily tick writes its runs; `configEditable: true` lets `PATCH /sources/{id}` change its config. Its run is a workflow of its own, never `signals_run`.

## Runs

`signals_run` (`workflows/`), on the worker; [docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md) has the steps.

- **Schedules:** a source's own Temporal schedule `signals-<sourceId>`, made on `signals.source_created` with its kind's fire times and one run at once when the first has passed, paused while the source is disabled, deleted with it, through the platform's `createSchedule`, `pauseSchedule` and `deleteSchedule` (bound by the worker). An enable that finds no schedule (a source saved before alpha-p8-020, or whose created event failed) makes it, with one run at once when a fire time has passed since the last run. A paused agent's source keeps its schedule, whose runs its start ends ("A paused agent" below).
- **By hand:** `POST …/run` writes the run, unless one of the source's is open (`signal_run_open`) or its agent is paused (`signal_agent_paused`); the worker starts `signals.run:<workspaceId>:<runId>` in the delivery's workspace, so a second delivery starts none.
- **Daily** (gamma-p5-040): the global schedule `signals.daily_runs`, every `DAILY_RUNS_TICK_MINUTES` (Settings), runs `signals_daily_runs_tick`, whose one step, `signals_start_daily_runs`, lists tenancy's workspaces and, in each one's own run as the service `signals`, writes a run of every source due as Run now writes one (`scheduled` and `signals.run_requested`, which the worker starts). Due (`domain/schedule/daily.ts`): enabled, its kind's `cadence` `daily` (the four posts kinds), no run of it scheduled, running or waiting, none started on its account's quota day, whatever started it (its save, Run now or a tick), its account's window open and more than `SIGNALS_READS_KEPT_FOR_SENDS` reads left today (20 unset, "Settings") (linkedin's `readsLeftToday`, no provider call: 0 under a kill switch, for an account not connected, paused or held by its `retry_at`); a source whose account is gone is skipped, and so is a paused agent's ("A paused agent" below). A workspace that fails leaves the others started, then fails the tick with its code; the next tick tries again.
- **The read budget** (`patched("read-budget")`, asked once at the start, so a run started before replays on its old path, without one): at its start a run takes its account's quota day and its share of the day's reads, `floor(floor((left − SIGNALS_READS_KEPT_FOR_SENDS) / groups) / inGroup)`, never below 0: the sources that share, its own, the account's other sources with a run reading now and its other enabled daily sources without a run of that quota day, go in groups by agent, the sources in no agent one group (gamma-p5-045), a paused agent's in none (paused-agent-sources), `groups` counting the groups among them and `inGroup` the sources in its own (`domain/schedule/share.ts`), so that one due source in agent A and three in agent B, with 62 reads left, give A's run 21 and each of B's 7; both go to the run's row, `quota_day` and `read_budget`, so a retried start keeps them. Every run started since has one, of any kind and however started; every read counts against it, a posts page's, an engagers page's and a profile's, and each is reserved with linkedin's `keep` of `SIGNALS_READS_KEPT_FOR_SENDS`, checked under the day's lock, so runs at once never read the send path's reads, whatever their shares. linkedin's own refusals answer first: a kill switch, the pause or a disconnect ends the run as before.
- **The end of the day's reading:** a read refused for the run's budget (`read_budget_reached`, the slot linkedin granted released at once), for linkedin's cap or the reads kept (`read_cap_reached`) or for the day's 200 actions (`daily_actions_cap_reached`) opens no wait: the profiles read are processed, and the run ends `done` with `counts.budget_reached` 1, keeping its whole watermark, so that the next day's run reads on from the same cut and drops the engagers it processed by their dedupe keys before any profile read. Ended among the pages, it selects nothing. Its start dates no undated engager (`floorOf`): only a done run that read every post to its cut does. Outside the working hours a run still waits.
- **The page limit:** a budgeted run's page reads, posts' and engagers' together, take half its budget at most, rounded down, one at least (`pageLimitOf`, `PAGE_READS_SHARE`, `domain/schedule/share.ts`). There its paging stops as at the end of the day's reading, its whole watermark kept and `counts.budget_reached` 1, but it selects the candidates staged and reads their profiles, in the read order, with the rest of its budget: a post's reactions carry no date and never reach a cut, so without the limit a source with more reaction pages than its budget would read the same pages each day and never a profile.
- **The read order** (`domain/process/select.ts`): after the cheap drops, authors and commenters first, then the reactors whose staged headline passes the run's audience ("Processing") on the title alone (qualification's `titleFits`, the hard filter of the title until title-check-ai), then the other reactors, in the staged order within each, so that a run its budget stops has read the likeliest first. Since signals-triage-ai the triage orders the reads ("Triage" below), and this title rule orders only a batch the triage could not answer, and a run started before it.
- **Stop:** `POST …/runs/{runId}/stop` sets the run's `stop_requested_at`; on `signals.run_stop_requested` the worker cancels its workflow by the id its start recorded (`workflow_id`, a schedule's run's too), else Run now's, and the run ends `cancelled` as any cancellation ends it (below). The worker cancels a `running` or `waiting` run only: one not started yet is left to its start (`signals_run_start`), which finds the stop, ends it `cancelled` and starts nothing; one that ended meanwhile needs nothing. A run the schedule started before fix-run-stop has no recorded workflow id, so Stop reaches it only through the derived id, Run now's, which a schedule's run does not have: cancel such a run in Temporal ([docs/runbook.md](docs/runbook.md)).
- **Deleting a source** stops its open runs: `deleteSource` reads their workflow ids before the delete takes the rows, `signals.source_deleted` carries them, and its subscriber cancels each after the schedule's delete.
- **A paused agent** (paused-agent-sources): a source in an agent that is not active reads nothing, whatever the pause's reason, a member's (`user`) or its owner's removal (`member_removed`); a source in no agent and an active agent's run as before. ## Processing

`domain/process/` and `domain/triage/`, after the reading, in the run's steps ([docs/flows.md](docs/flows.md)), the ICP being the run's audience (below):

1. **The cheap drops**, before any read, each counted: a company page (`dropped_company`, no hash: no person); the workspace's own staff by the headline (`dropped_own`): onboarding's company domain and the organization's name, else the workspace's, as whole words in any case; a competitor's staff by the headline (`dropped_competitor`; the noise rules, below), and in a run started before signals-triage-ai a recruiter or a job seeker by the headline too (`dropped_recruiter`, `dropped_job_seeker`); an erased or rejected hash (`dropped_hashed`, contacts' `isHashed`); an engagement the workspace knows by its dedupe key (`dropped_known`).
2. **The triage** ("Triage" below), in a run started since signals-triage-ai: the AI judges the candidates left by their headline, 25 a call; it skips a recruiter or a job seeker the ICP does not target (`dropped_triage`, no hash) and sets the read order; a batch it could not answer is decided by the rules, as a run before it decides them all.
3. **A profile read** for each candidate left, in the read order: one read of linkedin's quota (`read_profile`, waiting on the cap and the hours as a page does), linkedin's `linkedin_read_profile`, its request and the profile as files in the run's folder. A profile the provider cannot read (the refusal `linkedin_provider_error`, on the read's third attempt) is settled as read and dropped as a gone one, `dropped_unread`. Three such in a row (`UNREADABLE_IN_ROW_MAX`, `domain/process/keys.ts`; any other answer resets the count) stop the reading: the profiles read are processed, and the run ends `failed`, `linkedin_provider_error`, keeping the watermark, so that an outage drops no more. A read refused for its account (`READING_ENDS`: disconnected, or since beta-p6-020 `account_paused` or `linkedin_rate_limited`, the account paused or held by its `retry_at` after the read was reserved, as linkedin's third error within the hour does, or since beta-p6-040 `platform_stopped`, `workspace_stopped` or `account_stopped`, a kill switch thrown after it) stops the reading the same way, with that code; the next run waits at its reservation. Within the hour that third error comes before the count in a row: the third profile the provider cannot read pauses the account on its first attempt, and its next attempt is refused `linkedin_rate_limited`.
4. **The profiles, 25 at a time**, in one step each: the own staff again by the profile's headline, company and company page (`dropped_own`); a competitor's staff again, by the headline, the company and its page (`dropped_competitor`; since signals-triage-ai no recruiter or job seeker is dropped after the read: one the triage or the rules kept is the assessment's to judge, as the title is); qualification's hard filters, prepared once per step, on the country (contacts' `countryOf`), the size unknown and passing; a miss leaves contacts' salted hash with its reason and nothing else (`dropped_filtered`, `country_mismatch`). The title stops nobody since title-check-ai: a person whose country fits is stored as a pending lead whatever the title, and qualification's assessment judges the title, so one outside the ICP's titles ends `not_qualified`, at an assessment's credits and model call. A pass: the person (`linkedin`, its member id under `unipile` in `providerRefs` since gamma-p5-020, the key campaigns' first invite reads, which then makes no profile read), the company, the lead (`signal`, at the engagement, with the source's label and the candidate's post URL, which contacts' lead list shows), the event and its evidence; `created` for a new lead, `attached` for a known person, `dropped_known` for an event the workspace has. `profiles_read`, and `dropped_unread` for a profile gone.
   The dedupe key, the lead's source URL and the evidence take the staging row's `object_url`, the candidate's post (null in a row staged before gamma-p5-020: the source's post). A posts kind stages each post in its unnamed form, `https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:<kind>:<id>`, from either form the port gives. Its evidence: an author's own post is their `excerpt`; the first 500 characters of the post a commenter or reactor engaged with are `post_excerpt`, someone else's words.
5. **The hand-off:** a step's new leads go out as `signals.person_found` `{ workspaceId, personIds }`, whose subscriber, signals' own, starts qualification's batch; its known persons as qualification's re-score request.

**The noise rules** (`domain/process/noise.ts`, gamma-p5-030), as whole words in any case, the terms in `config.ts`, English only. Since signals-triage-ai the recruiter and job-seeker rules decide only a batch the triage could not answer, and a run started before it, on the headline alone; the competitor rule runs in every run. A recruiter: a term of `NOISE_RECRUITER_TERMS` (recruiter, talent acquisition, headhunter, sourcer, staffing; a plural too) in the headline; off when one of the ICP's titles holds one. A job seeker: a phrase of `NOISE_JOB_SEEKER_PHRASES` ("open to work", "#opentowork", "looking for opportunities", "seeking new opportunities", "looking for a new role") in the headline; off when the ICP's titles or must-haves hold one, or "job seeker". A competitor's staff, never off: a competitor's name after "at" or "@" in the headline; after the read, also a company of its name or its LinkedIn page, in any case. The competitors are the `companyName` and `companyUrl` of the workspace's `competitor_engagement` sources (gamma-p5-025's kind, read by its name), enabled or not, read once per step. A noise drop leaves no hash.

**The run's audience** (gamma-p5-045, `domain/agents.ts`): the run's start names the source's agent (playbooks' `playbookOfSource`) and hands its id, never the audience, to the select, the triage and the profile steps; each reads the agent's audience by it (`getPlaybook`'s `icpId`, then icp's `readIcpForScoring`), and a source in no agent the workspace's default audience, as a run started before does. The hard filters, the read order's titles, the triage's ICP and the noise rules' exceptions all read it.

## Reduction

- **The rule:** an `evidence` row not reduced whose `captured_at` is older than its kind's lookback (the kind that declares its event's kind; post engagers and own posts: 30 days; a topic: 14) keeps `url`, `author`, `reaction`, `captured_at` and its event; `excerpt`, `comment_text` and `post_excerpt` become null and `reduced_at` is set; its event's `relevance_rationale` becomes null too, its `relevance` and `intent_class` kept (gamma-p5-030). No summary is stored: the lead page's panel words the line from `signals.evidence.reduced` with the reaction and the date (alpha-p8-050). `captured_at` is set once, with the event (`domain/process/`): a second engagement is an event of its own, the same one seen again is dropped, a re-import writes no evidence.

- **The erasure's scrub** (gamma-p5-020): `erasePerson` also removes the person where signals names them, by their public id, the slug of contacts' LinkedIn URL, decoded and in lower case (contacts erases the person after signals; without the person, nothing is scrubbed). ## Rating

An event's rating (gamma-p5-030), on `signal_events`: `relevance` (0 to 1), `relevance_rationale` (one line, which the reduction nulls), `intent_class` (one of qualification's `INTENT_CLASSES`, null for an event without the person's words), `rating_status` (`unrated`, then `rated`) and `decision_record_id` (ai's record of the call). Relevance weighs a signal in qualification's intent and never drops one.

**The rating step** (`domain/rate/`, the agent `agents/rate-engagement/`), at the end of a run that read to its end or to the end of its day's reading (gamma-p5-040), `done` or `failed` with `insufficient_credits`, never after another early end; behind `patched("rate-engagement")`, so a run started before it replays without one. `signals_select_ratings` picks the source's oldest unrated events whose evidence, not reduced, holds the person's words or the post's excerpt, at most `SIGNALS_RATE_MAX_EVENTS_PER_RUN` (100 unset, "Settings"); none while Company Knowledge's offer (one-liner, products, pain points) is empty. `signals_rate_events` rates them ten at a time (`RATE_BATCH_SIZE`): each event its kind, the person's role (a like and a repost are reactions), their words and the post, scrubbed (`domain/rate/scrub.ts`: links, email addresses, phone numbers of seven digits or more and each part of the engager's name of three letters or more, as written, become `[link]`, `[email]`, `[phone]`, `[name]`), against the offer; each rating is written to its event while unrated, with the decision record's id, and the rated persons go to qualification's `requestRescore`. An `ai_failed` answer or a batch over the agent's budget leaves its events unrated, and the next batch goes on; a model failing past the activity's three attempts stops the rating, and the run ends as it would have. `signals_count_ratings` adds `rated` and `unrated` (picked, not rated) to the run's counts. The next run picks the oldest unrated first, until the reduction takes their words. The history holds event ids and counts only. The runs panel shows both counts (`signals.runs.rated`, `signals.runs.unrated`).

**The signal reader** (`domain/reader.ts`), which loading the module registers with qualification's `defineSignalReader` (`domain/register-reader.ts`, imported by `index.ts`, `events.ts` and `workflows/index.ts`, the files the worker loads), reads in the caller's tenant run, through `tenantClient`:

- `intentInputs({ personIds, since })`: the persons' events since `since`, of qualification's `INTENT_KINDS`, as `{ personId, eventId, sourceId, kind, role, relevance, intentClass, occurredAt, ownerUserId, count }`: `role` is `author`, `comment` or `reaction` (a like or a repost), `relevance` and `intentClass` are null while unrated, `ownerUserId` is the source's `created_by`. ## Triage

The triage of a run's candidates (signals-triage-ai; `domain/triage/`, the agent `agents/triage-engagers/`), between the cheap drops and the first profile read, behind `patched("headline-triage")`, so that a run started before it replays without one and decides by the rules. `signals_select_candidates` answers the ids left, oldest staged first, and `signals_triage_candidates` (kind `ai`) takes them 25 at a time (`TRIAGE_BATCH_SIZE`): in a tenant run, the ICP's titles, seniorities, industries and must-haves and the batch's candidates still staged; outside any run, one call of `signals.triage-engagers` with each candidate's index, role (`author`, `comment`, or `reaction` for a like or a repost) and headline, scrubbed as the rating's words are (`domain/rate/scrub.ts`); never a name, a profile URL or a member reference. Then a request file for each candidate read, and in another run the skips deleted and counted.

- **The decisions:** per candidate `skip`, `read_first` or `read`; a skip with its code, `recruiter` or `job_seeker`, the others none. The gate `decisions` (`domain/triage/checks.ts`) wants each candidate decided once, no other index and a code exactly for a skip; refused, the call is asked again once with the error, then ends `ai_failed`. The model skips only a recruiter or a job seeker the ICP does not target by its titles, seniorities, industries or must-haves, never an agency's owner or founder ("Founder, XYZ Staffing"), and reads when in doubt. A skip is deleted and counted `dropped_triage`, with no hash, as a noise drop; a candidate the answer leaves out is read.
- **The read order:** authors and commenters first, unless skipped; then the reactors the triage reads first, whose headline does the work of one of the ICP's titles, combined ("RevOps & GTM Lead") or shortened; then the rest; the staged order within each, across the run's batches.
- **The fallback:** an `ai_failed` answer and a batch over the agent's budget are decided by the rules in the same step: the noise rules' recruiter and job-seeker terms on the headline (`dropped_recruiter`, `dropped_job_seeker`), then the title rule's rank ("The read order", above). A step failing past its three attempts, or with the agent not loaded (`signal_triage_agent_not_loaded`, never retried), goes to `signals_triage_fallback` (kind `db`), which decides the batch the same way. A run never stops for the triage.
- **The cost:** one billed model call per batch, priced in ai's cost event; no credit is charged to the workspace (`maxCostCredits` 0, as the rating's), and a batch the cache answers makes no call. People the keywords dropped are read and assessed now: each costs a profile read's credit, a LinkedIn read and an assessment.
- **The record:** ai's decision record of each call, its subject the workspace: a batch names several persons, staged by any source's run. Its raw prompt, the scrubbed headlines of the skipped and the read, is kept until its `raw_purge_after`, 30 days (ai's purge, by hand in alpha); `erasePerson` erases every triage record of the workspace (ai's `eraseDecisionRecords`), so the next run asks the model again. The history holds candidate ids only; no log line, span or event holds a headline.

**Tests** (signals-triage-ai): the batch, the schemas, the gate, the decisions and the rules `tests/triage.test.ts`, the noise rules `tests/noise.test.ts`, the erasure `tests/erase.test.ts`; the worker's `tests/signals-triage.test.ts` on the LinkedIn, files and LLM fakes (the skips and the order, the code drops unchanged, the fallback on `ai_failed` and on a model failing past the attempts, a recruiter's profile stored as a pending lead, the fake's calls, the history, the logs and the outbox read for names and headlines), the rules' drops in `tests/signals-process.test.ts`; `workflows/__histories__/signals_run_triaged.json`, replayed by `pnpm -F worker test:replay`; the evals `core/packages/ai/evals/signals.triage-engagers/`.

## Decided here

- A Member writes the sources they added, an Owner every one (gamma-p6-015): the check is the domain's, `writableSource` after the source is found, in the four commands that write one, so the routes keep their actions and the erasure's and the daily runs' calls, a service's, pass. A source made by a service, `service:<name>`, is `signals.manage`'s only. The hint is `signals.sources.not_owner`: the task's camelCase name fails the key check. The screen reads `GET /v1/me` and compares the source's `createdBy` and the member's role with `SIGNALS_MANAGE_ROLES`, which `permissions.ts` declares `signals.manage` from; the api decides. A create, an import and its check read through the caller's own sender account, unless she holds `signals.manage`: else a Member's source, enabled at its create and run each day, would read through a teammate's account (RB-1.1.1). The check is `assertSenderAccount`'s, after the account is found, so a missing one stays `signal_sender_account_invalid`; an Owner's source on a Member's account stays allowed, as before (gamma-p6-070).
- `kind` is a string, not the data model's enum; the run status is an enum of the six states.
- A kind's settings live in its folder; the registry is a module-level map filled as each kind's file loads, like the platform's registries.
- DELETE removes the source and its runs (`ON DELETE CASCADE`), not the data model's soft delete: nothing else refers to a source yet.
- The post URL is stored canonical, without the query: share links carry tracking ids.
- A short link is resolved once, at the save, so a run never sees one: one GET to lnkd.in, its path without the query, whose `Location` is read and never followed, since the app reads no LinkedIn page outside the person's connected account. A failed request is `signal_post_link_unreachable`, any other answer `signal_config_invalid`.
- The create is a `whytide:own-runs` route (`addSource`): the kind's schema, which may call lnkd.in, runs outside any run, which waits on no call and ends after 5 seconds (core/packages/db README "One run at a time"); `createSource` then reads the canonical config again in its own run, with no request.
- The next fire times count from the last run, not from the clock: right after a save, the three fire times are all still to come, the run at save first (alpha-p8-040).
- A waiting run's resume time is its sender account's next window start, linkedin's `nextWindowAt`, read by the runs panel: a refusal for the hours waits for it, and one for the cap wakes at the next quota day and waits for it then; no column holds the refusal's `retryAt` (alpha-p10-110).
- The runs panel opens by `?runs=<id>`; the screen and the evidence panel read in the browser, so that their loading and error states show and a retry asks again; the times are in next-intl's zone (the server's until a user setting exists), named, a resume time in the account's.
- Run now is refused only while a run is `running` or `waiting` (decided with alpha-p8-020): a `scheduled` one is about to start, and the 409 is a code of signals', `signal_run_open`.
- Stop (fix-run-stop) cancels by the workflow id the run's start records, the only place a schedule's run's id is known. A `scheduled` run is not cancelled but ended by its start: a cancel could reach its workflow before that first step, which would leave the row `scheduled`. The start sets `running` only while no stop is asked, so a stop that commits meanwhile is seen. A stop of another source's run is `signal_source_not_found`, as the path names nothing; a second stop keeps the first time.
- Deleting a source stops its open runs through `signals.source_deleted`, which carries their workflow ids (`workflow_ids`, absent without one, so an event written before the change still reads): one event, whose subscriber already removes the source's schedule, rather than a stop event per run, which would need the id as well, as the rows are gone.
- The line under Run now (fix-run-wait) takes the run's status from the list and the reason from linkedin's usage, no new column: an account not connected first, since no resume time holds then; then the cap; then the hours; neither (a wait about to end), the runs panel's "Waiting until". A usage read that fails leaves the line at "Waiting" rather than failing the screen.
- A page reaches the workflow as a file's key, never as engagers: the history holds ids, the post URL, keys and counts.
- A read that answered, a page or a refusal, is committed as one read, in a cancelled run too; a read whose activity failed, or that a cancellation found not yet made, is released.
- Credits go in blocks, each settled before a wait, before the next block and at the end, so no wait outlives a lease and a run past the estimate pays for every 30 engagers it reads (alpha-p8-030).
- A cancelled run settles like a failed one: the engagers it read are paid, the rest released. Its steps wait for the one under way (`WAIT_CANCELLATION_COMPLETED`), so it knows what it holds.
- A scheduled run makes its id in the workflow; its first step writes the row. Sources saved before alpha-p8-020 have no schedule until an enable makes it (alpha-p10-110).
- A refused block ends the reading, not the run: the pages read and paid for are processed, then the run ends `insufficient_credits` and keeps the watermark (alpha-p8-030): pages come newest first, so a watermark moved to them would skip the older engagers never read. The next run reads and pays for those pages again; its dedupe keys and hashes drop what this one processed before any profile read, so only the page reads repeat (and the profile reads of own staff known only by their profile, and of profiles gone).
- An event's dedupe key is the SHA-256 of its kind, the engager's member reference, the post and the reaction. The cut keeps the watermark's own engager, so a known key drops it before its profile read.
- Every candidate left after the cheap drops, and since signals-triage-ai the triage, gets a profile read, the cap showing the cost; its profile goes through a file, never the history, and every end deletes the run's folder (raw/'s 72 hours catch a miss).
- The excerpt is the person's own words, the first 500 characters of the comment, null for a reaction: the reader has no post text, and other people's words would reach the model (qualification's anonymizer).
- `signals.person_found`, the module's prefix, carries a step's new leads' person ids; the re-score request carries the known persons' with a new event. Its subscriber is signals': qualification imports nothing of signals (decisions.md, 2026-10-22).
- An event keeps its source and run by id, `ON DELETE SET NULL`: deleting a source keeps the person's events and evidence. Companies from a profile have `provider` null; persons have `unipile` since gamma-p5-020, the key of their member id.
- The reduction (alpha-p10-020): one global daily tick that starts a run per workspace, not a schedule per workspace (decisions.md, 2026-10-27); the workflow carries the module's prefix, `signals_evidence_reduction` for the task's `evidence_reduction`. No summary text is stored (decisions.md, 2026-10-27).
- A row's clock is `captured_at`, as the task says; its lookback is its event kind's: two kinds of one event kind take the shorter, an event kind no kind declares (a folder removed) the shortest of all, so a new kind changes nothing here.
- A reduction is one activity that loops its batches, each batch its own run, so a retry goes on from what committed and a run logs one count; the command calls the same function in runs of its own.
- The evidence route answers the attribution and the sources' labels too (alpha-p8-050), so that the panel reads once: contacts' lead holds the sources' ids, signals their labels. Contacts never imports signals, so the processing gives contacts the source's label and post URL with the lead's source (fix-lead-source-post), which the lead list shows; the panel reads the sources' labels as they are now.
- A candidate's credit has the candidate as its subject, the person being known only after the read (beta-p1-090); since the staging row goes with the run, the event a candidate becomes carries its ULID in its id (`sev_` and the candidate's ULID, `ids.ts`), so `personReads` finds a person's candidates with no new column or table. An event made before has an id no candidate had, which matches no credit.
- `patched("engager-price")` is asked once, at the start of `signals_run`, and kept: in a replay without the marker it answers false, but once the replay is over a later ask would answer true and switch an old run to the new path mid-run.
- The posts kinds (gamma-p5-020) read in `signals_run` behind one patch, `posts-reader`; the competitor and creator kinds, their form and the creator sources' scrub are gamma-p5-025, to keep this change within its size.
- A posts kind stages, stores and reads each post by its unnamed URL, which names no author and still opens the post, so the scrub only has post engagers' URLs to find; post engagers keep their config's URL, so their events keep their dedupe keys.
- `object_url` and `post_excerpt` are nullable: a row staged before them takes the source's post. The unique key adds `object_url`, so one person on two posts is two candidates; rows staged before, with nulls, stay distinct as before.
- An author's own post is their own words, their `excerpt`, and no comment; another person's post is `post_excerpt`, null for authors and for post engagers. A company page's posts have no author candidate.
- An undated engager is dated at the later of its post's date and the last done run's start, never later than the listing, so it never dates a reaction before its post, and the 30-day rule's deletions stay out of every lookback.
- A kind scrubs its own config (`scrub`): a post engagers source on the person's post is disabled, not given the unnamed URL, since a new URL would change its events' dedupe keys. The disable goes through `updateSource` and the stop through `stopRun`, so their events go out as a user's would.
- An engager read refused `linkedin_not_found` skips that post, which leaves the watermark; any other refusal ends the run with its code. A topic's `reactor` keeps likes and reposts; a topic with neither commenters nor reactors reads no engagers.
- A read failing past its attempts processes the profiles read before the run ends (gamma-p5-020), under the same patch, so a run recorded before replays as it ran.
- A source tracks an author when its kind's posts reader reads one author's posts (`by: "author"`), read from the kind, so no list of kinds is kept; the tracked limit counts the sender account's enabled sources under the workspace's lock and before the workspace's limit, so with both at 10 the 11th tracked source of one account is `signal_tracked_limit` (gamma-p5-025).
- An author's page is stored as `https://www.linkedin.com/<company|in>/<id>`, without the query (`trk`, `originalSubdomain`) or the fragment, as a post is; another host (a country's, `uk.linkedin.com`), a page's sub-page (`/company/<id>/posts/`) and a showcase page are refused, as the port reads `/company/<id>` and `/in/<id>` only.
- A creator source the erasure finds is disabled, not re-pointed: it reads one creator, and their erasure ends it.
- Rules before the model (gamma-p5-030): the noise rules run in code, on the staged headline before any read slot, credit or profile read, then on the profile. The audience's own terms turn a rule off; the competitor rule never is. A noise drop leaves no hash, unlike the hard filters, as the rules follow the ICP. Since signals-triage-ai the model judges recruiters and job seekers by the headline, and the rules only a batch it could not answer.
- Relevance weighs and never drops: qualification clamps it into its weight, and an unrated event weighs as qualification's default.
- The reader collapses per person and source in windows of the event kind's lookback from each window's newest event, not from `since`: qualification asks for 90 days, and each lookback counts once. A deleted source's events are one source's, as close as can be to the intent they had before the delete.
- `rating_status` is text (`unrated`, `rated`), not an enum like the run status: an enum's type would reach the inferred type of `listEvidence`'s rows.
- The rating is a run's last step, ten events a call and 100 a run, oldest first, so a run's cost and length stay bounded and the rest waits for the next run. Its subject is the source: a batch names several persons. An empty offer rates and counts nothing. A model failing past its attempts stops the rating, as the next batches would fail too; an `ai_failed` or over-budget batch fails on its own words, so the next goes on, and its events are picked again until their evidence is reduced.
- The name scrub takes the engager's name as written, its parts of three letters or more, so that "will" or "rose" in a sentence stays; the words of the post engaged with may name others, which the prompt tells the model never to repeat.
- The share (gamma-p5-040): a run's budget is its account's reads left less the 20 kept, split evenly between the sources that read the account's day, its own, those with a run reading now and the enabled daily ones still to run that quota day, rounded down and set once, at its start. It is fair, not exact: runs that start together may see the same reads left; linkedin's `keep`, under the day's lock, is the floor that holds whatever the shares. Every run started since has one, a post engagers source's and Run now's too. Since gamma-p5-045 the split goes by agent first (below).
- The 20 kept (`SIGNALS_READS_KEPT_FOR_SENDS` since env-settings-b, a constant before): the send path's checks need a few of the day's reads, and 20 of the 100 is a first guess. A source is not due with 20 or fewer left, and each budgeted read is reserved with that `keep`.
- A run its budget, the cap or the 200 ends keeps its whole watermark, not each post read to its cut: engagers staged on such a post may wait unread for their profiles, and a moved mark would skip them; the next day's run reads those pages again and drops what was processed by its dedupe keys, page reads only. For the same reason its start dates no undated engager.
- In a budgeted run the cap and the 200 end the day's reading, as the budget does, rather than wait for the next quota day: the run would hold the source open overnight, and the next day's run reads on from the same cut anyway.
- linkedin's refusals answer before the budget: a kill switch or a pause leaves a budget of 0, which, checked first, would end the run `done` instead of `failed` with its code. A slot granted past the budget is released at once.
- The page limit, half the budget (gamma-p5-040, from the quick review): a run's pages could take its whole budget every day, as a reaction page never reaches a cut; with the limit each day's run reads profiles. What stays limited while the whole watermark is kept: pages past the limit, older posts' reaction tails and, for a post engagers source, comments listed after many reactions, are not reached until the newer engagers are known; each day's run reads the same first pages and drops the engagers it processed before any profile read.
- `quota_day` and `read_budget` are written when a run starts reading, an account connected; the card's line reads the run its source's `last_run_at` names, so it follows the last run the card shows. The read order's ICP is the run's audience (the workspace's default before gamma-p5-045), on the title alone.
- The triage (signals-triage-ai) is a step per batch of 25 (`TRIAGE_BATCH_SIZE`, the processing's chunk), between the select and the reads: no model call runs in a tenant run, and a batch that fails falls back alone. 25 headlines of 600 characters at most, with the ICP, stay within the agent's 24,000 input tokens.
- One count, `dropped_triage`, for both of the triage's codes: the rules' `dropped_recruiter` and `dropped_job_seeker` stay theirs, so a run's counts show which decided.
- A triage record's subject is the workspace, not the run or the source: a person is staged by any source's runs, a deleted source's runs are gone, and a candidate staged again is triaged again in another batch. `erasePerson` erases them all, which empties the triage's cache too. The source stays the rating's subject.
- The recruiter and job-seeker drops after the read go in every run, not behind the patch: the profile step is an activity, which a replay does not run again, and a run started before the patch dropped them by the headline before the read.
- A triaged run's select writes no request file: the triage's step writes one for each candidate read, before it deletes the skips, so that a retried step finds the same batch and the cache's answer.
- The rules decide an `ai_failed` or over-budget batch in the triage's step, and a step failing past its attempts in a step of its own, `signals_triage_fallback`, so that a run never stops for the triage.
- Signals reads playbooks (gamma-p5-045), never the other way: an agent's sources are playbooks' links, read through its index (`playbookOfSource`, `getPlaybook`, `listPlaybooks`, `sourcesOf`, `detachSource`), and playbooks checks a link through the source checker signals registers as it loads (`domain/source-checker.ts`, `domain/register-checker.ts`), as the signal reader is registered with qualification. The `module-boundaries` lint holds the direction.
- The checker reads the ids' rows in the caller's tenant run, `FOR SHARE`, so a delete at the same time either waits for the link and detaches it, or goes first and the id is not found. It reads each account once; an account linkedin no longer finds answers found with both ids null, which playbooks refuses as `playbook_source_wrong_owner`, and any other error is thrown, never read as a refusal.
- The limit's groups are read from playbooks' links at each count, under the workspace's lock. A link is not refused here: playbooks caps an agent at 10 links, the default, so an agent's group passes the limit only under a lower setting, and is then checked at its next enable. A detach can leave more than the limit enabled in no agent; the next create or enable there is refused until one is disabled or linked.
- A run's agent is read once, at its start, and its id travels in the history, never the audience: each step reads the audience by it, so a link changed during a run does not move the run to another agent. A run started before has no id and keeps the default audience; no patch, as the commands stay the same (the steps' inputs gain an optional field).
- The share by agent: the groups first, then the sources in a group, each rounded down, so an agent with one source reads as much as one with three, and a run's share never passes the reads left less the 20.
- A paused agent's sources read nothing (paused-agent-sources, 2026-11-15): one rule, the agent's status not `active` (`READING_AGENT_STATUS`), whatever the reason, checked where a read would begin: the tick, Run now, the share and the start. Their Temporal schedules are not paused with the agent: the start's check is needed anyway (a run written before the pause, a retry), a skipped fire time ends as a paused schedule's would, and no second state has to follow a link, an unlink or an enable while paused; the cost is one start step per fire time while paused. The start answers `gone`, the row `cancelled` as a stop before the start leaves it and `last_run_at` untouched, so the workflow is unchanged and replays as before. The pause's subscriber reads the agent's status at its delivery, not the event's: that holds in any order of the events, and a rename of a paused agent, which publishes `paused` again, finds the stops asked. Run now's checks: expired first, which is final, then the agent, then an open run. A pause that commits between the tick's read of the agents and a source's Run now check fails that workspace's tick with `signal_agent_paused`, and the step's next attempt leaves the source out.
- A research source is a row of `signal_sources` with `sender_account_id` null (the migration `signals_source_account_optional`, expand only); a LinkedIn kind's account is required in code (`accountOf`, `signal_sender_account_invalid` naming `senderAccountId`), and so is the refusal of one given for a research kind. The source checker answers a source with no account as found, no reading account and its creator, `created_by`, as the reading owner; playbooks links it when that creator is the agent's owner (its `refusalOf` no longer asks for an account).
- No Temporal schedule per research source: the tick writes the run, so the first run reads the audience of the agent the source joined, not the default one at its save. The cadence `research` is a third answer of `cadence`, with `everyDays`.
- A research run is a workflow of its own, `signals_research_run`, with `endRun` shared with `signals_run`'s end, so that no `patched()` is added to `signals_run`, whose histories replay unchanged; `signals_run` refuses a research or account-less source.
- `lookbackDays` of the kind is 30 (`LOOKBACK_DAYS_MAX`), its `evidenceOf` a minimal mapping (url, reaction, author); both are placeholders until the steps of gamma-wr-040 and after set them.
- Start pages are deduplicated and the fragment is dropped; `languages` are lower-cased and a duplicate is refused; `region` and `languages` are not stored when empty.

## Importing sources

A CSV file of sources, checked, then imported (sources-csv-import; `domain/import/`, `api/source-imports.controller.ts`). The header row names the columns, in any case and order: `kind` (required: a kind's name, or `post`, `creator`, `company` or `competitor`, `topic`), `url`, `name`, `phrase`, `roles` (a topic's, separated by `;`) and `label` (else the kind's: a link without `https://www.`, the company's name, the phrase; 100 characters). Each kind maps its cells to its config in its own folder (`csv` on `defineSourceKind`); a kind without `csv`, the own posts, is not importable. Limits: 1 MB, 200 data rows, UTF-8 (a byte-order mark is fine), a blank row skipped; a ZIP, a NUL byte or a malformed file is refused whole.

A row's error is its line in the answer, never the request's: a create's code (`signal_kind_unknown`, `signal_config_invalid`, `signal_topic_blocked`, `signal_post_link_unreachable`) with the file's columns it names, or `duplicate_in_file` (an earlier row has its kind and config) or `source_exists` (a source of the workspace has). Every row reads through the one sender account the request names, the caller's own unless she holds `signals.manage` (`forbidden` otherwise, after the file's own refusals and before any row is checked). The import creates the valid rows in the file's order; a row past `SIGNALS_MAX_ENABLED_SOURCES` (the sources in no agent, where a new source is) or `SIGNALS_MAX_TRACKED_PER_ACCOUNT` is created disabled, its schedule paused, and counted as `disabled`. Only the file's name and the counts are kept (`signal_source_imports`); a source keeps its import's id.

The screen: "Import sources from a CSV", beside the add form's title, opens a card under it: the sender account, the file, the columns and a sample file. A file picked is checked at once (its size first, in the browser), and again when the account changes: the figures, the rows to import, the rows with an error and their download; then "Import the valid rows" and the result: Created, Created disabled and Not imported. A source's card says "Imported from {file}"; Imported files lists each import of sources, newest first among discovery's, without View leads.

Decided here:
- Created disabled past a limit, rather than the row refused: the file is imported whole, and the user picks which to enable.
- The file travels as base64 in JSON: the api host takes no CSV or multipart body for this route, and a megabyte fits.
- `post`, `creator`, `company`/`competitor` and `topic` name the kinds in the file besides their own names; the own posts take no row.
- A repeat of an existing source is a row error (`source_exists`), so that an import run twice creates nothing the second time.

## Web research

The run's plan (gamma-wr-040) and its search (gamma-wr-041). Where a research source looks is decided at each run, by the AI, from what a member gave in the app: nothing about a trade, a country or a language is stored in advance, in the code, a prompt, a migration or a seed.

- **The list of source types** (`domain/research/places/`), a registry of the kinds of page worth searching, called a *place* in the code since `source` and `kind` are taken. It ships with the product and is the same for every tenant. An entry is a file `<id>.ts` that calls `definePlace({ id, labelKey, shows, finding, pageKind }, import.meta.url)`: the id in snake_case and the file's name, its label `signals.places.<id>.label` in `messages/en.json`, two short texts for the plan (what such a page shows, and how it is found by a web search), and what its page is for the next step (`pageKind`: `company_site`, `company_list` or `notice`). The nine: `web_search`, `dealer_lists`, `distributor_lists`, `fair_exhibitors`, `association_members`, `public_tenders`, `contracts_awarded`, `supplier_registries`, `customer_sites`. **To add one:** a new file in that folder and its label; `pnpm -F @whytide/signals kinds:generate` (run by `build`, `typecheck` and `test`) writes `places.generated.ts`, git-ignored, which imports every file as the kinds' list does; no other file changes. A definition off its form is `signal_place_invalid`, an id twice `signal_place_duplicate`, both stopping the start. A type that needs an adapter (procurement data, a business directory) comes later, with its port.
- **The plan** (`domain/research/plan.ts`; `agents/research-plan/`, its README): the step `signals_research_plan`, after the start. It reads, in a tenant run, Company Knowledge (`readCompanyKnowledge`), the run's audience (industries, size ranges, countries, titles, seniorities, must-haves and disqualifiers), the source's `focus`, `region` and `languages`, the list of source types, and the queries the source asked within `SIGNALS_RESEARCH_RECHECK_DAYS`; the member's texts go through the rating's scrub (links, email addresses and phone numbers become placeholders) and reach the model as labelled data blocks. The agent `signals.research-plan` answers the trade's words by language, the source types to use, and the queries, each with its source type, a country and a language. A plan the run already has (a retry of the step) is answered as it is, with no second call.
- **The gates**, in code (`domain/research/checks.ts`), refuse a part and keep the rest, counting each refusal by the gate's name in `signal_research_plans.refused`: a source type off the list (`place`), a country outside the audience's (`country`), a language outside the source's when it names some, else off the kind's list (`language`), a query that is blank or longer than the search port takes, by the port's own schema (`query`), a query the source asked within the re-check days (`recheck`, compared with its text squeezed and lower-cased) and a repeat within the plan (`duplicate`). Words in a language the gate refuses are dropped, and blank and repeated words with them.
- **Kept** in two tenant tables ([docs/data.md](docs/data.md)): `signal_research_plans`, one per run, with the words by language, the source types and the refusals' counts, and `signal_research_queries`, the plan's queries in order, each with its source type, country, language and text, and `asked_at` and `result_count`, null until the search asks it. The plan's memory is the queries asked: a later run is told them and asks new ones, and after the re-check days one may be asked again.
- **The search** (`domain/research/search.ts`; gamma-wr-041): the step `signals_research_search`, one query of the plan a step, by its position, in the plan's order, after the plan. Each query goes through enrichment's `searchWeb` (the counted search, the run's only search call) with its country, its language, 10 results and page 1. Before it: a query already asked is skipped; the run's searches at `SIGNALS_RESEARCH_SEARCHES_PER_RUN` (asked and failed both count) end the run's searching with `counts.budget_reached` 1; and a code gate (`holdsContact`, in `checks.ts`) refuses a query that holds an email address, a link or a phone number (years do not count as one), counted `searches_refused_contact`. An answer goes to the files port as `raw/signals/<runId>/search/<position>.json` (`placeId`, `country`, `language`, `adapter`, `results`; raw/'s 72 hours; gamma-wr-045 reads them), then in one tenant run the query gets its `asked_at` and `result_count` and the run's counts `searches_asked` and `search_results` grow; both writes belong to the one step, so a step run again finds its file and finishes the row, never searching twice, and a query asked is skipped. A search that fails is counted (`searches_failed`), skipped and left unasked, so that a later run may ask it. The step answers a status only: `asked`, `failed`, `refused`, `skipped`, `budget`, `spent`, `unavailable` or `gone`.
- **The searches' ends:** none is a failure but the third. At the limit the run ends `done`, `counts.budget_reached` 1; with every adapter spent (`spent`) it ends `done`, `counts.adapters_spent` 1, its other queries unasked; with no search adapter loaded (`unavailable`) it ends `failed`, `research_search_unavailable`, its plan kept, since with no search the source can find nothing and the member should see why. The run's counts are `queries_planned`, `queries_refused`, `searches_asked`, `search_results`, `searches_failed`, `searches_refused_contact`, `budget_reached` and `adapters_spent`, as they happen. The plan's route and the card read the last plan.
- **The run's end:** a plan the model failed twice (its schema, or over its budget) ends the run `failed`, `research_plan_failed`, with nothing kept; a plan whose queries every gate refused still ends it `done`. The step answers counts and codes; no word, query or text reaches a log, a span, a metric label or the workflow's history.

Decided here (gamma-wr-040):
- The gates run after the model's call, not as the task's own gates: a task gate refuses the whole answer and asks again, where the task asks that the rest of the plan be kept. The task has none; a schema failure is retried once, as every agent's.
- A plan names the source types it picked and the queries name theirs; the plan keeps its own picks that are on the list, then any a kept query names, so that no query's type is missing from its plan.
- The plan's memory is the queries asked, as the task says: a planned query not asked yet (a run that stopped before its searches) is not told to the next plan and is not refused by it.
- Two settings and the model's are the task's; the setting of searches per run comes with the search (gamma-wr-041).

Decided here (gamma-wr-041):
- A step searches one query, so that its `asked_at`, its result count and its file belong to one retried step; the workflow loops over the plan's positions, from the count the plan step answers.
- The searches' limit counts the failed searches too: each one used an allowance.
- A failed search is left unasked, so that the next run's plan may ask it again; a refused one too.
- `no_adapter` ends the run `failed` (`research_search_unavailable`) and `spent` ends it `done`, as the task says; each is the first such answer, so the run stops searching at once.
- The contact gate finds an email address or a link with the rating's scrub and a phone number by its own rule: a run of 7 digits or more with single separators (space, dot, dash, parentheses) or a leading +, where a group of four digits from 1900 to 2099 (a year) never counts, so that tender and award queries may carry years.
- The plan step's timeout is 10 minutes under the new patch (the old runs keep 3), since the model's retries take minutes; the search step's is 2 minutes.

### The results picked and the pages read (gamma-wr-045)

After its searches the run chooses which results to read, reads them, and keeps each page for the next task, which says what a page is. The three steps are in `domain/research/` (`pick.ts`, `queue.ts`, `page.ts`; the address rules and the queue in `pages.ts`, the one fetch in `read-page.ts`); every file is under `raw/signals/<runId>/` (72 hours) and is found by its key with `files.get`, `files_not_found` meaning "not there", never by a listing.

- **The pick** (`signals_research_pick`, kind `ai`, once for each position of the plan, from 1): the search's answer file is read; a result on `linkedin.com`, `lnkd.in` or a social network (`NEVER_FETCHED_HOSTS`), off http and https, or with a user or password in its address is dropped in code (`pageUrlOf`), counted `results_dropped`; the rest go to the agent `signals.research-pick-results` ([its README](agents/research-pick-results/README.md)) numbered, each with its address, title and snippet, with the source's focus and region, the query and what its source type shows; the member's texts and the results' words go through the rating's scrub. A number the answer holds that is no result, or a number twice, is refused by the task's gate and asked again once; a second failure (or a call over its budget) is counted `picks_failed` and the search's pages are not read. The picks are cut to `PICKS_PER_SEARCH` (5), the likeliest first, and kept as `pick/<position>.json` (`placeId`, `urls`), counted `pages_picked`. A query never asked has no answer file and no pick. A step run again answers from its pick file.
- **The queue** (`signals_research_queue`, once): the source's start pages first, then each search's picks round by round (every search's likeliest, then every search's next), so that the limit goes to the likeliest of each; an address once; at most `PAGES_PER_HOST` (3) pages of one host (`www.` and the bare host are one), the start pages counted; at most `PAGES_QUEUE_FACTOR` (3) times the limit in all, since a page that fails does not use the limit. Kept as `pages/queue.json`; the step answers a count.
- **A page** (`signals_research_page`, kind `provider`, once for each place in the queue, told how many pages were read before it): at the limit nothing is fetched and `counts.budget_reached` is set once (a run whose searches stopped at their limit has it already); a page whose file is there already is not fetched again; else platform's `readPage` reads it (robots.txt of its host first, asked once a run through one map of rules per run, kept in the process; a PDF file as text; HTML or a PDF only; the egress client refuses `localhost`, a private address or an IP literal a start page may name, so that none is requested). A page with under `THIN_PAGE_CHARS` (200) of text is asked again of the `browser` port when `PROVIDER_BROWSER` is set, which renders only a page robots.txt allowed, and its text replaces the plain page's when longer. The page is kept as `pages/<n>.json` (the address asked and the one it ended on, the source type of the search, title, text, links, `mailto`, `status`, whether the browser rendered it); counts `pages`, `pages_closed` (robots.txt closes it: the page is never requested) and `pages_failed` (no readable page, an egress code, or a redirect onto LinkedIn or a social network, refused before the hop is requested). The step answers `read`, `closed`, `failed`, `skipped` (no such place in the queue) or `budget`.
- **The run's end:** at `SIGNALS_RESEARCH_PAGES_PER_RUN` pages read the run ends `done` with `counts.budget_reached` 1; the pages not read are not kept in this task (the candidates of the pages read are made by the next steps, gamma-wr-046, below). The history holds ids, counts and codes: never a link, a title or a page's text.

Decided here (gamma-wr-045, part 1):
- The pick answers numbers, not links, so that a refused answer is a number that is no result; the gate is the task's own (a retry once), as the triage's, since the answer is only a list and the rest of it is not worth keeping when one number is wrong.
- A search's picks are cut to five, the pages' limit being small; a result is dropped in code by its address alone, before the model reads it.
- The pages' limit counts the pages read, not the ones asked for: a page robots.txt closes or that fails costs nothing but its request, and the queue is bounded by `PAGES_QUEUE_FACTOR` times the limit. The workflow tells each step how many pages were read, so that the limit is the workflow's own tally and a replay repeats it.
- A step's counts follow its file: a step that fails between writing a file and adding its counts leaves the counts one short, never a page read twice.
- A thin page is asked of the browser by a small function of signals' own (`read-page.ts`), as enrichment's company-site reader asks it: enrichment's is private to its site read, and a page here is one the caller names.
- The test hook `useReadPage` (`testing/`) replaces platform's `readPage`, because the web search's results are https addresses and the tests serve their pages over http from 127.0.0.1; it only changes the scheme and host, and the rest is platform's reader.
- LinkedIn, `lnkd.in` and the social networks are refused to platform's `readPage` (its `refuseHost` option, gamma-wr-020's code, changed here) for every request it makes, a redirect hop's too: a page that redirects to one is never requested there; the reader answers the existing `egress_denied_host` (no new code), which the run counts as `pages_failed`. The browser retry cannot take the list (the `browser` port renders one address): its rendered page that ends on one of those hosts is not used.

Open (gamma-wr-045): after a redirect to another host, that host's robots.txt is not read (platform's `readPage`, gamma-wr-020).

### The pages understood and the candidates (gamma-wr-046)

After the pages are read, each page read is understood and its companies become candidates; a candidate's site is read by the next section (gamma-wr-050) and no person is taken (gamma-wr-060). The steps are in `domain/research/` (`read.ts`, `candidates.ts`; the gates in `read-checks.ts`); every file is under `raw/signals/<runId>/`, found by its key.

- **The carry** (`signals_research_carry`, kind `db`, once, first): the candidates earlier runs left `pending` for the source are checked again against the workspace's own company and the never-contact list, and the ones that now fail are dropped with a code (`candidates_carried` counts those kept pending, `dropped_<code>` the others). The next run takes them first: `pendingCandidates(sourceId)` returns them oldest first for the judge (gamma-wr-050).
- **The read** (`signals_research_read`, kind `ai`, once for each page read; `signal_research_read_agent_not_loaded` is not retried): the agent `signals.research-read-page` ([its README](agents/research-read-page/README.md)) gets the source's focus and region, what the page's search source type shows, and the page (address, title, text cut to 20,000 characters, up to 100 links; the text and title go through the rating's scrub); it says what the page is (`company_site`, `company_list`, `notice`, `other`) and the companies it names, each with a name, a link, a place, a line and a quote. The gates run in code after the call, so that a part is refused and the rest kept (`read-checks.ts`): the name must be on the page, and holds no `@`; the link must be among the page's links and a site (`isNotAHomepage`, never a never-fetched host, no IP literal, no user); a link of a listed company onto the list page's own site (its host, a subdomain of it, or a subdomain of the same registrable domain, `sameSite`) is the directory's, so it counts as no link and the company is searched by its name; the place and the quote must be on the page, the quote at least 8 characters and with no email address, link or phone number (a refused quote drops the line with it); a repeated name and, on a company's own site, a second company, are refused. Each refusal is counted `refused_<gate>`; a page with no company kept is `pages_empty`; a failed or over-budget call is `reads_failed`. A company's own site (`company_site`) takes the page's host as its domain. The answer is kept as `candidates/<n>.json` (a step run again answers from it).
- **The candidates** (`signals_research_candidates`, kind `provider`, once for each page read): a company named with no link gets one search by its name and place, through enrichment's counted `searchWeb` (5 results, no country or language) inside the same searches' limit (`searches_asked`, `searches_failed`, `searches_refused_contact`); the site is chosen by enrichment's `pickWebsite` and no other way, and a company still with no site is counted `candidates_no_site` and not kept. Then in one tenant run each company is checked: the workspace's own domain (`getCompanyDomain`) is dropped `own_company`; a domain on the never-contact list (contacts' `suppressedDomains`, the blocklist and the customers and competitors) is dropped `never_contact`, `customer` or `competitor`; a domain the source holds `pending` is `candidates_known`; a domain it read or dropped within `SIGNALS_RESEARCH_RECHECK_DAYS` is `dropped_recent`; the others are kept `pending` (`candidates`). All of this is before any read of the site. A row is one for each source and domain.
- **The counts:** `candidates`, `candidates_carried`, `candidates_known`, `candidates_no_site`, `dropped_recent`, `dropped_own_company`, `dropped_never_contact`, `dropped_customer`, `dropped_competitor`, `pages_empty`, `reads_failed` and `refused_<gate>`. `searches_asked` also counts the searches by name. The history holds ids, counts and codes: never a name, a link or a quote.

Decided here (gamma-wr-046):
- The gates run after the call, not as the task's gate, so that one wrong company does not refuse the page's others.
- The candidates are rows of a tenant table, one for each source and domain, not a file: a later run reads them. `run_id` is a plain id, so that a run's deletion does not delete a candidate. A company with no site is counted and not kept.
- The search by name has no country, no language and no words of its own, so that it fits any trade and country; it shares the plan's searches' counters, so a company named after the limit is counted `candidates_no_site`.
- A drop is a row with a code, so that the same company is not found and dropped again in the days of the re-check; an older row is decided again.
- A refused quote drops the line with it; a company's own site gives its own host, whatever link the model returns.
- Contacts' `suppressedDomains` (new, one read of the entries for domains, a domain's parents counting, a subdomain's entry not holding the parent) and enrichment's `pickWebsite` and `domainOf` (now exported) are the only calls into the other modules.

Open (gamma-wr-046, 2026-11-19): a name that holds a `.com`-style word is not refused, only an `@`; the plan's search limit is shared with the searches by name, so a source with many unlinked companies may have them counted `candidates_no_site` after the limit.

### The company's site, the verdict and the found company (gamma-wr-050)

After its candidates the run reads each company's own site, has the judge answer, decides the verdict in code and keeps the found company. The steps are in `domain/research/` (`judge-site.ts`, `judge.ts`, `found.ts`; the gates in `judge-checks.ts`, the rule in `verdict.ts`); every file is under `raw/signals/<runId>/`, found by its key.

- **The queue** (`signals_research_judge_queue`, kind `db`, once): the source's pending candidates, the earlier runs' first, at most `JUDGE_QUEUE_FACTOR` (3) times the companies' limit, as ids in `judge/queue.json`.
- **The site** (`signals_research_site`, kind `provider`, once for each place in the queue, told how many companies were judged before it): at `SIGNALS_RESEARCH_COMPANIES_PER_RUN` nothing is read and `counts.budget_reached` is set once; else enrichment's `readCompanySite` reads the home page and the preferred pages (robots.txt first, the browser for a page that shows nothing without scripts, at most `SIGNALS_RESEARCH_SITE_MAX_PAGES` pages, never a host `NEVER_FETCHED_HOSTS` names) and the pages go to `site/<n>.json`. - **The judge** (`signals_research_judge`, kind `ai`, 5 minutes, once for each site read; `signal_research_judge_agent_not_loaded` is not retried): the agent `signals.research-judge-company` ([its README](agents/research-judge-company/README.md)) gets the source's focus and region, the audience's must-haves, disqualifiers, industries, size bands and countries, what the page that named the company said, and the site's pages; it answers closed questions (`shown`, `contradicted`, `not_stated`, each with a line and a quote of a page) and where the site puts the company and how it can be reached. The gates run in code and refuse a part, counted `judge_refused_<gate>`; a call the model fails twice drops the candidate `judge_failed`, counted `judges_failed`.
- **The verdict** is code (`verdict.ts`): `not_fit` when the focus or a must-have is contradicted, a disqualifier is shown, or the country is outside the audience's; `fit` when the focus and every must-have are shown; `unsure` otherwise. - **The found company** (`signal_found_companies`, [docs/data.md](docs/data.md)): one row for each source and domain, with the verdict, its reasons (kind, the criterion's text, the line, the quote, the page, the date read), the country, town and state, whether it is one person, and the general address, phone numbers and postal address the site prints. The company also goes to contacts through `upsertCompany`, with its name, website and country stamped as read from its website, so that enrichment does not ask for them again; a business of one person is passed with a headcount of 1, so that contacts keeps its facts on the workspace's row only, and it keeps no address, phone or postal address here. The candidate becomes `read`. No person is created. A company met again after the re-check days is judged again and its row replaced, a member's own mark kept; deleting the source keeps the row under its label.
- **The counts:** `companies_judged`, `companies_fit`, `companies_not_fit`, `companies_unsure`, `sites_not_read`, `judges_failed`, `judge_refused_<gate>` and `budget_reached`. The history holds ids, counts and codes: never a name, an address, a quote or a page's text.

Decided here (gamma-wr-050):
- The gates run after the call, as the page reader's: one invented quote costs its answer, not the company's.
- The model never decides the verdict: it answers closed questions and a page that instructs it can only change an answer the code then checks against the page's own words.
- A disqualifier the site does not state counts for nothing against the company; industry and size add reasons and decide nothing, qualification judges them for each lead.
- The queue is longer than the limit, since a site that cannot be read does not use it.
- `readCompanySite` and platform's `readSite` take the caller's page limit and a `refuseHost`, so that the judge never reads LinkedIn or a social network a site links.
- The price of a company and the plan's second prompt version are gamma-wr-051's, below.

### The price of a company, and what the plan reads (gamma-wr-051)

- **The price** (`signals.researched_company`, unit `company`, `SIGNALS_CREDITS_PER_RESEARCHED_COMPANY` credits; `prices.ts`): a company read and judged costs the same whatever its verdict. ### The work address of a name (gamma-wr-080)
