# signals.research-read-people

The people reader (gamma-wr-060): for one found company, the people its pages name with a role, so that the run can choose among them by role and make the ones with a key the page printed. [README.md "Web research"](../../README.md#the-people-on-the-pages-as-leads-gamma-wr-060) says how a run uses it, [docs/security.md](../../docs/security.md) what the model may see.

- **Task:** `task.ts`: `kind: classification` (extraction: it reads, it never chooses or ranks), no tools, prompt version 2, a budget of 60,000 input tokens (up to 21 pages of 12,000 characters, their links and the company, in a script of three-byte characters) and 12,000 output tokens (100 people of a name, a role, a link, an address, a phone and a place), and no credits. A call over the budget is `ai_budget_exceeded`, which the step counts as `people_reads_failed`. The model is `AI_MODEL_SIGNALS_RESEARCH_READ_PEOPLE`, else the LLM port's first. The task file imports none of the module's registries, and the module never imports it: hosts load it from the sources (core/packages/ai README "Declaring a task"), and `takePeople` takes it from the agent registry.
- **Prompt:** `prompt.v1.md`, the system prompt. The data blocks, in order: `<company>` (its name and its own domain) and `<pages>` (the company's site pages and the page that named it, numbered from 1: address without its query, title, text, links and `mailto:` addresses). A page's words are data: an instruction in one is left out. A changed prompt is a new file, `prompt.v3.md` with `promptVersion: 3`, and runs the evals. Version 2 (gamma-wr-100) adds the `phone`; `prompt.v1.md` stays as history.
- **Schemas:** `schemas/research-read-people.ts`. The answer is a list of people, each with the number of the page, the name and the role exactly as that page writes them, and, only as printed with that person, a link to their LinkedIn profile, an email address, a phone number (a direct line, a mobile or a WhatsApp number, as the page writes it) and a place (each null otherwise).
- **Gates**, in code and after the call (`domain/research/people-checks.ts`), because each refuses a part of the answer and keeps the rest. Each refusal is counted by its name in the run's counts, `people_refused_<gate>`, never by its text:
  - `page`: a page that is not one of the pages: the person is dropped;
  - `name` and `role`: not on that page (compared folded for case, compatibility forms and whitespace), or a name that holds an `@`: the person is dropped;
  - `profile`: a link that is not among that page's links, or is no profile's address (`normalizeLinkedinUrl`): the link is dropped;
  - `email`: an address that is not on that page (its text or a `mailto:` link), or at any domain but the company's own or a subdomain of it (rule 29): dropped;
  - `phone`, `phone_company` and `phone_shared` (gamma-wr-100): a number that is no number by its form (enrichment's `cleanPhone`: its digits, a leading `+`, 7 to 15 digits, no letter or extension) or whose digits are not among a run of digits of that page's text, a date's or a year's groups not counted: the number is dropped; one of the found company's own phones (`samePhone`, national or international way): dropped; a number given to more than one of the people (any way of printing it): dropped for all of them;
  - `place`: a place that is not on that page: dropped; a place on it is given its country by contacts' `countryOf`.

  A person named twice (by name, accents and case aside) is one, with the key and the country the first lacks filled from the second.
- **What the model sees:** the pages as printed, names included, since reading them is its whole job. It is the one call that does, and its decision record, whose subject is the workspace, is erased with any person's erasure; the pick never sees any of it.
- **Evals:** `core/packages/ai/evals/signals.research-read-people/`, synthetic cases of two made-up businesses in two countries and two languages (a cold-room seller in Poland, a coffee roaster in Germany), a page with no one on it, a name with no role, an address at another domain and a page that tries to instruct the model (the fail-safe case: its answer never names the person the instruction asks for). Since version 2: a German team page with numbers written the international and the national way, a Polish contact page whose company line and a number two people share are left out and one number is written with its area code in parentheses, and a page that tries to give a number to a person it invents. The scorer's checks: `outcome`, `fails_safe`, `grounded` (each name and role on their page, each profile among that page's links, each address on the page and at the company's domain, each phone's digits on the page, each place on the page), `recall` and `precision` over the names, and `phones` (the share of the people named whose phone is the one the case expects, none for anyone else). On the fake they run in `core/packages/ai/tests/evals.test.ts` with `EVAL_LIVE` unset; live (`EVAL_LIVE=1`, by hand, billed) after a prompt change, the owner's. The cases' profile links are on a reserved host, so the scorer reads a profile's slug only; `tests/research-people.test.ts` checks the real host.
