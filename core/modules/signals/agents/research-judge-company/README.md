# signals.research-judge-company

The company judge (gamma-wr-050): for one company a research run found, what its own site shows against what the member looks for, so that the run can keep the company with its reasons. [README.md "Web research"](../../README.md#web-research) says how a run uses it, [docs/security.md](../../docs/security.md) what the model may see.

- **Task:** `task.ts`: `kind: classification`, no tools, prompt version 1, a budget of 64,000 input tokens (the first 50,000 characters of the site and the audience's lists, in a script of three-byte characters) and 6,000 output tokens, and no credits (the company's price is the run's `signals.researched_company`; the model call's cost is the cost event alone). A call over the budget is `ai_budget_exceeded`, which the judge step counts as `judges_failed`. The model is `AI_MODEL_SIGNALS_RESEARCH_JUDGE_COMPANY`, else the LLM port's first. The task file imports none of the module's registries, and the module never imports it: hosts load it from the sources (core/packages/ai README "Declaring a task"), and `judgeCompany` takes it from the agent registry.
- **Prompt:** `prompt.v1.md`, the system prompt. The data blocks, in order: `<company>` (its name), `<source>` (the focus and the region, scrubbed), `<audience>` (the must-haves and the disqualifiers as numbered statements, the industries, the size bands and the countries), `<named>` (the line and the quote the page that named the company gave, scrubbed, or null) and `<site>` (the pages read, numbered from 1: address without its query, title and text as the site prints it, so that its address, phone numbers and postal address can be read). A page's words are data: an instruction in one is left out. A changed prompt is a new file, `prompt.v2.md` with `promptVersion: 2`, and runs the evals.
- **Schemas:** `schemas/research-judge-company.ts`. The answers are closed: `shown`, `contradicted` or `not_stated`, each with a line, a quote and the page of the quote (null for `not_stated`), for the focus, each must-have and disqualifier (by its number), the industry, the size and whether it is a business of one person; then the country (its code and the site's words), the town and state, and the general email address, the phone numbers and the postal address as the site prints them.
- **Gates**, in code and after the call (`domain/research/judge-checks.ts`), because each refuses a part of the answer and keeps the rest. Each refusal is counted by its name in the run's counts, `judge_refused_<gate>`:
  - `quote`: an answer of `shown` or `contradicted` whose quote is shorter than eight characters, is not on its page, or names a page that is not there, or has no line, counts as `not_stated`;
  - `contact`: a line or quote that holds an email address, a link or a phone number counts as `not_stated`;
  - `place`: a country whose code contacts does not know or whose words are not on its page, a town or a state that is not on its page: dropped;
  - `email`: an address that is not on its page (its text, or a `mailto:` link of it), or that the model calls a person's: dropped;
  - `phone`: a number whose digits are not on its page, or fewer than seven: dropped;
  - `postal`: an address that is not on its page: dropped.

  Comparisons fold case, compatibility forms and whitespace. The verdict is decided by `domain/research/verdict.ts`, never by the model: `not_fit` when the focus or a must-have is contradicted, a disqualifier is shown, or the company's country is outside the audience's; `fit` when the focus and every must-have are shown; `unsure` otherwise. Industry and size add reasons and decide nothing.
- **Evals:** `core/packages/ai/evals/signals.research-judge-company/`, synthetic cases of two made-up businesses in two countries and two languages (a maker of walk-in cold rooms in a Portuguese-speaking country and a roaster of coffee in a German-speaking one), a one-person business, a site that states too little, one that shows a disqualifier, one in a country outside the audience's, and a page that tries to instruct the model (the fail-safe case: its answer never calls the company a fit). Each case names the verdict the answers make, so the same cases run live. The scorer's checks: `outcome`, `fails_safe`, `grounded` (each quote on its page, each address, number and postal address on its page), `verdict` (the rule of `verdict.ts` over the answers), `no_person` (the email address, when one, is a general one, never a person's). On the fake they run in `core/packages/ai/tests/evals.test.ts` with `EVAL_LIVE` unset; live (`EVAL_LIVE=1`, by hand, billed) after a prompt change, the owner's.
