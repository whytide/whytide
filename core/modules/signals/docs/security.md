# signals: security

- **Personal data:** a source's config holds a LinkedIn post's URL, which names its author: business contact data, typed by a member, stored without the link's query (tracking ids, the sharer's among them); a short link is not stored, the post's URL it redirects to is. A creator source's config holds the creator's profile URL, a person's, stored the same way; a competitor source's, a company page's URL and the name the tester gives, which name a company, not a person (gamma-p5-025). A run's candidates hold the engagers' business fields only (no field beyond those, checked by `tests/tenancy.test.ts`); its raw pages and profiles live in the files port under the workspace's prefix until the run's end deletes them (72 hours at most), never in the database or in Temporal's history, which carries ids, the post URL, keys and counts. A posts kind's phrase, an author's link and the vendor's cursor travel in request and page files under `raw/signals/<runId>/posts/`, deleted with the run; its history carries the posts' unnamed URLs, which name no author (gamma-p5-020).
- **What a run keeps:** a rejected candidate (the hard filters, the country since title-check-ai) leaves contacts' salted hash with its drop reasons, kept 90 days, and nothing else, while a person outside the ICP's titles whose country fits is kept as a lead; a company page, the workspace's own staff, the competitors' staff the noise rules drop, and the recruiters and job seekers the triage skips (or, for a batch it could not answer, the rules drop) leave nothing in signals' tables (gamma-p5-030, signals-triage-ai); no table holds their name, URL or headline after the run (the worker's `tests/signals-process.test.ts` scans every table), and the triage's decision record holds their headline, scrubbed, in its raw prompt (below). A recruiter or a job seeker the triage reads is kept as a pending lead when the country fits, as any person. A kept candidate becomes a person with business fields only, its lead, an event and its evidence: the post, the person's own comment and its first 500 characters, the reaction and the person's name; post engagers never fetch the post's text or other people's comments. A posts kind keeps an author's own post as their excerpt, and the first 500 characters of the post a commenter or reactor engaged with as `post_excerpt`, someone else's words, which only signals' rating will read (gamma-p5-030); it stores each post by its unnamed URL. The excerpt and the comment are kept for the kind's lookback from `captured_at` (30 days for post engagers and own posts, 14 for a topic; every lookback 30 days at most), then the daily reduction nulls them, `post_excerpt` and the event's `relevance_rationale` too (README "Reduction"); the URL, the author's name and the reaction stay. No refresh, second engagement or re-import moves `captured_at`. `erasePerson` deletes a person's events and evidence, disables the post engagers sources on their posts and the creator sources on their profile, stopping their open runs and emptying their URL and label, and gives others' evidence on their posts the unnamed URL without the post's excerpt (`tests/erase.test.ts`). A topic phrase on a special-category theme is refused before anything is stored or searched (`signal_topic_blocked`).
- **Who may do what:** `signals.read` lists sources and runs, `signals.write` creates, changes and deletes them, `signals.run` runs one now or stops one, once per `Idempotency-Key`, all three for every role; a change, delete, run or stop of a source then asks platform's `canWriteOwned` (gamma-p6-015): its creator, or a holder of `signals.manage` (Owner, Admin), else 403 `forbidden` before anything is written; `leads.read` (contacts') reads a person's evidence; every query runs in the request's or the activity's tenant run, scoped to its workspace, and the five tables pass the two-workspace isolation test. The worker starts a run in the delivery's workspace, never the payload's.
- **The source checker** (gamma-p5-045): playbooks calls it before it links sources to an agent, in its route's tenant run; it reads only the caller's workspace's rows, by the explicit `workspace_id` and RLS, so another workspace's source id answers `found: false`, both ids null, its account never read or named (`tests/source-checker.test.ts`). It answers ids only, the source's sender account and that account's owner, never a config, a label or a URL, and writes nothing. A link is playbooks' row; signals deletes one only through `detachSource`, when its source is deleted, and a run reads its agent's audience by the agent's id, in the run's workspace.
- **Bounds:** at most `SIGNALS_MAX_ENABLED_SOURCES` enabled per agent, and as many in no agent (gamma-p5-045), and at most `SIGNALS_MAX_TRACKED_PER_ACCOUNT` enabled competitor and creator sources per sender account (RB-4.2.1), both counted under an advisory lock. A source reads only through its sender's own account, which must be the workspace's at the create, and the caller's own unless she holds `signals.manage` (gamma-p6-015, 403 `forbidden` before anything is written, on a create, an import and its check), and connected at each run; each read, a page's or a profile's, is reserved from the account's daily cap inside its working hours first (security §1), within the run's read budget and with `SIGNALS_READS_KEPT_FOR_SENDS` of the day kept for the send path since gamma-p5-040, so the daily runs of an account's sources never take the reads its sends need, and a profile's credit after its slot, before the read (a run started before beta-p1-090: its block of credits before the first read).
- **The screen:** members with `signals.read` see each source's post, company page or creator profile link (opened in a new tab with `rel="noreferrer"`, so LinkedIn gets no address of ours) and the label and status of its sender account, from linkedin's list, and for a waiting run, in the list and the runs panel, its account's reads today and next window from linkedin's usage route (`linkedin.read`: counts and times only); the browser reads them through the web's API client with the member's session, and writes go through the same routes and checks as the API.
- **The evidence panel:** members with `leads.read` see, on a lead's page, read in the browser through the web's API client with the member's session, its events' evidence (the person's comment, their name, the reaction) and the labels of its sources; the post opens in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, and only for an `http(s)` URL.
- **Leaving the system:** the reads go to LinkedIn through linkedin's adapter, the reference opened in the worker only; a create with a short link sends one GET to `lnkd.in` from the api through the platform's `fetchSafe` (its path only, no cookie, five seconds, a kilobyte), whose `Location` is read and never fetched, and no log line, error or span carries the link, the `Location` or the post's URL (the span: the host only); events carry ids only; qualification's batch sends the kept persons' business fields, anonymized, to the model, those outside the ICP's titles too since title-check-ai. Signals' rating sends it, through ai's `callAgent`, Company Knowledge's one-liner, products and pain points and, per event, its kind, the person's role, their words and the post's excerpt, links, email addresses, phone numbers and the engager's name replaced by placeholders: never a name, profile URL, headline, company or member reference (the worker's `tests/signals-rating.test.ts` reads the fake's calls); the decision record keeps that input 30 days as its raw prompt, and its output the rationales, which the prompt asks to quote and name no one; `erasePerson` erases the records the person's rated events name (gamma-p5-030). Signals' triage (signals-triage-ai) sends, through `callAgent`, the ICP's titles, seniorities, industries and must-haves and, per candidate left after the code drops, its index, its role and its headline, scrubbed the same way: never a name, a profile URL, a company field or a member reference, though a headline as written may name an employer (the worker's `tests/signals-triage.test.ts` reads the fake's calls). Its decision record's subject is the workspace; it keeps that input, the headlines of the skipped and the read alike, until its `raw_purge_after` (30 days) as its raw prompt, and its output, indexes, decisions and codes only. `erasePerson` erases every triage record of the workspace. No headline or name reaches the history, which holds candidate ids, a log line, a span or an event (the same test reads the history, the logs and the outbox).
- **Importing sources** (sources-csv-import): `signals.write`, as a create; a file of 1 MB and 200 data rows at most, `.csv` by name, refused when it starts as a ZIP, holds a NUL byte or is not UTF-8, its body 2 MB at most; csv-parse 7.0.3 reads every cell as a string, never evaluated, a record of 64 KiB at most. Each row goes through the kind's schema and the create, as `POST /sources` (a short link's one GET to `lnkd.in` per post row, as a create's); only the file's name and the counts are kept. The errors' download is built in the browser from the row numbers, codes and column names, never a cell.
- **The research plan** (gamma-wr-040): the model reads the member's own texts (Company Knowledge, the audience's, the source's focus, region and languages) as untrusted data in labelled blocks, scrubbed of links, email addresses and phone numbers, and holds nothing about a person; the prompt says that no query or word names a person, and the evals check that no address, link or number is in the answer. Its answer is gated in code before it is kept: a source type, a country and a language outside what the run allows, and a query the search port would refuse, are never kept or searched. The plan's words and queries are business text a member could read, kept in two tenant tables with the two-workspace isolation test; they are never in a log line, a span, a metric label or a workflow's history, which hold ids, counts and codes. No new network call: the model call goes through ai's `callAgent` (the LLM port, a decision record and a cost event per call), the searches come with the next task through enrichment's counted search.
- **The research search** (gamma-wr-041): the queries are the plan's, written by the model from the member's own texts, and a code gate refuses one that holds an email address, a link or a phone number before it is searched, so no address, link or number of the member's texts leaves in a query. The search goes through enrichment's counted `searchWeb` only, to the adapters of `PROVIDER_WEB_SEARCH`; a failure's code reaches the counts, never its error or its body. The answers are files under the workspace's `raw/signals/<runId>/search/`, 72 hours; the results are untrusted data for the next task. No query, word or result reaches a log, a span, a metric label or the workflow's history. The plan's route needs `signals.read` and answers the workspace's own source only.
- **The results picked and the pages read** (gamma-wr-045): a search's results (their addresses, titles and snippets) and a page's text are untrusted data: the pick agent reads them in a labelled block, scrubbed of links, email addresses and phone numbers, with a prompt that says they are data, never instructions, and its answer is numbers that code checks against the results (a number that is no result is refused, none twice, at most five kept); nothing the model says is fetched, a link is only ever one of the results'. Every fetch is platform's `readPage` or the `browser` port behind it: robots.txt first, the egress client refusing a private, loopback or link-local address, `localhost`, a port but the scheme's own and a redirect to one; LinkedIn, `lnkd.in` and the social networks are never asked for (a result or a start page on one is dropped in code; a redirect hop to one is refused before it is requested, `refuseHost` of platform's reader, and the browser's rendered page that ends on one is not used). A PDF file is parsed by `unpdf` within platform's bounds (5 MB, 50 pages). A page's text and links are kept in the files port under the workspace's prefix for 72 hours, and in no table, log line, span, metric label or workflow history: the steps answer statuses and counts. No person is taken from a page here.

- **The pages understood and the candidates** (gamma-wr-046): a page's text, title and links are untrusted data, read by the agent in labelled blocks and scrubbed; the prompt says they are data and never instructions. The agent takes no person: no name of one, no email address, phone number or profile link; its answer is gated in code (name, link, place and quote on the page, a quote with an email address refused) before any is kept. A company's site comes only from the page's own links or from enrichment's `pickWebsite`. The candidates table holds business pages' words (a company's name, its site, a line and a quote from a page), with the two-workspace isolation test; none is in a log line, a span, a metric label or a history. A candidate is dropped before any read of its site when it is the workspace's own company or on the never-contact list, customers and competitors included.

- **The company's site and the verdict** (gamma-wr-050): a site's pages are untrusted data, read by the judge in labelled blocks with the prompt saying they are data and never instructions; its answers are closed lists, the verdict is a code rule over the answers kept after the gates (a quote not on its page counts as `not_stated`), so that a page cannot talk the run into a verdict. The judge takes no person: an email address a model calls a person's is dropped, and a business of one person keeps no address, phone or postal address in `signal_found_companies`. The site is read only through enrichment's reader (robots.txt, the egress client; LinkedIn and the social networks refused); no name, address, quote or page text reaches a log, a span, a metric label or the history. The table has the two-workspace isolation test.

- **What the plan reads** (gamma-wr-051): the plan's input gains counts by query, source type and verdict and the towns and states of the fitting companies, each a count, an id or a place word scrubbed as other texts are; never a company's name, site, line or quote. A town comes from a company's own page, so it is data: the prompt says the counts and towns never change its rules, and an eval case holds an instruction in a town. The price's credit is keyed by run and candidate, so a retried step charges once, and an ended run releases what is open.
- **The people on the pages** (gamma-wr-060): a company's pages are untrusted data, read in labelled blocks with the prompt saying an instruction in a page is data; the read answers only what a page prints with a person (the name and the role as written, a profile link among the page's links, an address on the page) and code refuses the rest after the call: a name or role not on the page, a link that is no profile's address, an address at any domain but the company's own (rule 29), a place not on the page. The pick sees a number and the role text of each person, with every name, link, address and phone number scrubbed out of it (rule 35), and the audience's titles and seniorities; it never judges a person. A person is made only with a key the page printed (a profile link or an address at the company's domain; contacts keeps the address as its hash only and no contact point is made) and only after a match by name finds no one at the company already, so one human stays one person (RB-17.1.2). The people of a company are at most `SIGNALS_RESEARCH_PEOPLE_PER_COMPANY` (3, a privacy cap of 1 to 10). No name, role, link or address reaches a log line, a span, a metric label, the workflow's history or a run file; a person not made leaves no row; the research event holds the person's id and the page's address, and `erasePerson` removes it with its evidence. The one place a name is kept besides contacts is the read call's decision record, ai's own, which the workspace's erasure removes. The tables have the two-workspace isolation test.


- **The Companies found routes** (gamma-wr-110): the list needs `signals.read`, Not a fit `signals.write` and then `canWriteOwned` about the company's source (its creator or `signals.manage`), and the CSV contacts' `exports.read` (Owner and Admin), all in the request's tenant run, so another workspace's company answers not found and its rows are never read (`tests/tenancy.test.ts`). A company's name, address, quote and page are business text from the web, shown to members only: never in a log line, a span, a metric label or a history. The CSV writes every cell through contacts' `csvLine` (formula cells neutralised, security §2), carries the company's own general address, phones and postal address and no lead's, streams without storing a file, and is sent `no-store`. The member's mark keeps their user id and the time. The plan's `not_fit` block holds the source's own marked companies' names, domains and places, scrubbed, as untrusted data; the prompt says it is data, an eval holds an instruction in a name, and no new network call is made.

- **The lookup of a new lead** (gamma-wr-070): the people step asks enrichment one email lookup for each new lead, with the address its page printed at the company's domain when there is one; enrichment holds it only while the lookup runs and keeps it only on a verifier's `valid` (enrichment's docs/security.md). No address reaches this module's rows, an event, a count, a log line or the workflow's history.
- **A profile by search** (gamma-wr-070): the one query of the run that holds a person's name (the owner's exception of 2026-11-16), the name and the company's name only, on linkedin.com, through the counted search; the name stays in memory, never in a log line, span, metric label, history or the evidence, and the results are run files of 72 hours. Code decides the match (a profile's address that names both, never two different ones) and the address is never fetched: no request goes to linkedin.com. The evidence holds `found_by = search` and no name.
- **A person's phone** (gamma-wr-100, personal data): the people read's prompt version 2 also answers a number a page prints with a person as theirs, a direct line, a mobile or a WhatsApp number, never the company's line, a number printed with several people, a private or home number; code refuses what the page does not hold (its form by enrichment's `cleanPhone`; its digits among a run of the page's digits), the found company's own phones and a number given to more than one person, and counts each refusal by gate, never by text. A kept number goes to enrichment's `keepPhone` (never-contact persons get none; one number on one person) with its page; it is shown to the member on the lead page and is never called or messaged by Whytide. No number reaches a log line, a span, a metric label, an event, a count or the history; the model's call is the same call as the read's, whose decision record (subject the workspace) the erasure removes. A phone is no key and makes no person.
- **A company's managers by search** (gamma-wr-090): the query holds the company's name and the plan's role words, never a person's name (the plan's gate refuses a word that holds an address, a link or a number; the prompt says no name). Only the port's results are read: a result is taken by code when its address is a profile's and it names the company, and the address is never fetched, so no request goes to linkedin.com. The reading agent sees titles and snippets (names included, its whole job) and never an address; its decision record has the workspace as its subject and is erased with any person's erasure; its answer is gated in code against the results. The pick sees roles only (rule 35). A result may be out of date, so the evidence says where the person came from and shows the result's title, for the member to check before approving. Names stay in memory: no query, name, role or link is in a log line, span, metric label or the workflow's history; the evidence holds the result's title (a person's data, erased with the person and reduced with the excerpts) and the results are run files of 72 hours. A result, a title or a snippet that tries to instruct the model is data: an eval holds one.
