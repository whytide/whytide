# signals: data

Five tenant tables in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/signals.prisma`, created by the migrations `20261022150800_signals_sources_and_runs`, `20261022193000_signals_candidates` and `20261022212000_signals_events_and_evidence`; `20261022196000_signals_run_cancelled` adds the status `cancelled`, `20261028130900_signals_run_stop` a run's `workflow_id` and `stop_requested_at`. Ids are `src_<ULID>`, `run_<ULID>`, `cand_<ULID>`, `sev_<ULID>` and `evd_<ULID>` (`ids.ts`), an event's ULID its candidate's since beta-p1-090, the subject of the candidate's credit; times are `timestamptz`.

| Table | Columns | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| `signal_sources` | `workspace_id`, `kind` (a registered kind's name), `config` (jsonb, the kind's config), `label`, `sender_account_id`, `enabled` (true), `created_by` (a user id), `created_at`, `last_run_at`, `watermark` (jsonb, the reader's position) | index `(workspace_id, enabled)` |
| `signal_runs` | `workspace_id`, `source_id`, `status` (`signal_run_status`: `scheduled`, `running`, `waiting`, `done`, `failed`, `cancelled`), `started_at`, `finished_at`, `counts` (jsonb, `{}`; a run's `{ pages, read, staged, created }`, then the processing's `profiles_read`, `attached` and drops `dropped_company`, `dropped_own`, `dropped_hashed`, `dropped_known`, `dropped_filtered` and `dropped_unread`, each when not 0), `error` (a code), `workflow_id` (the id of the workflow that runs it, recorded at its start; null before, and for a run started before fix-run-stop), `stop_requested_at` (null; when a stop was first asked) | index `(workspace_id, source_id, started_at DESC)`; `(workspace_id, source_id)` references `signal_sources (workspace_id, id)` `ON DELETE CASCADE` |
| `signal_candidates` | `workspace_id`, `run_id`, `provider_member_ref`, `profile_url`, `name`, `headline`, `reaction`, `comment_text` (null), `engaged_at`, `raw_ref` (the page file's key) | unique `(workspace_id, run_id, provider_member_ref, reaction)`; `(workspace_id, run_id)` references `signal_runs (workspace_id, id)` `ON DELETE CASCADE` |
| `signal_events` | `workspace_id`, `person_id` (contacts' person), `source_id` and `run_id` (null once deleted), `kind` (`post_engagement`), `dedupe_key` (SHA-256 of kind, member reference, post and reaction), `occurred_at`, `created_at` | unique `(workspace_id, dedupe_key)`; index `(workspace_id, person_id, occurred_at DESC)`; `source_id` and `run_id` reference `signal_sources (id)` and `signal_runs (id)` `ON DELETE SET NULL` |
| `evidence` | `workspace_id`, `event_id`, `url` (the post), `excerpt` (the comment's first 500 characters, null), `comment_text` (null), `reaction`, `author` (the engager's name), `captured_at` (set once, at the event's creation), `reduced_at` (null; once past its kind's lookback, the reduction sets it and nulls `excerpt` and `comment_text`) | unique `(workspace_id, event_id)`; `(workspace_id, event_id)` references `signal_events (workspace_id, id)` `ON DELETE CASCADE` |

No foreign key to `workspaces` (tenancy's) or to `persons` (contacts'). Personal data, marked `/// pii`: `signal_sources.config`, a post's URL names its author (PII class business contact; provenance: the user who created the source); a candidate's member reference, profile URL, name, headline and comment, and an evidence row's post URL, excerpt, comment and author (business contact; provenance: the post's engagers, read through the sender's account). Deleting a source deletes it, its runs and their candidates, and keeps its events and evidence; `erasePerson` deletes a person's. Every run's end, a failed or cancelled run's too, deletes its candidates and its files, `w/<workspace>/raw/signals/<runId>/`: the pages, `<page>.json`, and each profile's request and profile, `profiles/<candidateId>.request.json` and `.json`; the bucket's `raw/` lifecycle deletes what a failed delete leaves after 72 hours.
