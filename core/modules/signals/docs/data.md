# signals: data

Ten tenant tables in `core/packages/db/prisma/schema/signals.prisma`, created by the migrations `20261022150800_signals_sources_and_runs`, `20261022193000_signals_candidates` and `20261022212000_signals_events_and_evidence`; `20261022196000_signals_run_cancelled` adds the status `cancelled`, `20261028130900_signals_run_stop` a run's `workflow_id` and `stop_requested_at`, `20261031000307_signals_post_sources` a candidate's `object_url` and `post_excerpt`, its new unique key, and evidence's `post_excerpt` and its index on `url`, `20261031021832_signals_event_ratings` an event's rating and its index, `20261031022621_signals_run_budget` a run's `quota_day` and `read_budget` (gamma-p5-040), `20261102204946_signals_source_imports` the table `signal_source_imports` and a source's `import_id` (sources-csv-import), and `20261118203959_signals_source_account_optional` makes `sender_account_id` nullable (gamma-wr-030), `20261118212536_signals_research_plans` adds the plans' and queries' tables (gamma-wr-040) and `20261118224754_signals_research_candidates` the candidates' (gamma-wr-046); `20261119010000_signals_research_people` (gamma-wr-060, expand only) makes evidence's `reaction` and `author` nullable, gives an event its `found_company_id` and a found company its `people_taken_at`. Ids are `src_<ULID>`, `run_<ULID>`, `cand_<ULID>`, `sev_<ULID>`, `evd_<ULID>` and `simp_<ULID>`, `rpl_<ULID>`, `rqy_<ULID>` and `rcd_<ULID>` (`ids.ts`), an event's ULID its candidate's since beta-p1-090, the subject of the candidate's credit; times are `timestamptz`.

No foreign key to `workspaces` (tenancy's) or to `persons` (contacts'). Personal data, marked `/// pii`: `signal_sources.config`, a post's URL names its author and a creator source's profile URL the creator (PII class business contact; provenance: the user who created the source; a competitor's page and name name a company); a candidate's member reference, profile URL, name, headline, comment, post and post excerpt, and an evidence row's post URL, excerpt, comment, post excerpt and author (business contact; provenance: the post's engagers, read through the sender's account); an event's `relevance_rationale` (business contact; provenance: the model's rating of those words). Deleting a source deletes it, its runs and their candidates, and keeps its events and evidence; `erasePerson` deletes a person's and scrubs their URL from sources and others' evidence (README "Reduction"). The triage keeps no table of its own: its decisions are ai's decision records (task `signals.triage-engagers`, subject the workspace, the scrubbed headlines in the raw prompt until its purge), which `erasePerson` erases, all of the workspace's (README "Triage"). Every run's end, a failed or cancelled run's too, deletes its candidates and its files, `w/<workspace>/raw/signals/<runId>/`: the pages, `<page>.json`, each profile's request and profile, `profiles/<candidateId>.request.json` and `.json`, and a posts kind's `posts/<page>.request.json`, `posts/<page>.json` and `posts/<n>/<page>.json`; the bucket's `raw/` lifecycle deletes what a failed delete leaves after 72 hours. A research run's search answers, `search/<position>.json` (gamma-wr-041: `placeId`, `country`, `language`, `adapter`, `results`), are the exception: no end deletes them, since gamma-wr-045 reads them, and the bucket's lifecycle deletes them after 72 hours; they hold business pages' titles, links and snippets, no person's profile.

The run's files under `raw/signals/<runId>/` for a research run (gamma-wr-045; 72 hours, README "Web research"): `search/<position>.json` (gamma-wr-041), `pick/<position>.json` (`placeId`, `urls`: the picks of that search), `pages/queue.json` (`pages`: `{ url, placeId }`, the start pages' `placeId` null) and `pages/<n>.json` for the `n`th page of the queue that was read (`url`, `finalUrl`, `placeId`, `title`, `text`, `links`, `mailto`, `status`, `rendered`). `candidates/<n>.json` (gamma-wr-046; `pageUrl`, `placeId`, `companies`: the page's companies as the gates kept them, with the sites found by name) is the step's file for the `n`th page read. `judge/queue.json` (gamma-wr-050; `candidates`: ids), `site/<n>.json` (`candidateId`, `pages`: `url`, `title`, `text`, `mailto`) and `verdict/<n>.json` (the gated answers, the verdict and the reasons) are the judge's. The candidates are kept in a table (`signal_research_candidates`, above), not in a file: the run's files hold what a step reads next, and a candidate is read by a later run.

A Web research event (gamma-wr-060) is one row of `signal_events` (`kind` `web_research`, the person's id, the source, the run and the found company, no rating) and one of `evidence` (the page's address without its query, and nothing of the person). A person who is not picked, or who has no key the page printed, leaves no row anywhere: the people's names, roles, links and addresses are read, picked and made inside one step and never kept by signals, apart from the read call's decision record (ai's table, subject the workspace, which `erasePerson` removes, with every other record whose subject is the workspace). The run's file `people/queue.json` (`companies`: found companies' ids) holds no person.
