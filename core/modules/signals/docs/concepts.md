# signals: concepts

- **Source**: what a workspace watches for buying signals, a row of `signal_sources` with a kind, the kind's config, a label and the sender account whose own LinkedIn account reads it. Enabled when created, in no agent; at most `SIGNALS_MAX_ENABLED_SOURCES` of an agent's are enabled at once, and as many of those in no agent (gamma-p5-045).
- **Agent** (gamma-p5-045): playbooks' agent a source is linked to, on the agent's page, one at most. A source's **group** is its agent, or no agent: a group's enabled sources count together against `SIGNALS_MAX_ENABLED_SOURCES`, and an account's reads are shared between the groups first. A run filters by its agent's audience, a source in no agent by the workspace's default. Deleting a source removes its link. A paused agent's sources read nothing, whatever the reason (paused-agent-sources): no run of them starts reading, the pause stops their open runs, and a resume starts nothing at once.
- **Source checker**: what playbooks asks before it links a source, signals' answer for each id: found in the caller's workspace or not, the sender account it reads through and that account's owner.
- **Kind**: what a source watches and how it is read, a folder under `sources/` that declares its config schema, its schedule (the instants a source runs at, from the time it was saved), its reader, its evidence and its lookback. The registry holds every kind by name; a source's kind is checked against it on every write. `post_engagers`, one LinkedIn post's engagers, runs at save, then three and seven days later, within its lookback; the posts kinds (gamma-p5-020), `topic_engagement`, the posts that hold a phrase, and `my_post_engagement`, the sender account's own posts, run at their save, as do `competitor_engagement`, a company page's posts, and `creator_engagement`, one member's posts (gamma-p5-025); the four then run daily (gamma-p5-040).
- **Posts reader**: a posts kind's reader: the run reads the source's posts with linkedin's `linkedin_read_posts`, a page at a time, then each post's engagers; the kind says whose posts, whether their authors count, and which reactions it keeps. A post goes by its **unnamed URL**, `…/feed/update/urn:li:<kind>:<id>`, which names no author.
- **Tracked source**: a competitor or creator source, whose posts reader reads one author's posts, a company page's or a member's; a sender account has at most `SIGNALS_MAX_TRACKED_PER_ACCOUNT` enabled, and one more is refused (`signal_tracked_limit`).
- **Fire time**: an instant a source's kind schedules it to run at, counted from its save; for post engagers, at save, then three and seven days later. Its next fire times are those after its last run; a disabled source has none.
- **Daily run** and **read budget** (gamma-p5-040): a daily kind's source runs once per quota day of its sender account, from the hourly tick, when its account's window is open and more than 20 reads are left; each run reads within its budget, its share of the account's reads left less the 20 kept for the send path, split between the groups first (gamma-p5-045), its pages taking half of it at most so that it reads profiles too, and a run its budget, its page limit, the cap or the 200 ends is done with `budget_reached`, the next day's run reading on from the same cut.
- **Run**: one reading of a source, a row of `signal_runs`: `scheduled`, `running`, `waiting` (for the read quota or the working hours), `done`, `failed` with an error code, or `cancelled`, with its times and counts; the workflow `signals_run` does it, on the source's schedule or by hand.
- **Open run**: a run `running` or `waiting`; while a source has one, a run by hand is refused (`signal_run_open`) and the screen's Run now is disabled. A waiting run's **resume time** is the next start of its sender account's working hours, when it reads again.
- **Stop**: asked of a run that has not ended, it ends the run `cancelled`: the worker cancels its workflow, or its start does, when it had not started. Deleting a source stops its open runs.
- **Watermark** and **cut**: the newest `engagedAt` a done run staged, per post for a posts kind, kept on the source (a run that ends early keeps the old one); a run stages only what came at the cut or after it, the cut being the watermark or, when later or absent, the lookback's start; the one at the watermark is staged again, and dropped by its dedupe key.
- **Candidate**: someone a reader found and what they did, business fields only, staged in `signal_candidates` until the processing keeps or drops it.
- **Noise filter**: the drops before the hard filters: company pages, the workspace's own staff, a competitor's staff, the hashed, and engagements already known. Recruiters and job seekers are the triage's since signals-triage-ai, the noise rules' only for a batch it could not answer.
- **Triage** (signals-triage-ai): the AI's read of the candidates' headlines against the ICP, 25 a call, after the noise filter and before any profile read. It skips only a recruiter or a job seeker the ICP does not target (`dropped_triage`) and orders the reads: authors and commenters, then the headlines that do the work of an ICP title, then the rest. A batch it cannot answer is decided by the rules, as before it.
- **Hard filter**: qualification's, on the read profile, the country alone here (the size is unknown); a miss is `dropped_filtered` with a hash. The title is no filter since title-check-ai: a person outside the ICP's titles is stored as a lead, which the assessment judges.
- **Event**: what a kept person did, a row of `signal_events`, once per dedupe key (its kind, the person, the post and the reaction); **evidence**: what a lead's page shows of it, the post, the person's comment, the reaction and the author; once its kind's lookback has passed since its capture, **reduced**: the comment and its excerpt gone, the rest kept.
- **Price per engager**: what a run charges, since beta-p1-090: `signals.engager`, a credit per engager whose profile it reads (`SIGNALS_CREDITS_PER_ENGAGER`, 1 by default), committed when the profile comes back and released otherwise. The pages of engagers, and a candidate dropped before its read, cost nothing. A run started before it pays `signals.engager_read`, a block per 30 engagers read.
