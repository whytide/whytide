# signals: concepts

- **Source**: what a workspace watches for buying signals, a row of `signal_sources` with a kind, the kind's config, a label and the sender account whose own LinkedIn account reads it. Enabled when created; at most `SIGNALS_MAX_ENABLED_SOURCES` are enabled at once.
- **Kind**: what a source watches and how it is read, a folder under `sources/` that declares its config schema, its schedule (the instants a source runs at, from the time it was saved), its reader, its evidence and its lookback. The registry holds every kind by name; a source's kind is checked against it on every write. Alpha's one kind is `post_engagers`, one LinkedIn post's engagers, run at save, then three and seven days later.
- **Fire time**: an instant a source's kind schedules it to run at, counted from its save; for post engagers, at save, then three and seven days later. Its next fire times are those after its last run; a disabled source has none.
- **Run**: one reading of a source, a row of `signal_runs`: `scheduled`, `running`, `waiting` (for the read quota or the working hours), `done`, `failed` with an error code, or `cancelled`, with its times and counts; the workflow `signals_run` does it, on the source's schedule or by hand.
- **Open run**: a run `running` or `waiting`; while a source has one, a run by hand is refused (`signal_run_open`) and the screen's Run now is disabled. A waiting run's **resume time** is the next start of its sender account's working hours, when it reads again.
- **Stop**: asked of a run that has not ended, it ends the run `cancelled`: the worker cancels its workflow, or its start does, when it had not started. Deleting a source stops its open runs.
- **Watermark** and **cut**: the newest `engagedAt` a done run staged, kept on the source (a run that ends early keeps the old one); a run stages only what came at the cut or after it, the cut being the watermark or, when later or absent, the lookback's start; the one at the watermark is staged again, and dropped by its dedupe key.
- **Candidate**: someone a reader found and what they did, business fields only, staged in `signal_candidates` until the processing keeps or drops it.
- **Noise filter**: the drops before the hard filters: company pages, the workspace's own staff, the hashed, and engagements already known.
- **Event**: what a kept person did, a row of `signal_events`, once per dedupe key (its kind, the person, the post and the reaction); **evidence**: what a lead's page shows of it, the post, the person's comment, the reaction and the author; once its kind's lookback has passed since its capture, **reduced**: the comment and its excerpt gone, the rest kept.
- **Price per engager**: what a run charges, since beta-p1-090: `signals.engager`, a credit per engager whose profile it reads (`SIGNALS_CREDITS_PER_ENGAGER`, 1 by default), committed when the profile comes back and released otherwise. The pages of engagers, and a candidate dropped before its read, cost nothing. A run started before it pays `signals.engager_read`, a block per 30 engagers read.
